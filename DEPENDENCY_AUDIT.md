## Finding

The published A0 differs from the author's clarified philosophical position in two substantive respects: it locates awareness as the “knowing aspect” of U, whereas the clarification attributes a provisionally named quality to the unknown ground and excludes knowing in that foundational sense. The original monograph already separates A0's ontological role from numerical construction. The graph, predictive object and lineage computations do not require an additional numerical awareness variable. That supports preserving the mathematics while explicitly distinguishing the clarification from the published ontology. It does not establish that the two philosophical statements are equivalent or authorize a silent formal revision.

A1 genuinely assigns perspectives under its stated sufficient law, including a certified minimal one-bit self-return case. A2 identifies the complete endogenous predictive organization and its actual point with phenomenal organization; it is not simply a claim that every stored autobiographical memory is recalled at once. Neither identification is independently established by the mathematical construction. The finite completion theorem remains a determinacy result conditional on a certified R* and the named laws. The conservativity theorem fixes the physical kernels and therefore proves no changed physical transcript law from adding their aspect interpretation.

## A0: exact statement and interpretation

Constitutive law 17.1 (`monograph.tex`, lines 2287–2289) begins:

> Awareness is the knowing aspect of $\unsplit$, reality prior to the operational source/readout distinction.

The remaining statement says the capacity is not produced ex nihilo by a vessel and is not a localized subject or second independently acting substance or force. The next paragraph explicitly uses “knowing aspect” ontologically without personality, scene, memory or intention. Chapter 1 additionally says “knowing” names the proposed condition of manifestation rather than a cosmic agent acquiring information (line 583). The publication therefore already disavows a cosmic person. That is not the same as the newer clarification that foundational awareness does not denote knowing at all.

The source defines `\unsplit` as `\mathsf{U}` at line 445. Its convention distinguishes U from S and T, not as a computed physical state. Lines 493–495 say no theorem treats U as a set, Hilbert space, field or computable transition-law argument. Appendix F repeats its ontological status. Thus the current clarification can preserve the distinction between ground, U and mathematical representations without claiming that the original A0 already states the clarified account.

The phrase “unsplit ground” in fixed publications should be preserved as publication text and accompanied, where useful, by an external editorial note. Current editorial explanations should follow the clarified distinction. A0's universal philosophical truth, the ground's nature, and the validity of the quality attribution are not established by this source inspection.

## A1: admission and the minimal-memory consequence

Constitutive law 17.2 assigns one localized perspective to an internal SCC meeting Definition 15.5. It is a sufficient admission law within SPC-2, not merely a record that recurrence was observed. Its actual inputs include executable whole-core return in the current protocol/operating type, native resource context, distinguishable native future laws, native representation closure and predictive-fibre congruence. Maximality excludes additional overlapping subassembly subjects under this doctrine.

The supporting physical predicate can be evaluated before consciousness is assigned. Neither a graph SCC nor positive retained influence independently proves interpretive significance or experiential unity; the monograph says A1 makes that further identification openly (lines 2302–2303). Certification failure means the specialization is unavailable, not that the physical system is proved unconscious.

A persistent bit has physical memory. The source explicitly states that a genuinely realized one-bit self-return process with distinct native future laws can satisfy the complete certificate (lines 2305–2313), and no general theorem excludes passive memories. Greater realization diligence alone therefore cannot exclude every minimal case. A stronger requirement for automatic interpretation, concern, autopoiesis or another form of unity would be an additional constitutional refinement, not a consequence already proved. The source identifies “world-for-the-vessel” and minimal concern as candidate motivations that are not hypotheses of its completion theorem (lines 2317–2319).

Editorial consequence: preserve A1, expose its conditional status and minimal consequence, and distinguish physical memory from the scaffolded interpretation the author intends. No necessary-only A1, replacement rule, adult-language threshold or universal deliberate-choice requirement is adopted by this update.

## A2: complete organization, actual point and current content

Constitutive law 17.3 is stronger than a correlation claim. It copies the complete endogenous predictive object over all physically admitted finite continuations and maps the actual intrinsic state to its actual phenomenal point. It preserves labeled operations and outcomes, finite test laws, horizon compatibility and class transitions. It excludes an additional manifested qualitative remainder apart from primitive presence within the constitution.

The predictive construction uses actual intrinsic states, including internally retained memory needed for the update (Chapter 16, lines 1937–1945). Future laws characterize present dispositions; they are not future events acting backward and need not be computed by the vessel (lines 2327–2328). Two states can be in the same predictive class if all admitted native future test laws agree. This is not an assertion that all stored memories appear as recalled images at once, nor that the whole set of possible future experiences is simultaneously actual.

Chapter 2 already distinguishes an implicit contributing process from an object presented in a current scene (lines 642–649). It also explicitly refuses to shrink A2's complete object by that explanatory gloss and says unconscious-processing adequacy needs independent phenomenal comparison. Chapter 17 adds that a structural-copy proof does not prove every retained endogenous distinction is experienced (line 2331). These caveats are present in the original; they are not new discoveries of conditionality.

The flower example in the brief is coherent as an editorial distinction among stored organization, causal influence and current content. A recalled person can be current content when the recollection occurs without making the whole memory store current content. But this does not settle whether the full predictive structure is the correct phenomenal organization or how to map all implicit distinctions to actual presentation. Formal restriction of A2, a new selective content mapping or independent empirical validation would require further work. None is silently implemented.

## Relational-awareness bridge and terminology

The original main PDF §2.2 already treats perception as interpretative construction rather than a raw image shown to a spectator. §2.4 explicitly distinguishes removing all acquired scaffolding and memory formation from merely removing naming or commentary; it recognizes that the recollective draft uses “experience” more narrowly than this paper's current qualitative appearance. A universal recollective claim is not established by the local current-act result.

Main §2.6 and supplement Module B §2.14 expressly develop the current-act argument independently of assuming full A1–A3. A completed SPC-2 application would have to justify a qualifying physical core, identify the current relational mode as an aspect of its scene and apply the declared native time/provenance doctrine. Neither the paper nor this audit completes that application, validates A2, identifies first onset or infers subject membership from every causal contributor. Thus the current-act argument is not simply an invalid derivation from A2, and changing A0's philosophical gloss does not automatically invalidate its local appearance-facing argument.

The PDFs retain older “knowing aspect” language. Separate editorial notes can explain the newer clarification while preserving their dates and content. No blanket source rewrite, new appearance law or completed boundary-selection rule is justified by this update.
