Marked integration and the coupling reset

A marker is a finite algebraic label used to keep track of an insertion in a product. Distinct markers multiply only when their modes are allowed and disjoint. This nilpotent algebra makes the insertion bookkeeping finite, while the underlying scalar activity gas still requires absolute summability. The construction below first restores the actual insertions, then performs a coupling reset as an exact exponent identity, and finally integrates the primitive density with all its marked and forced terms.
