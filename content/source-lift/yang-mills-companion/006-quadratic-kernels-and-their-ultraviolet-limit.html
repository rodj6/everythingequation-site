Quadratic kernels and their ultraviolet limit

The quadratic analysis constructs the linear response around the flat configuration. There are three separate issues. The evaluated quadratic form on link potentials must converge; its curvature representation must remain positive and exponentially local; and the stationary predictor must have a regular Fourier symbol at the apparent zero-momentum singularity. The auxiliary metric amendment changes only directions not detected by curls. Thus retaining the physical potential precision is a proved identity, not an inference from positivity.
