Phase decomposition and Wightman reconstruction

The reconstruction has three levels. Finite reflection-positive correlations first give Hilbert vectors and bounded slab operators. The commutative ground-compression algebra then produces characters and a full-support phase measure. Finally tube continuation and tempered boundary limits construct fields on a common cyclic domain in each selected phase. The proof below keeps these levels separate: it does not assume a one-dimensional ground space before the phase argument establishes vacuum uniqueness.
