Convergence of the interacting scale histories

Convergence requires one common numerical schedule for all fixed finite demands. The order is: choose the finite stage profile, then its block length, then its post-length constants and coupling-entry delay, and only afterwards the terminal cutoffand physical period. Increasing a delay does not reselect a previously fixed block. This prevents circular parameter choices in the stationary estimates, shooting construction and actual coupling running.
