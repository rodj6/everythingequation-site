Free shell charges and separation of the Ward kernel

The free shell calculation is the comparison model for physical Ward charges. Compact cutoffs ensure that all shell integrals avoid the insertions, and the fixed exterior degree horizon controls the tests used to separate defects. The argument does not assume that a low-degree test alone detects an arbitrary high-degree label; the displacement and Wick calculations below provide the additional separating probes.

We give the polynomial calculations underlying the free charge separation used in the rotation and trace arguments. This also specifies the exterior tests needed when a charge acts on high-degree fields.
