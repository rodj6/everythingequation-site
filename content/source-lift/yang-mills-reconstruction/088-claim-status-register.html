Claim-status register

The register separates the origin of a statement from the recovery established here. A denotes a native admission result; B, a result conditional on the realized branch; C, a result of the supplied Yang–Mills construction; D, a recovery statement proved in this reconstruction; and E, an open selection question. A row can combine classes when a supplied construction also receives a new recovery proof. The hypotheses and exactness classes are part of the stated result.
