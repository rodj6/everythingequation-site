The source support of the realized family, its lift groupoid and its interfaces

The preceding two sections give the lift of one finite lattice: an admitted antecedent (Proposition 6.1), a redundant realization, its readout and its section (Theorems 7.1 and 7.2). The supplied construction, however, is not one lattice. It is a countable family of finite histories, indexed by a cutoff, a period and a scale level, together with comparisons between them, auxiliary reference volumes, coefficient spaces of field labels, an imported free comparison theory and several successive limits. This section develops the source-side structure that is needed to carry that family, and states what that structure does and does not determine. It has four parts: the admitted support; the lift groupoid of a single leaf; the sectors and interfaces of the supplied construction, with the status of each discrepancy; and the separation of what follows from the source side from what is supplied as realization data. Nothing in this section is used as a hypothesis by the supplied derivations that follow; it is the frame in which their source–readout formulations are stated.

8.4 What follows from the source side and what is supplied

The division is the following, and it is used without further comment in the formulations below.

From the source side. The admitted fan provides a support with countably many marked cells (Proposition 8.1); no relation between distinct cells and no preferred labelling (Proposition 8.2); and the reduction of every finite statement to an admitted finite support. On each leaf the realized branch has contractible lift groupoids (Theorem 8.3). These are structural facts. They contain no dynamics.

Supplied as realization data. Everything that makes the family a Yang–Mills construction is chosen in the realized sector: the compact group) with simple nonabelian Lie algebra; the four-dimensional periodic lattices and the Wilson action; the blocking maps and the stationary corridors; the calendar c; the calibration value ̄ and the blocking penalty one; the label algebra and the free comparison theory; and every estimate. The labelling of leaves by (), and hence the notions of finer, larger and coarser, are of this kind.

Derived within the realization. Some quantities are neither chosen nor source-side: they are computed from the realization data by the supplied construction. The slope) is the coefficient of the logarithm in the explicit annular integral (DR5); the amplitude = 3 dim g

is a Wick contraction (Theorem 24.1); the one-dimensionality of the translation Ward kernel of the free comparison theory (FS10) and the(4) covariance of the hierarchy (Theorem 19.3) are consequences.

Not obtained. No selection theorem is proved, and none is claimed. The method’s test for a source-derived premise is to look for inequivalent raw realizations over the same admitted support and the same literal preparations. Here they exist in abundance: replacing the group, the dimension of the lattice, or the Wilson action by another gauge-invariant local action gives a different realized sector over the same fan Φ, with the same lift-groupoid theorem. Admission therefore does not select the gauge group, the dimension, the action or the coupling history. The recovery proved in this paper is conditional in exactly this sense: given the supplied construction as realization data, its objects, operations, limits and conclusions are recovered through the readouts, with the stated exactness classes. This distinguishes an explicit mathematical recovery theorem from selection of the physical realization.
