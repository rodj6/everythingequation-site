5.1 Notation and the roles of recurring symbols

The construction uses several local alphabets. The following register fixes their roles; later local uses are qualified by their section and indices. In particular, a local parameter called a mass or a gap need not be a spectral gap of the reconstructed physical theory.

Terms such as bill, payment, price and purchase denote the explicitly stated inequalities and the order in which their constants and thresholds are chosen. A calendar is the common sequence of scales, stage assignments and entry thresholds. A beacon is a specified finite reference view used to read a local coefficient, and a path book retains the path-series and insertion data of a chosen history. These words do not supply extra hypotheses beyond their mathematical definitions. Their exact weights, derivative orders and choices remain in the corresponding sections.
