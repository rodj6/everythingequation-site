6 A first-domain route to full-wave continuity

The deterministic flow theorem requires a continuous representative of the complete wave. In configuration dimension eight, an ordinary H2H^2H2 estimate does not give this. Conversely, asking for sufficiently many powers of the Hamiltonian can exclude the intended entrance: a bounded radial step need not preserve H3H^3H3, and an uncut Gaussian need not lie in the second domain of a Coulomb Hamiltonian. We use one genuine Hamiltonian graph together with higher tangential regularity.
