A final-time margin is not the whole-history premise in this corollary. To infer faithful copying of an earlier actual outcome, the ideal model must itself provide that relation and its entire promised hold; its failures must be added to β\betaβ. The theorem transfers a specified robust history. It does not construct a detector, a source law, a fresh receiver or a reset operation. Likewise convergence of complete paths in probability does not by itself imply total-variation convergence of arbitrary configuration laws. Such a conclusion additionally requires an appropriate regularity and inverse-map transfer estimate.
