## 1 Agency as constituted participation

A person can understand a source of their conduct without having created the capacity to understand it. That understanding can nevertheless change what the person does. A complete physical history can contain both the earlier absence of understanding and the later competence it makes possible. This account develops these claims together. Its negative argument limits ultimate independent self-authorship under explicit premises. Its positive argument explains how a constituted participant can acquire an account of a recommendation, assess its authority and change its subsequent use while retaining memory, practical identity and the ability to act.

The motivating image is a sandpit. People, cities and civilizations are distinguishable organizations of its material without obtaining a separately constituted existence outside it. The analogy identifies a question rather than proving a conclusion. If a person’s body, understanding, self-image and decisions are constituted within a common reality, what limits follow for authorship? What changes when the person understands those limits? The intended shift is from a self-originating spectator to an organized participant. It does not make the person a helpless piece controlled by an external puppeteer.

Within Shadow Theory the fundamental Ground remains unknown. Awareness provisionally names an attributed intrinsic quality; localized experiencing occurrences require further constitutive and organizational laws. A nominated mathematical source, a physical history, an available record and an interpreted self-description are therefore not interchangeable. Common constitution neither establishes one cosmic subject nor makes every physical interaction conscious. Its positive explanatory role is to make personal understanding and its realized activity one occurrence rather than competing causes. Reality’s activity through this organization need not be the action of another person instead of this person.

The block-universe question sharpens that proposal. Relativity represents events through spacetime geometry with invariant causal structure, local durations and frame-dependent simultaneity. A complete-history interpretation places perception, retained records, anticipation and decisions within that structure. From a situated stage, some later events remain inaccessible and some actions remain responsive to deliberation. Describing the whole history does not remove those differences. The substantive question is whether their reality requires an independently originating chooser, or whether the organization and its admitted alternatives can explain them.

We defend the second possibility without assuming that it excludes every rival. A complete actual occurrence fixes what actually happens, but does not by itself establish uniqueness from earlier conditions. A dependent agent might claim to settle an otherwise open continuation from within reality. The strongest supported argument has distinguishable dependencies: common constitution excludes a separately constituted author; inheritance and well-founded authorization obstruct ultimate authorship of the whole enabling basis; suitable complete-data uniqueness excludes same-prior-data alternatives; a further token-causal constitution premise excludes an irreducible initiating addition. The argument investigates rather than hides those additional steps.

The positive thesis is more specific than the observation that conditioned systems learn. Understanding can alter the authority of a self-model while preserving useful information. A disposition’s origin does not automatically disqualify it, and recognizing conditioning does not automatically warrant revision. The needed relation is selective: acquire a credible account, assess it under independently defensible standards and actual costs, then carry warranted retention or revision into action. Verbal assent, unused description, compensation, authority revision and passivity are distinguished by actual routes.

The retained construction follows that sequence. A finite organization probes its own recommendation-producing mechanism, retains a predictive model, assesses an installed route, archives its old authority state and acts on fresh inputs. Diagnosis outperforms a declared source-blind comparator by $1/16$ in the costed example, falling to $1/256$ under specified noise. A direct neutral write achieves the same raw accuracy without diagnosis. Recognition’s value concerns selective assessment and costs rather than a monopoly on successful behavior. Model error, missing actuators and relapse expose failure conditions.

The connection to complete histories is explicit. An exact 64-history ensemble separates improving self-mechanism knowledge from task uncertainty and action prediction. A finite causal-order result makes compatible event records independent of the serialization of disjoint spacelike operations. Revealed-forecast fixed points distinguish deterministic evolution from universally successful disclosed prediction. Compatibility and compactness arguments test whether distributed choices can jointly specify a block’s boundaries. These are worked applications and deductions from standard mathematics, with no unsupported priority claim.

His objections separate complete-state determination from fragmentary power, entailment from efficacy, and free parameters from an explanation of authorship. Hoefer contests an unwarranted explanatory privilege of the past without supplying a complete positive theory. Thyssen’s libertarian synthesis contributes a serious alternative and a precise compatibility problem. Their disagreements shape the argument rather than serving as authorities for one conclusion.

The physical discussion makes only the inferences its regimes support. Newtonian uniqueness requires conditions; relativistic simultaneity is not a universal present; general-relativistic uniqueness is conditional and gauge-relative; quantum theory does not give an interpretation-free proof of unique outcomes. An experienced present is proposed as organized local access involving retention and anticipation, without an external awareness sweeping through spacetime in a second time.
