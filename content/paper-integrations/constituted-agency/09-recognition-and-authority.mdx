**Recognition becomes consequential when warranted assessment enters action.** Origin, accuracy and authority are different properties of a recommendation. Inquiry can defeat a particular claim to compulsory priority while leaving memory, commitments and useful practical identity intact. This chapter states the representational, evaluative and implementation warrants that make the proposal assessable, together with the precise decision-theoretic condition under which an additional attribution supplies no further task warrant.

<a id="section-9"></a>

## 9 Recognition as a change in warranted authority

<a id="section-9-1"></a>

### 9.1 Understanding, assessment and incorporation

The authority of a self-model is not identical to its truth. A person may accurately describe a response as learned, defensive or conditioned while still treating its recommendation as compulsory. Conversely, they may inhibit a response effectively without changing the authority it receives when inhibition is unavailable. These differences matter to freedom through recognition. The relevant achievement is not merely believing a doctrine about dependence. It is understanding a specified process well enough to reassess how it should govern action and incorporating that reassessment into later conduct.

There is a narrow inferential bridge from the constitutive argument. A self-model may claim compulsory authority on the ground that it expresses an absolutely self-originating author. If inquiry warrants the common-constitution and non-origination premises, that particular argument for authority loses its basis. This does not show that the model’s contents are false, its recommendations harmful or its commitments unjustified. Reasons, evidence, responsibilities and legitimate projects may independently support them. What is withdrawn is the alleged entitlement supplied solely by absolute origination. An account based instead on an unchosen but appropriately exercised power requires its own assessment; the constitutive argument has not refuted it.

Recognition becomes practically consequential when the philosophical and practical bridges both hold. The constitutive argument can defeat a specified origination-based reason for authority. The finite construction shows how a warranted assessment can become causally operative through retained interpretation and a write route. Accepting the philosophical argument does not itself supply that route, and the functional example does not establish the philosophical premises. Their conjunction explains how recognition of dependence could change participation while preserving practical identity. The acquired model concerns a particular recommendation-producing mechanism; its warrant does not require an understanding of the whole fundamental ground.

Three warrants give this proposal content. *Representational warrant* connects a retained account to independently nominated features of the organization’s own activity, including predictions under fresh inputs or interventions. *Evaluative warrant* connects assessment to commitments, evidence, costs and protective constraints specified independently of the desired verdict. Learning how a recommendation arises does not, by itself, show that it should be ignored. *Implementation warrant* connects the retained assessment to actual later selection. A model that is correct but never read and an assessment whose installation route is blocked fail different requirements.

These warrants do not identify phenomenal insight with functional performance. They establish a minimal, defeasible interpretation of self-mechanism recognition. The model represents a part of its own recommendation-producing process and is used to assess that process’s future role. Human recognition could involve much richer conceptual, affective, embodied and experiential organization. Applying this interpretation to people would require independent evidence for the proposed role mapping, not a declaration that a measured variable is insight.

The distinction between ownership and warrant is central. A recommendation can be mine in the sense that it arises through my history and organization without being entitled to unconditional obedience. Rejecting that entitlement need not reject practical identity. Information about one’s body, responsibilities, skills and commitments can remain indispensable. A person can correctly retain a protective constraint or a commitment after inquiry. The proposal is selective: assess the grounds on which particular content receives authority within a continuing organization.

### 9.2 A conditional evaluative principle

An elementary decision-theoretic result isolates one case in which an additional self-attribution supplies no further task warrant. Let $Z$ include relevant evidence, useful practical self-state, legitimate goals, costs and availability. Let $S$ be an additional self/source attribution, $Y$ a target, $\mathcal A(Z)$ a finite action set and $\ell(a,Y,Z)$ a bounded measurable loss. Assume the indicated conditional laws and measurable Bayes rule exist; in the finite application they do. All compared rules use that same action and cost domain.

<a id="statement-9-1"></a>

**Proposition 9.1** (Conditional irrelevance of an additional attribution). Suppose $\mathbb P(Y\mid Z,S)=\mathbb P(Y\mid Z)$. A $Z$-measurable conditional Bayes rule has expected loss no greater than any randomized rule $\pi(a\mid Z,S)$. The inequality is strict if $\pi$ gives positive joint mass to actions with a positive conditional loss gap above the Bayes minimum.

*Proof.* Write $L_z(a)=\mathbb E[\ell(a,Y,z)\mid Z=z]$. Conditional irrelevance makes this also the conditional loss given $(z,s)$. Every $\pi$-average of these losses is at least $\min_{a\in\mathcal A(z)}L_z(a)$. Integrating proves the first claim. Integrating the nonnegative gap proves strictness under the stated condition. ◻

This is standard conditional decision theory, used here to expose a premise rather than derive a universal value judgment. If personal attribution changes legitimate preferences, costs, admissible actions or the target law, the antecedent fails. There is no theorem that everything self-related is irrelevant. In particular, removing useful practical self-information from $Z$ can worsen action. The philosophical claim is that mere attribution to oneself supplies no additional warrant *when* the relevant epistemic and evaluative work is already represented and the conditional-irrelevance premise holds.

That premise is not entailed by monism. It must be justified separately for an application. Recognition of common ground is neither necessary nor sufficient for competent revision. One can reject monism and understand a learned bias; one can assent to monism while preserving an unexamined authority relation. The contribution of the common-ground interpretation is to explain how the understanding, criterion and revised route can all be the participant’s activity without appealing to an independent controller behind thought.

### 9.3 Continuity without an inner proprietor

Inquiry is already an organized activity. A discrepancy can recruit attention, memory and comparison; a retained commitment can guide assessment; the resulting interpretation can change later reliance. No additional chooser must first create these capacities for the process to occur. This does not imply that all thought is chosen or that every discrepancy produces learning. The developmental corpus explicitly separates capacity, acquisition, selection and incorporation and allows failure at each stage ([Rodgers 2026k](/consciousness/constituted-agency/21-references#ref-rodgersdevelopment), [2026l](/consciousness/constituted-agency/21-references#ref-rodgerslearning)).

The contemplative materials sharpen the question of why a constructed thinker should receive authority over the activity from which it arises. The supplied Krishnamurti document is a synthesized study guide, while the separately checked 1978 discussion is a primary conversation with its own disagreements about will and desire ([“Understanding Consciousness Through Krishnamurti: A Question Led Study Guide” n.d.](/consciousness/constituted-agency/21-references#ref-krishguide); [Krishnamurti 1978](/consciousness/constituted-agency/21-references#ref-krishnamurti)). Neither settles the argument. Its independently defensible point is that adding an inner controller whose dispositions remain unexplained does not solve self-origination. What matters positively is whether inquiry changes the organization’s practical relation to those dispositions.

Timalsina’s discussion of dependent agency is useful here: dependence on instruments and conditions does not automatically eliminate an agent’s activity, and conventional agency and ultimate non-action are distinct theses ([Timalsina 2013, 196](/consciousness/constituted-agency/21-references#ref-timalsina) and surrounding discussion). The present account does not require declaring persons ultimately unreal. They are real organized participants under the constitutive hypothesis. Their continuing identity can involve memory, plans, commitments and relationships even when a self-image loses an inappropriate authority. The following construction makes one small instance of that possibility assessable.
