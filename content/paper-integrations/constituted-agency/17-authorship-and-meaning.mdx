**The achievement is consequential participation.** The constructed mechanism is deliberately open to alternative implementations, broader comparators and failed correspondences. Its contribution is a warranted route connecting an acquired account to selective use, not an equation declaring all human understanding solved. The objections developed here locate the remaining semantic, causal, ethical and experiential questions while retaining the practical difference understanding can make.

<a id="section-17"></a>

## 17 Authorship, recognition and consequential participation

<a id="section-17-1"></a>

### 17.1 Does a thin mechanism explain understanding?

The finite mechanism is deliberately less than a theory of human understanding. A lookup table can encode its selected policy. Indeed, the direct neutral-write arm matches its raw performance without self-mechanism acquisition. The positive claim concerns the organization of a particular route: a retained model is acquired from the actual process it represents, predicts fresh cases, is used under an independent criterion and contributes through a real installation path. Those relations are demonstrated; richer semantic and phenomenal interpretations are not.

A skeptical rival can ask whether a model-free learner, a different causal representation or broader access obtains the same selectivity more cheaply. The exact value theorem does not answer that broader comparison. It states its candidate rules, phase boundary and costs. Its constructive significance is that self-mechanism understanding can have an identifiable practical role while its necessity for raw success is false. A universal claim that understanding is indispensable would have been weaker because the example itself refutes it.

The correspondences between the logical, evaluative and physical constructions are equally explicit. They share the generator, retained estimate, authority selector, archive and fresh-action equations. The selective rule is justified by the declared $q,H,c$ inequalities; the physical circuit implements that finite decision under those constants. The 1,874-window schedule executes one audit, installation and one fresh case. The $H=4$ reward ledger concerns a four-case future block, not four cases within that count. A physical four-case implementation needs additional input, output, holding and restoration resources, or a larger history lift; those costs must enter any device-level value comparison. The local schedule does not derive the chosen reward prices or demonstrate positive net value for a manufactured apparatus. The noisy diagnostic, expanded-generator and relapse extensions also require their own paid implementations; their formulas do not inherit the 1,874-window count.

For human applications, the predictions are therefore discriminating hypotheses rather than reported findings. A correct self-account should predict nominated fresh responses; model-read disruption should affect assessment if the model is being used; an installation disruption should preserve the account while blocking its later effect; and removing a compensatory support should distinguish downstream cancellation from a changed authority relation. Useful practical memory and legitimate protections must be tested alongside improvement. Similar intact outcomes can arise through different routes, and psychological interventions may not isolate those routes cleanly. Nothing here reports such an experiment or justifies diagnosing a person from a verbal statement.

<a id="section-17-2"></a>

### 17.2 Does realization turn the person into a spectator?

The spectator conclusion assumes that a cause explained through physical organization is no longer the person’s act. Common constitution offers the contrary interpretation: the relevant organization is how the person acts. A detailed explanation can distinguish deliberation from bypass or deviant causation instead of replacing deliberation with an alien actor. The present circuit establishes a minimal functional route, not sufficient conditions for every intentional action. Human authorship also involves appropriate reasons, capacities, circumstances and histories.

This interpretation remains available in deterministic and indeterministic models. Deterministic explanation is not explanation of an outcome occurring regardless of inquiry; it can explain an outcome occurring through inquiry. Counterfactual tests compare different admitted preparations or operations under a common contract, rather than holding an entire complete world fixed while changing one of its parts. Indeterminism, conversely, does not make a random fluctuation the independent author. An agent-causal account can insist that the person settles the result; an event-causal account can identify self-forming effort with its indeterministic resolution. Both owe an account of attribution and control. Neither is excluded merely because the choosing capacity was received.

The irreducible-settler objection consequently survives at exactly the point it should. If successful, it changes the account of present choice. It does not retrospectively give the person authorship of all the conditions under which that power exists. Nor does a realized mechanism’s predictive adequacy prove token-causal constitution beyond its tested regime. The paper’s positive account is compatible with disagreement about that further metaphysical claim.

<a id="section-17-3"></a>

### 17.3 Why this is more than renaming conditioning

Conditioning describes an origin or dependence; it does not specify present authority. A retained experience can inform judgment, serve as a threat cue, remain inaccessible, or automatically override relevant evidence. These are different organizations of the same preserved information. The new construction identifies acquisition and application routes that distinguish them. In its costed task, understanding supports retention when appropriate and revision when worthwhile. In its relapse model, retained description can outlast its practical use. The dependence of each process does not make the differences unreal.

The distinction also prevents a romantic interpretation of freedom as unlimited openness. A commitment can properly close options; a protection can be necessary; available resources can warrant postponing inquiry. The inherited revision-preservation fixed point describes one way to preserve future auditability, but it is not a universal obligation never to make an irreversible commitment. What matters is the quality of assessment and its actual circumstances, not a maximum rate of amendment.

Unpredictability supplies no shortcut to this conclusion. Krumm and Müller distinguish temporal prediction limits from their proposed computational sourcehood, whose strongest structure-preservation claim remains a conjecture ([Krumm and Müller 2023](/consciousness/constituted-agency/21-references#ref-krumm)). The organizational alternatives considered by List likewise concern an agency level requiring its own control account ([List 2025](/consciousness/constituted-agency/21-references#ref-list)). Anderson’s supplied undated manuscript distinguishes levels of freedom, but its stronger cosmic claims lack the physical derivation needed here ([Anderson n.d.](/consciousness/constituted-agency/21-references#ref-anderson)). A novel or unpredictable action can still lack ultimate self-authorship; a predictable action can still express a person’s competent judgment.

<a id="section-17-4"></a>

### 17.4 Responsibility and meaning

The absence of ultimate self-creation does not make truthfulness, care, teaching or repair inconsequential. Their reasons can concern affected people, commitments and future capacities. The present account identifies processes through which those reasons can matter. It does not derive an ethical theory from a transition kernel or identify success at a task with moral goodness.

Historical ownership can deepen through criticism, acknowledgment and retained commitment without becoming authorship of the whole developmental basis. A person’s capacities can be socially formed and genuinely exercised by that person. Conversely, shared constitution does not excuse manipulation or erase the difference between understanding and imposed compliance. Those distinctions concern actual routes of acquisition and control as well as further normative judgments. They are reasons to investigate conditions of agency, not to replace all accountability with either absolute desert or passivity.

The positive interpretation is consequently concrete. Understanding can change what a recommendation is permitted to do. Memory can remain while an old rule loses priority. A relationship can become a retained competence without becoming an ongoing external controller. The individual is an organized participant whose acts have consequences through that organization. Their reality does not depend on having first created the whole basis from which they arise.
