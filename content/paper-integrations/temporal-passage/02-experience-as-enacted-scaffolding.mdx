<a id="section-2"></a>
## 2. Experience as enacted scaffolding

<a id="section-2-1"></a>
### 2.1 Complete enactment and experienced character

Experience is identified here with an actual organized construction within a bounded interpretive unit. Operative distinctions, expectations, retained information, evaluative associations, bodily organization and relevant context shape how incoming information is interpreted. Implementation, causal embedding and formative history belong to that organization. The target is an actual process token, not merely a description of a possible computation.

<a id="constitutive-premise-1"></a>
<Definition title="Constitutive premise 1: complete enactment">
Experience, including felt character, is the complete qualifying organization actually enacted within the relevant interpretive unit. Once that qualifying organization occurs, no additional witness, experiential output or actualization event converts it into experience.
</Definition>

The premise supplies the constitution used throughout this argument. It does not admit every computation, representation or report. What matters is the operative organization and its actual history. A reduced analytical model can also leave out constitutively relevant facts: if $K$ denotes complete qualifying enactment, a structural temporal projection $\mathcal T(K)$ need not contain every contribution to how the experience feels. An incomplete projection is different from an experiential ingredient supposedly still missing after the complete enactment.

Two people can hear the same music differently because their organization differs. Familiarity, personal association, expectation and bodily response can all alter the construction. They are possible contributors, not mandatory autobiographical or emotional conditions for every sound experience. Basic understanding can be implicit. The listener does not need to think “this is music” in order to discriminate, retain and relate sounds.

Construction also does not mean inaccuracy. A constructed experience can track source order correctly or misidentify it. Feeling passage establishes a temporal appearance; the inference from that appearance to a fundamental external flow needs its own argument.

<a id="section-2-2"></a>
### 2.2 Actual uptake and later retrieval

**The relevant reflection is actual interpretive uptake.** It can be immediate, implicit and nonverbal, with a brief processing delay within the activity. It requires neither deliberate introspection nor a verbal description, durable autobiographical recall or recognition of the source of a response.

The paper distinguishes five functional roles:

- $D$: actual reception or internal retrieval.
- $I$: interpretation through operative scaffolding.
- $C$: consolidation of the relevant contributions.
- $M$: retention sufficient for their operative availability.
- $U$: their actual interpretive uptake within the same unit.

In uptake, an available contribution participates as content in ongoing recognition, relating, appraisal, orientation or response organization. These are roles in a construction rather than five necessarily separate serial modules. Interpretation, consolidation and uptake can overlap.

Causal influence alone is weaker. A trace can change a threshold without entering the person's construction as an interpreted contribution. Unconscious processing can nevertheless bring forward a bodily response, emotion, image or concept that is immediately taken up. The current response and its inaccessible formative cause occupy different roles; the person need not identify that earlier association for the current response to participate in experience.

<a id="equation-1"></a>
$$
\operatorname{Exp}(A,J)\iff
\operatorname{BoundedUnit}(A,J)\land
\operatorname{Enacts}_{J}(A;D,I,C,M,U)\land H(A,J).
\tag{1}
$$

Here $A$ nominates the interpretive unit and $J$ scopes the relevant activity. The enactment predicate requires the actual operations in their relevant organization. Writing the role names on a diagram does not instantiate them. Retention $M$ can be brief: its contribution must survive long enough for uptake, without becoming a durable autobiographical memory.

A capacity $R_{\mathrm{later}}$ for later addressed retrieval is **not** a universal conjunct of [equation (1)](/consciousness/temporal-passage/02-experience-as-enacted-scaffolding#equation-1). Someone can take up an occurrence now and be unable to retrieve it later. An unused retrieval route, conversely, does not establish uptake during the earlier interval. A later qualifying retrieval is another current construction concerning that event. It neither retroactively creates nor completes the earlier experience.

An addressed query can test an access route and its use. It cannot, by itself, establish every earlier unprobed uptake. The distinction therefore sharpens the reflection condition without turning one response into a consciousness detector.

<a id="section-2-3"></a>
### 2.3 History, unit and awareness

This publication retains a mandatory formative-history condition:

<a id="equation-2"></a>
$$
H(A,J)\iff
\exists B\ne A,\ J':
\operatorname{Exp}(B,J')\land
\operatorname{FormHist}(B,J';A,J).
\tag{2}
$$

The other experiencer's actual activity must have helped form scaffolding operative in $A$ during $J$. Merely imagining another person does not establish that history, while continued interpersonal contact is unnecessary. Together, [equation (1)](/consciousness/temporal-passage/02-experience-as-enacted-scaffolding#equation-1) and [equation (2)](/consciousness/temporal-passage/02-experience-as-enacted-scaffolding#equation-2) constrain assignments across a developmental history. The principal melody construction starts with an already qualifying adult and an admissible assignment under those premises. It preserves that operative history; it does not derive the origin or uniqueness of every experience assignment.

For the human example, the unit is the embodied person with the relevant state, integration and access organization. Notes, memories and event descriptions do not become additional subjects merely because they are distinguishable. Nor does $J$ impose a uniquely bounded natural packet: nested and overlapping content descriptions can belong within continuing activity.

The framework accepts thin fundamental awareness as an ontological premise. In the site's current clarification, awareness provisionally names a quality attributed to the unknown Ground. It is not a cosmic subject, universal observer or time-advancing field. The temporal argument does not derive that premise; it explains organized temporal experience within the qualifying unit.
