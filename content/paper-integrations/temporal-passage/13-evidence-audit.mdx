## Appendix B. Evidence audit

The publication's source inventory contains 25 files representing 23 distinct papers. Two LSD files are byte-identical; an astronaut article is repeated with a cover. Reviews, preprint/publication versions and likely shared cohorts are not additional independent replications. The publication identifies file hashes and reference metadata in its accompanying research record. The inventory below preserves the reported study identities, overlaps and evidential limits.

The seven targeted additions address different missing functions: Saija et al. (2014), order versus fusion; Sankaran et al. (2024), pitch change and expectation; Riecke et al. (2007), auditory continuity; Zuo et al. (2020), online integration in amnesia; Wacongne et al. (2011), predictive omission responses; Rozenberg et al. (2019), physical circuit primitives; and Lamprou-Kokolaki et al. (2024), matched duration and passage judgments.

Their limitations are correspondingly different. Correlational recordings do not prove necessity. A single case cannot establish universality. A component circuit does not certify an experiencing device. Each constrains part of the proposed organization without independently proving the constitutive premises.
