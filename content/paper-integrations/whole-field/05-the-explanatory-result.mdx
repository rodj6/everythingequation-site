## 10. The explanatory force of enacted constitution

**The account begins with actual organization.** Reception, operative retention, interpretation and uptake specify how contributions are present and usable in an encounter. Complete-enactment identity identifies this organized activity with the occurrence, including its character. A completed image awaiting a recipient is not the explanatory starting point.

The identity makes substantive organizational commitments. An unread archive differs from actual uptake. A live estimate differs from a recollection. A copied source label without a realizing route differs from implemented occasion binding. Changing one of these relations can alter the proposed current organization while leaving selected ingredient values or output accuracy unchanged. The linked realization witness specifies the difference an application must implement.

Retained scaffolding and current content remain connected through actual operations. Dispositions constrain interpretation. Some produce a presently incorporated image, expectation or affective qualification. Others influence the construction without appearing as separately presented items. The current field is the organization of what is taken up; the complete enactment includes the realization and relevant powers through which that organization exists.

The philosophical argument is an identity argument within an explicit constitution. Its warrant is the placement of experiential presence in the actual activity that interprets contributions, rather than the addition of an unspecified experiential object after that activity is complete. Its mathematics articulates consequences and realization requirements of this identification. It distinguishes a within-unit whole from unrelated capacities, a partial description and a surrounding container. A processing score does not prove the identity.

The contributor question remains meaningful. To ask whether a process is only a contributor is to compare its actual relations with the complete target organization. If a scent binder omits the recollection currently qualifying the encounter, its description omits part of that occurrence. If a reconstruction preserves those relations in fewer descriptive variables, it need not omit them. **Completeness concerns constitutive coverage, not the size of the diagram.**

At the declared scope, the answer is determinate. The experiencing occurrence of the qualifying unit is the complete actual organization. Its current field consists in the interpreted incorporation enacted there. No additional witness is required to convert that otherwise complete organization into the occurrence it already constitutes.

This gives the consciousness programme a concrete within-unit account. It specifies how source roles, memory, temporal differentiation, affective qualification and actual uptake belong to an occurrence. The unit's independent qualification and its complete realization remain premises. Their presence keeps the conclusion anchored to the target it actually explains.

## 11. The whole is its organized enactment

Whole-field constitution requires more than interacting contributions: it requires an account of their participation in one actual occurrence. Enacted scaffolding supplies that account through complete-enactment identity, current incorporation and unit unity. The linked realization witness specifies one actual construction while allowing distributed implementation and overlapping roles. Within the qualifying unit, the complete enactment is the occurrence itself.

Scent and melody show why values, source roles and temporal modes must be preserved together. The memory of another occasion can qualify the present encounter without being a live perception of that occasion. Retained and incoming notes can form an experienced transition without representing their source events as simultaneous. A present anticipation concerns a possible continuation without receiving that future event.

Retained scaffolding can shape the field without every memory becoming current content. Later retrieval can concern an occurrence without completing it retrospectively. Common-prefix invariance preserves the earlier occurrence against later record loss, while the predictive compatibility requirement keeps a current projection distinct from complete organization.

The resulting explanation is unified: **a qualifying unit's experience is its actually enacted interpretive organization, including its current contributions and relations.** A further product for a separate observer performs no required constitutive step. The explicit premises and complete-realization requirement state exactly where this answer applies.
