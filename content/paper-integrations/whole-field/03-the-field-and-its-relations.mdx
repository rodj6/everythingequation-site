## 5. Whole-field constitution

**The whole is relational as well as inclusive.** Preserving several contribution values does not necessarily preserve which one qualifies another, what occasion a recollection concerns or which signal is anticipated. Those relations belong to the current interpreted construction.

Fix a current stage $t$ at the chosen activity scope. Let $\mathcal W_A(t)$ collect the incorporated construction witnesses of $A$, together with their bindings. Shared-source relations, temporal modes, affective qualifications and actual recruitment remain in this structure. It is not an unordered bag of witnesses.

Let $K_A(t)$ denote the complete actual enactment at this scope. Its current interpreted organization is

<a id="equation-3"></a>
$$
\operatorname{Cur}_A(t)=\pi_{\mathrm{cur}}\bigl(K_A(t),\mathcal W_A(t)\bigr). \tag{3}
$$

Here $\pi_{\mathrm{cur}}$ names the constitutive correspondence for current incorporation. Applying it requires the actual witnesses and their interpreted roles. It is not a task-output summary. This notation separates current content from the complete organization realizing and organizing it.

Completeness is relative to a unit and target. An account is constitutively complete when it preserves all organization relevant to that occurrence under the adopted law. Establishing that a physical inventory meets this condition requires evidence. A short representation can preserve the relevant relations faithfully; a long description can omit one that matters. The decisive issue is omission of a constituent or relation required by the target, not descriptive length.

<a id="proposition-5-1"></a>
### Proposition 5.1: within-unit constitutive closure

Fix stage, activity scope and grain. Assume all three conditions:

1. $A$ is an independently warranted qualifying interpretive unit with an admissible operative history.
2. $K_A$ specifies its complete relevant actual enactment, including linked constructions, bindings and constitutive realization, rather than only a task projection.
3. Complete-enactment identity, current incorporation and unit unity apply to this organization.

Then the experiencing occurrence attributed to $A$ is $K_A$ itself. Its current experiential organization is given by actual incorporation in Equation (3). An account omitting an actual constituent or relation required by that occurrence does not exhaust it. No further experiential product or separate observer is required after the complete qualifying enactment.

**Proof.** The first two assumptions fix both the unit and its complete target organization. Complete-enactment identity identifies this actual organization with the occurrence; it does not identify a causal predecessor awaiting another experiential output. Current incorporation specifies the interpreted contributions and relations participating in the present organization. Unit unity makes their coordinated enactment one perspective at the fixed scope. An account omitting a required constituent or relation cannot exhaust that same target. A further conversion or observer is not an operation left unsupplied by these premises: requiring one would add a condition to the adopted constitutive identity.

The conclusion follows from the three principles together. The unit and realization premises specify physical organization; the principles assign that organization's experiential significance. The account completes the within-unit whole without placing another object or recipient beyond the enactment. A causal contribution has not been substituted for the complete occurrence: the target includes the unit's other actually incorporated relations.

This distinction also explains why merely adding items cannot establish a field. The whole possesses its organization through the relations of the incorporated constructions. It is not an unstructured total laid over otherwise independent ingredients.

<a id="section-6"></a>
## 6. Scent, recollection and melody

<a id="section-6-1"></a>
### 6.1 A currently qualified encounter

Take a present scent that is affectively qualified and accompanied by a recollection experienced as remembered. This is a philosophical construction for separating the relevant roles. The scent's physical cause, the acquired disposition producing familiarity, the represented past occasion and the current recollection are distinct contributions to its description.

When the recollection is actually taken up, the current organization contains more than a scent value beside a memory value. It includes the relation through which the latter appears as remembered in the present occasion. Familiarity can qualify the scent without presenting the entire history that formed the disposition. A bodily or affective response can participate without its formative association being explicitly identified.

Suppose a local mechanism $O$ realizes familiarity. If the unit also incorporates the recollection and its qualification of the encounter, $O$ does not exhaust the occurrence merely because familiarity is indispensable. The additional constituent and its actual relations within $A$ matter. Finding another stored record elsewhere would not establish the same addition. Familiarity by itself likewise supplies no reason to insert a recollection that is not occurring.

The distinctions lie in enacted organization. Changing a remembered contribution into a live perception changes its occasion role even if selected ingredient values remain fixed. Changing source attribution can alter what the contribution means in the encounter. The linked realization witness must track those relations. A value-only summary cannot determine them.

<a id="section-6-2"></a>
### 6.2 Temporal differentiation within one activity

A melody makes the temporal case explicit. A listener receives a note, retains its operative contribution, and interprets a subsequent note as following it while anticipating a continuation. Retained predecessor, live estimate and anticipation are jointly available in the current construction, but they have different modes. The [temporal-passage article](/consciousness/temporal-passage) develops the reception and integration schedule in full.

The organization can be represented schematically as

<a id="equation-4"></a>
$$
\bigl(c_{\mathrm{ret}},e_{\mathrm{live}},g_{\mathrm{ant}};\ c\prec_{\mathrm{rep}}e,
\text{ common stream},\text{ actual uptake}\bigr). \tag{4}
$$

The semicolon separates contribution values and modes from interpreted relations. It does not demand pitch names or an explicit proposition about before and after. Implicit recognition and relating can enact the transition.

An order-insensitive code cannot distinguish a sequence from its reversal under a fixed readout. Yet even an ordered archive can remain dormant. A retained note can be attributed to another stream. Sequential contributions can be interpreted as fusion rather than the nominated succession. Retained order information therefore needs source binding, temporal differentiation and actual use to realize this construction.

Under complete-enactment identity, receiving, retaining, relating and taking up the contributions constitute the experienced transition within the qualifying listener. The activity need not wait for the melody to finish and deliver a completed display to another observer. Anticipation does not require an actual signal from the future: it is a current disposition or interpreted contribution concerning a possible continuation. Delivery routes, retention and timing remain physical requirements, developed in the [block-universe source-to-use construction](/consciousness/block-universe).

Scent and melody show how a field can be qualitatively and temporally differentiated without fragmenting into unrelated ingredients. What is experienced together includes the different roles in which contributions are taken up. It does not assert simultaneous occurrence of their sources.
