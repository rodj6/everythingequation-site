## 3. Current incorporation and operative scaffolding

**Being stored, being available and being taken up now are different relations.** A retained disposition can shape an interpretation without being recalled. A representation can be accessible without being read. A contribution can enter experience without being endorsed as a reason for action.

Five functional roles specify the activity:

- $D$: actual reception or internal retrieval.
- $I$: interpretation through operative distinctions.
- $C$: consolidation of relevant relations.
- $M$: retention sufficient for their use.
- $U$: actual interpretive uptake.

These are roles, not a demand for five separate modules or an invariant serial pipeline. One implemented operation can perform several roles. Distributed operations can jointly perform a role. Here $U$ denotes uptake; the unsplit discussed elsewhere in the framework is a different use of the symbol.

Uptake occurs when what has become available participates as an interpreted contribution in ongoing recognition, relating, appraisal, orientation or response organization. A residue that alters a threshold does not establish that relation merely by causing a change. An image or bodily response can nevertheless enter current interpretation without the person recognizing the unconscious association that produced it. The interpreted response and its inaccessible formative cause have different roles.

The distinction has three parts. First, a disposition can be operative without appearing as a recollection. Second, accessibility can remain unused. Third, actual interpretive use does not require deliberate endorsement or practical authority. Hearing a sound need not mean accepting it as a reason to act.

The [constituted-agency mechanism](/consciousness/constituted-agency), especially its acquisition, assessment and installation analysis, makes related distinctions inspectable. A retained self-mechanism model can remain unread. It can be assessed while its installation route is blocked. Downstream compensation can reproduce successful performance while the original recommendation retains its authority. A direct neutral write can even match raw success without acquisition of the model. Successful output therefore cannot substitute for the actual organization of acquisition, interpretation and use. Practical authority is an agency question; current interpreted incorporation is broader.

Let $R_{\mathrm{later}}$ denote the capacity for later addressed retrieval. It differs from $U$. A construction can be taken up now and subsequently become irretrievable. An unused retrieval route supplies no uptake now. Operative retention $M$ need only keep the contribution available sufficiently for its actual use. A later recollection is another current construction concerning the earlier occasion, rather than an acknowledgment needed to complete that earlier occasion.

The complete organization $K_A$ exceeds the inventory of foreground contents. A learned disposition, an implementation condition and a currently recalled image need not have the same constitutive role. **What realizes and organizes the occurrence differs from what is currently incorporated as interpreted content.** Memory and acquired scaffolding can matter without every retained item being presently remembered.

## 4. Linked realization of one construction

A capacity list does not specify a construction. Imagine an enclosure containing a sensor, a word interpreter, an unrelated binding process, an archive and a controller using another input. Each role occurs somewhere. Yet no single construction has thereby been received, interpreted, consolidated, retained and taken up.

The weak condition is merely a separate witness for every role:

<a id="equation-1"></a>
$$
\bigwedge_{r\in\{D,I,C,M,U\}}\exists k_r\,W_r(A,k_r). \tag{1}
$$

Even writing the same identifier $k$ on all five components adds no actual relation between their operations. The requirement is one compatible realization of the relevant construction.

<a id="definition-4-1"></a>
### Definition 4.1: linked realization witness

Let $\mathcal R_k$ be a typed role structure for construction $k$. Its nodes specify the required role instances. Its relations specify operative source and occasion binding, retained-carrier availability, and actual recruitment or producer-to-consumer routes.

Let $\mathcal P_{A,J}$ be the unit's actual process structure over activity $J$. It includes event tokens, operation ports, retained carriers, causal order and relevant boundary conditions. A linked realization witness is a map

<a id="equation-2"></a>
$$
w:\mathcal R_k\longrightarrow\mathcal P_{A,J} \tag{2}
$$

that preserves the required types and actual relations under the declared realization contract.

The map need not be injective. Roles can overlap, and implementation can be distributed. A template for a temporally extended construction can place several roles in one physical operation while specifying the internal relations that operation realizes. If an event needs another event's output, the actual route and physical ordering must support use of that output. A retained contribution needs an operative carrier, rather than an analyst's reconstruction of what once happened.

Source and occasion binding make this stronger than transporting values. The same value can participate as a live estimate, a memory or an anticipation. A faithful witness preserves the relation actually asserted by the construction. An externally imposed probe also differs from the endogenous operation that uses a contribution. Semantic calibration belongs to the application; the witness records the implemented relations to which that calibration applies.

<a id="lemma-4-2"></a>
### Lemma 4.2: linked realization and separate capacities

A linked realization witness supplies instances of its required roles. Finding each role somewhere in an apparatus does not generally supply such a witness, even when those instances carry matching written identifiers.

**Proof.** A type- and relation-preserving map supplies the images of every required node and relation. For the converse, take isolated events with the five role types and no operative edges between them. Every separate role predicate holds, but a template requiring a cross-role use relation has no witness. Matching identifiers do not create the missing physical relations.

This is the difference between an actual construction and a collection of ingredients. The witness's physical content is the set of actual relations that realization must preserve. Its experiential significance follows from the constitutive principles in the previous chapter. Two constructions can each possess a linked witness without belonging to a conjoint construction of the whole unit. A unit-wide account must therefore specify the wider organization and the actual relations among incorporated constructions. Common enclosure cannot replace that work.
