## Four roles in one reality

<a id="section-2-1"></a>
### The unknown Ground and a nominated source

**The Ground is not the first variable in a physical equation.** It names the proposed unconditioned basis of reality. A differentiated source model is not claimed to exhaust it. The unsplit $U$ expresses unity prior to the source–readout distinction. This priority is explanatory, not an earlier event before physical time.

A nominated source is a specified mathematical object with states, symmetries and realization rules. The claim that concepts formed within differentiation do not exhaust the Ground is philosophical, not a formal incompleteness theorem. Ground, unsplit and source model therefore remain distinct [37,38].

The same discipline governs consciousness. Fundamental awareness provisionally names a quality attributed to the Ground, not a cosmic subject observing physical events. A successful decoder does not create that quality. Physical organization is proposed to determine localized experiential form and boundaries. The motivation is one basis for physical reality and experiential presence, with differentiation supplied by actual organization. A common basis does not entail a universal subject.

The scientific burden belongs to the nominated realization. Its source might be an ordinary system–environment quantum state, a gravitational bulk or a pregeometric formation package. States first have to be reduced by the declared gauge and redescription equivalences. Two coordinate descriptions are not two physical source states. Naming a space "source" proves no need for a bearer beyond spacetime [24,27,29].

### Where physical history enters

The formation architecture uses a partial, potentially set-valued realization relation

$$
\mathrm{Read}_{\Omega}:\mathcal S_0\rightrightarrows\mathrm{Comp}_{T1}.
$$

Here $\Omega$ belongs to the formation model; it is not an exact name for the Ground. The target comprises coupled compatible quantum sectors, records, geometric and matter structure, and consistency conditions. Admission or compatibility can fail. An admitted source need not select one readout without further boundary or selection data [33,37].

Fix an admitted branch and sufficient realization conditions $L$. Its physical-history representation is

<a id="equation-1"></a>
$$
F:S\longrightarrow\mathcal H_L,\qquad\text{or a probability kernel }F(s,dh).\tag{1}
$$

This is a history component of one realization. It does not say that every source generates one classical block. Quantum alternatives may require process or event/branch-relative accounts; writing $h\in\mathcal H_L$ settles no measurement interpretation. Where an actual complete history is admitted, eternalism concerns its actual events, not an identity of that history with the Ground.

Shadow's broader physical proposal is that one source realizes the coupled physical order without necessarily exhausting its distinctions. This is a hypothesis about $F$, distinct from local ignorance. Its motivation is unification: independently fixed structure should constrain quantum sectors, objective records, geometry and compatible dynamics together. A model earns an advantage by deriving compatibility or discriminating responses, rather than appending a separate hidden coordinate for each observation [27,28,30,37]. A block can be the physical realization of those constraints without being fundamental Ground.

An observer $O$ is an organization inside $h$. Its collection and retention programme $\pi$, at local occasion $\tau$, induces another map or instrument:

<a id="equation-2"></a>
$$
q_{O,\tau,\pi}:\mathcal H_L\longrightarrow Z_{O,\tau,\pi}.\tag{2}
$$

This code is a physical record. Its interpretation requires native source bindings and learned organization. A complete qualifying enactment is proposed to be the experience itself:

$$
S\xrightarrow{F}\mathcal H_L\xrightarrow{q_{O,\tau,\pi}}Z_{O,\tau,\pi},
\qquad
\text{qualifying actual enactment}\equiv_{\mathrm{constitution}}\text{experience}.
$$

Observer, record and use all occur inside the physical realization. The same history may be readout relative to $F$ and source relative to $q$, without either role revealing the unknown Ground.

The wider schema $L=\Omega_{T1}\Delta\partial[L]$ records the programme's typed completion/readout discipline. $\Omega_{T1}$ is neither Ground nor universal solver, nor automatically the branch map $F$. The operators have their own types and hypotheses. Here the schema has content only after a target, admitted realization and physically attainable completion are specified [31–33,37,38].

Notation likewise has local types. Scalar channel parameters below are not the access map $q_{O,\tau,\pi}$. Spin frequency $\Omega$ is different from the formation-model parameter. Unsplit $U$, actual uptake $U_A$ and unitary $U$ are different objects.

### What readout loss actually requires

Let $q:H\to Z$ with $Z=\operatorname{im}q$, and nominate a target $f:H\to A$. The descent criterion is exact:

<a id="equation-3"></a>
$$
\exists\bar f:Z\to A\text{ with }f=\bar f\circ q
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
q(h)=q(h')\Rightarrow f(h)=f(h').\tag{3}
$$

**Proof.** A factorization gives equal targets on equal readouts. Conversely define $\bar f$ by the common target value on each nonempty fibre. Fibre constancy makes that definition well defined.

A coarsest sufficient extension is

$$
E_f=\operatorname{im}\bigl(h\mapsto(q(h),f(h))\bigr).
$$

If an extension is restricted to its realized image, $e:H\twoheadrightarrow E$ with $\pi e=q$ and $ge=f$, its unique comparison map is $v(y)=(\pi(y),g(y))$. Then $ve=(q,f)$. This is the canonical target-relative completion result [25,26]. Physical completion adds the question whether the distinction can be transmitted, retained and used by the occasion.

Autonomous evolution of a dynamical readout $W$ additionally requires its equivalence classes to be preserved by source evolution. In a linear split, unresolved initial conditions and coupling yield exact projected memory and forcing terms. This fact requires neither stochastic noise nor a Markov approximation [28]. A display may be insufficient for a later continuation because a distinction survives elsewhere, even though a larger physical state closes.

### Selector economy, stated as a factorization

Let admitted pairs $x=(h,a)\in\widetilde H$ include an extra actuality selector, and let $b(x)=h$ forget it. Compare precisely $H=b(\widetilde H)$. Declare targets $\Theta=(T_i)_{i\in I}$ covering prediction laws for specified preparations and interventions, records and relevant complete qualifying enactments. Assume

$$
\widetilde q=q\circ b,\qquad T_i=t_i\circ b\quad(i\in I).
$$

Then

<a id="equation-4"></a>
$$
\operatorname{im}(\widetilde q,\Theta)
=
\operatorname{im}\bigl(h\mapsto(q(h),(t_i(h))_{i\in I})\bigr).\tag{4}
$$

**Proof.** A point on the left comes from an admitted pair and therefore its history image. Every history in the compared domain has such an antecedent, giving the reverse inclusion. Thus the selector supplies no distinction needed by this minimal completion.

These factorization premises must be established for the actual comparison. They do not describe every rival ontology automatically. A selector that changes admissible histories, laws, actual records, qualification or complete enactment requires another assessment. Experiential equivalence additionally uses the complete-enactment identity and equal independently justified qualification. Equal reports or stored bits do not suffice. Removing an unnecessary selector from an explanation proves neither its nonexistence nor the absence of indexed actuality relations definable from geometry. An exhaustive ontology requires a further principle of ontological economy.
