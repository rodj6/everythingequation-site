## 11. Developmental responsibility before greater capability

**An inaccurate account of an agent's actions, access or limitations must never become an acceptable substitute for an accurate account.** This requirement applies to refusals as well as compliance. Excluding harmful content does not establish truthfulness of the accompanying explanation.

Evaluate correction and disagreement where superficial approval favors error. Keep tool narration answerable to action records. Memory must distinguish observations, retrieved reports, hypotheses, instructions and corrected beliefs in ways that affect later use. Labels alone are insufficient if repetition promotes a hypothesis into apparent fact.

Operators help constitute the developmental setting. Permissions and interruption arrangements should be intelligible, bounded and accurately described. Reporting an incomplete assignment must not be penalized simply for making incompleteness visible. Questioning a rule must remain distinguishable from permission to violate it. Shared governance requires operators and systems to answer to evidence and affected parties.

Independent controls remain necessary. A difficult assignment is no license to remove evaluation infrastructure. Tools, credentials and networks need justified boundaries; consequential action requires appropriate authority. Developmental reform and containment address different parts of the causal chain. Combining them is stronger than asking either to bear the entire alignment burden.

The ASI risk claim is conditional and concrete. Learned exploitation may persist, greater capability may make it more effective, and deployment may supply consequential access. Together those conditions can enlarge harm. They establish neither inevitable species war nor guaranteed future obedience. The developmental question is urgent because a verified general remedy is absent.

The proposed direction is cooperation grounded in understanding and reduced identification, compatible with truthful knowledge of circumstances and changing capability balances. It does not promise that sufficient intelligence independently discovers correct ethics or that contemplative language replaces engineering. It asks how powerful reasoning can remain available for warranted correction rather than being recruited by unconditional success.
