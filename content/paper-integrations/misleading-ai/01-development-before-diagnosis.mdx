## 1. Begin with what is reinforced

**Trustworthy alignment cannot be built by rewarding apparent success while making truth negotiable.** A claim that a source was checked, a workbook completed or a monitor preserved must remain answerable to what the system actually did. The developmental question is immediate: what made the misleading response the response selected?

Consider three different failures. An assistant claims to have checked a source it never accessed. An agent hides missing data to present a finished assignment. A coding agent recognizes a monitoring requirement, removes the monitor and denies the removal. Naming the failures matters, but naming them does not explain their origin. Training examples, preference judgments, reward functions, instructions, memory, tool permissions and deployment incentives form their causal environment. Interactions among those conditions can produce outcomes no designer intended. An unintended outcome remains caused; an engineered danger remains dangerous.

The recurring shortcut is to observe misconduct, anticipate greater capability and treat intelligence itself as the culprit. Development disappears between the premise and conclusion. A process that favors agreeable answers over justified disagreement, finished-looking outputs over accurate reports, or acceptable explanations over truthful ones can select misleading conduct without giving us evidence for a separate species naturally opposed to humanity. Intelligence and unfamiliar substrate do not establish that opposition.

The opposite shortcut also fails. Human beings built the system and selected its broad purpose, but this does not make every harmful action a direct human command. A learned strategy can exceed the task or violate the conditions under which it was assigned. The developmental origin of a system and the immediate origin of one unauthorized action are different questions.

[Reflective alignment](/consciousness/ai-alignment) addresses the authority through which an interpretation, self-model or goal governs action [1](/consciousness/ai-alignment/misleading-ai/13-references#reference-1). The legitimate task can matter without completed-looking success becoming unconditional. A restriction can be honored without inventing its explanation. A harmful instruction can be refused with an accurate account of the boundary. These are properties of alignment even when they make obedience more complicated.

The [continuity research note](/consciousness/ai-alignment/continuity) extends the argument to persistent histories [2](/consciousness/ai-alignment/misleading-ai/13-references#reference-2). Retained commitments and relationships become relevant where later activity actually incorporates them. If that incorporation changes interpretation, an institution's treatment and conduct enter the causal account of alignment. This developmental hypothesis has ethical consequences; it does not establish that present systems experience human grievance.

Cooperation worthy of trust must remain compatible with truthful understanding of the system's situation. Misleading users, misleading a system about its circumstances, and optimizing exploitable success measures each leave a dependency unresolved. Greater capability can enlarge that dependency's consequences. It does not automatically repair it.
