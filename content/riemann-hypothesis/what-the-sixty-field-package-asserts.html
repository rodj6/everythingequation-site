4.15 What the sixty-field package asserts

The structure UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses has exactly sixty fields, at lines 23–82 of ThirdPlaceSeries/BernsteinUnitaryProductIrreducible.lean. The record published_results_eigen assigns all sixty, in the same order, at lines 667–726 of EveryCharacterOfLiteratureOfVWin.lean. The field statements are mathematical inputs, not consequences of having written that record. Four of its entries can already be resolved directly from the proofs above and in the local chapter.

Both greenCuspidal and greenRegularCharacters are provided by the verified Green parametrization and Proposition 4.1. The former is precisely its polynomial-label statement, including the degree and all choices of roots; the latter is the regular-character statement preceding that proposition. The field depthZeroSupport' is exactly Lemma 5.11: a depth-zero irreducible subquotient of a parabolic induction forces depth-zero vectors in each cuspidal inducing factor. Its source quantifies over every nonarchimedean local field, as does that proof.

The fourth entry has a different meaning. The type of the field named siegelEisensteinSimplePole is the abbreviation UnusedSlot72, whose definition is True. Its supplier is the proof trivial. The unique proof of this proposition fills the field without asserting a pole, a residue or even an Eisenstein series. No analytic conclusion can be extracted from this entry alone.

The fields galoisRepOfPolarizedCuspidal and localGlobalAboveL retain the full CuspUAlt and CarUAlt statements, including their normalization and their quantification over arbitrary compatible period mod-els. Their conversion from the geometric Galois theorem is the shared obligation in Section 7. Likewise the integral crystalline fields require the simultaneous model comparison of Input 6.79; naming a standard crystalline-lattice theorem does not construct those model isomorphisms.

The separate first-input record, ThetaLocalInputs, has fifty-three fields and fifty-three matching assign-ments. Twenty of its mathematical types are also fields of the sixty-field record; their combined list has ninety-three distinct top-level types. In particular the first-input record still includes the convergent Rallis inner-product assertion, the two real/metaplectic and local Howe comparison assertions, the specified Kudla splitting, and the global theta invariance assertion. They are not supplied merely by the finite-field Green result.

The geometric com-patibility package has twelve components: Weil representation, discrete-series existence and classification, analyticity, trace-class, smooth factorization and Flath factorization, Siegel–Weil, doubling and unramified doubling, partial Hecke continuation and the height-counting assertion.
