A local index a specifies a rational prime ℓ, a coefficient ring O_{a}, and a finite set X_{a} of eigencharacters. The ring O_{a} is the ring of integers of a finite extension of ℚ_{ℓ}; the associated integral Hecke algebra is a separating subalgebra T_{a} ⊂ O_{a}^{Xa}. There are subsets Z_{a}^{good} ⊂ Z_{a} ⊂ X_{a}. The representations below are the compatible integral Galois representations attached to these data, rather than arbitrary representations with the same dimension.

This description of O_{a} follows from the actual coefficient-ring hypotheses. They make it a discrete valuation domain, finite over ℤ_{ℓ}. The map from ℤ_{ℓ} is injective: a nonzero prime kernel would be (ℓ), making O_{a} finite over the finite field 𝔽_{ℓ}, hence a field, contrary to the discrete valuation ring hypothesis. Its fraction field is therefore finite over ℚ_{ℓ}. Every element of O_{a} is integral over ℤ_{ℓ}; conversely an element of its fraction field integral over ℤ_{ℓ} is integral over O_{a} and lies in O_{a}, since a discrete valuation ring is integrally closed. Thus it is exactly the integral closure of ℤ_{ℓ} in that finite extension.
