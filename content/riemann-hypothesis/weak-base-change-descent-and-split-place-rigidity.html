Theorem 4.42 (Global twisted-vs-unitary trace comparison dichotomy). Theorem 4.43 (Split-matching rigidity and weak base-change descent). Let E be a CM field with E/E^{+} unramified at all finite places, let n ≥1, and let π be a cuspidal automorphic representation of GL_{n}(𝔸_{E}) satisfying DualIsConj.

(i) (has_of_split) If D ∈ CD(E, n) is regular (D.Reg) and matches the Satake multiset of π at cofinitely many split places, then D.Has(π).

(ii) (descWeakAll) If π is regular algebraic and admits a Satake multiset at every non-split finite place,

then there exists an automorphic representation σ of the definite unitary group U(n)(𝔸_{E}+) that is unramified at every non-split finite place and matches the Satake multiset of π at cofinitely many split places.

(iii) (descWeak) In particular, UpperTheory.V.Z.Desc.DescWeak holds: for every prime p, every finite set S

of places of E^{+} containing all places above p such that every place of E over S is split, every rank 2 ≤ n ≤ 4, and every regular algebraic cuspidal representation π of GL_{n}(𝔸_{E}) satisfying DualIsConj that admits a Satake multiset at every place of E not lying over S, there exists a definite unitary automorphic representation σ as in (ii).

Proof. For (i), Proposition 4.38 (unitOf) shows that [π] is unitary, and Lemma 4.39 (partNorm absInf) shows that every factor D.P(i) of D is unitary. Applying Proposition 4.40 (splitRigid) to [π] and the finite family (D.N(i), D.P(i))_{i∈Fin(D.c)} yields an index i with (D.N(i), D.P(i)) = (n, [π]), which is precisely D.Has(π).

For (ii), apply Theorem 4.42 (runTh). If the second alternative held, it would supply a regular isobaric datum D ∈ CD(E, n) with ¬D.Has(π) matching π at cofinitely many split places, contradicting (i). Hence the first alternative holds, proving descWeakAll.

For (iii), since 2 ≤n implies 1 ≤n, and every non-split place w ∈ Pl(E) cannot lie over S (as every place over S is split by hypothesis), the off-S Satake hypothesis supplies a Satake multiset for π at every non-split place. Applying (ii) therefore yields UpperTheory.V.Z.Desc.DescWeak.
