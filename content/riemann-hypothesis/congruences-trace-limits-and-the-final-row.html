The weight-changing approximation first alters the weight only at the conjugate pair of embeddings over v^{′}, giving the large-gap weight there. To impose generic Satake parameters at finitely many places Z, the source uses one test operator at each z ∈Z whose eigenvalue is nonzero exactly when the chosen nonzero genericity polynomial is nonzero on the Satake parameter. Append these test eigenvalues, and the scaled Satake power sums at all controlled places above ℓ, to the original Hecke family. The scalars are chosen nonzero so the enlarged operators preserve the required integral bounds. Apply the weight and elliptic approximations to this entire family. In Lemma 7.88 take Q to be the product of the appended test-eigenvalue variables. It retains simultaneous nonvanishing at every z, while retaining approximation of every polynomial in all the other eigenvalues and power sums.
