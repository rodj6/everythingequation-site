Proposition 7.108 (Assembly of the local row). Suppose the construction has given, for every regular alge-braic polarized cuspidal π, one continuous semisimple weakly attached representation r, and the following upgrades for every such weakly attached r: spherical crystalline and Hodge comparison; the plain-toral local comparison; and the local comparison at first-congruence fixed vectors with the support-separation condition described below. Suppose also the local Langlands generic-support implications used below hold. Then these upgrades give the full row in Definition 7.1, retaining that same r.

Proof. First define the integer weight multiset. Regular algebraicity says that each infinitesimal-character entry has the form (a −1)/2 + n for an integer n. Consequently the entries (a −1)/2 −z are integers. Lift them, with their multiplicities, to a multiset H_{τ} in ℤ. Polarization supplies one integer w such that complex conjugation reflects the infinitesimal-character multiset by the affine map

infChar_{τc}(π) = (a −1) −w −z : z ∈ infChar_{τ}(π) .

Substitute the formula for H_{τ} into that affine equality. The resulting equality over ℂ is (H_{τc})_{ℂ} = ({w −h : h ∈ H_{τ}})_{ℂ}. Injectivity of ℤ → ℂ gives the equality of integer multisets, including multiplicities. This chooses the same w, H before any local coefficient bridge is considered.

Let y = r_{c}(algNorm σ) at a fixed local component. Its dimension is a, by the dimension law for r_{c}. The associated Galois Weil–Deligne object also has dimension a, so the dimension clause is automatic.

For the commutator alternative assume N_{y} = 0. The local component of a cuspidal GL_{a} automorphic representation is generic. The generic local Langlands support implication in the source says that the com-mutator condition and N_{y} = 0 make its normalized support plain toral: all cuspidal support factors have rank one and are unlinked. The plain-toral upgrade applies to this weakly attached r. Away from ℓ it identifies its full local object with y. Above ℓ it supplies de Rham and potentially semistable comparison, N = 0, and equality of all Weil characteristic polynomials. Frobenius-semisimple Weil–Deligne objects with zero monodromy are semisimple Weil representations; equality of their characteristic polynomials on all Weil el-ements identifies them by the semisimple character criterion. Proposition 7.109 (Normalization of the twists and integer weights). The source’s algNorm σ is σ ⊗| det |^{(1−a)/2}. Its formula for the integer Hodge–Tate multiset has the same affine shift as the weight convention in BLGGT, Theorem 2.1.1.

Proof. The source defines ν^{s} by the s-power of the module character of additive Haar measure on the local field, and algNorm σ = σν^{(1−a)/2}. On a uniformizer ϖ, the module character is q^{−1}: the valuation ring is the disjoint union of q translates of ϖ𝒪, each of equal Haar measure. On a unit the module character is one, since it preserves the valuation ring. Multiplicativity therefore identifies this character with the normalized local absolute value. Twisting through the determinant gives the claimed normalization.

Use zero-based highest-weight coordinates λ_{0} ≥· · · ≥ λ_{a−1}. The infinitesimal-character entries are λ_{k}+(a−1)/2−k. The source’s subtraction (a−1)/2−z gives exactly k−λ_{k}. There is a published theorem covering a substantial part of the desired conventional output. Barnet-Lamb, Gee, Geraghty and Taylor [37, Theorem 2.1.1], Potential automorphy and change of weight, construct a continuous semisimple representation for a regular algebraic cuspidal polarized pair. It gives Frobenius-semisimplified local Langlands compatibility away from ℓ, de Rham restrictions and the stated Hodge–Tate weight formula above ℓ, and compatibility above ℓ at Iwahori level; spherical components are crystalline. This theorem is an appropriate candidate for replacing the full construction when only its stated conclusions are used. Its hypotheses are of the required CM, regular algebraic, cuspidal and polarized type. However, it is not by itself a drop-in replacement for every clause in Definition 7.1: identification of every recognized local Langlands map with the conventional parameter, its arbitrary induced period-ring bridges, and the above-ℓ tame-squarefree/commutator alternatives must be matched explicitly (which is why Subsection 7.15 proves

Definition 7.1 directly). In particular, an Iwahori theorem cannot be applied to an arbitrary local component without a further argument.

For comparison, Caraiani [38, Theorem 1.1], Monodromy and local-global compatibility for ℓ = p, proves the full Frobenius-semisimplified above-ℓ comparison for conjugate-self-dual cohomological cuspi-dal representations over CM fields, of rank at least two. Its theorem therefore removes the Iwahori restriction in that specific setting. Passing from a general polarized pair in this manuscript to that conjugate-self-dual setting requires a compatible Hecke twist or base-change-and-descent argument; it is not a change of notation. Rank one is a separate class-field comparison.
