Abstract

We reconstruct the ordinary Riemann Hypothesis argument rooted at riemannHypothesis : RiemannHypothesis := rhOfAll in the supplied Lean project. The underlying mathematical argument is credited to Levent Alpöge and Claude; this manuscript is a human-readable reconstruction prepared by Jeremy Rodgers for referee checking. We preserve the source’s hypotheses, conventions, and quantified estimates, reconstruct all forty-five handoff gap groups and all fourteen original frontier branches across Sections 2–17, match every established literature input by precise statement and source, and assemble the full argument from the terminal row and local-parameter witnesses to ordinary RH in Proposition 17.4 and Theorem 17.5 (including the uniform Breuil–Kisin Mittag–Leffler containment in Proposition 7.102, the ramification-based prime-cyclic base-change induction in Proposition 7.114, the global face-cutting strictness of the integral Siegel fan in Lemma 10.65, and the explicit accounting for Mathlib’s total-function value at s = 1 in ZF). Standard published theorems cited along the way are explicitly identified. A transitive UTF-8 textual source scan of the local modules imported by Main.lean (175,187 modules) reports zero missing local modules and zero textual axiom, sorry, or admit declarations; that textual scan is distinct from a Lean 4 kernel check, as no local Lean 4 elaboration or kernel typechecking of the pinned environment has been run here. Review passes applied to this manuscript were targeted audits of specific steps and interfaces rather than a complete line-by-line independent verification of all pages.
