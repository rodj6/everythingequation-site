9.5 A precise geometric citation: point separation

One of the fifteen inputs can be matched to a specific established theorem, including its numerical constant. Kollár, Singularities of Pairs, Theorem5.9, pp.30–31 of arXiv:alg-geom/9601026v2, states the following: for a proper klt pair (X, ∆) and a line bundle M ≡ K_{X} + ∆+ N, where N is a nef and big ℚ-Cartier ℚ-divisor, two distinct closed points are separated by global sections of M if positive c(e) satisfy

(N^{e}·Z) > c(e)^{e} for every positive-dimensional irreducible Z through either point, ≤1.

We use exactly this nef-and-big version [51, Theorem5.9].
