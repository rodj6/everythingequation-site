4.19 Matching finite tensors and factoring the integral

Fix quotient maps p_{∞}, p^{′}_{∞} and p_{v}, p^{′}_{v} as in Input 4.77. For a finite set T containing the ramification set, a polynomial adelic function is a finite sum of pure functions

with f_{i} in the polynomial Fock space, each ϕ_{i,v} Schwartz–Bruhat, and ϕ_{i,v} the specified vacuum outside T. Two such sums match over T when their images agree in Π_{∞}⊗⊗_{v∈T}Π_{v}. This equality concerns sums of tensors, so it does not require matching the individual summands.

Lemma 4.83 (Enlargement and linearity of polynomial matching). If a pair matches over S and S ⊂ T, with the ramification set contained in S, it matches over T. The set of polynomial matching pairs, allowing such a finite set, is a complex vector subspace of the product of the two function spaces.

Proof. For v /∈S the images of the two vacua coincide; call that vector z_{v} ∈Π_{v}. The multilinear map

is well defined by the universal property of the finite tensor product, with factors placed in their indexed positions. Tensoring it with the identity on Π_{∞} carries the equality over S to the equality over T. The unchanged local functions outside S are exactly these vacua, so this is the desired enlarged matching equality.

The pair (0, 0) is represented by empty sums. For two matching pairs choose the common set T_{1}∪T_{2} and enlarge both equalities by the first argument. Multiply the archimedean factors in the first pair by any c ∈ℂ and concatenate its finite sums with those of the second pair. Linearity of p_{∞} and of the tensor product turns their images into c times the first tensor sum plus the second. The same operation on both sides preserves the equality, proving closure under (x, y) →→cx + y and hence the vector-subspace assertion.

Lemma 4.84 (The good-place action on matching pairs). Fix a split finite place v and a splitting embedding E → F_{v}. Assume the local quotient maps are onto and the two local Weil actions respect their common quotient: equal quotient images have equal quotient images after the simultaneous right action of any h ∈ U(W)(F_{v}). Assume also that the global action supported at v acts on a pure adelic function by its local action at v and leaves all other factors unchanged. Then the simultaneous action of h preserves polynomial matching.

Proof. Enlarge the matching set to contain v. Let O, O^{′} denote the two local action operators on the local Schwartz–Bruhat spaces. Choose a linear section s : Π_{v} →𝒮_{v} of p_{v}, and set A = p_{v}Os. Compatibility applied to sy and any x^{′} ∈𝒮_{v}^{′} with p^{′}_{v}x^{′} = y gives Ay = p^{′}_{v}O^{′}x^{′}. Conversely, for any x ∈𝒮_{v} choose x^{′} with p^{′}_{v}x^{′} = p_{v}x. Compatibility and the preceding equality give p_{v}Ox = p^{′}_{v}O^{′}x^{′} = A(p_{v}x). Thus A is the same induced operator for both local models; in particular its value is independent of the chosen section.

Apply the identity on Π_{∞} and on every finite factor other than v, and A on the v factor, to the matching tensor equality. Its two new sides are exactly the images of the simultaneously acted functions, by p_{v}O = Ap_{v}, p^{′}_{v}O^{′} = Ap_{v}^{′} and the assumed local description of the global action. Schwartz–Bruhat membership is preserved by O, O^{′}. All factors outside the enlarged finite set are still the vacua. This proves polynomial matching of the acted pair.
