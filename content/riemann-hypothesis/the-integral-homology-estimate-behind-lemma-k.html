The construction uses primitive families of chain functionals: they vanish on boundaries, commute with stabilisation, and evaluate a block diagonal repetition of d copies as d times the original value. The coefficient group is killed by q = ℓ^{ν}. These are three separate requirements; stability of the homology groups alone does not imply the repetition rule.

Lemma 8.18 (Counting values from a rank bound). Let H be an abelian group with finite torsion subgroup, J ≤H of ℤ-rank at most r, and f : H →B an additive map, where qB = 0, q ≥1. Every finite subset of f(J) has at most q^{r}| tors H| elements. If g : Y →B, β : Y →A are additive, A is finite, and every finite subset of g(ker β) has at most b elements, every finite subset of g(Y ) has at most b|A| elements.

Proof. For a finite set of values choose preimages and let J^{′} ≤ J be their finitely generated integer span. The finitely generated torsion-free group J^{′}/ tors J^{′} is free of rank s ≤r. Choose lifts x_{1}, . . . , x_{s} of a basis. Every x ∈J^{′} differs from ∑a_{i}x_{i} by a torsion element. Under f, each integer a_{i} may be reduced modulo q. Thus all the chosen values lie in the image of

{0, . . . , q −1}^{s}× tors H −→B, (a, t) −−→ a_{i}f(x_{i}) + f(t).

Its domain has at most q^{r}| tors H| elements. For the second assertion, choose preimages of the finite set and partition it according to their β-values. Subtracting a fixed value in any nonempty fibre embeds that fibre into g(ker β); each fibre has at most b elements and there are at most |A| fibres.
