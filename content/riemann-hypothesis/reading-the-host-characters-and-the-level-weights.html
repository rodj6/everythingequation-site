The following description states exactly the host information used by the analytic argument. Let r : (ℤ/Nℤ)^{×} ↠Γ be surjective. For ψ ∈Γ̂, let χ_{ψ} be the character modulo N whose value at a unit is ψ(r(u)), and let p_{ψ} be its inducing primitive character. Different ψ’s give different p_{ψ}’s: equality of their primitive characters gives equality after induction to N, then equality on the image of r, which is all of Γ. In particular p_{ψ} = 1 if and only if ψ = 1.

This last conductor criterion follows directly from the Chinese remainder decomposition of the unit group into its prime-power factors: for each factor choose the least exponent through which its character factors.
