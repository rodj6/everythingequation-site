Proposition 7.117 (The row-to-window interface). The row used by the window construction is Defini-tion 7.1, with the source’s recognition predicate IsRecT. Once that base-change input and the local, automorphic, cohomological and CM inputs of the window construction are established, the result is the quantitative assertion VWin of Definition 8.1, with constant 401.

Proof. The wrapper vwin_of_row passes precisely three arguments to automorphic_window_of_inputs: the record formed from the row, the independently constructed baseChangeN, and the row itself. The resulting literal automorphy input is the one used for the window estimate. Consequently the numerical conclusion is exactly the assertion whose arithmetic reduction is proved in Proposition 8.4; no loss or change of the constant occurs in this wrapper. This proves the interface and identifies the independent hypotheses needed to use it.
