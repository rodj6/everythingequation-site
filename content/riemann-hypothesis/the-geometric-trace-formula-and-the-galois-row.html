7 The geometric trace formula and the Galois row

The geometric branch is used to supply Galois representations attached to regular algebraic polarized cuspidal automorphic representations. Its output is stronger than the existence of a representation with the expected unramified Frobenius polynomials: it includes specified local comparisons at the coefficient prime. We state that output first, then explain the geometric trace calculation and the two distinct passages from geometric realizations to one representation. These passages must be distinguished because the first geometric assertion permits different representations at different places.
