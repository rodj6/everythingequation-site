Background fact 15.6 (Characteristic-free nef inputs from the original papers). For a coherent sheaf ℱ on a projective scheme and a nef line bundle L over an algebraically closed field,

h^{q}(X, ℱ ⊗L^{m}) = O(m^{r−q}) (q ≥1), r = dim Supp ℱ, (NN.4)

where negative exponents mean eventual vanishing. This is Fujita’s Theorem 6.2, pp. 370–371 [47]; the possibly stronger exponent involving his index of positivity is not needed. His curve criterion Theorem 2.7, p. 361, identifies numerical semipositivity with nefness on integral varieties. Applied to the reduced compo-nents of the support, it gives the hypothesis in (NN.4). The proof explicitly treats positive characteristic in

Lemma 5.8 and nilpotent schemes in 5.10; there is no characteristic-zero restriction.

We also use: if H is ample and L is nef on a projective scheme over a field, then H ⊗L^{m} is ample for every m ≥0 [48, Proposition 2.13]. The cited author version includes the 2018 correction; that correction leaves this proposition unchanged.

Proof. First suppose d = dim X ≥ 1 and L_{1}, . . . , L_{d} are nef. Choose an ample H. By Fact 15.6, H ⊗ L_{i}^{n} is ample for every positive integer n. Mixed top intersection of ample line bundles is nonnegative, including on nonreduced schemes, for the following reason. Replace each bundle by a positive very ample power. Successively choose sections avoiding the associated points of the coherent quotient produced at the preceding step. This is possible over the infinite field k. Each multiplication is injective, and each cokernel has support of one smaller dimension. After d steps, it is supported in dimension zero (or is zero). By additivity of Euler characteristics the intersection is its Euler characteristic, hence its nonnegative section dimension. Undoing the positive powers divides by their positive product, using the multilinearity proved in Lemma 15.5.

Apply this to H ⊗L^{n}_{i} and divide the resulting inequality by n^{d}. Expanding by multilinearity, its limit as n →∞ is I_{X}(L_{1}, . . . , L_{d}); every term containing an H has a smaller power of n and disappears. The limit is nonnegative. For d = 0, the empty intersection is χ(𝒪_{X}) = h^{0}(𝒪_{X}) ≥0. This proves NefProductNonneg. In particular no reduction or equidimensionality assumption has entered the argument: the sections avoided all associated points, including embedded ones, and the final zero-dimensional sheaf retains their lengths.
