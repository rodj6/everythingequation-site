10.4 The integral polarized retract

Lemma 10.19 (A polarization and an integral positive quasi-inverse). Let A/S be a projective abelian scheme with relatively ample line bundle L, and suppose S is connected. Its polarization λ : A →A^{∨} has constant finite locally free degree n ≥1. There is a relatively ample line bundle N on A^{∨} whose polarization µ : A^{∨}→A satisfies

µλ = [n]_{A}, λµ = [n]_{A}∨. (AAG.4)

Proof. Take N = Norm_{λ} L. The norm for a finite locally free map is defined on functions by the deter-minant of multiplication. Applying it to the transition units of a line bundle defines its norm line bundle. Determinants show directly that it commutes with base change, tensor product, and pullback from the target, with Norm_{λ}(λ^{∗}Q) = Q^{n}. These are the canonical norm and induced line norm of [14, Tags 0BD2,0BCY]. The norm of an ample line is ample; the section proof is [14, Tag 0BD0]. Applied after arbitrary affine base restriction it gives relative ampleness of N.

On the dual abelian schemes this norm induces a homomorphism u : Pic^{0}(A/S) → Pic^{0}(A^{∨}/S). The preceding power identity gives uλ^{∨}= [n]. A polarization is symmetric, so λ^{∨}= λ under biduality. For any scheme-valued point x of A, translation by x above λ and translation by λx below it form a cartesian square. Base-change compatibility of the norm therefore gives

φ_{N}(λx) = Norm_{λ}(t^{∗}_{x}L ⊗L^{−1}) = u(λx) = [n]x.

Since λ is faithfully flat, this equality determines φ_{N} = u. Put µ = φ_{N}. We have µλ = [n]. Composing with λ and cancelling its faithfully flat right factor gives λµ = [n] as well. All arguments concern group schemes, not just geometric points, so include inseparable polarizations.

We still need a symmetric line representative, not merely the symmetric principal homomorphism. For an abelian scheme and a polarization Λ, the sheaf of symmetric representatives of its Néron–Severi class is a torsor under B^{∨}[2]. The exact sequence is [17, (3.7),p. 19]; it is fppf exact, so has no assumption that 2 be invertible. This torsor is a finite locally free, hence proper, algebraic space over R (in fact finite over the scheme base, hence a scheme). Its generic fiber has a C-point, since C is algebraically closed. The valuative criterion of properness extends this point to an R-point. Here Frac R = C: for every nonzero z ∈C either z or z^{−1} belongs to the valuation ring. Finally, if a scheme-valued point x has translation difference t^{∗}_{x}M^{′} ⊗(M^{′})^{−1} trivial locally over that scheme, its relative Picard class is zero. This class is φ_{M}′(x) = Λ(x). The inverse in (AAAG.1) forces x = 0. This is precisely KTriv, including its quantification over arbitrary affine test schemes. Inclusion of the first copy of A into B and projection onto that copy are group homomorphisms whose composite is the identity. Taking P = (B, M^{′}) and these two maps proves the stated retract.
