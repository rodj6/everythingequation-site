7.1 The precise output required of the row

Let E be a CM number field, E^{+} its maximal totally real subfield, E/E an algebraic closure, ℓ a prime, and ι : ℚ_{ℓ}≃ℂ a field isomorphism. Write G_{E} = Gal(E/E). The source fixes a local Langlands recognition predicate ℛ_{v}. Thus ℛ_{v}(r_{c}) says that a map r_{c} from irreducible representations of GL_{a}(E_{v}) to Weil–Deligne representations has the prescribed normalization. Quantification over r_{c} below is part of the assertion, rather than a choice of a different normalization for each application.

Here is a convenient way to record exactly the weaker local relation used in the final row. For two local Weil–Deligne objects x, y, write x ⪯_{v} y if the following conditions hold:

(i) dim x = dim y.

(ii) If every commutator [s, t] has characteristic polynomial (T −1)^{dim y} on y, and y satisfies the source’s

predicate mono0, then x = y.

(iii) Suppose E_{v} is continuously isomorphic to ℚ_{p}, with a = 1 or 16a^{2} < p. If y is tame, satisfies the

source’s squarefreeness condition, and satisfies mono0, then x satisfies mono0 and x, y have equal characteristic polynomials on every Weil element of degree zero.

The squarefreeness condition here has a specific meaning. For a tame-inertia generator, every repeated root z of its inertia polynomial must satisfy z^{qv−1} = 1. Neither this condition nor the commutator condition is replaced by a generic assertion of local–global compatibility. We retain the notation mono0 for the condi-tion N = 0, where N is the monodromy endomorphism. Indeed the source defines this condition on an isomorphism class by descent of N = 0 on a representative, and proves that equality of isomorphism classes preserves it. Its wdOf also uses Frobenius semisimplification, as specified by IsFrobSS, rather than replacing the monodromy operator by zero.
