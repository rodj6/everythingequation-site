2.1 Conventions and established analytic inputs

We write ζ for the continued Riemann zeta function and L(s, χ) for the continued Dirichlet L-function of a character χ modulo a positive integer N. For positive real x we use x^{s}= exp(s log x) with the real logarithm. The Lean functions are total functions: a value is assigned even at a pole. All substantive applications below either avoid the pole or explicitly retain the source’s convention at it.
