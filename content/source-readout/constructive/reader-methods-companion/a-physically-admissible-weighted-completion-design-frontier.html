A physically admissible weighted completion-design frontier

The deterministic collision test identifies whether a target is exactly fixed by a record. A physical quantum reader also needs a quantitative error analysis when different targets can produce the same finite record. Here rational record likelihoods and exact optimization close that gap for one finite, explicitly specified phase-source experiment. This is a new programme-specific construction, independent of the ETH theorem; it demonstrates a substantive consumer of the weighted-completion interface. It is not a faster implementation of the ETH algorithm.
