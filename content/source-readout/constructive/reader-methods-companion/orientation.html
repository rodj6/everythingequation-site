Abstract

We give supporting methods and restricted benchmarks for common-sign readout of a fixed four-state qubit source: deterministic measurement allocation, an expanded rational menu, continuous-risk lower backups, fixed-nuisance polynomial certificates and physical timing/calibration interfaces. Every result retains its own measurement and stability contract. These methods support the associated reader frontiers without settling the remaining adjacent copy thresholds or a universal total-resource optimum.

This supplement develops optimization and deployment interfaces within Shadow Theory’s pre-existing source–readout programme [5]. It supplies independently checkable methods for the fixed-label four-state source. Copies are independent conditional on one label throughout a block; the preparation controller is inaccessible.
