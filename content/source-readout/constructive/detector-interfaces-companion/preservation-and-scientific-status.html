Preservation and scientific status

The companion’s proof interfaces differ in observable task and access permissions. Their numerical examples are synthetic; tests corroborate the proofs rather than constitute empirical evidence. Original source/controller, reference, clock, preparation, null/loss and retained-output conditions survive every reuse. The general qutrit method estimates a full effect family; the two-template method certifies a smaller task and does not identify the same quantum output. Their sample counts are therefore not a universal tomography comparison. No physical horizon-to-biological channel has been supplied; no actual-event affinity is inferred from an averaged density equation. The source–readout completion framework remains compatible with unrestricted operational surrogates, so a constrained detector distinction does not establish unique ontology.
