The same support argument gives an observable matrix witness and a nonzero finite-record approximation floor. The constants are deliberately conservative; they are not proposed experimental accuracy targets.

What changes. The earlier exact dimension statement was robust only along a specified noise curve. The new witness supplies a finite-output accuracy obstruction against all ordinary qutrit-cut realizations, including time-dependent alternatives and complete null/loss records. It is a quantitative outer bound, not a sharp approximate-memory frontier, a large practical separation, or a resource theorem for nonlinear ontic assignments. Larger horizons inherit the same floor by retaining the four-record marginal. It compares the declared classical record law; it identifies no microscopic source or conscious perspective.
