The result preserves every finite output word, and its cone constraints are certified on an entire parameter interval by exact rational arithmetic. A separate analytic construction gives a narrower interval without computer-assisted positivity checks. Both constructions use ordinary quantum instruments; neither derives the Born rule or an actual-event selection law.

What the construction establishes

The original trine phase geometry supplied a four-dimensional realization; its zero-noise optimality proof did not settle noisy qutrit models. The present construction supplies such models explicitly and proves exact minimality on a full interval. It introduces a hierarchical encoding of the two individual labels and their joint Fourier components, with amplitudes that shrink at different rates. This permits the update maps to remain inside the CP cone while retaining the complete order-two law. The sharper

interval uses a rational qutrit frame and a convex polynomial certificate; it does not claim the endpoint is optimal. The finite circular phase representation draws on the geometric phase and angular-register descrip-tions in [6, abstract and Sections 2–3] and [7, pp. 64–68]. The noisy qutrit encoding, update maps and interval certificates are the constructions established here. The CP-realization problem, nonorthogonal quantum process memories, quantum dimension advantages and lower bounds are established research areas [3–5, 8, 11]. The contribution is the explicit full-law construction, interval certificate and matching dimension bound for this declared delayed process. Targeted primary-literature checks do not establish publication priority. No claim is made about reduced complete environment memory, a Hamiltonian mechanism, CSL dynamics, Born-rule selection, actual-event affinity, or conscious perspective.
