What is being certified

This paper develops task-relative validation and retained-record resource comparisons within Shadow Theory’s pre-existing source–readout programme [6]. The results concern the declared reader and validation interfaces. A reader is frozen before validation. Each complete-copy experiment returns a sign at its deadline, retaining all settings, signs, nulls, root seeds and declared timings. A trusted evaluator knows the source label for scoring; the reader does not. The deployment contract is the nominated maximum label error, not recovery of a hidden source or characterization of all unread outputs. For the structured comparisons, the informative trajectory alphabet is the root seed, commanded actions and ternary outcomes (+ 0). Declared timing fields are determined by these records or have the same source-and model-independent conditional law in all compared worlds. They add no likelihood factor. A source-or visibility-dependent arrival-time observation would enlarge the statistical experiment and requires its own response model; it is not supplied by the declared effects or by a different detector construction. We separate three interfaces: unrestricted terminal Bernoulli risks; richer records with specified trusted ancillary laws; and a shared physical response model whose trial likelihood can be evaluated. Their information minima need not agree. Both sides of each resource comparison receive the same preparation and response promises. Validation stock, policy/controller state, archive access, clocks, phase references and arithmetic are separate resources. Unless a finite-cap theorem explicitly says otherwise, a finite expected stopping cost does not imply a finite worst-case duration. The concrete reader is the 697-node frozen rational fourteen-copy DAG in the supplement, with action/three-child pointers and terminal signs.
