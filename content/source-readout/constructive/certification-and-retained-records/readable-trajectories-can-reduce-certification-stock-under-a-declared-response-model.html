Deployment, resources and limitations

The evaluator needs one trusted source label, a fresh uniform two-bit reader seed per whole experiment, access to all commanded actions and outcomes, and exact or conservative log-likelihood accumulation. Its own acceptance controller is outside the fourteen-copy deployment reader. Counting fourteen source preparations per observation already includes nulls and every complete experiment; history extraction, arithmetic, references, source readiness, reset and clock resources remain additional. Finite worst-case counters and elapsed time are not bounded by this expectation theorem. The reported cost bounds concern the defined exact likelihood crossing. Here its rational kernel ratios permit an exact product and threshold comparison. A conservative approximate log accumulator can preserve false-certificate control, but retaining the same acceptance and cost bounds requires charging its rounding or drift loss separately.
