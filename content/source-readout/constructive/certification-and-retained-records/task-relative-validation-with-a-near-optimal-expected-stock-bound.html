Task-relative validation with a near-optimal expected stock bound

Response-level calibration can certify more than one frozen reader needs. We validate its four terminal-error probabilities directly. Exact binomial gates give a finite-budget interface; likelihood-ratio gates give an anytime interface with a sharp expected-stock comparison. These are established testing methods, newly applied and fully proved for the declared source–reader contract, not a new quantum probability law.

The validation interface and indispensable stability promise

Fix a complete reader policy Π with horizon, root randomness, null handling and final decoder before opening validation data. A trusted calibration controller requests one of the four source labels , supplies fresh copies independent conditional on that fixed label, and records the terminal error Π decides the wrong sign. The label is available to the external auditor for scoring, but is not an input to the reader policy. Every ordered setting, sign, null and controller seed remains retained; no record is postselected. The physical stability contract is that the repeated complete experiments, conditional on the fixed device and trusted label, have one error probability and fresh independent stock. Predictably choosing the next label from past calibration data is allowed, provided the conditional error law remains . Deployment must have the same conditional policy-error probabilities. This promise is not inferred from reset, past calibration, or a detector’s average density equation. Drift, changed preparation, unread environment memory, policy changes and reference depletion require separate allowances. The target is max. The gate reads only the terminal error bits and trusted label identifiers for its decisions. Richer archives are appended but not queried. Thus it certifies one frozen policy’s loss, not a continuous response curve, arbitrary future interventions or a complete retained quantum instrument. This restricted information interface is also the class of the matching stock lower bound in Section 3.
