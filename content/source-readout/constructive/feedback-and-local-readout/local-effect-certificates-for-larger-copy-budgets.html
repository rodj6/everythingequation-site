Verification and practical limits

The two delivered research11/bounds/ entry points verify the full nominal and robust certificates, respectively. An additional independent rational tensor-basis check verifies the normalized symmetric-power identity on 420 small-sector entries; its source and output accompany the supplement. These finite identity checks support implementation inspection, while the displayed representation and residual-corrected dual proofs supply the universal argument. Solver messages such as optimal_inaccurate are retained as proposal metadata; they are not proofs. The efficient exact core uses Python’s standard library. Optional proposal generation requires NumPy, CVXPY and an SDP solver. The computational improvement is material: a polynomial-size local-effect relaxation gives repro-ducible all-angle bounds without enumerating angular grid policies or exponential outcome trees. It does not establish that this relaxation is tight or that an SDP optimizer can be implemented as a reader. Its physical role is a comparison certificate. No Born-selection, consciousness, trine-resource or unique-source conclusion follows.
