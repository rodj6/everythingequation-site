Point-based synthesis and exact separation from its numerics

The offline construction specializes point-based POMDP value iteration [8] to a static four-label terminal-cost problem. For a collection of previous feasible policy vectors and a design belief, choose separately for each action/outcome a child minimizing), sum its four conditional errors, then select an action of least design cost. The resulting object is an actual policy graph, even if floating-point comparisons select a suboptimal child. Duplicate action/child triples are merged, and unreachable nodes are removed when freezing a nominated root. Beliefs were sampled both from Dirichlet distributions and actual random measurement trajectories, with the four source symmetries included. The pilot used 21461 distinct design beliefs and thirteen rational actions. Explicit controller and retained-record resources

The fourteen-copy graph has 697 internal nodes and two terminal decisions. A global node pointer therefore needs ten bits; two independent root symmetry bits are additional ready/controller stock. A direct ROM upper account uses four bits per base action and three ten-bit child pointers per internal node, plus two terminal decision bits: at most 23700 bits, excluding phase-command constants, decoder/control arithmetic, physical clock, references and actuator state. This is an attaining upper account, not a controller minimum. There are at most 26 physical phase commands after the root axis transform. A conservative archive uses five command-ID bits and two outcome bits per trial, plus root-seed and timing metadata. All histories remain retained even though feedback uses only the node pointer. Exact mathematical rational commands do not assert infinite physical phase precision. A deployment must separately bound implementation error, preserve preparation/detector stability, charge phase controls and references, and validate the actual frozen reader if the nominal model is untrusted. No hardware, energy, clock-speed or thermodynamic advantage follows from the ROM count or the error improvement alone. The separate calibration route can consume this finite policy as a declared object without identifying its entire residual quantum instrument.
