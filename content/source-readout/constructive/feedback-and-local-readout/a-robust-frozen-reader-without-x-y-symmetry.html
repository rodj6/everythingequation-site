Calibration and physical/resource limits

The theorem’s intervals are admitted premises until certified by a trusted calibration experiment or an independent device model. Four trusted source preparations at the actual frozen setting can directly bound the four sign/null kernels without inferring the entire channel or nuisance parameters uniquely. Such a calibration must pay for its probes, confidence coverage, source trust and stability into deployment. A control model claiming a lower gain or bounded phase over an interval of settings needs additional justified coverage; samples at one angle do not certify all angles for an arbitrary detector. The frozen majority controller needs a running signed count and deadline counter, plus a fair terminal tie bit; it has no setting-feedback branch. The angle itself must be prepared and maintained, with its phase reference and accuracy charged. Each physical realization must state whether the phase is applied separately to every fresh copy or configured once in a held passive reader. Their timing costs differ. The source copies are provided independently, not cloned from an unknown specimen, and the ordered archive is retained even when the decision uses only a count. Robust quantum discrimination is established literature [6]; its uncertain-state model is not the detector model optimized here. The ordinary degradation argument has classical antecedents[2], and its special case is proved above. Our additions are the explicit within-programme robust angle frontier, physical Pauli-channel realization and exact algebraic operating region. No worldwide priority, complete retained-output equivalence, empirical detector validation, Born-selection law or source ontology is asserted. The executable tests compare seventy-two independent ordered ternary-path sums, 1,500 rational geometry inequalities and 180 algebraic comparison/ enclosure cases. These supplement, rather than replace, the analytical minimax proof. The parameter-independent decoder, phase/gain uncertainty, retained nulls and access restrictions are part of every numerical result.
