A universal one-step qutrit dilation bound

The branch-image dimension forces a lower bound on Choi rank. The following elementary proof treats every complex qutrit CP map with the stated linear rank, without a covariance or source-encoding assumption.

A CP map of qutrit linear rank three needs at least three Kraus operators

Universal one-step bath consequence. By (30), every branch of any minimal qutrit realization needs at least three Kraus operators. Its classical-record channel has Choi rank at least 3 + 3 + 3 = 9. This is a universal lower bound for the declared qutrit realization class. The exact normal measurement-model apparatus rule is an established antecedent: Pellonpää and Tukiainen, Rep. Math. Phys. 79, 261–278 (2017), Eq.(22), Corollary 1 and the finite-dimensional apparatus bound, DOI 10.1016/S0034- 4877(17)30040-X, https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.08886. Pure initial apparatus and a sharp pointer are retained hypotheses.
