A continuous family of minimum-Kraus qutrit sources

A coordinate-plane Weyl encoding gives exact stationary qutrit sources with recorded one-step Choi rank nine throughout an explicit noise interval. Its two real seed weights and complex phase can be classified exactly within the minimum-Kraus subfamily. This class is not the class of all qutrit sources. In particular, its fixed full-history cost does not preclude the mixed-support improvement constructed below. The source laws and CP maps are defined explicitly below.
