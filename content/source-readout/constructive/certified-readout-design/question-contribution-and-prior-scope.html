The source model in the

main constructions is classical and finitely described. Its menu can impose incompatibilities, but it is not a simultaneous-value model for incompatible quantum observables. Resource prices, available ports and prediction deadlines are premises. A returned compatible preimage does not identify which source actually occurred. Probabilities require a nominated measure; no Born-selection law follows from counting. The finite model does not identify Boolean gate workspace with reusable physical memory. A source assignment here, a quantum symbol map generating a word law, a single-copy detector outcome, and a retained validation trajectory are different mathematical objects. To use a physical intervention as a menu item one must first specify its admitted preparations, joint protocol, disturbance, accessible records and finite representation; deterministic values for incompatible observables are not supplied by the compiler. The appendices preserve related exact weighted and tensor reductions without making them dependencies of the finite synthesis theorem. Their factorization/context hypotheses and costs are explicit. This separates the unconditional finite algorithms from external asymptotic counting claims.
