Coverage, verification and retained limits

The implementation checks all 4,096 octonion identities, all 16 quotient basis contexts and 40 additional signed contexts. It verifies the two projected rank matrices, explicit integer Bell factors in both directions, exact ranks through three retained bits, 1,306 pattern-unranking cases, 2,145 right-inverse queries, 30 signed rational networks and independent full small-network contractions. Tests and exact parameters are supplied. Appendix D’s sparse checks were examined but not modified into a claimed faster algorithm. A target that annihilates some unwanted assignments can restrict a coverage obligation only after that vanishing is proved and its representation cost charged. A target with full support need not remove any obligation. Signed cancellation of the final answer does not justify dropping assignments from the paper’s nonnegative stopping certificate. No general reduction in check count or load bound is proved. Likewise, the graded operators in Appendix B supply an algebraic factorization; they are not a derived physical interaction, detector, Born selector or consciousness mechanism. This component extension preserves the source–readout distinction between information required for a target and a uniquely identified physical source. It changes no delayed-trine memory or complete-history resource bound.
