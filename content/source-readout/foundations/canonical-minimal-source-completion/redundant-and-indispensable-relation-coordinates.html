Remark 23 (Irredundancy is not uniqueness of coordinates).

A family in which every member is indispensable is irredundant relative to the nomination. This is a statement about the given list, and it confers no uniqueness on the list. Different irredundant families may induce the very same equivalence relation ∼A\sim_A∼A​ and hence, by Proposition 3.4, the very same completion, Proposition 7.3 below exhibits an entire class of such rewritings. An irredundant list of relation coordinates must therefore not be confused with a preferred coordinate system on the completion.

The correct statement of the coordinate situation is a relative one, and we make it precisely. Relative to the fixed nominated family {rα}α∈A\{r_\alpha\}_{\alpha\in A}{rα​}α∈A​, the completion EAE_AEA​ does carry canonical structure maps: the readout projection uA ⁣:EA→Tu_A\colon E_A\to TuA​:EA​→T and the relation coordinates ρα ⁣:EA→Vα\rho_\alpha\colon E_A\to V_\alphaρα​:EA​→Vα​ of (3.3), which are part of the data of the terminal object and are determined by it. What EAE_AEA​ does not possess is a family-independent, intrinsically unique coordinate presentation: a system of coordinates singled out by the quotient alone, without reference to a nomination. Faithful reparameterizations (Proposition 7.3) and distinct irredundant families may present one and the same quotient in different coordinates, and the quotient does not choose among them. The invariant is EAE_AEA​ together with its structure maps for the given nomination; the coordinates are a presentation relative to that nomination.
