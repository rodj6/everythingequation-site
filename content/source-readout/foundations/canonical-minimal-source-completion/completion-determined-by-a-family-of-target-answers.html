Remark 31 (Two different things are being retained).

The distinction encoded in Theorem 8.3 is substantive and is easy to lose. The relation completion EAE_AEA​ retains the nominated relation values themselves. The target completion EQE_{\mathcal Q}EQ​ retains only those distinctions among relation values that affect the nominated answers. If two source states carry different relation signatures but the mediating maps ΦQ\Phi_QΦQ​ send both signatures to the same answers for every Q∈QQ\in\mathcal QQ∈Q, then EAE_AEA​ separates them and EQE_{\mathcal Q}EQ​ does not. Hence the strict-coarsening possibility in (8.6) is not an artifact; Section 11 exhibits it concretely, where the relation completion retains an orientation that no answer in the nominated spectral family can detect.
