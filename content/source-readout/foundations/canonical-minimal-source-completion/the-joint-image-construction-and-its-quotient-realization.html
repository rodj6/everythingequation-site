The completion EAE_AEA​ refines the readout partition, but, this is the whole point of minimality, it need not refine it all the way down to individual source states.

Proposition 3.6 answers exactly the question that “completion” invites one to ask badly. What sits above a readout value ttt in EAE_AEA​ is not a preferred source state in FtF_tFt​, and not an arbitrary enrichment of ttt by extra labels. It is the set of realized relation signatures over ttt: the distinct values that the nominated family actually takes on the fiber. Two source states in FtF_tFt​ that agree on every rαr_\alpharα​ contribute the same completed state; nothing in the construction selects between them, and nothing in the construction can. We record this as a standing prohibition, because it is the failure mode that a naive completion invites.

Remark 10 (No selector, no section, no representative).

The construction of EAE_AEA​ uses no selection function on the fibers of ppp, no section of ppp, and no canonical representative of FtF_tFt​; it requires no choice principle beyond forming the image of a function, and it produces none of these objects either. This should be stated precisely, since the point is about what the construction supplies, not about what is set-theoretically possible.

If one separately chooses a representative of each ∼A\sim_A∼A​-class, a choice that in general requires an appeal to a choice principle, and which is available when such a choice can be made, then the resulting set of chosen representatives is in bijection with EAE_AEA​, by Proposition 3.4. Such a set is a noncanonical presentation of the completion. It carries no invariant information beyond what EAE_AEA​ already carries: the bijection depends on the choice, different choices give different presentations of the same quotient, and nothing in the completion distinguishes one of them. Crucially, no such presentation is supplied or privileged by the universal construction. The object EAE_AEA​ is defined without any choice whatever, as an image; and terminality (Theorem 5.1) yields only the surjections ϕE ⁣:E→EA\phi_{\mathcal E}\colon E\to E_AϕE​:E→EA​ onto the completion. It furnishes no inverse to eAe_AeA​, no canonical splitting, and no canonical representative selector. Whatever a representative-based presentation may be used for, it is extra structure imposed from outside, not a consequence of the theorem.
