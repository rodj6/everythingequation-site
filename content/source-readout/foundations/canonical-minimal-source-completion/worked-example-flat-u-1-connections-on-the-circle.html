Worked example: flat U(1)U(1)U(1) connections on the circle

We now exhibit every phenomenon of the general theory in a single, completely explicit gauge-theoretic example. It is the extreme case of readout loss: the readout is constant, so it distinguishes nothing at all, and the completion must supply everything.

The reduced source and the readout

Consider U(1)U(1)U(1) connections on the trivial bundle over the circle S1S^1S1, restricted to the flat ones. Gauge reduction (2.1) identifies gauge-equivalent connections; on the circle, the resulting reduced moduli space of flat connections is classified by the holonomy around the loop, so that Sflat ≅ U(1),z=eiθ,θ∈R/2πZ.(11.1) S_{\mathrm{flat}}\ \cong\ U(1), \qquad z=e^{i\theta},\quad\theta\in\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z} .\tag{11.1}Sflat​ ≅ U(1),z=eiθ,θ∈R/2πZ.(11.1) This is the standard classification of flat connections on the circle by their holonomy (see, e.g., [5, 7]); we take it as given, and stress that SflatS_{\mathrm{flat}}Sflat​ is already the reduced source in the sense of Section 2: gauge potentials and gauge transformations have been quotiented away, and it is not available to us to reintroduce them.

The readout is the curvature. For flat connections the curvature vanishes identically, so pF ⁣:Sflat⟶T={0},pF(z)=0.(11.2) p_F\colon S_{\mathrm{flat}}\longrightarrow T=\{0\}, \qquad p_F(z)=0 .\tag{11.2}pF​:Sflat​⟶T={0},pF​(z)=0.(11.2) The readout fiber is the whole source: F0=Sflat≅U(1)F_0=S_{\mathrm{flat}}\cong U(1)F0​=Sflat​≅U(1). Note carefully what is not being claimed: we do not claim that curvature is the complete physical observable content of a gauge field. On the contrary, the example exists precisely because it is not, the Aharonov–Bohm effect [6] and the Wu–Yang analysis [7] are the physical statement that holonomy carries information the field strength does not. The readout pFp_FpF​ is a readout, not an ontology.

The full oriented holonomy retains an orientation: it distinguishes transport of charge around the loop in one sense from transport in the other, i.e. zzz from z−1z^{-1}z−1. Each completion is minimal for its own nomination. Which is appropriate depends on which targets one has nominated, and that is a physical decision made before the mathematics begins, not one the mathematics can make.
