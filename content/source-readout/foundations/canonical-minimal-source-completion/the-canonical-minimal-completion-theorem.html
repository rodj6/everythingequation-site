Corollary 5.2 (Uniqueness up to unique isomorphism).

Any two terminal objects of the category Suff(p;{rα})\mathbf{Suff}(p;\{r_\alpha\})Suff(p;{rα​}) are isomorphic by a unique isomorphism, and this isomorphism commutes with the completion maps from SSS and with all of the structure maps. In particular, any relation-sufficient extension that satisfies the universal property of Theorem 5.1 is canonically isomorphic to EA\mathcal E_AEA​.

Remark 18 (The sense in which the completion is a construction).

Theorem 5.1 constructs the coarsest relation-sufficient extension in the paper’s category Suff(p;{rα})\mathbf{Suff}(p;\{r_\alpha\})Suff(p;{rα​}) of set-theoretic source quotients: the object EAE_AEA​ exists, is terminal, and is unique up to unique isomorphism, and those claims are unconditional. What the theorem builds it builds from the nominated relations themselves. The joint map jA=(p,RA)j_A=(p,R_A)jA​=(p,RA​) takes the rαr_\alpharα​ as given input, so the construction presupposes the relation data rather than producing it; in particular it furnishes no procedure for recovering the rαr_\alpharα​ from the readout ppp when they fail to descend, if such a procedure existed the relations would already be functions of ppp, and the completion would be TTT itself. Nor does terminality establish that EAE_AEA​ is experimentally accessible, measurable, smooth, geometric, or realized as a physical state space independent of its defining coordinates; EAE_AEA​ is, as constructed, a quotient of SSS recording exactly the nominated values. A concrete realization must independently supply and justify the relation data, and any additional geometric or physical structure carried by EAE_AEA​, on its own terms. That realization question is not settled here; it is deferred to the companion manuscript on geometric realization discussed in Section 13.

Remark 19 (What “minimal” means, and what it does not).

Minimality here means: least retained distinction among sufficient extensions. It is a statement about the ordering of equivalence relations by inclusion, equivalently about the ordering of partitions by refinement. It does not mean minimum cardinality selected without regard to structure, the one-point set is smaller than EAE_AEA​ and is not, in general, sufficient, and it does not mean that EAE_AEA​ is a small or economical description of SSS in any other sense. Above all, minimal completion is not reconstruction of SSS: by Proposition 3.5 the map eAe_AeA​ need not be injective, and Section 12 determines exactly when it is.
