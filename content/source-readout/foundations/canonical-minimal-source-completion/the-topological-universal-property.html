Quotient topology versus subspace topology

EAE_AEA​ carries two a priori distinct topologies: the quotient topology of Definition 10.1, and the subspace topology inherited from T×VAT\times V_AT×VA​ via the canonical injection ι ⁣:EA↪T×VA.(10.1) \iota\colon E_A\hookrightarrow T\times V_A .\tag{10.1}ι:EA​↪T×VA​.(10.1) These must not be conflated. The universal property of Theorem 10.3 is a statement about the quotient topology, and it is the quotient topology that the completion carries by definition.

Remark 38.

Without hypotheses of this kind the two topologies may genuinely differ, and the abstract completion does not require them to agree: Theorem 10.3 holds for the quotient topology regardless. One should not assert the agreement, nor treat EAE_AEA​ as a subspace of T×VAT\times V_AT×VA​ in the topological sense, without verifying Proposition 10.4  or a sufficient condition such as Corollary 10.5.
