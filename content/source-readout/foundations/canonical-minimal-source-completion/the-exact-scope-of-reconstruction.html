The exact scope of reconstruction

Completion always reconstructs the joint readout/relation signature; that is what it is. Whether it reconstructs the source state is a separate question with a sharp answer.

Remark 42 (Two senses of “reconstruction”; only one is automatic).

Distinguish carefully:

  1. Reconstruction of the joint signature. The completed state determines the readout value and every nominated relation value: this is (3.4), and it holds always, by construction. It is the entire purpose of the object.

  2. Reconstruction of the reduced source state. The completed state determines s∈Ss\in Ss∈S itself. This holds if and only if the nominated family separates points, by Proposition 12.1, and in general it fails, necessarily so, since by Corollary 5.3 EAE_AEA​ is the coarsest sufficient extension and therefore retains nothing the nomination does not force it to retain.

Neither sense licenses a third: recovery of raw configuration data in Ω\OmegaΩ, or selection of a preferred representative of a physical equivalence class or of a readout fiber. Corollary 9.1 forbids the former and Remark 3.7 the latter. The word “reconstruction” should not be used in this subject without saying which of (1) and (2) is meant.
