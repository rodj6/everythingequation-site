Nested families, refinement maps, and reparameterization

Nested families

Let A⊆BA\subseteq BA⊆B be sets of indices, the AAA-relations being a subfamily of the BBB-relations (i.e. the relations indexed by AAA are the same functions in both families). Then, directly from (3.5), ∼B ⊆ ∼A,(7.1) \sim_B\ \subseteq\ \sim_A ,\tag{7.1}∼B​ ⊆ ∼A​,(7.1) since agreement of all BBB-relations (and the readout) entails agreement of the sub-collection indexed by AAA (and the readout). In words: a larger nomination is at least as demanding, hence yields an at least as fine completion.

Remark 25 (Strictness is not automatic).

The completion for a larger family is at least as fine as that for a smaller one, and may be strictly finer, but need not be. If every relation indexed by B∖AB\setminus AB∖A is redundant relative to ppp and the AAA-relations, then ∼B = ∼A\sim_B\ =\ \sim_A∼B​ = ∼A​ by the argument of Corollary 6.3, and πBA\pi_{BA}πBA​ is a bijection. Adding relations to the nomination adds information to the completion precisely to the extent that the added relations separate pairs that the existing nomination identifies.

Faithful reparameterization

The completion depends on the distinctions carried by the nominated relations, not on the labels in which their values happen to be recorded. We make this precise.

Remark 27 (The precise form of the invariance).

The value spaces VαV_\alphaVα​ and WαW_\alphaWα​ are in general different sets, so EA⊆T×VAE_A\subseteq T\times V_AEA​⊆T×VA​ and EA′⊆T×WAE'_A\subseteq T\times W_AEA′​⊆T×WA​ are subsets of different products. Proposition 7.3 therefore asserts an isomorphism of the induced quotients over TTT, equivalently, of the two objects as completions of the readout, and not an identity, nor an isomorphism internal to a single fixed category of relation signatures valued in one fixed product. The relation-signature coordinates are a presentation; the quotient is the invariant. Faithful relabelling of the presentation does not move the invariant.
