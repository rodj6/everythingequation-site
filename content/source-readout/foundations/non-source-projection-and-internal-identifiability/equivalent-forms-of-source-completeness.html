Equivalent forms of source completeness

We now fix the vocabulary that the literature routinely conflates.

Definition 3.1 (Three completeness notions).

Let p ⁣:S→T1p\colon S\to T_1p:S→T1​ be an exact readout.

  1. ppp is kinematically source-complete if ppp is injective (hence bijective, by Lemma 2.3).

  2. ppp is complete relative to a family R\mathcal RR if every rαr_\alpharα​ descends, i.e. there is rˉα ⁣:T1→Vα\bar r_\alpha\colon T_1\to V_\alpharˉα​:T1​→Vα​ with rα=rˉα∘pr_\alpha=\bar r_\alpha\circ prα​=rˉα​∘p. Equivalently (Lemma 3.2), each rαr_\alpharα​ is constant on ppp-fibers. Relative to a target QQQ, completeness means aQa_QaQ​ descends.

  3. ppp is dynamically sufficient (or closed) for a source evolution Φt\Phi_tΦt​ if Φt\Phi_tΦt​ preserves ppp-fibers, so that an autonomous law exists on T1T_1T1​ (§5).

These are genuinely distinct, and it is worth saying why before proving anything, since the entire architecture would collapse into triviality if they were not. (1) implies (2) and (3) trivially. Neither converse holds: Example 5.5 exhibits a dynamically sufficient, hence perfectly autonomous, readout that is kinematically incomplete; Example 4.13 exhibits a readout complete relative to one relation family and incomplete relative to a larger one. Relative completeness is always completeness with respect to a nominated family, and this relativity is not a weakness of the framework but its content: it is what allows the question “does the lost distinction matter?” to have a nontrivial answer.

Let p ⁣:S→T1p\colon S\to T_1p:S→T1​ be surjective and let r ⁣:S→Vr\colon S\to Vr:S→V be any map. The following are equivalent:

Note that surjectivity of ppp is used twice: once for existence of rˉ\bar rrˉ on all of T1T_1T1​, once for uniqueness. This is the first of the two hypotheses whose location the following theorem is designed to expose.

Assumption 3.3 (Separating family).

Throughout this section, R={rα}α∈A\mathcal R=\{r_\alpha\}_{\alpha\in A}R={rα​}α∈A​ separates the points of SSS: s≠s′⟹rα(s)≠rα(s′) for some α∈A.(9) s\neq s'\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r_\alpha(s)\neq r_\alpha(s')\ \text{for some }\alpha\in A.\tag{9}s=s′⟹rα​(s)=rα​(s′) for some α∈A.(9) Equivalently, RAR_ARA​ is injective.

Assumption 3.3 is what prevents the next theorem from being a tautology. Without it, “every rαr_\alpharα​ descends” is a statement about a possibly impoverished family and carries no information about ppp: the empty family descends vacuously through any readout whatsoever. With it, the family is rich enough to detect any source distinction, and descent of all of it becomes equivalent to there being no source distinction to detect. It is satisfiable: R={idS}\mathcal R=\{\mathop{\mathrm{id}}_S\}R={idS​} separates trivially, and in the geometric setting (9) is precisely the (nontrivial, and sometimes false) assertion that the nominated invariants classify the orbit space.

Let p ⁣:S→T1p\colon S\to T_1p:S→T1​ be an exact (hence surjective) readout and let R\mathcal RR satisfy Assumption 3.3. Then the following are equivalent.

  1. ppp is injective.

  2. Every fiber p−1(t)p^{-1}(t)p−1(t), t∈T1t\in T_1t∈T1​, is a singleton.

  3. Every rα∈Rr_\alpha\in\mathcal Rrα​∈R descends to T1T_1T1​.

  4. The completion projection uA ⁣:EA→T1u_A\colon E_A\to T_1uA​:EA​→T1​ of (6) is bijective.

  5. ppp has a two-sided inverse: there is p−1 ⁣:T1→Sp^{-1}\colon T_1\to Sp−1:T1​→S with p−1∘p=idSp^{-1}\circ p=\mathop{\mathrm{id}}_Sp−1∘p=idS​ and p∘p−1=idT1p\circ p^{-1}=\mathop{\mathrm{id}}_{T_1}p∘p−1=idT1​​.

Remark 15 (Where the hypotheses enter).

Surjectivity of ppp is used in (C1)⇔\Leftrightarrow⇔(C2) (nonempty fibers), in (C1)⇔\Leftrightarrow⇔(C5) (two-sidedness), and inside Lemma 3.2 (existence and uniqueness of rˉ\bar rrˉ). Assumption 3.3 is used only in (C3)⇒\Rightarrow⇒(C1) , and it is indispensable there. Drop it and the implication fails at once: take S={s0,s1}S=\{s_0,s_1\}S={s0​,s1​}, T1={t}T_1=\{t\}T1​={t}, R=∅\mathcal R=\varnothingR=∅ (or any family of constant maps). Every relation descends vacuously, EA≅T1E_A\cong T_1EA​≅T1​ so uAu_AuA​ is bijective, yet ppp is not injective. So (C3) and (C4) are strictly weaker than (C1) in general, and the theorem’s content is that the separating hypothesis closes exactly this gap.

Corollary 3.6 (Nontrivial completion criterion).

Under Assumption 3.3, EAE_AEA​ properly refines T1T_1T1​ , i.e. uAu_AuA​ is not injective , if and only if ppp is not kinematically source-complete.

Proof. Immediate from (C1)⇔\Leftrightarrow⇔(C4) of Theorem 3.4, since uAu_AuA​ is always surjective, so failure of bijectivity is failure of injectivity. ◻

Theorem 3.4 thus supplies five interchangeable diagnostic sites for source incompleteness: noninjectivity, nontrivial fibers, failure of relation descent, nontrivial completion, and absence of an inverse. A physical model may find it convenient to test any one of them.
