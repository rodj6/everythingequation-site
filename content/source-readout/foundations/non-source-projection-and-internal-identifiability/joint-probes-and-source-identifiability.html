Remark 40 (Direction of (26)). The map goes from the finer quotient to the coarser one. More probes means a finer partition, hence a larger quotient set, which surjects onto the smaller. There is in general no map back: information present in S/ ⁣∼JS/\!\sim_JS/∼J​ cannot be manufactured from S/ ⁣∼IS/\!\sim_IS/∼I​.

Thus tomography is the intersection of probe fibers: one adds probes, and the joint fiber {s′:s′∼Is}\{s':s'\sim_I s\}{s′:s′∼I​s} shrinks. Source recovery occurs precisely when that intersection reaches the diagonal. But we must be exact about what is and is not being claimed. Theorem 6.2 says when a family separates; it does not say that any particular physically available family does. Whether the probes an observer can actually run separate the source is a contingent fact about the model and the observer, not a theorem. The next section studies the case where, by hypothesis, they do not.
