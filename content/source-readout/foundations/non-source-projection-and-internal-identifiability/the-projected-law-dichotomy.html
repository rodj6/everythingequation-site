Corollary 5.3 (Coherence does not imply source completeness).

Case I is compatible with ppp noninjective. Autonomous, deterministic, internally coherent readout laws establish that the dynamics preserves the readout equivalence classes; they do not establish that those classes are singletons.

Proof. It suffices to exhibit an instance, which Example 5.5 does. Formally: (17) constrains Φt\Phi_tΦt​ to map ∼p\sim_p∼p​-classes into ∼p\sim_p∼p​-classes; it places no constraint whatsoever on the cardinality of those classes. ◻

Corollary 5.3 is the single most important negative result of this paper, and the one most easily lost. An observer inside T1T_1T1​ in Case I sees a world with exact deterministic laws, perfectly closed, admitting no anomaly and no residue. That observer’s evidence is entirely consistent with T1T_1T1​ being the whole of physical reality, and entirely consistent with T1T_1T1​ being a proper quotient of a strictly larger source. Dynamical closure is evidence of fiber preservation, not of fiber triviality.

What failed descent actually requires

In Case II the instantaneous readout state is insufficient, and one must say for what. The three targets below are routinely conflated; they are not the same, and only the first is an exact reconstruction.

  1. Exact trajectory prediction. To recover t↦p(Φt(s))t\mapsto p(\Phi_t(s))t↦p(Φt​(s)) for the individual realized sss, one needs strictly more than p(s)p(s)p(s): either additional state information (a completion variable, i.e. a refinement e ⁣:S→Ee\colon S\to Ee:S→E sufficient for the target “future readout”, in the sense of Definition 4.3), or an equivalent history-dependent description that encodes the same information in the past of the trajectory. Which of the two one uses is a matter of representation; the information content required is fixed by the fiber structure.

  2. Statistical prediction. One may instead supply a probability distribution over the unresolved states within the fiber p−1(p(s))p^{-1}(p(s))p−1(p(s)). This yields a distribution over future readouts. But note carefully: this is a prediction only after an additional probabilistic model is supplied , namely the within-fiber distribution, which is not determined by the deterministic data (S,p,Φt)(S,p,\Phi_t)(S,p,Φt​). It is an addition to the model, not a consequence of it. And what it delivers is a law over futures, not an exact reconstruction of the individual source trajectory.

  3. Memory representation. One may retain a functional of the past readout. This is exact only when the past readout in fact determines the relevant fiber coordinate; in general it does not, and then memory too is only a partial substitute.

We do not assert that any of these is always available. Case II asserts a negative , that the instantaneous readout state does not suffice , and nothing more.

Four points must be made about (24), because it is the most frequently over-read equation in this circle of ideas.

First: PΨP\PsiPΨ is a source-side coordinate, not the declared readout. The projection formalism evolves PΨP\PsiPΨ, the component of the source state in (ker⁡K)⊥(\ker K)^{\perp}(kerK)⊥. The declared readout of our architecture, by contrast, is KΨK\PsiKΨ. These are different objects living in different spaces, and the identification of the two requires an argument. Under the closed-range hypothesis it is available: the restriction K∣(ker⁡K)⊥ ⁣:(ker⁡K)⊥⟶RanKK|_{(\ker K)^{\perp}}\colon (\ker K)^{\perp}\longrightarrow \mathop{\mathrm{Ran}}KK∣(kerK)⊥​:(kerK)⊥⟶RanK is a bounded linear bijection between Banach spaces (injective because (ker⁡K)⊥∩ker⁡K={0}(\ker K)^{\perp}\cap\ker K=\{0\}(kerK)⊥∩kerK={0}; surjective onto RanK\mathop{\mathrm{Ran}}KRanK by the orthogonal decomposition Ψ=PΨ+Q ⁣⊥Ψ\Psi=P\Psi+Q^{\!\perp}\PsiΨ=PΨ+Q⊥Ψ and KΨ=KPΨK\Psi=KP\PsiKΨ=KPΨ), and RanK\mathop{\mathrm{Ran}}KRanK is closed, hence complete; so the bounded inverse theorem supplies a bounded inverse. Without closed range this stability fails , the inverse need not be bounded , and although the set-theoretic quotient (4) still holds, one may no longer freely trade PΨP\PsiPΨ for KΨK\PsiKΨ in any topological or approximation-theoretic claim.

Second: (24) is exact and deterministic. No stochasticity, no coarse-graining approximation, and no thermodynamic limit has been invoked. The second and third terms are not noise; they are definite functionals of the initial unresolved data Q ⁣⊥Ψ(0)Q^{\!\perp}\Psi(0)Q⊥Ψ(0) and of the resolved history {PΨ(τ)}τ≤t\{P\Psi(\tau)\}_{\tau\le t}{PΨ(τ)}τ≤t​ respectively.

Fourth: what the unresolved-initial-state term signifies. It depends on Q ⁣⊥Ψ(0)Q^{\!\perp}\Psi(0)Q⊥Ψ(0), which is exactly the fiber coordinate , the information the readout discards. Calling the term “noise” is an additional modelling decision (cf. §5.2(2)), not a theorem; and so, equally, is any replacement of it by a memory kernel.

This is a special feature of this example , yyy happens to be both conserved and immediately visible in the readout increment , and not a general remedy; in general the past readout need not determine the fiber coordinate at all.
