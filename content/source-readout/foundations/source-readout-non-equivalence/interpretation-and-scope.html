Interpretation and scope

The research program under which the present results are developed , Shadow Theory , posits a reduced source-realization domain SSS and a readout domain T1T_{1}T1​, related by a physically invariant readout p ⁣:S⟶T1,(44) p\colon S\longrightarrow T_{1},\tag{44}p:S⟶T1​,(44) in exactly the sense of Section 2. The mathematics of the preceding sections is stated in standard language and requires no commitment to the program; what the program supplies is a candidate instantiation of the hypotheses.

Within that setting, Theorem 10.1 yields the following statements, each conditional on the hypothesis it invokes. If a realization-relevant relation varies within a T1T_{1}T1​-fiber, that relation is not a function of the T1T_{1}T1​-state, and no construction taking only T1T_{1}T1​-data as input can evaluate it. If, in addition, a fiber carries a readout-invisible symmetry with no invariant representative, then no symmetry-compatible deterministic reconstruction selects the realized source state from T1T_{1}T1​-data. Both conclusions coexist with the exactness of the quotient presentation, so that the structure is T1  ≅  S/ ⁣∼pwhileT1  ̸≃source  S,(45) T_{1}\;\cong\;S/\!\sim_{p} \qquad\text{while}\qquad T_{1}\;\not\simeq_{\mathrm{source}}\;S,\tag{45}T1​≅S/∼p​whileT1​≃source​S,(45) the second relation holding relative to the lost relation or the reconstruction class established by the theorem. The qualification is not a hedge; it is the content. Non-equivalence is always non-equivalence with respect to something identified, and (45) means nothing more and nothing less than that.

The picture that results is coherent but modest, and its modesty should not be trimmed away. Proposition 3.1 establishes that T1T_{1}T1​ is an exact presentation of S/ ⁣∼pS/\!\sim_{p}S/∼p​: as a description of ∼p\sim_{p}∼p​-classes it is neither approximate nor defective. It does not establish that the T1T_{1}T1​-level description is lawful, or internally consistent as a dynamical theory, or dynamically closed. A T1T_{1}T1​-level description may be all of these while nevertheless being a readout of a richer realization carrier , there is no tension whatever between the two, and demonstrating exactness does not argue against a richer source , but establishing lawfulness or closure at the readout level requires descent results for the specific laws in question, and those are not proved here. This paper neither asserts nor denies that projected dynamics descends; it fixes the framework within which that question can be posed.

Establishing that a particular physical theory has this structure is a separate task from proving the theorems above, and it requires that the theory independently specify all of the following:

  1. the reduced source domain SSS, and the realization space Ω\OmegaΩ from which it is obtained;

  2. the physical-equivalence relation ≃phys\simeq_{\mathrm{phys}}≃phys​, that is, an explicit declaration of which transformations are redundant;

  3. the readout map, and hence ppp and the fibers FtF_{t}Ft​;

  4. which distinctions within a fiber are physically meaningful rather than gauge , the declaration whose reversal, in Remark 7.4, dissolved the obstruction entirely;

  5. either a non-descending physically meaningful relation rrr, or a reconstruction class (equivariant, continuous, smooth, measurable, local, covariant) together with an applicable obstruction in that class.

Absent these, Theorem 10.1 is a theorem with no instance. Supplied with them, it is a theorem with a specific and bounded conclusion. In neither case does it license the inference that reality is a projection, or that some particular observed physics has been shown to be one; those are claims about instantiation, and instantiation is not proved by abstraction.
