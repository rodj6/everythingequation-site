Abstract

A surjective, noninjective readout map p ⁣:S→Tp\colon S\to Tp:S→T from a reduced source space necessarily discards distinctions among reduced source states. It does not follow that the discarded distinctions matter. Whether they matter is a property of the question being asked, not of the readout alone. This paper makes that dependence precise. A target is specified by a correct-answer map aQ ⁣:S→AQa_Q\colon S\to A_QaQ​:S→AQ​, and we prove that QQQ admits an exact readout-only solution if and only if aQa_QaQ​ is constant on every fiber of ppp; consequently a lost source relation obstructs QQQ precisely when it is active for QQQ, i.e. when the realized answer varies across some readout fiber. We then characterize what any repair must contain.
