Remark 11.5 (What is and is not claimed about cos⁡θ\cos\thetacosθ).

Theorem 11.4 says that cos⁡θ\cos\thetacosθ coordinatizes the coarsest completion sufficient for QspecQ_{\mathrm{spec}}Qspec​: its level sets are exactly the spectral equivalence classes, and it separates exactly what must be separated. It does not say that cos⁡θ\cos\thetacosθ is the unique possible numerical coordinate on EQspecE_{Q_{\mathrm{spec}}}EQspec​​. Any injective function of cos⁡θ\cos\thetacosθ , for instance 12(1−cos⁡θ)\tfrac12(1-\cos\theta)21​(1−cosθ), or α(θ)\alpha(\theta)α(θ) itself, or the ground-state energy λmin⁡=α(θ)2/L2\lambda_{\min}=\alpha(\theta)^2/L^2λmin​=α(θ)2/L2 , has the same level sets and coordinatizes the same quotient equally well; the completion is the quotient, and the coordinate is a labeling of it. Nor does Theorem 11.4 say that cos⁡θ\cos\thetacosθ is minimal for every physical target on SflatS_{\mathrm{flat}}Sflat​. It is minimal for the nominated spectral target, and for that target only.
