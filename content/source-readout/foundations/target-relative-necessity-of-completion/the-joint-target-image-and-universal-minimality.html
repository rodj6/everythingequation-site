The joint target image and universal minimality

Theorem 7.1 gives a lower bound on the distinctions a completion must carry. We now show that the bound is attained within the category of readout extensions defined above: there is a completion carrying exactly those distinctions and no others, and it receives a canonical map from every other completion. Attainment here is a statement about the abstract construction EQ=Im⁡(p,aQ)E_Q=\operatorname{Im}(p,a_Q)EQ​=Im(p,aQ​); whether a given physical situation supplies an operational realization of that completion is a separate matter, taken up after Theorem 8.3.

Several points about Theorem 8.3 and Corollary 8.4 require care, and we state them explicitly.

The direction of the universal map. The canonical map runs E⟶EQ,E\longrightarrow E_Q,E⟶EQ​, from an arbitrary sufficient extension to the joint target image , not from EQE_QEQ​ to EEE. This direction is forced and is not a convention. A surjection E→EQE\to E_QE→EQ​ expresses that EEE is at least as fine as EQE_QEQ​, since it says the EEE-value determines the EQE_QEQ​-value. There is in general no map EQ→EE_Q\to EEQ​→E over SSS: EEE may retain distinctions that EQE_QEQ​ has discarded , distinctions irrelevant to QQQ but possibly relevant to other targets , and no map out of EQE_QEQ​ could recover them. Reversing the arrow would assert the opposite of what is true, namely that EQE_QEQ​ is the finest rather than the coarsest sufficient extension.

EQE_QEQ​ does not reconstruct the source. The joint target image retains exactly two things: the readout value p(s)p(s)p(s), and the correct answer aQ(s)a_Q(s)aQ​(s). It discards everything else, and in particular it identifies any two source states that agree on both. This is usually a massive collapse. In Section 11 the target is the spectrum of a charged Laplacian, and EQE_QEQ​ turns out to be a one-dimensional interval’s worth of data , the inversion quotient of holonomy , even though the source moduli space U(1)\mathrm{U}(1)U(1) is strictly finer and the raw connections finer still. Nothing in Theorem 8.3 says or implies that the source is recoverable; on the contrary, Corollary 8.4 says the source is generally not recoverable from EQE_QEQ​, because id⁡S\operatorname{id}_SidS​ is one of the sufficient extensions eee and the canonical map goes S→EQS\to E_QS→EQ​, not back.

One target at a time. Theorem 8.3 is a statement about a single correct-answer map aQa_QaQ​. If one nominates a family of targets, the coarsest extension sufficient for all of them simultaneously must, by Theorem 7.1 applied to each member, separate every pair of source states on which some member’s answer differs. It is therefore at least as fine as the completion required by each individual member, and it may be strictly finer. It need not be: should the remaining members contribute no distinctions beyond those already forced by one of them, the family completion coincides with that member’s individual completion. Which case obtains depends on the family, and the construction in general requires machinery we do not develop here; that is the subject of [9]. Within the present paper, “minimal” always means “minimal for the nominated QQQ,” and we will see in Section 11 that the minimal completion for the spectral target is strictly insufficient for a transport target on the same source space.

What the universal property does and does not supply. Theorem 8.3 establishes that EQE_QEQ​ is the coarsest sufficient extension in the category of set-theoretic source quotients and readout extensions defined in this paper: among all QQQ-sufficient extensions, it is the one carrying the fewest distinctions, and every other maps canonically onto it. Because the construction of EQ=Im⁡(p,aQ)E_Q=\operatorname{Im}(p,a_Q)EQ​=Im(p,aQ​) refers explicitly to the correct-answer map aQa_QaQ​, the theorem is an information-theoretic characterization of the least distinctions any sufficient extension must carry; it identifies the floor abstractly, from the data (p,aQ)(p,a_Q)(p,aQ​). It does not, however, furnish an operational procedure for extracting aQa_QaQ​ from the original readout ppp , indeed Corollary 3.2, Proposition 3.4 show that no such extraction from ppp alone exists when QQQ is obstructed , and it does not by itself exhibit a physically measurable, experimentally accessible, or independently specified realization of EQE_QEQ​. A concrete physical repair is a separate task: it must independently provide an extension e ⁣:S→Ee\colon S\to Ee:S→E and an answer map b ⁣:E→AQb\colon E\to A_Qb:E→AQ​ through which aQa_QaQ​ factors, and must justify that eee and bbb are available in the situation at hand. The universal property then locates any such repair relative to the floor , it certifies that eee carries at least the distinctions of EQE_QEQ​ , but the provision and justification of eee and bbb lie outside what the abstract quotient construction delivers. The flat-connection example of Section 9, Section 11 supplies exactly such an independently specified extension, the charged spectrum, and it is that concrete construction, not Theorem 8.3 alone, that makes the repair physical.
