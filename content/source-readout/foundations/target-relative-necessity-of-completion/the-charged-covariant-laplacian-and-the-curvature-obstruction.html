Remark 10.6 (Physical reading).

The physical content of Theorem 10.4 is the Aharonov–Bohm phenomenon in spectral form [1, 11]. Along the circle the field strength vanishes , identically, and for dimensional reasons , so there is no local electromagnetic field anywhere on the particle’s accessible configuration space for it to feel. Nevertheless the charged particle’s energy levels depend on θ\thetaθ: the covariant derivative (36) twists the periodicity condition felt by the wavefunction, shifting the Fourier momenta from 2πn/L2\pi n/L2πn/L to (2πn−θ)/L(2\pi n-\theta)/L(2πn−θ)/L. At θ=π\theta=\piθ=π the shift is maximal, the momentum lattice is displaced by half a spacing, no mode has zero covariant momentum, and the ground-state energy is lifted from 000 to π2/L2\pi^2/L^2π2/L2. The obstruction is not an artifact of the formalism; it is a measurable spectral shift produced by information that curvature does not record.
