Effective metric response

We finally allow the metric to participate, and ask what elimination of χ\chiχ does to the gravitational field equations. The answer is a stationary-elimination (envelope) identity, which converts the eliminated sector into a covariant variational response rather than an unexplained additive term.

The logical structure is therefore this. Separate diffeomorphism invariance supplies separate off-shell identities, (10.14) and (10.15), and nothing more. Separate conservation of a sector’s stress tensor requires, in addition, that that sector’s Ward term vanish on the combined solution, i.e. that Wν(δSvisible/δφ)=0\mathcal{W}_{\nu}(\delta S_{\mathrm{visible}}/\delta\varphi)=0Wν​(δSvisible​/δφ)=0 there, equivalently (by (10.16) and linearity) that Wν(δΓcorr/δφ)=0\mathcal{W}_{\nu}(\delta\Gamma_{\mathrm{corr}}/\delta\varphi)=0Wν​(δΓcorr​/δφ)=0 there. This holds, for instance, when Γcorr\Gamma_{\mathrm{corr}}Γcorr​ does not depend on φ\varphiφ at all, or when the visible field happens to satisfy its own equation δSvisible/δφ=0\delta S_{\mathrm{visible}}/\delta\varphi=0δSvisible​/δφ=0 on the combined solution, or when Wν\mathcal{W}_{\nu}Wν​ annihilates the relevant Euler operator. It is not supplied by invariance: diffeomorphism invariance of a sector gives that sector its Ward identity, but it does not put the sector on shell, and it therefore cannot by itself force the exchange current to vanish. Absent such a structure, the eliminated sector exchanges energy–momentum with the visible one, and only the sum is conserved. ◻

Remark 49 (Invariance versus covariance).

Three statements must be kept apart, and they are frequently run together.

  1.
