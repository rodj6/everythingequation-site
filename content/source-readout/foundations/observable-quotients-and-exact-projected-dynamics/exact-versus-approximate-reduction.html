Exact versus approximate reduction

It is worth collecting, in one place, what has been proved exactly and where an approximation would have to enter.

Exact. Theorem 2.3 (the quotient and the closed-range criterion), Proposition 3.2 and Corollary 3.3 (functional descent), Theorem 3.4 (stable observability of a linear functional, i.e. boundedness of the descended functional in the inherited norm iff a∈Ran⁡K∗a\in\operatorname{Ran}K^{*}a∈RanK∗), Theorem 4.4 (the truncation error, which is an exact evaluation of an approximation’s cost), Theorem 5.2 (descent iff invariance), Theorem 6.2 (the projected identity), Theorem 6.4 (closure iff PLQ=0PLQ=0PLQ=0), Theorems 7.4 and 7.6 (minimal completion and Volterra closure), Proposition 7.7 (finite computation of NQN_{Q}NQ​), Theorem 8.2 (nonlinear projectability), Theorem 9.2 (Schur complement), Proposition 10.2 and Theorem 10.5 (envelope identity and total conservation). Apart from the explicitly identified rank-rrr approximant in the Eckart–Young–Mirsky result, these identities introduce no approximation, limiting procedure, ensemble, or small parameter; the theorem itself evaluates the truncation error exactly.

The physical reading. What Sections 5–7 establish is this. A model may have a completely coherent observable theory, an honest Hilbert space of observable states, an honest algebra of observable functionals, an honest exact quotient, and still fail to be autonomous on that observable state. When it does fail, the exact projected law records precisely three things beyond the naive autonomous term PLP xPLP\,xPLPx: dependence on unresolved initial data (the term ηz0\eta_{z_{0}}ηz0​​), history dependence through the memory kernel K\mathcal{K}K, and, if one insists on restoring a genuine initial-value problem, the additional state information EdynE_{\mathrm{dyn}}Edyn​ needed to close it. Eliminating a field sector instead of a state sector produces the same structure in field-theoretic guise: an effective operator A−BC−1B∗A-BC^{-1}B^{*}A−BC−1B∗, whose correction may be nonlocal when the composed operator BC−1B∗BC^{-1}B^{*}BC−1B∗ retains the nonlocal Green-operator directions, and a covariant effective stress-energy TeffT^{\mathrm{eff}}Teff defined by variation.

None of this is evidence that our universe has hidden source variables, memory, or any particular effective correction. The theorems are conditional: if a model declares a source/readout architecture, then these are the necessary and sufficient conditions for descent, and these are the exact reduction identities when descent fails. A memory kernel is not a physical discovery; it is a formula. It becomes physics only when the model that produced LLL and PPP is itself physics.
