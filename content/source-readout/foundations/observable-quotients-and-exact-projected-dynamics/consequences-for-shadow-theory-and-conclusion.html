The observable architecture is now precise: Hsrc → K  Ran⁡K ≅ Hsrc/ker⁡K,(12.1) \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{src}}\ \xrightarrow{\ K\ }\ \operatorname{Ran}K\ \cong\ \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{src}}/\ker K ,\tag{12.1}Hsrc​  K ​ RanK ≅ Hsrc​/kerK,(12.1) the isomorphism being that of Theorem 2.3, and topologically faithful in the inherited norm exactly when Ran⁡K\operatorname{Ran}KRanK is closed (Remark 2.4). Static source quantities descend exactly when they are constant on cosets of ker⁡K\ker KkerK (Proposition 3.2). Source dynamics descend exactly when the source semigroup preserves ker⁡K\ker KkerK (Theorem 5.2). When it does not, exact projection produces dependence on unresolved initial data and a memory kernel (Theorem 6.2), and autonomous closure for all source states is precisely the invariance condition (Theorem 6.4). The minimal dynamical completion retains exactly those unresolved distinctions that generate different observable response functions (Theorem 7.4), and no more (Remark 7.5); in finite dimensions it is computed by a rank condition (Proposition 7.7). The nonlinear counterpart is fiberwise constancy of the projected velocity (Theorem 8.2). Eliminating a source field sector produces Schur-complement effective operators (Theorem 9.2) and, in the presence of gravity, a covariant effective stress-energy that is a genuine variational response with total covariant conservation (Definition 10.3, Theorem 10.5).

This establishes how a coherent observable field theory can be an exact quotient theory without being an autonomous presentation of every source degree of freedom, and it supplies the exact price of that fact, in three separable currencies: initial-state dependence, memory, and additional retained state.

Observable reduction therefore has three mathematically distinct static forms: exact identification by a kernel (D1), unstable inversion along small singular directions or a nonclosed range (D2), and controlled approximation by singular-value truncation (D3). Projected dynamics adds a fourth, which is independent of (D2) and (D3) and, though implied by neither, strictly stronger than (D1) rather than independent of it: a static quotient with a nontrivial kernel may fail to support autonomous evolution because its equivalence classes are not preserved by the source dynamics (D4). A nontrivial kernel is necessary for this failure, by (1.1), and never sufficient, whether the kernel is invariant is a separate, dynamical fact, decided by Theorem 5.2 and not by any property of KKK alone. The exact projection identity, the minimal response completion, the Schur-complement reduction, and the variational effective stress-energy are the field-theoretic machinery connecting source structure to observable laws. They are supplied here as mathematics, with their hypotheses stated and their limits marked, as the dynamical layer of the sequence [22, 23, 24, 25].
