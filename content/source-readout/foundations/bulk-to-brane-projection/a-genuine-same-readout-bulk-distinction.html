Remark 3.3 (Scope).

Corollary 3.2 is conditional on the source domain admitting the normalizable bulk radiation used in the construction. If the model is defined to have a unique stationary vacuum bulk state with all homogeneous incoming data excluded, the witness is outside the domain and the fiber must be reassessed for that restricted domain; the forced retarded KK response and the effective-action results of Sections 6–11 survive unchanged. See Section 15, item 1.

The construction also identifies what must be added. At linear order the required completion is the bulk gravitational initial data modulo the subspace whose retarded brane response vanishes identically; in a KK decomposition, the coupled continuum amplitudes modulo zero-response modes. In the nonlinear theory it is the bulk initial/boundary data or, after elimination, the induced nonlocal brane history functional. Adding the instantaneous value of EμνE_{\mu\nu}Eμν​ alone does not in general close its future evolution , as Section 4.3 makes precise.
