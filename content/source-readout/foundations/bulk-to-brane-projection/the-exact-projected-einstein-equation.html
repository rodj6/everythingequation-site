Conservation, Bianchi, and nonclosure

With no exchange of matter energy into the bulk, the Codazzi equation Dμ(Kμν−Kqμν)=(5)RABnAeBν=23Λ5 nAeAν=0D^\mu\bigl(K_{\mu\nu}-Kq_{\mu\nu}\bigr) ={}^{(5)}R_{AB}n^Ae^B{}_\nu=\tfrac23\Lambda_5\,n_Ae^A{}_\nu=0Dμ(Kμν​−Kqμν​)=(5)RAB​nAeBν​=32​Λ5​nA​eAν​=0 combined with (24) gives ∇μTμν=0.(41) \nabla^\mu T_{\mu\nu}=0 .\tag{41}∇μTμν​=0.(41) Applying the four-dimensional contracted Bianchi identity ∇μGμν=0\nabla^\mu G_{\mu\nu}=0∇μGμν​=0 to (38) then yields   ∇μEμν=κ54 ∇μΠμν.  (42) \boxed{\; \nabla^\mu E_{\mu\nu} =\kappa_5^4\,\nabla^\mu\Pi_{\mu\nu}. \;}\tag{42}∇μEμν​=κ54​∇μΠμν​.​(42)

This is the exact nonclosure statement in local form. Equation (42) fixes the divergence of EμνE_{\mu\nu}Eμν​, which is four of its components’ worth of information, but leaves its transverse-traceless content , precisely the free bulk gravitational response , undetermined by brane data. The projected system (38)–(42) is covariantly consistent but is not in general a closed four-dimensional initial-value system.
