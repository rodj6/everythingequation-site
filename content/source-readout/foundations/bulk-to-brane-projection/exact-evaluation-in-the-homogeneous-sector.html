The signs and scalings are fixed and physically transparent. Positive brane tension gives a positive ρ2\rho^2ρ2 correction, enhancing the expansion rate at high density. The Weyl contribution scales as radiation, but its magnitude is set by a bulk-state parameter, not by brane matter. For ρ/λ≪1\rho/\lambda\ll1ρ/λ≪1 and μ/a4≪H2\mu/a^4\ll H^2μ/a4≪H2, standard four-dimensional cosmology is recovered.

The homogeneous sector already exhibits the exact division between what the projection fixes and what it leaves open:

  • the form and coefficient of the ρ2\rho^2ρ2 term are fixed once κ5\kappa_5κ5​ and λ\lambdaλ are specified;

  • the exponent a−4a^{-4}a−4 is fixed by symmetry and conservation alone;

  • the constant μ\muμ is bulk initial/boundary data, undetermined by the brane matter state. It is not a prediction of the model without a bulk-state condition.
