Scope, and what it does and does not do.

The relation (85) contains no unknown brane parameter once G4G_4G4​ and the phenomenological coefficients are measured. It is independent of the dark-radiation constant μ\muμ, whose a−4a^{-4}a−4 scaling is separable from ρ2\rho^2ρ2 when the matter equation of state and energy density are independently constrained. Its content is exact within its intersecting regimes:

  • BBB comes from the exact homogeneous thin-brane equation (48);

  • AΦ,AΨA_\Phi,A_\PsiAΦ​,AΨ​ are the leading terms of the controlled r≫ℓr\gg\ellr≫ℓ weak-field expansion (75);

  • the same ℓ\ellℓ and the same tuned source action govern both sectors;

  • no fit of ℓ\ellℓ remains after the coefficients are compared.

A violation of either equality falsifies minimal classical RS2 under the stated assumptions. A statistically significant nonzero common signal satisfying both equalities is a parameter-saving, out-of-sample success for RS2 and favours it over pure four-dimensional GR in model comparison. It does not falsify a four-dimensional theory that independently parameterizes all three coefficients: such a theory fits either agreement or violation. Only a restricted alternative predicting an incompatible relation is excluded.
