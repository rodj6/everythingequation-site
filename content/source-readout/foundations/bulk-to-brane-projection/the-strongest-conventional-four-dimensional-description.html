Linear sector, and the CFT realization of the 1/r31/r^31/r3 residue

The quadratic action of Section 6 is already a four-dimensional nonlocal action once GR\mathcal G_RGR​ is taken as its primitive kernel. That such nonlocal braneworld actions with explicit KK form factors can be written down constructively is shown, in the two-brane setting, by Barvinsky et al. [11]; we invoke that work only as a constructive control on the form of the eliminated action, not as an identity for RS2, whose single-brane spectrum is a continuum rather than a discrete tower. It contains the same massless pole, the same continuum branch cut, the same causal response, and the same weak-field coefficients as the five-dimensional description.

The four-dimensional alternative is not hypothetical. The stress-tensor vacuum-polarization loop of an appropriate four-dimensional conformal sector corrects the graviton propagator with the same 1/r31/r^31/r3 coefficient as the classical RS2 bulk calculation [9, 10, 18]. Thus even the most characteristic weak-field residue of the model has a conventional four-dimensional realization. This is the concrete form taken, inside RS2, by the general obstruction proved in Section 11.
