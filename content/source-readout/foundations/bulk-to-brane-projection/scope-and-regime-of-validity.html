Scope and regime of validity

The results derived above apply to the following physical domain and assumptions.

  1. Bulk-state domain. The same-readout witness requires that admissible initial data include normalizable bulk radiation. If the model definition fixes a unique stationary vacuum and excludes all homogeneous incoming data, the witness lies outside the restricted domain. The forced KK response and the effective-action equivalence remain, but the claimed instantaneous fiber must be reassessed for that domain.

  2. Minimal action. A brane Einstein term, a Gauss–Bonnet term, additional bulk fields, finite brane thickness, a second brane, or broken Z2Z_2Z2​ symmetry changes the junction condition, the propagator, and the coefficient relation. In such theories D1=D2=0D_1=D_2=0D1​=D2​=0 need not hold.

  3. Positive-tension branch. The sign and stability statements use λ>0\lambda>0λ>0. The negative-tension branch can reverse kinetic signs or the effective Newton coupling; it is not a harmless sign convention.

  4. Homogeneous sector. Equation (48) assumes an FRW brane, conserved brane matter, and no bulk matter flux. With bulk exchange, both ρ˙+3H(ρ+p)=0\dot\rho+3H(\rho+p)=0ρ˙​+3H(ρ+p)=0 and U∝a−4\mathcal U\propto a^{-4}U∝a−4 are modified.

  5. Weak-field sector. The coefficients AΦ,AΨA_\Phi,A_\PsiAΦ​,AΨ​ assume a static weak source, r≫ℓr\gg\ellr≫ℓ, and the regular retarded/no-incoming bulk condition. They are unreliable in the strong-field region and at r≲ℓr\lesssim\ellr≲ℓ.

  6. The state parameter μ\muμ. Neither the sign nor the magnitude of μ\muμ is fixed by the projected brane equation. Positivity follows only after imposing a positive-mass regular AdS–Schwarzschild bulk. Treating dark radiation as a universal fixed prediction would overstate the model.

  7. Observable versus decomposition. The induced metric and matter records are observable; the split CRS=κ54Π−E\mathcal C^{\mathrm{RS}}=\kappa_5^4\Pi-ECRS=κ54​Π−E is source-model dependent. A four-dimensional theory may reorganize the same total response into different fields or a nonlocal kernel.

  8. Unrestricted alternatives. Nonlocality, colored noise, a branch cut, or preparation dependence does not by itself refute a Tier-1 theory. Each is permitted in the exact pushed-forward history law (95). A contrary conclusion requires an independently imposed restriction , finite-pole local quadratic dynamics, fixed perturbative content, or Markovianity. Locality, or finitely many interacting fields, is not enough.

  9. Flat-brane tuning. The cross-regime relation was derived at Λ4=0\Lambda_4=0Λ4​=0. A detuned or de Sitter brane changes the background junction relation and can modify the weak-field coefficients. Applying the flat-brane relation to a weakly curved universe is an approximation requiring Hℓ≪1H\ell\ll1Hℓ≪1 and negligible curvature/detuning effects; it is not a complete late-time cosmological fit.

Two further parameters are simply undetermined rather than failure modes. The numerical value of ℓ\ellℓ , equivalently λ\lambdaλ or κ5\kappa_5κ5​ once G4G_4G4​ is fixed , is not predicted; only bounds on it follow from data. And the theory is a five-dimensional effective field theory, so nothing here constrains its ultraviolet completion above the corresponding cutoff.
