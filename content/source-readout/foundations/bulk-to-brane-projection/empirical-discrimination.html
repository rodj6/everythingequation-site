What no experiment of this kind can decide

No statistic constructed solely from induced brane fields can decide between RS2 and the exact Tier-1 process functional built from RS2. This is not a limitation of experimental sensitivity; it follows from equality of probability measures, (96). An experiment that couples directly to a bulk coordinate could distinguish the source fiber, but it would thereby enlarge the observable algebra and would no longer be confined to the readout used in the theorem.
