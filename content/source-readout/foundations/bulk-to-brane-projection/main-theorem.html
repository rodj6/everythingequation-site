Conversely, for every brane protocol, push the RS2 source-history measure forward through the complete brane readout. Theorem 12.1 establishes a concrete, quantitative source-projection realization together with its exact discrimination boundary. A stronger conclusion requires an independently justified restriction on admissible Tier-1 dynamics , fixed finite-pole local quadratic dynamics, prescribed finite perturbative content, or finite memory.
