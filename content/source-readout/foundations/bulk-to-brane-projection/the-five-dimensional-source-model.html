Physical equivalence and the reduced source space

Definition 2.1 (Source configuration).

A source configuration is a tuple s=(gAB, XA, ψ; initial and boundary data),(8) s=\bigl(g_{AB},\,X^A,\,\psi;\ \text{initial and boundary data}\bigr),\tag{8}s=(gAB​,XA,ψ; initial and boundary data),(8) solving the five-dimensional constraints, with the brane embedded as a timelike hypersurface.

Definition 2.2 (Physical equivalence).

Two source configurations are physically equivalent when related by a five-dimensional diffeomorphism carrying the brane and its orientation to the same physical hypersurface, composed with an internal gauge transformation of the brane matter fields. The reduced source space is SRS={(gAB,XA,ψ; data) solving the constraints}Diff5⋉Gm.(9) S_{\mathrm{RS}} =\frac{\{(g_{AB},X^A,\psi;\ \text{data})\ \text{solving the constraints}\}} {\mathrm{Diff}_5\ltimes\mathcal G_m}.\tag{9}SRS​=Diff5​⋉Gm​{(gAB​,XA,ψ; data) solving the constraints}​.(9)

This quotient does not remove bulk transverse-traceless gravitational waves, Kaluza–Klein continuum amplitudes, or the AdS–Schwarzschild mass parameter. Each changes gauge-invariant five-dimensional curvature invariants, energy flux, horizon structure, or the later induced brane metric, and hence is not pure gauge. This point is what makes the witness of Section 3 a genuine physical distinction rather than a coordinate artifact.

The instantaneous brane readout

Choose a five-dimensional Cauchy surface C5\mathcal C_5C5​ intersecting the brane worldvolume in a three-dimensional surface C3\mathcal C_3C3​.

Definition 2.3 (Tier-1 readout).

The instantaneous brane readout is the map p:SRS⟶Tbrane,p(s)=(γij,Θij; ψ,πψ)∣C3,(10) p:S_{\mathrm{RS}}\longrightarrow T_{\mathrm{brane}}, \qquad p(s)=\bigl(\gamma_{ij},\Theta_{ij};\ \psi,\pi_\psi\bigr)\big|_{\mathcal C_3},\tag{10}p:SRS​⟶Tbrane​,p(s)=(γij​,Θij​; ψ,πψ​)​C3​​,(10) where γij\gamma_{ij}γij​ is the three-metric induced on C3\mathcal C_3C3​, Θij\Theta_{ij}Θij​ is the extrinsic curvature of C3\mathcal C_3C3​ regarded as a spatial hypersurface within the brane worldvolume, and (ψ,πψ)(\psi,\pi_\psi)(ψ,πψ​) are the brane matter Cauchy data. The constraints induced from the five-dimensional equations and the matter gauge constraints are imposed; spatial diffeomorphisms of C3\mathcal C_3C3​ and matter gauge transformations are quotiented.

Two features of this definition matter. First, Θij\Theta_{ij}Θij​ records the gauge-independent first normal derivative of the induced metric along the brane without presupposing that the brane possesses a closed intrinsic Einstein Hamiltonian , which is exactly what is at issue. Second, the quotient by spatial diffeomorphisms and the imposition of the constraints means that no lapse-, shift-, or time-coordinate-dependent velocity enters, so the readout is not made noninjective by a trivial gauge artifact.

The readout is physically motivated inside the model: standard-model matter is confined to Σ\SigmaΣ, and local rods, clocks, detectors, and gravitational-wave interferometers respond to induced brane fields. Bulk data can be inferred only through their subsequent influence on those fields; they are not components of the present brane state.

Remark 2.4 (Instantaneity is essential).

If the readout were redefined to include the complete brane history for all times, any bulk distinction that changes that history would by definition already be distinguished. The dynamical question is precisely whether the present brane state closes under time evolution. Sections 3 and 11 answer the two versions of the question in opposite directions, and it is essential not to conflate them.
