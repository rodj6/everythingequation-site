Local data and global descent

We now establish the first leg of the progression: compatible local data glue to global fields. This is the theorem that fixes the domain of geometric realization. The material is classical [6, 11, 3]; what is new is only the use to which it is put and the care with which the invariance and admissibility hypotheses are tracked.

Remark 16 (Changing the coordinate atlas is not a base diffeomorphism).

The uniqueness clause above is stated for changes of local trivialization, which induce vertical bundle isomorphisms covering idM\mathrm{id}_MidM​. A change of coordinate atlas is a a different operation: re-charting MMM changes the component arrays gˇi\check g_igˇ​i​ of (12) according to (13) while leaving the intrinsic tensor ggg, and hence the object Ψ\PsiΨ and its class [Ψ]∈Sgeo[\Psi]\in S_{\mathrm{geo}}[Ψ]∈Sgeo​, entirely unchanged. It does not automatically produce a nontrivial base diffeomorphism, and it does not move a point of SgeoS_{\mathrm{geo}}Sgeo​. Nontrivial base diffeomorphisms enter only through the arrows of Ggeo\mathfrak{G}_{\mathrm{geo}}Ggeo​ with f≠idMf\neq\mathrm{id}_Mf=idM​, which act on the geometry itself and not merely on its coordinate presentation.

Remark 17 (Descent and time orientation).

Theorem 3.4 glues the local metrics to a global Lorentzian ggg, but it does not produce the time orientation t\mathfrak{t}t that Definition 2.4 demands. Whether ggg admits any time orientation at all is a global topological condition on (M,g)(M,g)(M,g) (it holds if and only if the timelike cone bundle of ggg has a global continuous section of one of its components), and no purely local compatibility condition can force it. Two consistent options are available, and a model must declare one.

Option (a): impose it after descent. Glue ggg as above, then require, as an additional global admissibility condition, that ggg be time-orientable and that a time orientation t\mathfrak{t}t be chosen and recorded in B\mathcal{B}B. Under this option Theorem 3.4 reconstructs the metric, and membership of the reconstructed configuration in Cgeo\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{geo}}Cgeo​ is a further global condition, verified separately.

Option (b): descend it. Adjoin to the local data a choice, for each iii, of a gig_igi​-timelike vector field τi∈Γ(TUi)\tau_i\in\Gamma(TU_i)τi​∈Γ(TUi​), and impose the additional compatibility condition that on every nonempty overlap τi\tau_iτi​ and τj\tau_jτj​ lie in the same component of the timelike cone, gi(τi,τj)<0on Ui∩Uj(21) g_i(\tau_i,\tau_j)<0 \qquad\text{on } U_i\cap U_j\tag{21}gi​(τi​,τj​)<0on Ui​∩Uj​(21) (with signature (−,+,+,+)(-,+,+,+)(−,+,+,+), so that two timelike vectors have negative inner product exactly when they are causally co-oriented). Condition (21) is a genuine gluing condition: the relation “lies in the same cone component as” is an equivalence relation on timelike vectors at each point, and the local choices τi\tau_iτi​ define, by (21), a coherent global selection of one component of the cone bundle at every x∈Mx\in Mx∈M. That selection is by definition a time orientation t\mathfrak{t}t of the glued ggg, and it is unique given the τi\tau_iτi​. Descent then reconstructs the pair (g,t)(g,\mathfrak{t})(g,t), and no further global condition on time orientation is needed. (A partition of unity subordinate to {Ui}\{U_i\}{Ui​} shows that t\mathfrak{t}t is realized by an actual global timelike vector field, since the cone components are convex.)

Corollary 3.7 (Realizability boundary).

A collection of proposed local metric, connection, and matter expressions represents a global geometric source configuration only if the cocycle and overlap conditions (14)–(17) hold on all overlaps, and only if the resulting global tuple lies in the declared admissibility class Cgeo\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{geo}}Cgeo​, which, by Remark 3.6, includes the global time-orientation condition. Consequently, an abstract tuple of relation values that admits no compatible descent data lies outside the geometric realization domain: it is a point of the abstract product T×∏αVαT\times\prod_\alpha V_\alphaT×∏α​Vα​ that is not in the image of any geometric evaluation map.

Proof. Immediate from Theorem 3.4: any global configuration restricts to compatible local data, so failure of compatibility for every presentation of the proposed values is an obstruction to the existence of a configuration presenting them. Membership in Cgeo\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{geo}}Cgeo​ is a further restriction, since Theorem 3.4 produces a global tuple but does not certify that it satisfies the declared causal, boundary, or topological conditions. ◻

Corollary 3.7 is the first and most basic restriction on the abstract completion of Paper 3: its coordinates must be jointly realizable by global geometric objects. Two further restrictions, invariance (Section 4) and separation (Section 5), follow.

Remark 19 (Obstructions are genuine).

That the constraint is not vacuous is visible already in cohomological terms: the isomorphism class of PPP is the class of {hij}\{h_{ij}\}{hij​} in the pointed set Hˇ1(M,G‾)\check H^1(M,\underline{G})Hˇ1(M,G​) (Remark 3.2), and characteristic numbers computed from AAA are constrained by that class [4, 8]. A demand such as “a U(1)U(1)U(1) bundle over S2S^2S2 with first Chern number 12\tfrac1221​” fails at the cocycle stage; there is no compatible descent datum, hence no configuration, hence no point of SgeoS_{\mathrm{geo}}Sgeo​, hence no point of the realized completion. The Cartesian product of independently nominated relation values is, in general, strictly larger than what geometry attains.
