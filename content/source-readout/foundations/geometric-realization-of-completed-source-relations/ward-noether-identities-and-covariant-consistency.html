Ward–Noether identities and covariant consistency

Gauge and diffeomorphism invariance impose Noether identities [9], and those identities constrain every effective representation of a completed source relation. Two logically distinct routes to covariant conservation must be kept apart, and we present them separately.

Remark 56 (Ward identities as a constraint on realizations).

Theorem 8.4 and Proposition 8.5 function as selection rules. Every effective representation of a completed source relation must satisfy them, because they follow from nothing more than the invariance requirement (R2) that made SrelS_{\mathrm{rel}}Srel​ a functional of the physical orbit in the first place. Consequently, a proposed correction to the Tier-1 field equations that violates the relevant Ward identity is not a geometric realization of an invariant completion. This is a sharp, checkable necessary condition on any candidate coupling of completed relations to Tier-1 dynamics.
