Variational realization of completed relations

We now embed the completed relation data into dynamics. The claim to be established is narrow and precise: an invariant relation-dependent action yields, by functional variation, correctly normalized stress-energy, gauge-current, and matter responses, and nothing is postulated by naming it.

Matter pairings and independent components

Convention 7.4 (Matter variation and the dual pairing).

The representation ρ ⁣:G→GL(W)\rho\colon G\to\mathrm{GL}(W)ρ:G→GL(W) is not assumed unitary (Convention 2.1), and WWW may be complex. Accordingly the matter Euler operator is valued in the dual, and the variational pairing must be specified. We adopt the following, and use it consistently.

(i) Real independent components. Suppose Φ\PhiΦ denotes a collection of real independent field components. This is always achievable: decompose a complex field into its real and imaginary parts, or a section of a complex bundle into its underlying real bundle. Then δS/δΦ\delta S/\delta\PhiδS/δΦ is valued in the real dual bundle P×ρ∗W∗P\times_{\rho^{*}}W^{*}P×ρ∗​W∗, and the pairing ⟨ ⁣⟨⋅,⋅⟩ ⁣⟩ ⁣:W∗×W⟶R\langle\!\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle\!\rangle\colon W^{*}\times W\longrightarrow\mathbb{R}⟨⟨⋅,⋅⟩⟩:W∗×W⟶R is the canonical duality pairing. (ii) Complex fields treated holomorphically. Remark 49 (What has and has not been derived).
