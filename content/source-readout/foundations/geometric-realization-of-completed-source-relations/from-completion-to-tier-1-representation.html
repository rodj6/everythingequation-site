This is the whole of what the present paper establishes about dynamics, and the boundary of the argument should be stated precisely. Paper 4 constructs covariant response fields from Ggeo\mathfrak{G}_{\mathrm{geo}}Ggeo​-invariant actions. It does not determine whether the resulting Tier-1 evolution closes autonomously on TTT, which completion coordinates (if any) must be retained for a closed description, what happens when unresolved variables are eliminated, or whether such elimination produces memory or other history-dependent effective behaviour. In particular, no inference of Tier-1 autonomy may be drawn merely from the observation that some response functional happens to descend to a function of ttt at the kinematic level considered here: descent of a response at one level does not establish closure of an evolution, and the two questions are not the same. Autonomous closure, retained variables, elimination, memory, and projected evolution require the separate analysis of Paper 5, and are not treated here.
