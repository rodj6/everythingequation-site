Invariant global relations

Definition 4.1 (Invariant geometric relation).

A map J ⁣:Cgeo→VJ\colon\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{geo}}\to VJ:Cgeo​→V into a set VVV is an invariant geometric relation if it is constant on the arrows of Ggeo\mathfrak{G}_{\mathrm{geo}}Ggeo​, i.e. J(Ψ)=J(Ψ′)J(\Psi)=J(\Psi')J(Ψ)=J(Ψ′) whenever there is an arrow Ψ→Ψ′\Psi\to\Psi'Ψ→Ψ′. Equivalently, JJJ factors uniquely through q ⁣:Cgeo→Sgeoq\colon\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{geo}}\to S_{\mathrm{geo}}q:Cgeo​→Sgeo​, defining Jˉ ⁣:Sgeo→V\bar J\colon S_{\mathrm{geo}}\to VJˉ:Sgeo​→V. We write JJJ for both maps when no confusion arises.

Invariance under Ggeo\mathfrak{G}_{\mathrm{geo}}Ggeo​ means invariance under both gauge transformations and allowed diffeomorphisms (including preservation of orientation and time orientation). Both are required; neither alone suffices.

Example 4.3 (Spectral relations).

Let D[Ψ]\mathcal{D}[\Psi]D[Ψ] be an operator constructed naturally from the configuration data (g,A,Φ,B)(g,A,\Phi,\mathcal{B})(g,A,Φ,B), together with a specified domain determined by B\mathcal{B}B. Then any function of D[Ψ]\mathcal{D}[\Psi]D[Ψ] that is invariant under isomorphism is an invariant geometric relation. The spectrum, when it is well defined, is the leading example. The precise statement requires care, and we distinguish two cases.

(i) Intertwining. An arrow F ⁣:Ψ→Ψ′F\colon\Psi\to\Psi'F:Ψ→Ψ′ of Ggeo\mathfrak{G}_{\mathrm{geo}}Ggeo​ induces a linear isomorphism UF\mathcal{U}_FUF​ from the space of sections over Ψ\PsiΨ to the space of sections over Ψ′\Psi'Ψ′, carrying the domain of D[Ψ]\mathcal{D}[\Psi]D[Ψ] onto the domain of D[Ψ′]\mathcal{D}[\Psi']D[Ψ′] and satisfying the operator intertwining relation D[Ψ′]∘UF=UF∘D[Ψ].(27) \mathcal{D}[\Psi']\circ\mathcal{U}_F=\mathcal{U}_F\circ\mathcal{D}[\Psi] .\tag{27}D[Ψ′]∘UF​=UF​∘D[Ψ].(27) This holds by naturality of the construction and requires no metric structure on WWW. It already implies that any relation depending only on the operator up to linear isomorphism (its spectrum as a set, when the operator is, say, closed with nonempty resolvent set) is Ggeo\mathfrak{G}_{\mathrm{geo}}Ggeo​-invariant.

(ii) Unitary equivalence. If, in addition, the representation ρ\rhoρ is unitary (or, more generally, WWW carries a ρ\rhoρ-invariant Hermitian inner product), the associated bundle carries a bundle metric preserved by F^ρ\hat F_\rhoF^ρ​, the base measure d ⁣vol⁡g\mathrm{d}\!\operatorname{vol}_gdvolg​ is preserved by the allowed fff, and the declared boundary conditions define a self-adjoint realization of D\mathcal{D}D on the resulting Hilbert space, then UF\mathcal{U}_FUF​ is unitary and (27) is a unitary equivalence. In that case the spectrum as a multiset (with multiplicities), the heat trace tr⁡e−tD\operatorname{tr}e^{-t\mathcal{D}}tre−tD, zeta values, and eigenvalue gaps are all invariant geometric relations.

An arbitrary finite-dimensional ρ ⁣:G→GL(W)\rho\colon G\to\mathrm{GL}(W)ρ:G→GL(W) is not unitary, so case (ii) requires its stated hypotheses; absent them, only the intertwining statement (i) is available. In either case the specification of the domain is essential: without it the spectrum is not a function of [Ψ][\Psi][Ψ], and distinct self-adjoint extensions yield distinct invariant relations.

Remark 24 (Curvature does not determine transport).

Local curvature and global transport are related but not interchangeable. Curvature is the infinitesimal holonomy: the Ambrose–Singer theorem [2, 6] states that the Lie algebra of the holonomy group of a connection on a connected principal bundle is spanned by the curvature values FA(X,Y)F_A(X,Y)FA​(X,Y) at points reachable from the base point by horizontal curves, transported back along those curves. Nevertheless holonomy around noncontractible cycles is not determined by FAF_AFA​: flat connections with FA≡0F_A\equiv 0FA​≡0 can have arbitrary holonomy homomorphisms π1(M)→G\pi_1(M)\to Gπ1​(M)→G up to conjugacy. This is not a formal curiosity; it is the content of the Aharonov–Bohm effect [1] and of the Wu–Yang analysis of the global significance of gauge potentials [13], in which the physically operative datum is a holonomy in a region where the field strength vanishes identically. Section 6 is the sharpest mathematical illustration: there FA≡0F_A\equiv0FA​≡0 identically, and yet the reduced source is a full circle of physically inequivalent configurations, completely parametrized by holonomy.

Remark 25 (No finite separating list of curvature scalars).

It is not assumed anywhere below that a finite family of curvature scalars, or any fixed finite family of local invariants, separates arbitrary Lorentzian geometries. It does not: in Lorentzian signature there exist non-flat metrics all of whose polynomial scalar curvature invariants vanish identically, and which therefore cannot be distinguished from flat space by any such family; the plane-wave and more general Kundt-type “VSI” spacetimes are the standard examples [10]. Separation is therefore never invoked as a general fact. Where it is needed it is proved on the restricted domain of the model, as in Theorem 6.5.
