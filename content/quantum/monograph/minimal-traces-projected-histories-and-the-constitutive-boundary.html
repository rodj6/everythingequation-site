16.1 What the Jordan theorem does and does not select

The successive names “no-surplus incidence,” “single-channel exclusion,” “Jordan representation,” and “minimal positive boundary trace” describe closely related conditions in the checkpoint corpus. They are consolidated here into one equivalence theorem, with the statistical objects made explicit [C03, M30]. This prevents the same mathematical condition from being counted repeatedly as an independent derivation.

16.5 One chain, two possible constitutive choices

The variational selection chain can now be given precisely. For a fixed finite complete source and control domain:

  1. Supply linear coherent evolution, one actual sector, the declared fundamental resolution, and every future-active memory.
