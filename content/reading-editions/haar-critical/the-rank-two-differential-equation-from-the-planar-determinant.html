Continuation and resonances. For each fixed 0 < x < 1, (7.4) is meromorphic in h and agrees on the real base interval with the physical moment. The determinant small-ball estimate and the identity theorem extend the equality throughout ℜh > −1, with each pole of the sum removable. Uniform convergence of both hypergeometric series on compact interior x-sets and on parameter circles away from the resonances permits differentiation in x there. Taking the constant Laurent coefficient on such a circle commutes with these derivatives. Consequently (7.5) holds also at the removable parameters.

Independent controls. The companion rank2_check.py derives the integer-moment polynomials from the exact Taylor coefficients of the Bessel determinant and rotational symmetry, then tests every coefficient of the differential equation in rational arithmetic for h = 0, . . . , 10. It also compares the connection formula with a direct determinant integral, integrated by a finite-part Taylor series below 1 and oscillatory quadrature above 1. At cutoff 16384 the discrepancies were 4.49 × 10^{−10} and 1.81 × 10^{−11} for (h, x) = (−0.2, 0.13), (−0.125, 0.67). At the resonances (−0.5, 0.23) and (0.5, 0.37) they were 1.33×10^{−8} or less and 1.56×10^{−15} or less, respectively. The resonance values on the connection side were computed by taking a symmetric parameter limit at high precision. These quadratures are finite-cutoff numerical checks, not certified error bounds.
