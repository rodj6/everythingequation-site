In summary, the unscaled interior mixed moment tends to the circle mixed moment for all −1 < Re h < s + 1/2 at fixed N ≥ 2. The lower bound is necessary for an ordinary interior expectation because a noncolliding zero of F gives a positive planar density contribution when 0 < z < 1. For example at N = 2, s = 0, h = −1, every ordinary interior expectation diverges while (17.4) on the circle is finite. At N = 1 one has |Λ^{′}_{1}| = 1, so the radial limit holds for every Re h < s+1/2 by the one-angle majorant. The existence of circle values at arbitrarily negative exponents in (17.5) remains a separate statement.

Normalization controls. At s = 0, U(1 −N, 2 −2N, 2t) is a polynomial of degree N −1.
