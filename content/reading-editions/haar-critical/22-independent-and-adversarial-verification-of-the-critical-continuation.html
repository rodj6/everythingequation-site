What the checks do not establish

The finite comparisons do not prove coverage of the full parameter box. The connected-region claims instead depend on their complete closed-cell interval cover and analytic order/tilt majorants. The uniform finite formula still needs input-specific finite-arithmetic and nonzero-modulus gates. No scalar result is full-state multiwall transport. No rigorous numerical constant for the full paired stationary-phase remainder has been obtained, and the general rank-three adaptive method remains estimated. Near zeros, a small absolute allowance does not imply a relative guarantee.
