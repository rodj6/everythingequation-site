Support.

The transformation w = (λ −z)/(1 −zλ) gives S_{N} = Nz +^{∑}_{j}w_{j} with |w_{j}| = 1. The support of the untilted law is the circle |x −z| = 1 at N = 1 and the closed disk |x −Nz| ≤N at N ≥ 2. For two unit vectors their possible sums fill the disk of radius two. Adding a unit vector to a disk of radius r ≥ 1 fills the disk of radius r + 1, by rotation and the triangle inequality in both directions. The entire image is support because configurations with distinct angles have strictly positive density, are dense, and every open neighborhood of an image point has an open nonempty inverse image. The strictly positive weight for each real tilt preserves this support. In determinant language the entire Fourier transform determines this positive measure.
