Frequencies of the leading saddles

For even N there is a single balanced saddle class. Its directional frequency is Nz Re u. It vanishes in every direction at z = 0 and on the two imaginary rays for every real z > 0. For odd N the balanced frequencies are Nz Re u ± 1. Both undifferentiated coefficients are nonzero, and at least one frequency is nonzero, although one can vanish. At z = 0 with even N every positive-order source derivative has zero leading coefficient because S = 0. Thus the common derivative estimate is an upper bound.
