3.6 Bounded oscillatory modes: exact one-line and smooth Green’s quadra-

ture

For the bounded oscillatory modes h_{c} = z cos(ωt)j(ωz) and h_{s} = z sin(ωt)j(ωz) in (2.20), the exact one-line time-even integral (3.17) removes the need for any finite-dimensional variational trial basis, while for the time-odd mode h_{s} the triangular change of variables y^{′}= ys (s ∈[0, 1]) in (3.18)–(3.19) removes the y = y^{′} Green’s kink and yields a C^{∞} integrand. Table 3 reports the resulting 60- vs. 120-node Gauss–Legendre values at R = 1, which converge to < 3 × 10^{−17} and agree with all published entries of Rodgers [8, §5] within its six-decimal rounding (< 3.5×10^{−7}). Notice that the timelike HRT subtraction ratio ^{1}_{4}A_{ext}^{′′} /(−I) approaches the sharp upper bound 1/10 as ωR →0 (where j(ωz) →1 approaches O_{0}) and decreases monotonically as ωR grows, in full agreement with Theorem 3.2(iii). For known ω = 1, measuring two radii (R_{1} = 1, R_{2} = 2) separates the two quadrature amplitudes (c^{2}, s^{2}) in ch_{c} + sh_{s} with invertible 2 × 2 response matrix (det = 9.169 × 10^{−3}, condition number κ = 235.55).
