Remark 2.2 (Classical canonical energy versus CFT relative-entropy Hessian). Equations (2.9)– (2.12) are identities of classical vacuum Einstein gravity: the quadratic form ℰ_{B}(h) is well-defined on any linearized vacuum tangent h solving Dℱ[h] = 0 on the wedge Σ_{B}. Identifying ℰ_{B}(h) with the Hessian F_{B}^{′′}(0) of an actual one-parameter vacuum family additionally requires a second-order completion k solving (2.6) (which we construct explicitly in Section 3.5), while identifying F_{B}^{′′}(0) with the boundary CFT quantum Fisher information (second derivative of relative entropy D(ρ_{B}(λ)‖ρ_{B}(0))) further requires the holographic dictionary and an admissible family of finite-norm CFT states [7, 8]. We maintain this separation rigorously throughout.
