4 Rest-Frame Non-Identifiability and Symmetry-Breaking Lift-

ing

While Section 3 shows that every nonzero perturbation in V_{hom} ⊂ ker M_{1} has strictly posi-tive second-order canonical energy ℰ_{B}(h) > 0, positivity alone does not imply identifiability: distinct physical perturbations can share the exact same canonical energy across every rest-frame spherical boundary ball B(R, x_{0}) on the t = 0 slice. In this section we prove exact all-radii, all-centers non-identifiability theorems for rest-frame balls – both within a single po-larization and across both graviton polarizations – and compute the exact lifting produced by the three minimal symmetry-breaking extensions of the measurement family: boundary time shifts, deformed t = 0 regions (ellipses and quadrupoles), and Lorentz-boosted, time-translated balls, culminating in an explicit six-ball constructive recovery theorem with sharp conditioning bounds.
