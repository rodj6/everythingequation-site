1 Introduction

In the AdS/CFT correspondence, the entanglement entropy S_{B} and modular energy E_{B} = ⟨K_{B}⟩ of spherical boundary balls B(R, x_{0}) encode the bulk spacetime geometry via the Hubeny– Rangamani–Takayanagi (HRT) extremal-surface area formula [1, 2] and the Hollands–Wald covariant phase-space formalism [3–5]. At first order in a metric perturbation h around pure Poincaré AdS_{d+1}, the modular first law δS_{B} = δE_{B} is equivalent to the linearized vacuum Einstein equations in the bulk [5]. Yet even on shell, restricting observations to rest-frame spherical boundary balls B(R, x_{0}) on a single boundary time slice t = 0 leaves a large infinite-dimensional linear kernel ker M_{1}: every perturbation whose boundary energy density vanishes on t = 0 and whose spatial shear averages to zero over every hemisphere B̃(R, x_{0}) produces DE_{B}[h] =^{1}_{4}DA_{B}[h] = 0 across all radii R > 0 and all centers x_{0} ∈ℝ^{d−1} [5, 6].

At second order, the fundamental observable associated to a ball B is the Hollands–Wald canonical energy ℰ_{B}(h) = W_{ΣB}(γ, ℒ_{ξB}γ) [4], which in the holographic dual is conjectured to equal the relative-entropy Hessian (quantum Fisher information) δ^{(2)}S(ρ_{B}‖σ_{B}) = δ^{(2)}E_{B} − δ^{(2)}S_{B} for coherent/semiclassical boundary states [7]. For Poincaré AdS_{4} (d = 3), Rodgers [8] recently showed that the charge–area identity eliminates the second-order metric perturbation k from ℰ_{B}(h) completely, established strict positivity ℰ_{B}(h) > 0 on explicit time-even polyno-mial and oscillatory spatially homogeneous transverse-traceless (TT) gravitational waves inside ker M_{1}, and highlighted the central open inverse-problem questions for the quadratic readout map M_{2}|_{ker M1}: its full Lorentzian time-odd structure, its injectivity (identifiability) across radii and polarizations, the minimal geometric extensions needed to lift blind spots, its quantita-tive stability under measurement noise, and whether the same phenomena hold for localized, finite-energy bulk excitations.

The present paper develops a complete, self-contained mathematical resolution of these ques-tions in Poincaré AdS_{4}. Every theorem is proved analytically from standard four-dimensional Lorentzian general relativity, covariant phase space, extremal-surface geometry, and finite-dimensional conic optimization, and is backed by an independent symbolic and numerical re-producibility suite (verify_01–verify_06).
