1.2 Outline of the Paper

Section 2 sets up the Poincaré AdS_{4} geometry, modular Killing vectors, covariant phase space, charge–area identity, and polynomial/oscillatory bases of V_{hom}, while carefully separating classi-cal canonical energy from CFT relative-entropy Hessian hypotheses. Section 3 proves the exact Lorentzian HRT decoupling, ℍ^{2} Green’s function, one-line time-even integral identity, sharp time-odd spectral theorem (‖T‖_{op} = 1/10), direct Hollands–Wald corner audit, and closed-form Fefferman–Graham completions. Section 4 establishes the rest-frame non-identifiability theo-rems and derives the exact lifting laws for time shifts, m = 2/m = 4 boundary multipoles, and Lorentz-boosted balls. Section 5 develops the general PSD-quadratic optimal recovery theory, the n = 2 vs. n ≥3 SDP phase transition, the n = 3 zero-or-infinite dichotomy, and exponential radial extrapolation bounds. Section 6 proves the Axial Potential Theorem and computes ex-act closed-form wedge and finite global energies for localized wavepackets in ker M_{1}. Section 7 proves the exact real-space positivity identity for all centered balls, derives the polarized two-width master kernel and frequency-space kernels, proves our headline unconditional single-radius infinite-dimensional physical rank-one reconstruction theorem (determining every perturbation in the compact positive width-measure class ℳ_{ℂ}([a_{min}, a_{max}]) up to one overall sign at any sin-gle fixed centered radius R_{0} > 0 from any nonempty open time interval I), contrasts this with finite-K-radius lifted collapse and multi-radius lifted measure injectivity on (R_{1}, R_{2})×I, and es-tablishes exact quantitative stability (distinguishing continuous-window sign-quotient Lipschitz stability from conditional discrete-sample designs) and Bernstein-rate exponential/logarithmic ill-conditioning bounds (including the sharp unit-ball minimax error floor of 1). Section 8 situates these findings within the holographic bulk reconstruction, integral-geometry, shape-deformation, and phase-retrieval literatures and states all remaining open problems explicitly. Technical formulas and certificates are collected in Appendices A–C.
