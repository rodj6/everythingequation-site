The physical act and its qualitative mode

The decisive question is what bears the appearance already warranted in a current act. This chapter develops the positive principle of concrete performance and mode placement, then makes its alternatives precise enough to test the inference.

Grounding, causation, realization and identity license different inferences. This module examines the alternatives that motivated the current-performance account and the positive principle that supports it. Its finite models isolate implications; their qualitative and epistemic interpretations are stated premises, not experiences generated by a calculation. The models concern the attribution of an actual act and the limits of extending that attribution. The finite checks described here are source-reported checks preserved from the publication.

Use PPP for an independently nominated physical performance, A for an experience-inclusive act, and QQQ for its actual qualitative mode. RRR is a physically operative reason/control component, VVV a bodily expression, KKK a current acquaintance, and K∗K^*K∗ a composite knowledge performance. BBB denotes a genuinely exclusive qualitative instantiator outside the proposed mode-bearing PPP. It is distinct from the programme’s unsplit-ground notation UUU. A numerically distinct property instance of PPP is not such an exclusive bearer.

8.1 Current grounding and evidential parity

An actual relational presentation can be evidence even when its apparent objects are illusory. External inaccuracy is different from absence of appearance. Equally, a response about a relation need not itself contain the sensory counterparts. The appropriate parity principle is evidential: relational subject matter alone is no reason to disqualify otherwise credible current experiential evidence. It does not assign relational testimony the same error rate as unary-quality testimony. Retention, conceptual load and reconstruction can make relevant differences. Joint accessibility and phenomenal unity must also be distinguished (Bayne and Chalmers, 2003).

Nonfloating epistemic grounding

If KKK is genuine direct current acquaintance with this QQQ, then QQQ participates in its epistemic basis. A matching response generated independently of that basis is not thereby direct acquaintance with it. Under ordinary causal or synchronic constitutive ordering, an appearance wholly after KKK cannot supply its present basis. The motivation is contact with this actual occurrence rather than an accidentally or inferentially correct proxy. It names no favoured neural stage and permits a newly constructed QQQ during current comparison. Direct phenomenal concepts and their content-making basis are a substantive constitutional possibility (Chalmers, 2003; Balog, 2012); unchanged physical vehicles do not automatically preserve that basis.

This principle is compatible with noncausal justification. An abstract theorem can justify a belief without being a material part of it. Current qualitative acquaintance instead concerns a concrete instantiated basis, but concreteness does not by itself make that basis physical. A particular judgment also need not be knowledge merely because QQQ occurs: its classification may be mistaken.

The first three motivate pressure against an independently extra cause, but do not themselves prove the fourth or reduce qualitative property types. Causal completeness can support physical constitution while leaving reductive and nonreductive views open (Montero and Papineau, 2016).

A more direct powers proposal

Felt aversiveness makes relief-directed motivation intelligible from its character, rather than from a learned warning code alone. Experienced trying can nevertheless fail, so an intrinsic-power argument should distinguish a motive toward an attempt from successful movement (MMMørch, 2019). Competing goals and independently specified enabling conditions matter. The pain-asymbolia objection must distinguish changed affective character from failed implementation; a shared pain label does not establish identical total phenomenology, and defining the relevant quality by withdrawal would beg the question. Seeking painful stimulation can express an opposing motive; unchanged aversiveness with intact enabling conditions, no opposing motive and no avoidance attempt would instead threaten strict necessitation. These are distinct philosophical tests, not clinical findings established here. A felt urge to settle a comparison can be an operative motivational facet without containing the complete sensory pair. Efficacy of that motive does not transfer to every qualitative constituent of the scene; a genuine power of QQQ toward mental recognition also need not be a physical-report power. Fallible attribution of agency is a further reason to separate felt effort from its successful physical efficacy.

The stronger affective target makes the counterparts themselves aversive. Synchronized thermal-pair reports support jointly felt pain with connected/disconnected spatial content, including radiation at a neutral source location (Quevedo and Coghill, 2009). Those observations concern spatial appearance, not measured comparative aversive pull or relief priority. Felt badness, felt pressure, reason-giving force and implemented priority should not be defined by one another (Bain, 2019). For a proposed comparative-power test, relief alternatives require independently matched expected efficacy, delay, cost and side effects, understood targets and control capacity; the strongest felt pressure is not defined as whatever wins the motor competition.

8.9 Whole-act realization and literal mode preservation

Suppose K∗K^*K∗ genuinely guides VVV through RRR, QQQ is its literal current constituent or mode, and the whole K∗K^*K∗ has complete physical constitution. If the realization preserves those literal parts/modes rather than output-equivalent surrogates, a current physical act bears the actual qualitative basis. No separate Q→VQ\to VQ→V edge is required. The difficult premise is complete whole-act constitution; physical sufficiency for VVV realizes less.

The unrestricted rule “every whole with a physical effect is wholly physical” fails. For any effective physical RRR, a fusion RRR + BBB can inherit its effect while retaining a distinct constituent. The strongest mixed knowledge act makes BBB genuinely epistemic, so dismissing it as arbitrary grafting begs the question. Restricting to minimal causal support would instead remove real constituents irrelevant to one tested output, losing the route’s purpose.

Immanent organization improves the nomination. Part roles belong to what the organized whole is doing rather than to an observer’s inventory: an input is this activity’s source, a state its reason, and a movement its uptake because of their participation in the maintained exercise. Hylomorphic accounts explicitly treat organization as immanent activity and perceptual engagement as coordinated exercise (Jaworski, 2020). The role description adds participation in the maintained activity to the specification of transitions. 8.10 The corrected concrete-performance/mode principle

Process identity and multiple-description event individuation are established philosophical options (Place, 1956; Davidson, 1969). Mere systematic correlation is weaker than identity. Likewise, the stronger economy argument against irreducible psychophysical additions cannot simply be inherited when fundamental qualitative modes are permitted (Smart, 1959). The present principle therefore concerns actual instance placement, not a numerical event-saving proof.

Conservative mode placement

Given an independently established literal mode of a particular actual activity, presumptively assign it to that activity’s positively tracked concrete execution, at a fixed activity/phase grain and with its actual executing contributors included. Revise the attribution when independent evidence or a defended ontology identifies another instantiator, an omitted constitutive contributor, a merely supplied ground or a different activity. Descriptive vocabulary and irreducible property type alone do not establish a numerically different bearer.

The rule has three admission conditions. First, the mode is an actual way the activity is undergone or constituted, rather than only an external truth making a result correct. Second, the nominee is tracked through actor, source use, operations, uptake and participation history rather than output agreement. Third, the nomination respects known execution/phase counterexamples. These are grounds for a defeasible identity conclusion. “Complete experience-inclusive execution” must not be inserted into the physical premise.

Its positive warrant is continuity of a tracked instance across descriptions. A concrete property is a way an independently identified activity is; introducing a new property vocabulary need not introduce another performance. Positive execution paths distinguish this locus from its indicator, supplier or copy. At the same time, literal modes must not be transferred to every containing system. Consider the following corrections:

  1. A client’s input/output history does not execute remote multiplication. If multiplication is delegated, the remote processor belongs to the calculation; if it merely supplies an operand, it need not be part of a subsequent local calculation.
  2. A local signature can be the signing execution while protocol authorization additionally requires an actual remote verifier/ledger operation. Abstract validity rules are not automatically concrete parts; an actual completion operation is different.
  3. Biological control does not close the boundary of a prosthetic grasp or instrumental performance. Include the actual jaws/contact or instrument when the selected act requires them.
  4. Simultaneous saying and writing the same word can share actor, source, purpose and time while one vocal execution is only a member of the spoken-and-written whole. A continuously tracked single inscription instead supports bodily/intentional reidentification through actual stroke/mark participation.
  5. A promise now is not its fulfillment tomorrow. Shared actor and intentional content do not erase the change of temporal grain.
  6. A thermometer or copied inscription can track the same value/content without sharing the measured state’s instantiator or original execution. Common ownership and accuracy do not supply token identity.

These cases prevent selecting a desired bearer solely by actor, role or time. They supply constraints on the general rule rather than analogies proving qualitative reduction. In the main application, physically specified native sensory/comparison participation and independently ordinary joint-appearance/activity grounding converge on one current exercise. CMP supports reidentifying that experience-inclusive act with its organized physical engagement at the stated grain. That is the substantive, contestable extension; it is neither observed in a neural measurement nor a restatement that the desired stage appears.

A fair exclusive-bearer alternative

The rival can retain one agent, one well-bound K∗K^*K∗, actual QQQ, physical RRR and one simple generic content law. It denies that QQQ is a mode of the nominated PPP. It need not add another complete comparator, physical force or separately fitted law for every trial. If the agreed observation laws coincide, their likelihood ratio is one; an independently defended instance principle can still supply a rational preference, but another fit of shared data cannot supply experimental ontology selection.

A copy or view paired bijectively with each performance token may merely redescribe that token. An ontically distinct qualitative trope u with Instantiates⁡(P,u)\operatorname{Instantiates}(P,u)Instantiates(P,u) likewise permits PPP as bearer. The genuine rival adds Bearer⁡(B,Q)\operatorname{Bearer}(B,Q)Bearer(B,Q) and ¬Mode⁡(Q,P,τ)\neg\operatorname{Mode}(Q,P,\tau)¬Mode(Q,P,τ), or an equivalent nonlifting constitution denial. That substantive placement needs its own individuation/grounding rationale. The immanent account also owes occurrence/content and extension laws; calling its primitive mode free would prejudice the comparison. Raw event or symbol counts depend on the ontology/coding language and do not settle these debts.

The rule can have modest actual warrant without eliminating every coherent rival. Its preference combines independent actual mode, positive role-sensitive execution nomination and a general placement discipline with correction cases. Absence of evidence for BBB alone would be insufficient. An independently supported sensory executor outside the nomination, a bypassed reason, a distinct phase or a defended instantiator ontology can defeat or refine it.

8.11 Ownership, phase, and the broader programme

Identifying the ordinary experiencer with an animal identified through biological criteria can support literal ownership of current appearance (Olson, 2003). It does not establish which current organized event constitutes that appearance. A sole thinking-subsystem account can avoid duplicate subjects too. Moreover, strict current subject–animal identity has diachronic implications when the animal persists. The existing A3 perspective-continuation account treats a genuine qualification gap as ending a numerical experiential subject episode while biological/person continuity may remain (Rodgers, 2026e). These are different identity types. A subject-role or episodic-viewpoint claim is weaker than strict animal identity and must be named accordingly. Current CMP adopts neither a universal animalist persistence rule nor an equivalence with A3.

World involvement is also open at the correct grain. An external object need not itself perform the native comparison to be a constituent of the perceptual activity. Actual input, coupling and context should be explicit; the conclusion is not internal-only supervenience. Conversely, material source locations and represented locations are not the bearer. Direct cortical and neutral-site pain appearances constrain source-location shortcuts without fixing a unique current field.

The scaffold insight has a legitimate narrower role. Retained results can change future relevance, selection and interpretation; directed reconciliation of an actionable represented discrepancy differs from the mere existence of a physical difference. Thought produces a constrained representation rather than the source itself, and a successful local reconciliation can introduce new differences. This recursive development does not imply that every discrepancy controller has subjective will, that two communicating agents share one field, or that operational information is already qualitative (Rodgers, 2026c,a). It situates the present act within development while preserving the independently required occurrence/constitution distinction.

8.12 How the current connection differs from the conditional routes

The main inference separates occurrence projection from constitutive placement. Within the narrow physically nominated human regime, ordinary sensory experience, source-directed comparative practice and material continuity support projecting another current presentation. Prior task participation and sensory matching do not themselves provide dedicated measurements of jointness or basing. Present QQQ, seeing, current acquaintance and answer correctness are not eligibility predicates. The projected target is tethered to source-directed inspection/comparison, not just QQQ somewhere in a task window. Biological/operational continuity and current participation are partly ordinary inferences, not complete neural measurements.

CMP then supports the actual activity’s reidentification and literal mode placement. It does not create the occurrence predicted by the first rule. A current-Q interpretation may subsequently support the prediction, but cannot retroactively select the eligible class. An eligible mistaken answer can coexist with joint appearance; a credible field-free eligible case counts against projection.

The three strongest objections therefore have different targets. A serial-only sequence with only a relation proposition challenges jointness. A passive current field accompanying an independently automated answer challenges the activity tether and nomination of the answer-producing operation; split or narrow the activities instead of widening a containing time-window. An exclusive-B hybrid can grant jointness and tether while challenging CMP. A delayed account that first presents both counterparts and their relation at current uptake JJJ satisfies the target and need not be defeated. Keeping these alternatives separate yields a positive current-act connection without asserting first sensory onset, universal machine admission, exact anatomy or reduction of qualitative types.
