Presented form, content signatures and current sensory basing

A single presented form can already give us a relation. A bent contour presents differently directed arms; an extended patch presents differently situated aspects. This chapter develops that minimum, calculates the exact distinctions a content law would need, and separates the current sensory basis of an act from the question of its physical bearer.

These contributions have distinct warrants: phenomenological and constitutive argument, a conditional mathematical theorem, and a defeasible epistemic principle. They sharpen what is presented, how its content is constrained, and what makes it the basis of a particular act. They do not replace the positive performance-identification argument in Module H.

9.1 Relational aspects of one presented form

The minimum target requires qualitative counterparts and their relation together in an actual presentation. It does not require two independently existing objects, two original subjects, two input channels, or an overt comparison answer. One visibly extended patch can already have differently situated aspects; a visibly bent contour can have differently directed arms. Those aspects need not be numerically separate experiences or ontological parts of the bearer.

Principle 9.1: Finite spatial aspects

If a current presentation genuinely has spatially extended or differentiated qualitative character, with sufficient presented resolution for at least two apparent localities or directions, then their spatial arrangement is an aspect of that presentation’s character.

The support for this principle is the distinction between presenting extent and merely signifying it. A nonspatial signal can mean “extended”, support a shape name, or control an accurate drawing. Those achievements do not make the signal itself visibly extended. What distinguishes the proposed antecedent is an apparent spatial organisation, rather than a label that refers to an organisation elsewhere. At the stated finite grain, differently located or directed features and their arrangement belong to how this one form appears. This is an account of presented character, not an inference from the geometry of a stimulus or an encoding.

The presented-resolution restriction does real work. An unresolved point, an undifferentiated shape-associated quality, or a sequence of separately experienced positions followed by an inferred shape need not satisfy it. Accurate recognition alone does not establish resolved aspects. A critic can also deny that apparent form has the proposed aspect structure; behavioral success does not decide that phenomenological disagreement. The principle does not infer a phenomenal token for every mathematical point, an infinite-resolution continuum, or the simultaneous appearance of every detail later accessible by shifting attention.

Once the principle is accepted for an independently warranted form token aaa, the target extraction is elementary. Let

W=(a,F,i,j,qi,qj,rij) W=(a,F,i,j,q_i,q_j,r_{ij}) W=(a,F,i,j,qi​,qj​,rij​)

record its actual form character FFF, two genuinely given aspects qi,qjq_i,q_jqi​,qj​ and their given arrangement rijr_{ij}rij​. Then

W⟼(a;qi,qj,rij) W\longmapsto(a;q_i,q_j,r_{ij}) W⟼(a;qi​,qj​,rij​)

retains a witness to minimum relational appearance in the very same presentation. No inference combines separately anchored experiences into a new common field. This record already contains the relevant aspects; the projection is a certificate of their common witness, not a reconstruction algorithm that discovers jointness. The argumentative advance is the spatial-aspect justification for obtaining that witness from a form anchor.

This removes an evidential requirement for a two-object comparison answer. It does not establish that comparison, attention, drawing or covert inspection can be physically abolished while the same appearance survives. That causal preservation claim would require further evidence. A new form first constructed at current uptake JJJ can supply the witness: earlier sensory tokens need neither persist nor fuse. The spatial content also does not require a spatially congruent physical bearer. An exclusive qualitative bearer B\mathbb{B}B could instantiate precisely this form. Aspect structure therefore clarifies jointness while leaving the distinct bearer argument intact.

9.4 Stochastic content and limited observations

A stochastic equivariant law at an HHH-fixed base has an HHH-invariant distribution. On a finite transitive content orbit HcHcHc of size mmm, every member has equal probability, conditional on that orbit. This follows because any two orbit members are related by an HHH-action, which preserves their probabilities. The conditional entropy is log⁡2m\log_2mlog2​m. Different orbits may receive different total masses; uniformity across them does not follow. An intrinsically stochastic law can therefore have asymmetric realized outcomes without choosing a fixed preferred orientation. A deterministic description of the realized outcome would have to include its distinguishing draw, context or history.

For an unaugmented deterministic choice ccc at an HHH-fixed base, covariance holds for exactly ∣K∣|K|∣K∣ of the ∣H∣|H|∣H∣ transformations. Its mismatch fraction under uniform enumeration is therefore 1−1/m1-1/m1−1/m. For the line and square examples this is 11/1211/1211/12 and 2/32/32/3, respectively. These are symmetry-test fractions, not behavioral error rates.

9.5 Current basing without physical-port constitution

Current basing asks whether a particular actual relational presentation is the operative sensory basis of a nominated comparison, not merely an appearance accompanying a correct output. Let Bcur(Q,A)B_{\mathrm{cur}}(Q,A)Bcur​(Q,A) express that QQQ’s relational aspect is such a basis of the current activity AAA. This differs from identifying AAA with an independently nominated physical performance PPP, or making a physical content vehicle a literal constituent of QQQ. Those latter questions concern constitution and bearer placement.

Four inputs keep the basing inference assessable:

  1. An independently credible phenomenal anchor warrants a current QJQ_JQJ​ and its presented relational aspect ρ\rhoρ. Source geometry, a relation bit and answer correctness do not supply this alone.
  2. Genuine current acquaintance KJK_JKJ​ addresses ρ\rhoρ of this presentation. An equal-valued memory or another field is not the same source merely because its content agrees.
  3. A nominated reason vehicle RRR receives that comparative content nondeviantly, preserving its source and epistemic role. A start signal from KJK_JKJ​ followed by a cached answer is not such conveyance.
  4. The nominated activity is the agent’s current exercise of that apprehension, with RRR serving its authored comparative role. Ordinary source-specific practical knowledge, source selection, instruction-dependent retasking and checks for bypasses can support this application. Causal efficacy or acquaintance considered separately does not establish it.

These inputs separate phenomenal evidence, addressed epistemic contact, transmission history and practical use. Their conjunction is not established by defining the symbols. In particular the fourth input retains a substantive current-activity commitment; it cannot be inferred merely from a downstream controller’s success.

The proposed transmission warrant is ordinary and defeasible: when an agent genuinely apprehends a present relation, its content is conveyed without source substitution, and the agent knowingly exercises that apprehension in this current comparison, the relation remains an operative qualitative basis of the activity. The positive reason is source-guided execution, rather than accidental agreement between an output and a concurrent field. Failed source selection, misunderstood content, substitution or loss of the current exercise can defeat the inference. It is neither a theorem about physical bits nor a claim that every truth-maker must efficiently cause the action.

This warrant does not require RRR to constitute QQQ. An explicit consistent countercase has one agent, an actual QJQ_JQJ​ borne exclusively by B\mathbb{B}B, genuine KJK_JKJ​, faithful delivery to physical RRR, and a current psychophysical comparison AAA that uses this very apprehension. Then Bcur(QJ,A)B_{\mathrm{cur}}(Q_J,A)Bcur​(QJ​,A) can hold even though QJQ_JQJ​ is not a mode of RRR or of physical PPP. The rival expressly denies the transfer of the qualitative mode to PPP; merely assigning the name B\mathbb{B}B to a property instance of PPP would not make it exclusive. Identification of AAA with PPP remains the separate CMP argument. This countercase establishes nonnecessity of port constitution for current basing. It does not show that every complete contract using port constitution is logically stronger than every contract using addressed transmission: their other requirements can differ.

A cached-answer route lets genuine KJK_JKJ​ trigger an action while a different record supplies its comparative value. This defeats faithful comparative conveyance. A different countermodel preserves genuine ancestry:

Content fidelity and causal ancestry do not establish Bcur(Qa,A)B_{\mathrm{cur}}(Q_a,A)Bcur​(Qa​,A). If QbQ_bQb​ first appears at current uptake JJJ, it may itself satisfy the target. The correct repair is to anchor and nominate that current field, not to demand persistence of an earlier one.

The identity identifies a signed physical route contribution. It requires no reconstruction of the whole context. It does not identify a qualitative source, prove authorship, establish current basing, or label every individual episode in a heterogeneous ensemble. A gate inserted between MMM and QbQ_bQb​ in the preceding countermodel can affect the action even though QaQ_aQa​ is not its current basis. Conversely, if KaK_aKa​ is already independently warranted as the current comparison’s acquaintance, a gate after that uptake tests downstream expression; it does not discover the earlier basing. If the gate precedes field formation, separate anchors in the two arms can support separate ordinary applications, but not transport of one fixed pre-gate field.

The archival comparison in Module G contains no such selective outgoing gate. Its initial sensory calibration and appearance-directed instructions support the ordinary current-scene inference at their stated strength; jointness and basing were not measured by dedicated probes. Separating basing from port constitution therefore repairs an unnecessary premise without inventing additional validation. Epistemic ancestry, operative current basis and downstream expression remain distinct, as do ordinary warrant, physical intervention algebra and constitutive bearer attribution.
