4 Robust reconstruction of joint experiments

4.1 The information lost by deleting a view

The obstruction concerns exact target selection, not the existence of stable measures of joint information. A different question is available: after retaining only part of an observation, can one common reconstruction procedure reproduce the full experiment for every unknown preparation?

Let Θ\ThetaΘ be a nonempty finite preparation set. An experiment has laws PJ,θP_{J,\theta}PJ,θ​ on a finite joint alphabet YJ\mathcal Y_JYJ​. A retained view is the deterministic projection πL:YJ→YL\pi_L:\mathcal Y_J\to\mathcal Y_LπL​:YJ​→YL​, with image alphabet YL\mathcal Y_LYL​ and marginal laws PL,θP_{L,\theta}PL,θ​.

Definition 4.1 (Directed reconstruction deficiency)

The loss of reconstructing JJJ from LLL is

δP(J∣L)=min⁡G:YL⇝YJmax⁡θ∈ΘTV⁡(PJ,θ,PL,θG). \delta_P(J\mid L)=\min_{G:\mathcal Y_L\rightsquigarrow\mathcal Y_J} \max_{\theta\in\Theta}\TV(P_{J,\theta},P_{L,\theta}G). δP​(J∣L)=G:YL​⇝YJ​min​θ∈Θmax​TV(PJ,θ​,PL,θ​G).(4.1)

The stochastic row kernel GGG is the same for all unknown θ\thetaθ. It may use a declared public context, but not the unknown source label.

This is directed statistical deficiency in a target-first, half-L1L^1L1 convention, not a new information measure. Statistical comparison has its classical Blackwell/Le Cam lineage [6, 16]; deficiency has also been applied directly to feature quality [24]. Argument order, row versus column kernels, and the factor of two in variational divergence must be translated when comparing formulas.

The decoder reproduces laws. In particular it may resample even the retained coordinates. It need not recover the same realized event, preserve a particular archive carrier, respect a deadline, or be available as a native physical operation. Those stronger requirements belong to the execution contract in Section 5, Section 6. They are not consequences of Equation 4.1.

Proof

Finite stochastic matrices form a compact polytope and the objective is continuous. The absolute-value epigraph gives a finite linear program with rational coefficients; a rational optimum can be chosen. For a decision loss valued in [0,1][0,1][0,1], any decision procedure based on JJJ can be emulated from LLL, using an optimal GGG followed by that procedure, with additional risk at most δP(J∣L)\delta_P(J\mid L)δP​(J∣L) for every preparation and hence every prior. This forward implication follows from the TV bound on bounded expectations. No full statistical-comparison converse is needed below.

One may record the entire table δP(J∣L)\delta_P(J\mid L)δP​(J∣L). A derived deletion diagnostic is

uP(J)=min⁡j∈JδP(J∣J∖{j}), u_P(J)=\min_{j\in J}\delta_P(J\mid J\setminus\{j\}), uP​(J)=j∈Jmin​δP​(J∣J∖{j}),(4.5)

where the empty view is a one-point alphabet. Positive uP(J)u_P(J)uP​(J) means that each one-coordinate deletion loses experiment information. It is not an additive information decomposition, a physical edge attribution, or an exhaustive notion of integration. In particular, it should not be identified with a partial-information-decomposition atom; unique-information constructions answer their own specified comparison questions [5].

Independent noise and a copy of an already retained record satisfy this informational criterion. A coordinate whose marginal merely ignores the source need not satisfy it. Nor does snapshot redundancy imply that the coordinate is causally idle later: an otherwise redundant backup may become indispensable after the original register is overwritten.

4.4 Why a single TV increment is insufficient

Proposition 4.5 (Marginal independence and scalar increments)

No rule can discard every marginally source-independent coordinate while retaining all joint-only information. For a fixed contrast, or a common family of maximized contrasts,

σ(J)=d(J)−max⁡L⊊Jd(L)≥0, \sigma(J)=d(J)-\max_{L\subsetneq J}d(L)\ge0, σ(J)=d(J)−L⊊Jmax​d(L)≥0,

but σ(J)=0\sigma(J)=0σ(J)=0 does not imply that some proper view reconstructs JJJ exactly.

Proof

Let (Y,Z)=(ξ⊕b,ξ)(Y,Z)=(\xi\oplus b,\xi)(Y,Z)=(ξ⊕b,ξ) with ξ\xiξ fair. Both marginals are independent of bbb, while Y⊕Z=bY\oplus Z=bY⊕Z=b. Thus marginal independence cannot identify irrelevant padding. Nonnegativity of σ\sigmaσ follows from contraction under projection, including after taking suprema over one common contrast family. At ε=1/2\eps=1/2ε=1/2, the masked experiment has full and best-singleton contrasts both 1/21/21/2, yet Equation 4.9 gives δP(12∣1)=1/8\delta_P(12\mid1)=1/8δP​(12∣1)=1/8 and Equation 4.10 gives δP(12∣2)=1/4\delta_P(12\mid2)=1/4δP​(12∣2)=1/4.

□

There is a concrete decision witness. With source prior P(b=0)=3/4\Prb(b=0)=3/4P(b=0)=3/4, optimal binary classification has Bayes error 1/81/81/8 from the pair and 1/41/41/4 from its first coordinate. The calculation is in Appendix A. In (Y1,Y2,Y3)=(ξ,ξ⊕b,ξ)(Y_1,Y_2,Y_3)=(\xi,\xi\oplus b,\xi)(Y1​,Y2​,Y3​)=(ξ,ξ⊕b,ξ), the pairs 121212 and 232323 each have deletion diagnostic 1/21/21/2, while uP(13)=uP(123)=0u_P(13)=u_P(123)=0uP​(13)=uP​(123)=0.
