- A genuine scene-less qualification gap has no current localized experiential subject and ends an A3 episode. Organism/person continuity can remain. Failed recall or nonresponse alone proves neither presence nor absence of a scene.

- Artificial consciousness remains possible in principle, while the edition supplies no current-LLM consciousness certificate.
- Weak-coupling sensitivity, minimal-system permissiveness, selection obligations and transcript conservativity remain visible rather than being softened into stronger claims.
- Comparisons with other theories and cited neuroscience describe this publication's discussion. No new external literature claims, new clinical recommendations, species classifications or empirical validation results have been added.
- Contemplative and altered-state material motivates interpretation without proving ontology, personal survival or a universal subject.
