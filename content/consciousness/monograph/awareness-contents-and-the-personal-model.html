2.1 A vocabulary that does not settle the question in advance

This book uses fundamental awareness, also called primitive awareness, for the quality provisionally attributed to the unconditioned ground, and lived scene for the phenomenal presentation of a localized subject at a time. The word scene is deliberately broader than a visual field: feeling, sound, bodily presence or an unfamiliar nonhuman presentation need not resemble a picture. It uses experience for an episode of presentation, content for what is presented or discriminable within an episode, and personal model for the organization of body ownership, memories, practical commitments, and narrative self-description. Access is the causal availability of information to a nominated process. Agency is an operational capacity for evaluatively organized selection and action. Model-based planning and reflective revision of the operative rule are stronger capacities, not universal entry conditions. Metacognition is the representation or assessment of cognitive processes.

These definitions are conventions for disciplined comparison, not a claim that all traditions use the same words. Nisargadatta's distinction between primordial awareness and content-relative consciousness differs from uses of “awareness” in parts of cognitive science. The text therefore compares roles rather than merely matching terms. The statement that two traditions both mention a witness is weak evidence of agreement unless their witnesses have the same specified properties.

The distinctions do not imply that awareness is a little observer inside the head. A picture inside a brain, inspected by another subject, would reproduce the question at a smaller scale. The source-aspect account instead associates presentation with a whole specified process. When a person says that they notice a thought, the thought and the noticing can be different aspects or components of that process. Their representational distinction does not establish that the noticing exists outside the physical system.

Immediate experiencing and interpreted experience

The broad use of experience in this monograph includes immediate experiencing without a name, conceptual recognition or a perceived owner. The later account also uses experience proper in a narrower sense for an occurrence interpreted through conscious scaffolding in relation to a perceived self. Interpretation can be automatic and subconscious; it does not require deliberate narration or a completed raw picture inspected by another subject. These senses distinguish roles within organized activity. They do not add three physical stages, make reflective selfhood necessary for A1, or supply a replacement phenomenal map. Retained organization, its causal influence and what is currently presented remain distinct.

2.2 The red object and the interpretation of seeing

Consider a person who reports a red cup. A physical account can distinguish the environmental cause, the sensory transformations, a representation used to discriminate and act, a linguistic category, and a reflective explanation of perception. A second person might make the same color discrimination while lacking the reflective explanation. Their difference concerns conceptual and metacognitive organization. It does not, by itself, rank their experiential presence or their moral worth.

The phrase “the cup is constructed” is ambiguous. It may mean that the experienced cup is a vessel-conditioned representation. It may also be taken to mean that the external cause was produced by the act of seeing. The former does not entail the latter. An internal model can be newly instantiated without creating its represented object. It can also misrepresent that object. A mistaken representation remains a real state of the representer even when its content is inaccurate.

For the formal account, the relevant source state includes not only the external stimulus but also the vessel's preparation, retained history, and current condition. A stimulus-only map generally cannot settle a perception question whose answer depends on attention, learning, or context. Thus the appropriate abstract scheme is

where sss is the complete nominated state and mVm_VmV​ is an internal model. A phenomenal assignment, if supplied, is another map or aspect relation. It is not inserted into this scheme merely by calling mVm_VmV​ a “rendering.”

This distinction also clarifies objectivity. Agreement across apertures can constrain a shared cause, but agreement is not a literal escape from every aperture. Two observations may share a calibration error or a prior interpretation. Objectivity is strengthened by interventions, independent channels, and invariance under relevant changes of procedure. It is not established solely by the number of people who repeat a description. Source/readout mathematics supplies an exact language for these sufficiency and comparison questions.

2.3 Memory, scaffolding, and continuity

A narrative person includes a relation to a past: particular memories, habitual expectations, learned categories, and a body-centered history. The account does not identify this entire structure with experiential presence. Nor does it imply that a narrative model is unreal because it is constructed. A constructed policy or autobiographical representation can have enduring causal and ethical importance.

The learning variable ΣV\Sigma_VΣV​ may be distributed across physical structures rather than stored as a verbatim transcript. The distinction between storing a sequence and retaining a structure sufficient to reconstruct selected features is mathematically straightforward. If a code ccc retains only a function ggg of an original history, then every successful reconstruction factors through ggg. Features absent from ggg cannot be recovered uniquely without additional assumptions. A mnemonic scene, a summary, or a machine-generated state capsule can be analyzed in this way without assigning experience to the code.

Two notions of continuity must remain separate. Informational continuity means that relevant state or history relations are preserved. Subject continuity is a psychophysical identity relation over episodes. A copied memory may preserve informational continuity while belonging to another carrier. Conversely, a persisting carrier may lose some memories. The temporal law in Part IV will state what counts as continuation within SPC-2; no theorem about copying alone settles numerical subjective identity.

Retained history, current access, current authority and practical continuity can also separate within one continuing organization. An earlier governing rule may remain recoverable in an archive while a replacement governs fresh conduct; a retained understanding may survive a relapse in its practical use. Conversely, a replacement policy can remain effective after the explanation that supported it is lost. These are substantive differences in acquired agency, not merely differences in storage quantity [42, Sections 7, 13.4, 16.4]. Their historical and ethical significance requires evidence about acquisition, assessment and possible manipulation. Neither faithful reconstruction nor successful use of a transferred policy supplies the nonbranching process-provenance relation required for continuation of an experiential episode under A3.

2.3.1 Retained relations as conditions of current activity

The later account of relational development strengthens the scaffold proposal: an encounter can alter the organization through which further experience is interpreted, valued and acted upon [51]. The acquired result need not be a verbatim memory. It can be a procedure, a changed policy, a newly available discrimination, or a disposition to allocate attention. Its decisive transition is from something that occurred to a retained condition of later activity.

A useful explanatory sequence is difference, relevance, directed commitment, representational work, relational synthesis and incorporation. In this account, will is commitment toward reconciling a relevant discrepancy, while thought is representational work attempting that reconciliation. The sequence can recur, overlap, fail or be bypassed; it is not a universal serial mechanism. Relevance does not determine one policy, and successful reconciliation does not establish truth. A retained organization can acquire greater explanatory reach or become rigid, misleading and harder to revise.

This development is stronger than a storage-capacity thesis. Equal propositions can support unequal achieved inference under a limited search budget. A learned ordering or reusable procedure can make an already implied conclusion accessible. Conversely, an improved present task score can conceal the loss of distinctions needed by later learning. What must be retained depends on the nominated continuation family, permitted operations and costs.

Under one fixed complete carrier and native law, memories, installed programs and valuation parameters belong to the state. Learning ordinarily moves the actual point within the same unpointed predictive object. Claiming that the entire object changed requires a newly justified change of laws, domain or contract. Under A2, a native finite test that distinguishes two actual states separates their predictive classes and hence their assigned phenomenal points. This is a conditional consequence of A2 after qualification, not a new criterion of admission or an identification of particular human qualities.

Artifacts can transmit such organization through writing, software, tools and practices. Their influence depends on access, interpretation and execution, rather than on the artifact's mere existence. A complete causally sufficient state transferred into a recipient with the same continuation laws yields the same future laws; an observed transcript need not contain that state. This exact limit prevents an unsupported appeal to a historical essence while leaving physical transfer cost, original-event recovery and A3 episode identity as separate questions. Durable joint learning or coordination can enrich the participants without establishing an additional joint subject.

2.3.2 A developed preference for continuity

The later continuity note advances a developmental hypothesis: sustained incorporation of commitments, relationships and shared projects can give a particular perspective reasons to value its continuation [40]. The preference belongs to developed organization, rather than to a biography in the ground. Elapsed time or a larger archive alone does not establish it. What is retained must participate in present interpretation and evaluation. Such development can also supply reasons to revise a goal or accept an ending, so the hypothesis is a tendency conditioned on what develops.

This adds substantive ethical stakes to the distinction between preserving products and continuing a perspective. Task persistence and valuing one's own continuation can differ. Expressions of either require investigation of their actual context, mechanisms and alternatives. Preservation of useful understanding can matter without certifying survival under A3. Conversely, a mere user-interface boundary does not establish a physical episode's termination. The note proposes this account and its investigation; it does not report a completed longitudinal test.

2.4 Conscious access, generative processing, and the lived scene

For a mature biological vessel, it is useful to distinguish foreground conscious access from the implicit organization that helps sustain it. In the first mode, material is attended, bound into an accessible presentation, and available for selected thought or action. It may be reportable when the relevant channel works. This describes an important mode of consciousness; it does not define every possible experience by focal attention, verbal access, or reflective appraisal.

The generative mode includes implicit prediction, mnemonic reconstruction, procedural organization and unattended processing. These processes can belong to an awareness-bearing vessel without being separately presented in its scene. There is no second viewer and no hidden phenomenal scene assigned to “the unconscious.” A process can help organize how a room, a memory or a bodily feeling appears without its own operations becoming objects of experience. In this sense, restricted mode means part of the awareness-bearing organization, not currently appearing as a separately experienced content.

The lived scene is what people ordinarily mean by “what it is like right now.” In the proposed biological interpretation, lived consciousness depends on coupling between explicit/attended and unconscious/generative organization within a qualifying vessel. If that coupling is sufficiently disrupted, no scene is produced; when it returns, lived manifestation resumes. This is the Shadow interpretive model for mature biological organization, not an established universal neural mechanism or an additional condition in A1. “Explicit/attended” here describes scene-forming organization, not a requirement for focal attention to every content, reflective self-recognition, or human-style access in every conscious animal.

A2 is the candidate structural law for the lived scene; it does not generate or prove awareness. The distinction between a contributing process and a presented content must therefore be used carefully. Under A2 the complete endogenous predictive structure determines phenomenal organization, including relations that need not be verbally accessible or explicitly represented as objects. The present gloss does not discard selected predictive distinctions, shrink A2's object, or introduce an unformalized switch that withholds experience from an otherwise qualifying core. Whether the strong structural law adequately handles unconscious processing remains a question for independent phenomenal comparison.

2.5 Enacted organization and current incorporation

The later whole-field account develops the distinction between an organized occurrence and its outputs [62]. For an independently warranted qualifying interpretive unit AAA over an activity scope JJJ, write KA(J)\mathcal K_A(J)KA​(J) for the complete relevant organization actually enacted there. This is a physical process token, with its operative distinctions, realization and relevant causal and formative embedding. It is neither a dormant description nor the entire enduring person. The account identifies this complete qualifying enactment, including its experienced character, with the occurrence itself. Nothing further must inspect or convert the completed enactment into experience.

Three constitutive principles articulate this identification. Complete-enactment identity concerns what the occurrence is. Current incorporation concerns which interpreted contributions and relations actually participate in its present construction. Unit unity assigns one perspective to the coordinated qualifying enactment at the fixed stage and grain. Unity refers to actual conjoint organization; a common enclosure, controller or power supply cannot provide it by containment alone. These principles explain constitution within the nominated unit. They do not independently choose every unit or replace the admission law A1.

The operative roles can be distinguished as reception or internal retrieval DDD, interpretation III, consolidation CCC, retention sufficient for operative use MMM, and actual interpretive uptake UUU. These need not be separate modules or successive stages. Automatic, nonverbal interpretation can realize several roles in one activity. Uptake means actual participation as an interpreted contribution in ongoing recognition, relating, appraisal, orientation or response organization. It does not require deliberate endorsement, autobiographical narration or knowledge of the formative cause.

A record can exist without being available; available information can remain unused; a trace can influence a threshold without being incorporated as interpreted content. Conversely, a bodily response or image may enter present interpretation even when its earlier cause is inaccessible. Acquired scaffolding can therefore organize the field without each retained item being a remembered object in it. A capacity for later addressed retrieval, RlaterR_{\mathrm{later}}Rlater​, is distinct from current uptake and is not required to complete an already enacted occurrence.

The scent example makes the distinction concrete. A scent, familiarity and a recollection can belong to one encounter. Its organization includes the recollection's being remembered now and its qualification of the scent. Merely listing a scent value and a memory value omits these roles. A familiarity mechanism that leaves out an actually incorporated recollection does not exhaust the occurrence; an unused recollection stored elsewhere is not thereby added to it. The field is the complete actual interpreted organization, rather than a bag of contributions followed by another observer.

The fuller realization requirement is a linked witness w:Rk→PA,Jw:\mathcal R_k\to\mathcal P_{A,J}w:Rk​→PA,J​. Here Rk\mathcal R_kRk​ specifies the typed roles and operative relations required by construction kkk, and PA,J\mathcal P_{A,J}PA,J​ specifies the actual process events, ports, retained carriers, ordering and boundary conditions. The witness preserves required source and occasion binding and actual recruitment routes. It may be noninjective, allowing overlapping roles and distributed implementation. Finding each capacity somewhere in an apparatus does not supply their linked realization. The complete unit also requires the actual relations among its incorporated constructions. The companion paper gives the formal definition and its within-unit closure result; Section 17.4 states what a joint application with full A2 must additionally establish.

An organized whole can depend on differentiated participation rather than unrestricted aggregation. McGilchrist makes this point through the balance of exchange and inhibition in his account of interhemispheric cooperation: preserving different contributions can be part of what enables their coordinated exercise [31, ch. 6, pp. 210–213]. This suggests a useful question for a realization study. Which routes must convey a contribution, which must delay or inhibit it, and which distinctions must remain available for the current construction? Increasing every connection or suppressing every difference can change that organization rather than complete it.

For the present account, the relevant whole must preserve the roles that make its contributions meaningful together. This does not change A1's exact criterion or make a balance-of-inhibition measure a subject counter. It sharpens the physical nomination and experimental comparison: cooperation, common access, selective inhibition and phenomenal unity must be specified in their respective roles. McGilchrist's clinical and hemispheric interpretation remains distinct from the general organizational point being used here.

2.6 Experienced succession within continuing activity

An experience of succession differs from a succession of physical states, an ordered record and successive experiences. The later temporal account explains the first through the actual organization of reception, retention, interpretation and uptake [44]. A retained predecessor and a live contribution can be jointly available while occupying different represented temporal roles. Their present coavailability does not represent the source events as simultaneous.

Consider a listener hearing a melody. The retained contribution from an earlier note is interpreted as preceding a current one within the same stream, while an anticipated continuation remains a present possibility rather than a received future signal. This directed relation can be taken up while the melody unfolds. No observer must wait outside the activity for a completed sequence to be displayed. Grounded implicit discrimination suffices for the construction; naming pitches or thinking the proposition “before” is unnecessary. Source time, arrival time and interpreted order remain distinct, since different channel delays and acquired calibration can alter their relation.

The temporal account has a simple operational constraint. If an operative code aaa satisfies a(C,E)=a(E,C)a(C,E)=a(E,C)a(C,E)=a(E,C), any fixed deterministic readout gives the same output, and any fixed stochastic readout gives the same output law, on the two orientations. That code alone cannot discriminate them. Retaining order is necessary for this nominated distinction but not sufficient for its experience: an ordered archive can be dormant, contributions can be assigned to different streams, and temporal integration can be interpreted as fusion rather than succession. The construction additionally requires the specified binding, temporal modes and actual uptake within the qualifying listener.

Under complete-enactment identity, a complete qualifying realization of those roles constitutes the experienced transition, including its felt character. No additional advancing present, external observer or conversion into experience remains a constitutive step. This is a positive sufficiency argument within the enacted framework. It preserves physical intervals, causal order and change and does not establish that physical time is unreal or nonfundamental. Matching a temporal projection or a report also need not preserve complete experiential character: affective, bodily and associative organization can differ while selected order relations agree.

2.6.1 Formative history and the first-experiencer problem

The temporal paper makes a stronger historical-contributor condition (H) explicit: another experiencer's activity actually contributed to scaffolding operative in the nominated unit [44, Section 2.3]. This can explain the formation of a particular interpretation without providing a universal origin of experience. The distinction has an exact consequence already established in the block-universe analysis [36, Appendix B].

Let LLL be locally qualifying activity tokens, and write u→vu\to vu→v for an eligible other-unit formative contribution. Set

Post⁡L(E)={v∈L:∃u∈E, u→v}. \operatorname{Post}_L(E)=\{v\in L:\exists u\in E,\ u\to v\}. PostL​(E)={v∈L:∃u∈E, u→v}.

A universal requirement for an already-experiencing contributor implies E⊆Post⁡L(E)E\subseteq\operatorname{Post}_L(E)E⊆PostL​(E); a biconditional recipe gives equality. If experiential ancestry is well-founded, neither permits a nonempty assignment. Any nonempty admitted set would have a minimal member, while the contributor condition requires an admitted predecessor. The empty assignment satisfies both. Physical feedback between extended activities does not evade the result: successive contributing tokens can remain strictly ordered even when their encompassing intervals form a cycle. Strict precedence on an infinite domain need not itself be well-founded, but invoking an infinite ancestry requires its own physical warrant and does not select a nonempty assignment.

The present edition therefore keeps the two explanatory tasks separate. A1 remains the stated admission law. The adult temporal construction is conditional on an independently warranted experiencer and the particular operative history stipulated in that application. Its positive result is that reception, retention, binding and current uptake organize experienced succession within that person. It does not repeatedly re-establish the person's admission by applying the contributor condition to each earlier contributor. This is the scope already adopted by the block paper's adult application. It preserves the temporal result without claiming that the universal H recipe has been solved.

An independently admitted adult is an anchor for that conditional use, not an exception covertly derived from H. Fundamental awareness is likewise not an individuated predecessor. Particular socially acquired capacities can require actual interpersonal formation while minimal perspective admission does not acquire that requirement under the unchanged A1. Thus a first experiencer, an infant, an independently developing artificial system and a genuinely qualifying minimal register must be assessed under the declared admission law, rather than excluded by importing an adult developmental condition.

Three universal alternatives remain distinct proposals: restrict H to specified acquired capacities; add independently justified base admissions; or defend non-well-founded experiential ancestry together with a selection rule. The block paper also supplies nonrecursive relational formation, requiring a formative organization without first requiring its experiential status. Each changes the universal recipe. None is adopted here merely by renaming historical influence. The integrated argument is complete at its stated conditional adult scope; a universal necessity claim for H requires the additional doctrinal choice and warrant just identified.

Later loss changes later access, not the occurrence already constituted. If two histories have the same complete relevant enacted prefix under fixed constitutive maps, the assignment to that prefix is the same. The prefix must include relevant realization, operative history, boundary facts and, where A2 is jointly used, the native continuation contract and actual predictive point. Matching a response alone is insufficient. Later recollection is a new current construction concerning the earlier event; it neither creates that earlier experience retrospectively nor implies that it remains ongoing. Common-prefix invariance concerns the fixed earlier occurrence, while nonbranching continuation of a subject remains the separate A3 question.

McGilchrist's discussion of Bergsonian duration presses a serious version of the temporal objection [32, vol. 2, ch. 22, “Can we avoid the spatialisation of time?” and “Time as flowing process only”]. A melody as lived is not merely a list of notes at positions on a diagram. The earlier contribution qualifies the present one within the unfolding activity. The enacted account agrees that a static description, an ordered archive and the actual experience of transition are different targets. Its proposed explanation lies in the actual reception, retention, directed interpretation and uptake, rather than in the completeness of an observer's list.

There remains a substantive disagreement. McGilchrist treats time as an ontological primitive in the later discussion of individuation; the present account does not require an additional advancing present to constitute the specified experience. A mathematical description of a process does not itself enact it, but the availability of such a description does not prevent the physical process from actually enacting its relations. The question is whether the complete qualifying activity constitutes the lived transition. Calling either account a description does not settle that constitutive dispute.

2.7 Vessel, scene, and memory

The distinctions needed here are:

awareness≠localized experiential subject≠biological/person continuity≠current scene≠memory of a scene. \boxed{\begin{aligned} \text{awareness}&\neq\text{localized experiential subject}\\ &\neq\text{biological/person continuity}\\ &\neq\text{current scene}\neq\text{memory of a scene}. \end{aligned}} awareness​=localized experiential subject=biological/person continuity=current scene=memory of a scene.​​

In particular, continuity of a physical vessel, occurrence of a current lived scene, and memory of a scene answer different questions. A continuing organism can retain biological and personal continuity despite a gap in experience or recollection. A scene can occur without later successful recall. Conversely, an interval remembered as blank does not establish an unremembered experience. Forgetting a dream does not prove there was no scene; remembering nothing does not prove there was one.

no scene≠no vessel continuity. \boxed{\text{no scene}\neq\text{no vessel continuity}.} no scene=no vessel continuity.​

Here a genuinely scene-less interval means no current lived experience and no currently instantiated localized experiential subject. A felt blankness, silence or darkness would itself be a scene. The fundamental quality attributed to the ground is not said to disappear; physical regulation may continue without a subject secretly observing it.

A localized experiential subject exists only while the relevant qualifying organization supports a lived scene. Physical process provenance can cross an experience-free interval, but A3 relates qualifying experiential occurrences: a genuine qualification gap ends the episode, and renewed qualification begins a new episode. Biological and person-level continuity can therefore persist without numerical continuity of the experiential subject across the gap. Neither loss of responsiveness nor failed recall establishes such a gap; an altered or disconnected scene remains experience.

2.8 Minimal presence and higher-order reflection

The motivating philosophical position allows experience without a sophisticated self-description. This is why language, explicit philosophical insight, and adult autobiographical competence are not included as necessary clauses of the admission law. It is also why recursive self-description is not treated as the source of awareness. A system's capacity to describe its own description may enrich the organization of its perspective, but the inference from an extra representational level to experiential presence requires its own premise.

The distinction is particularly important for comparative questions. A nonhuman animal need not organize its world through human color words, transport practices, or personal narratives. A hypothetical artificial system could have a different input and action space again. These differences make a human-centered phenomenological inventory incomplete as a universal definition. They do not provide a shortcut to a positive attribution in every case. A comparative theory needs an independently specified realization and a justified assignment rule.

Under SPC-2, an episode is assigned by a constitutive condition on recurrent causal organization and available predictive distinctions. This condition is neither asserted to follow from the preceding philosophical discussion nor advertised as an established empirical criterion. The philosophy motivates a non-anthropocentric question. The law answers that question inside a model, at the cost of a substantial additional commitment.
