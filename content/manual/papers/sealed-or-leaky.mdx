## Information preserved, information accessible

*Sealed or Leaky* follows the seven-paper source–readout foundation. It gives explicit Bell-certified bounds on information absent from an initial classical readout, proves operational-surrogate limits, separates reversibility from accessibility, and constructs finite-resource witnesses in specified physical models.

[Read the substantial explanatory article](/articles/sealed-or-leaky), then [follow the complete technical argument](/sealed-or-leaky). The web edition includes every numbered result, full proof, mathematical calculation, table and reference. All content is readable directly on the site.

The Bell interpretation retains definite source outcomes, measurement independence and no-signalling conditional on the nominated initial classical information. Other results specify their own model and access conditions beside their statements.

[Download the original verification script](/publications/sealed-or-leaky/Sealed_or_Leaky_v3.1_Verification.py). Place it beside the matching LaTeX source to include its manuscript consistency checks. Run with Python 3 without the `-O` option. The script checks specified finite constructions and numerical bounds; its output records which checks ran.
