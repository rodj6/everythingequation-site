## The relation belongs to the current act {#overview}

When you compare how two things look, their relation can be present in the seeing itself. The account developed here identifies that relational appearance as a qualitative way in which the organised physical comparison occurs. It connects sensory evidence to a concrete bearer through two distinct warrants: the recurring native human capacity for current relational presentation, and a defended principle for identifying the experience-inclusive act with its materially tracked performance.

That connection is illustrated through an actual archived contextual-size comparison. Its retrospective status, prior archive exposure and absence of dedicated jointness and sensory-basing probes determine what the evidence supports. First construction of the relation at current uptake is enough: earlier experiences, their carriers and their subjects need not merge. The strongest constitutive rival grants the same current appearance and complete physical history while assigning qualitative instantiation to an exclusive bearer. The account meets that rival through the concrete-performance principle, whose defence is philosophical; the mathematics makes the surrounding constraints precise.

This is the complete main argument of *From Physical Comparison to Relational Appearance: A Defeasible Current-Act Account*, Jeremy Rodgers, Version 1.4, 16 October 2026. The [ten technical modules](/consciousness/relational-awareness/technical-guide) form its connected companion under the same [publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23408737). Together they develop a scoped positive account of actual current appearance, with its proofs, evidence, alternatives and full research provenance available as web text.

## 1. When a relation actually appears {#section-1}

A person looks at two objects and compares their apparent sizes. A physical system acquires signals, combines information, retains a descriptor and produces an answer. The relation between these descriptions is the problem of this paper. The physical sequence can be present without yet establishing that two qualitative counterparts and their relation are presented together. Conversely, accepting a current appearance does not identify which physical event bears it. The central question is how an independently specified organisation can support an actual relational presentation and a warranted attribution of its bearer.

The claim is that, in a continuing adult human visual comparison, there is reason to regard the presented relation as a way that the organised physical comparison itself occurs. This matters because producing the right answer, undergoing a sensory presentation and bearing that presentation are different achievements. The argument uses two warrants. Sensory calibration and ordinary evidence about what the person is doing support the occurrence of a current relational presentation. A general principle for tracking the concrete activity then supports attributing its qualitative way to the materially tracked performance. Both warrants are defeasible: relevant contrary evidence or a better constitutive account can overturn them. Their combination is a positive argument, with a defended philosophical premise at its centre.

The relevant native-human setting uses the participant’s own biological sensory and control organisation, in a materially continuing preparation. A qualitative mode means how an activity is actually undergone, rather than a message or product it emits. Physical eligibility is specified before interpreting the present appearance evidence. These restrictions keep the inference inspectable without claiming that a physical transition table alone establishes appearance or that every system producing the same answer qualifies.

This addresses an outstanding obligation in the relational-awareness programme. Earlier work distinguishes source organisation, accessible readout and perspective, and proposes conditional relations between native structure and experience (Rodgers, 2026e,f,h,c). Even a valid conditional assignment leaves a further obligation: its appearance-facing premise needs support beyond the assignment it licenses. The present contribution pursues that obligation through current human activity. The background concepts are introduced here; acceptance of the programme’s wider awareness-first ontology or its complete structural admission rules is unnecessary.

### 1.1. What must actually appear {#section-1-1}

<span id="definition-1-1"></span>

**Definition 1.1 (Relational appearance).** Relational appearance is an occurrence in which qualitative counterparts are presented together with a relation or arrangement among them. A counterpart is something as it appears within that presentation; it need not be an enduring external object or a preserved earlier phenomenal token.

For example, two differently situated apparent magnitudes may be jointly presented in a spatial scene. A subsequent proposition, “they are different”, can describe that presentation but is not identical with it. A person may instead experience only one remembered size, then another, and infer the proposition. Relationally meaningful language can itself appear without displaying the original poles: understanding that a mountain is taller than a hill need not present two visual heights together. The target therefore exceeds a relation bit, verbal competence or informational dependence.

It is also weaker than a universal theory of phenomenal fusion. An appearance generated at current comparison can contain new counterparts related within a new presentation. The old sensory tokens, their subjects or their physical carriers need not merge. Nor must the counterparts be experienced as one object. Two segregated objects can appear together; integration into a single attributed object and joint presence within a field are different questions. Experimental grouping and feature-integration measures accordingly bear on particular forms of organisation rather than defining minimal relational appearance (Vogelsang et al., 2024).

An externally described correlation is different again. Two registers can jointly encode a parity without any accepted phenomenal target. The investigator’s ability to describe their relation does not install that relation as an appearance in either register or in their union. Such a physical model is valuable when it specifies a mechanism or tests a proposed law; its informational relations are not observations of consciousness.

### 1.2. Presented form already has relational aspects {#section-1-2}

The minimal target does not require two separately recognised objects. A genuinely presented spatial form, with sufficient presented resolution for at least two apparent localities or directions, has those aspects in an arrangement. Taking two such aspects from that same presentation does not require combining two earlier experiences or first identifying them by name. A resolved visible outline can therefore support the thin relational minimum even when it is experienced as one object.

The argument begins from credible evidence of presented extent. Physical size, a successful shape label or an investigator’s partition of an image is insufficient. It also establishes no unity among otherwise separate fields. Its value is to remove an unnecessary multi-object requirement, rather than to infer consciousness from geometry. [Supplement Module I](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-i) develops the argument and its limits. This is consistent with the distinction between pre-reflective appearance and recognised personal experience: the relevant arrangement can be present without already being described as a red square or as an event in one’s history.

### 1.3. Five questions that must not be collapsed {#section-1-3}

Occurrence asks whether anything of the target kind actually appears. Jointness asks whether the qualitative counterparts and their relation are together in a presentation, rather than separately available for inference. Activity or basing asks whether this appearance is the sensory way of the particular comparative activity under discussion, rather than a concurrent field accompanying an automated answer. Timing asks when the appearance occurs, distinguishing physical event time from represented temporal content. Bearer asks which concrete event or organisation instantiates that qualitative way.

These questions interact without becoming interchangeable. A recorded response can support a current deadline on the ordinary interpretation of the task, but not the first onset. Selective stimulation can show a causal role in producing content without establishing that the stimulated site is the whole bearer. An animal can own an experience without the animal’s entire lifespan being identical with its current sensory performance. A retained description may suffice to generate a later presentation without having borne an earlier one.

The term physical engagement refers here to an organised activity at an explicit grain, including relevant acquisition, transformation and uptake. Correlation strength alone does not fix its boundary. Instruments that execute part of the act, and relevant world relations, may be constitutive of a spatially distributed performance. A container that includes a possible bearer is not thereby the bearer of the activity.

### 1.4. A serious delayed rival {#section-1-4}

Consider two accounts with the same acquisition, computation, bounded memory and reporting process. On an early account, a relational scene occurs during integration $I$. On a delayed account, the system stores an adequate relational summary and first constructs a joint scene during current inspection or judgment $J$. Both can subsequently produce the same testimony. The delayed account does not need an unlimited movie archive, a special law for every trial, or an absence of genuine current acquaintance. A generic parser acting on a bounded descriptor can construct new counterparts at $J$.

If the accounts stipulate the same observable likelihoods, refitting those observations cannot select their phenomenal stage assignment. This limitation calls for a different warrant; it is not a universal impossibility theorem about consciousness. The present argument targets current relational appearance, which both accounts can accept, and separates the positive occurrence inference from the constitutive attribution. An early onset is not a condition of success for this target.

The paper develops that route in seven sections. Section 2 recovers the motivating philosophical distinctions and earlier connecting proposals. Section 3 explains what formal constraints and sensory evidence contribute. Section 4 defends concrete-performance mode placement. Section 5 works through a retrospective illustration. Section 6 separates the strongest objections. Section 7 states the achieved scope and broader consequences. Essential inference and finalisation proofs are included in the appendices; the technical supplement develops the additional mathematics, evidence and philosophical alternatives.

## 2. Awareness, scaffolding and the constructed observer {#section-2}

### 2.1. Fundamental awareness and differentiated presentation {#section-2-1}

The motivating account takes awareness as fundamental: the knowing aspect of reality is not first produced by a reflective self-description. The monograph denotes reality prior to the operational source/readout distinction by $U$; this is not a separately acting observer. Differentiated presentation, on this proposal, arises within that basis through contrast, relation and the organisation of access. Awareness, sensory appearance, reflective recognition, appropriation as my experience, and a later account of what happened have different explanatory roles. Those roles can occur together within one ongoing activity; distinguishing them does not require a succession of separate experiential stages.

This is an explicit philosophical starting point, not the conclusion of the physical models below. Granting fundamental awareness does not yet specify which differentiated process presents a relation, whether several contributors share one presentation, or which concrete activity bears it. The current-act argument addresses a local part of that further obligation. Its sensory and constitutive warrants can be assessed without accepting the entire awareness-first ontology (Rodgers, 2026e,c).

### 2.2. Organisation, apparatus and acquired scaffolding {#section-2-2}

A list of ingredients is often inadequate to describe an occurrence. Hands, motion and air are components of a clap, but their inventory does not describe the organised act or its sound. The useful lesson is that organised occurrences can have properties whose attribution requires attention to their arrangement and activity. The analogy does not prove that any interaction is conscious or that an additional substance appears whenever a relation is realised.

The relational-awareness investigation begins with the same distinction. A third presentation can be a new organisation of counterparts rather than a third substance or a fusion of previously separate subjects. Once retained, that organisation can become an input to later reflection and action. Recursion combined with retention permits a scaffold to develop; recursion without retained consequences can merely repeat. Greater complexity alone does not identify one perspective: a coordinated crowd may have a complicated common activity without thereby constituting one experiential subject.

Apparatus and acquired scaffolding make distinct contributions to perceptual construction. Sensory and bodily organisation, acquired classifications, memories, expectations, feelings and beliefs shape the presentation through which the surroundings are encountered. They do not merely add descriptions after a consciously viewed raw image has already appeared. In this sense ongoing seeing is already interpretative perceptual processing. Reflectively naming what is seen, considering its significance and relating it to a personal history are further roles that can occur within the same activity. Suspending naming or inner commentary does not establish that perceptual processing or its acquired organisation has stopped. A presentation can be source-constrained without being source-complete, and the dependence of knowledge on access does not by itself establish the proposed numerical unity of its underlying basis (Rodgers, 2026e,c).

### 2.3. The constructed observer and personal experience {#section-2-3}

The observer in this account is itself a constructed central image: the model through which events acquire a personal location, history and significance. It need not be an additional spectator inside the person. Recognising that construction need not abolish useful memory or practical self-reference. The proposed change is a loss of the personal model’s privileged status as an exhaustive centre, while the model remains available for ordinary life (Rodgers, 2026c, §5.4). This is the motivation for the related recursively closed observer proposal, rather than a result established by successful self-description alone (Rodgers, 2026j,b).

Scaffolding provides a way to discuss this development without collapsing its levels. Acquired differentiations support memory, reflection and control. They shape ongoing activity, which can in turn revise the retained constraints and alter accessible source conditions. This reciprocal dependence is developed in the reflective-alignment paper (Rodgers, 2026b, §4.1). The operational feedback can be modelled without claiming that a functioning loop already establishes awareness or one phenomenal subject.

The related distinction between functional and subjective will is instructive. Mere difference does not entail will. Directed reconciliation of an actionable represented discrepancy can characterise a control function, while felt effort or intention raises a further phenomenal question. Such reconciliation can revise a model to fit the world rather than force the world to fit a defended self-image. This distinction locates control operations and their histories without deriving subjective will from a thermostat or treating every revised representation as experienced.

### 2.4. Occurrence, appearance and recollective interpretation {#section-2-4}

The companion draft Experience as Recollective Interpretation, dated 12 October 2026, gives these distinctions a particular use (Rodgers, 2026d). It calls an occurrence something that happens and is registered or retained, experience the interpretation of a retained occurrence as having occurred, and subjective or personalised experience its organisation through feeling, identity and personal significance. Here occurrence means actual taking place, without defining it by later retention; appearance names actual qualitative presentation, and personal experience names its appropriation as happening to someone. Appearance is the qualitative character of the constructed presentation, not a raw image awaiting a second internal viewer. Perceptual construction, reflective recognition and personal appropriation are distinguishable functions that can operate concurrently. The word experience, when used for a current qualitative mode in this paper, consequently has a broader use than the companion draft’s defined recollective use.

This difference in terminology does not settle the philosophical question. The companion draft preserves seeing without reflective classification while withholding its stipulated term experience; this does not require seeing to be unprocessed sensory material. Its Section 7 also argues that presence is no further ingredient beyond construction, and Section 8 explicitly declines an account that defines experience functionally while leaving presence as an additional open fact. That is a substantive proposal about constitution. It need not conflict with attributing a qualitative mode to an organised activity: denying a separate spectator or added substance does not deny the activity’s character. But an identification of actual appearance with retentive interpretation needs more than a decision about words. The present warrants do not establish that identification for every interpreting process, and they do not independently refute the companion proposal. The unresolved issue is the reach of that identification, rather than whether personal narrative is required for every appearance.

There is nevertheless a precise agreement about timing. Recollective uptake can occur within a continuing process over a short interval; the antecedent need not have finished before its recent history is interpreted (Rodgers, 2026d, §5). A descriptor retained earlier may first contribute to a relational presentation at current uptake, $J$. Earlier storage does not establish earlier appearance, and a present scene can concern an earlier occurrence. Neither observation proves that all appearance is recollective. The companion draft’s Section 10 expressly recognises that its universal recollective thesis goes beyond the relational-awareness result. The local current-act argument accordingly remains distinct from that wider thesis.

The limiting case that removes all acquired scaffolding and the ability to form memory is a stronger stipulation than the absence of naming, commentary or personal recognition. It should not be recast as an ordinary recognised scene with an omitted report. The October draft’s positive recollective model instead requires minimal retention, without requiring language or autobiographical memory. These are different conditions. If later interpretation varies with a particular antecedent, the dependence and its historical reference still need an account; the comparison supplies no retained-trace mechanism for the stronger limiting case. [Supplement Module B](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-b) develops this distinction and the conditional limits of inference without retention.

Descriptions can point to an occurrence without reproducing it. That limitation motivates restraint about an exhaustive description of fundamental awareness; it does not establish a mathematical incompleteness theorem or make every local claim inaccessible to evidence. Whether a relation appears, when it appears, how it informs an activity and where its mode inheres remain particular questions with particular warrants.

### 2.5. Why earlier articulation proposals were reasonable and insufficient {#section-2-5}

The first constitutive proposal considered here is a native articulation principle: elementary native physical performances are candidates for elementary presentations. Its motivation is immanence and structural fidelity. A phenomenal fusion across independently transforming systems, or a phenomenal division within one native performance, appears to require additional lawlike structure. This is a reasoned proposal rather than an arbitrary label.

Its vulnerability is that physical irreducibility does not alone individuate the experiential event. One interaction can contain several carriers or roles; a carrier-centred account need not treat the interaction’s native prime as one field. The argument therefore needs an independently defended event-centred criterion and an appropriate duration. Endpoint factorisation does not supply these: a swap can be implemented by an interacting sequence even if its endpoint admits a simple factorisation. Native physical organisation constrains a proposed realisation law without automatically fixing its phenomenal extension.

A second proposal, relational manifestation, reasons that intrinsic relations among qualitative terms should have a manifest aspect rather than remain wholly unmanifest glue. It has an intelligible ontological motivation. A rival can nevertheless admit qualitative intrinsic terms and real relations among them without one common presentation. Realising contributor supports also need not be phenomenal argument tokens: a compressed parity value can have a presented character without two identifiable original counterparts. The mathematical family of common contributors must therefore be distinguished from the family of actual co-presented occurrences.

A third proposal appeals to intrinsic categorical grounds of physical powers. Qualitative actuality may be an attractive candidate for such grounds, and plural grounds may jointly support a whole power. Neither point by itself establishes that those grounds appear in one field or inhere in the nominated physical performance. Pairwise grounded relations need not have a single common occurrence witness. A reciprocal additional field would make different physical predictions, but the programme contains no confirmation of that field or its coefficients.

The present argument retains these motivations while pursuing a narrower route. Credible appearance/activity evidence supports a projection of a recurring sensory capacity; a corrected concrete-performance principle then addresses the bearer of the resulting literal mode. No universal prime-to-presentation law, relational manifestation law or additional field is assumed. Supplement [Modules B](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-b) and [H](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-h) preserve the earlier arguments, formal distinctions and counterexamples.

### 2.6. Continuity has more than one object {#section-2-6}

The current appearance token, the organised performance, the living animal, the person and an experiential subject episode have different continuity conditions. In the monograph’s A3 proposal, a genuine interruption of qualification can end the numerical experiential subject episode while biological and personal continuity remain (Rodgers, 2026e). A strict identification of the experiencer with the continuing animal would carry different diachronic implications (Olson, 2003). Calling an episodic subject a role of the animal is weaker than strict identity and must be stated accordingly.

The current-act thesis does not silently settle this disagreement. Its bearer claim concerns the present organised performance and its qualitative phase. It neither infers a migrating subject from transfer of a relational distinction nor requires the original subjects of separate inputs to fuse. This allows a new current presentation to be constitutionally significant without becoming a universal theory of persistence.

The current-act argument does not assume the full A1–A3 constitution. A connection to the Shadow Psychophysical Constitution, SPC-2, would require a further application of its existing laws (Rodgers, 2026e, ch. 17). A1 admits a perspective through a physically justified qualifying core; A2 assigns its complete endogenous predictive object over all physically admitted finite continuations as phenomenal organisation; A3 specifies nonbranching process continuation. An appropriate phase of the present performance would have to be located within such a core, with $Q$ identified as an aspect of its current scene. Contributors to a performance do not thereby define its subject boundary, and causal involvement does not automatically make a worldly source or instrument part of the subject. Given a justified realisation and the accepted laws, SPC-2 assigns phenomenal states and their timing at the declared native resolution. This paper does not complete that application, validate A2 or select first phenomenal onset. Its modest current-appearance conclusion remains compatible with first presentation at uptake, $J$.

### 2.7. The existing scholarly baseline {#section-2-7}

Whole-process constitution and biological coupling are not new general ideas. The monograph already treats thought and noticing as aspects of a specified whole process and proposes a biologically realised coupling to a current scene (Rodgers, 2026e, ch. 2). Paper 2 distinguishes silent identity declarations from empirically constrained identification (Rodgers, 2026f). The development paper already articulates third structure, scaffold feedback and conditional conscious constitution, while requiring independent appearance-facing support (Rodgers, 2026c). Outside this programme, process identity and event individuation have long-standing philosophical defences (Place, 1956; Davidson, 1969); Adlam proposes that conscious agency may inhere in holistic input-to-decision relations while leaving the qualifying relations open (Adlam, 2026).

The contribution claimed here is more specific: the conjunction of a physically delimited native capacity projection, corrected role-sensitive performance reidentification, literal current-mode placement and an actual later-token application, with independently articulated counterexamples. This adds warrant and disciplined application to an earlier proposal. It does not assert priority for holistic constitution, event identity or relational scaffolding.

## 3. What mathematics and sensory evidence establish {#section-3}

### 3.1. What the formal programme contributes {#section-3-1}

The mathematical results specify what a source, access procedure or physical organisation can support under a declared contract. They are useful precisely because the contract can fail. A report law need not be an adequate description of its source; local marginals need not fix joint structure; a model of current access need not preserve all native interventions. These distinctions already constrain operational identification in the programme (Rodgers, 2026f,g,h). The new constructions in the supplement supply further exact examples and refinements.

The common-finalisation problem illustrates the interpretive boundary. Let $b$ range over future branches, $H$ be the retained causal-prefix history, and $C$ a nominated final content. Suppose the finite branch laws $P_b(h,c)$ have common prefix marginal $\mu(h)$. In an abstract class allowing residual future completions to be designed freely, the largest mass admitting one all-branch common history/content submeasure is

<span id="equation-1"></span>

$$
\alpha=\sum_{h,c}\min_b P_b(h,c). \tag{1}
$$

This is known maximal-common-mass mathematics (Makur and Singh, 2024, Theorem 2), applied here to causal-prefix finalisation. Three branch laws can each have pairwise overlap $1/2$ while their global overlap is zero. Pairwise compatibility is then insufficient.

A fixed passive apparatus presents a different question. Let $Q_b(c\mid h)$ be its future-content kernels, and let an early marker use only $H$ and a private seed conditionally independent of future innovations, without influencing the apparatus. Zero-error irrevocable marking requires one value certain under every branch at each marked history. Its maximal marked mass is

<span id="equation-2"></span>

$$
\beta=\sum_h\mu(h)\,\mathbf{1}\{\exists c\;\forall b:\ Q_b(c\mid h)=1\}. \tag{2}
$$

A fresh fair future coin at the only prefix history gives $\alpha=1$ but $\beta=0$. An engineered common coupling and an uninfluential prefix marker of a fixed future are not the same architecture. [Appendix B](#appendix-b) proves these claims; [Supplement Module C](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-c) distinguishes per-marker approximate guarantees from aggregate branch-error budgets.

These are constraints on immutable final content, not clocks measuring appearance. A provisional early quality may occur and later change. A later current appearance can also be generated from a retained summary. Transport of the same completed episodes under genuinely exogenous selection preserves an already warranted target; it cannot create an earlier anchor or a bearer. The formal results prevent an illicit timing inference while leaving the positive current target available.

Reconstruction bounds similarly require complete information contracts. Let $X$ be $n$ independent fair relation bits, $W$ the complete retained state and $R$ an included seed independent of $X$. The query index $J$ must be uniform and independent of the whole $(X,W,R)$, and chosen after storage. Let $e\in[0,1]$ be the implemented reader’s actual average bit error, and let $h_2$ denote binary entropy. Then

<span id="equation-3"></span>

$$
n[1-h_2(e)]\le I(X;W,R). \tag{3}
$$

This statement uses actual error even when it exceeds $1/2$. An upper error budget must instead be clipped at $1/2$, as proved in [Supplement Section 3.5](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-c#section-3-5). All bypass routes must be included in $W$. If the query is known before storage, one bit may suffice; if analogue states or hidden archives remain, an assumed finite codebook may be inappropriate. The exact finite-codebook risks and contour counts in the supplement are model results, not estimates of human memory. No independently warranted human ceiling in the present record excludes the strong bounded-summary rival.

Native causal models constrain a proposed mechanism in another way. A noisy four-unit parity system, at a fixed synchronous binary grain, permits exact complex selection and full relation unfolding under IIT 4.0 definitions (Albantakis et al., 2023). The constructed family has five distinctions and 31 positive relations over its stated parameter range, but symmetry obstructs a privileged line or planar four-point geometry under an intrinsic equivariant metric. These findings establish causal structure and the consequences of a proposed content law, rather than actual qualitative content. The explanatory identity proposed by IIT’s intrinsic-structure programme remains a theoretical commitment (Grasso et al., 2026). [Supplement Module E](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-e) retains both a failed majority construction and the corrected parity family.

A further result asks how much additional distinction a specific anchored content requires, rather than demanding full reconstruction. Let a finite group $G$ act on source descriptions and target contents. Fix a description $x$ and an independently warranted content $c$. Write $H=\operatorname{Stab}_G(x)$ and $G_c=\operatorname{Stab}_G(c)$ for the transformations leaving each unchanged. A deterministic equivariant assignment on the orbit of $x$, anchored at $c$, exists exactly when $H\subseteq G_c$. If the source description leaves transformations unresolved that change $c$, an added equivariant signature over $x$ needs at least

<span id="equation-4"></span>

$$
[H:H\cap G_c] \tag{4}
$$

distinct values; a coset construction attains the bound. [Supplement Module I](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-i) supplies the extended-source construction, proof and finite checks. For a coarser target, its own stabilizer may permit a smaller signature. This identifies a target-specific informational burden under the assumed covariance, rather than the number of neurons, fields or subjects required. A passive redescription of one anchored token does not justify active replacement by another system. In particular, the inherited acquaintance condition cannot be strengthened into a cross-token appearance law through this theorem.

Likewise, distinct physical networks can share a complete relative stochastic law under additive drives while native holds distinguish their mechanisms. [Module F](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-f) proves the dynamic result in addition to the established static resistance machinery (Dörfler and Bullo, 2013). Mechanism discrimination does not automatically identify which mechanism bears an appearance. This is why the following sensory and philosophical warrants cannot be replaced by a successful causal score.

### 3.2. Appearance-directed evidence and its interpretation {#section-3-2}

Ordinary sensory testimony is evidence that something appears, although it can be mistaken about the external stimulus, the experienced relation, or how an answer was reached. Refusing all such evidence until a neural detector is independently established would exclude the appearance-facing anchors needed to assess a detector. Accepting every response as a direct jointness label would make the opposite mistake. The relevant assessment asks what a person was instructed to compare, what sources and actions were involved, and whether the response is credibly an expression of current sensory activity.

Direct cortical stimulation contributes useful causal evidence. Local perturbation can alter facial appearance, and spatial stimulation patterns can occasion drawn forms or multiple phosphenes (Parvizi et al., 2012; Beauchamp et al., 2020; Bosking et al., 2022). These effects are harder to understand as arbitrary names assigned to unchanged sensory content, especially when initial drawings precede learned naming. They nevertheless leave open whether stimulated activity supplies a downstream current constructor. Reported multiplicity also varies: two stimulation sites do not uniformly yield two apparent spots.

Where credible testimony concerns a resolved extended form, its presented aspects can already support the minimal relational target. This is a positive phenomenal interpretation, not an inference from electrode separation or a drawing’s geometry alone. A newly formed current field can supply it. [Supplement Module J](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-j) applies this distinction to selected visual and dream findings, retaining each source’s timing, reporting and basing limits rather than treating every induced or tracked response as a direct jointness measure.

Other sources sharpen the same distinction. Report-independent accumulation establishes processing under differing report demands, but passive stimulus-intensity labels do not themselves label current seeing or jointness (Stockart et al., 2025). Grouping and feature integration can dissociate without deciding whether distinct objects share a scene (Vogelsang et al., 2024). A global measurement diagram requires compatible token and context indices before its local descriptions can be interpreted as one actual presentation (Lee-Youngzie et al., 2026). None is merely negative evidence. Each identifies which independent phenomenon or mechanism was established and which inference still requires a premise.

### 3.3. A physically delimited recurring exercise {#section-3-3}

Let $\mathcal{V}$ denote the narrow native-human visual regime used in the application below. Its components are specified without asking whether the present target appears:

1. A materially continuing adult participant with actual biological optical acquisition and native visual/control organisation, in the same experimental preparation.

2. The independently specified physical source pair, annulus contexts, optical geometry, illumination and acquisition arrangement.

3. A previously installed comparison instruction and response contingency, with current source-sensitive transformations entering the acquisition/control process.

4. The material and procedural session history, current acquisition episode and uptake interval, with relevant background alterations accounted for.

This is a macro physical nomination, not a measured complete neural state. Actual physiology and sampling participation are partly ordinary task/biological inferences. The record does not include EEG staging, so “confirmed awake consciousness” is not a measured admission field. Current $Q$, successful acquaintance and answer correctness are excluded from admission.

The initial verbal looked-equal staircase varied the real disc-size difference under contrasting annulus contexts to estimate each participant’s point of subjective equality. Later variation in real disc diameters and annulus arrangements, together with instructions to judge how the discs look, supports a recurring sensory comparison sensitive to context, rather than a context-free classification of physical diameter. Interpreting the apparent counterparts as jointly presented, and the comparison as based on that presentation, additionally relies on the ordinary current-scene interpretation. Neither jointness nor sensory basing was independently measured by a dedicated probe in this archive. The staircase and task history therefore support the modest capacity inference; they do not constitute a prior jointness or basing calibration ([Supplement §§ 7.2–7.4](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-g#section-7-2)).

This appearance-directed evidence supports a recurring capacity whose projected target is current relational $Q$ as the sensory way of source-directed comparative activity. Material continuity and relevant source/context variation make continued exercise of that capacity a reasonable causal expectation. The projected target is what the independently specified physical class is expected to occasion in a new exercise; it does not define class membership. The strongest projection is within the same participant and preparation. Transferring to an altered supplier or an unanchored machine incurs additional obligations.

This induction uses no numerical reliability estimate. Its content is a defeasible default: an episode in $\mathcal{V}$ is expected to occasion source-dependent apparent counterparts and a presented relation during some current inspection/comparison phase, by qualifying uptake $J$. It neither predicts the exact same/different content nor assumes appearance from stimulus onset. A credible eligible field-free case must count against the projection. One cannot redefine the class as “trials with conscious capacity” merely because a prediction failed.

The projection is therefore directional without being prospective validation. It permits physical nomination before present appearance evidence is interpreted. Its actual application in Section 5 shows that this separation can be maintained. The bearer question remains separate: a capacity for $Q$ does not by itself identify its instantiator.

## 4. The appearance as a mode of the current act {#section-4}

### 4.1. The two references and the substantive inference {#section-4-1}

Let $P$ name the independently nominated physical engagement at the relevant activity/phase grain. Let $A$ name the actual source-directed comparative act as independently supported by appearance-directed evidence, and let $Q$ name its qualitative way. These references are not defined as equal. $P$ can be nominated by material acquisition, transformations, control history and uptake even if the phenomenal claim is disputed. $A$ is identified through the person’s current comparative activity, whose sensory way includes the relational presentation. The conclusion to defend is that these references converge on one concrete performance.

Three distinct relationships must be kept apart. A cause can occasion a performance without being that performance. A containing system can include a performer without itself performing the attributed act at the relevant grain. A constituent can help constitute an act while remaining numerically distinct from it. Only justified reidentification, $A=P$, licenses substitution of the literal mode:

<span id="equation-5"></span>

$$
\operatorname{Mode}(Q,A,\tau),\qquad A=P\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\operatorname{Mode}(Q,P,\tau). \tag{5}
$$

where $\tau$ denotes the qualifying current phase, not the whole interval between stimulus and keypress. [Equation (5)](#equation-5) is elementary identity logic. The philosophical work lies in warranting its premises, especially $A=P$.

Neither physical closure nor mental efficacy supplies that conclusion alone. A physical member $R$ may produce an answer while a qualitative constituent $Q$ genuinely grounds a composite reason $K^*$. Efficacy inherited through $R$ does not make every member of $K^*$ physical, and genuine grounding need not be an independently manipulable causal arrow. Conversely, irreducible qualitative properties do not force a new concrete bearer whenever the property vocabulary changes. These are the two shortcuts the argument must avoid.

### 4.2. A concrete-performance/mode principle {#section-4-2}

<span id="principle-4-1"></span>

**Principle 4.1 (Conservative mode placement).** When a literal mode of an actual activity is independently grounded, presumptively attribute that mode to the activity’s independently and positively tracked concrete performance, at a fixed activity/phase grain and with its actual executing contributors included. Revise the attribution when evidence or a separately defended instantiation account supports a different bearer, an omitted constituent, a merely supplied ground or a different activity. A new property type or descriptive vocabulary alone is not evidence of a numerically different instantiator.

This is an openly contestable constitutive principle. It does not say that every mode is physical or that every activity has a phenomenal mode. The concrete performance nominated here is physical because its acquisition/control execution is independently tracked materially. The principle supplies a defeasible preference for treating its independently supported qualitative way as a mode of that execution. It permits fundamental qualitative properties without deriving them from the current physical description.

Its admission conditions are substantive. There must be credible evidence of a literal mode of this activity; the nominee must be tracked through actual source use and participation, rather than selected by matching output; and known executor, phase and indicator counterexamples must be respected. The absence of an identified extra executor checks for a defeater; it is not the positive ground for attribution. The conclusion remains weaker where activity reference is supported only by one actor, a common time or an answer code.

The principle is not “if $Q$ occurs, assign $Q$ to whichever stage is preferred”. It constrains which nomination is eligible for attribution. It can favour current judgment rather than early integration, demand inclusion of a remote physical executor, or require separating sensory participation from an automated answer. Those consequences occur independently of the motivating phenomenal assignment.

### 4.3. Independent motivation: continuity of the tracked instance {#section-4-3}

The general rationale is explanatory continuity across ways of describing a concrete occurrence. A particular act can be tracked materially, intentionally, skilfully and semantically. The fact that those descriptions have different explanatory roles does not itself multiply the acts. A handwritten inscription may be described through contact and strokes, through a skill being exercised, and through its legibility or meaning. The realisation of a new property can require a richer state description or a new constitutive rule without requiring another performance elsewhere.

This rationale belongs to a tradition distinguishing token identity from simple correlation (Place, 1956; Davidson, 1969). It does not commit the present argument to reduction of qualitative property types or to a complete causal-profile criterion for events. Such a profile has not been measured for $A$ and $P$. There is a positively tracked candidate for the particular execution and independently anchored evidence of the qualitative way of the comparative activity. Convergence on the nominated physical execution is the defended inference. Maintaining one instantiator preserves the supported reference and participation facts unless there is reason to divide them.

Three cases show why correlation is insufficient. A thermometer reading can track a body’s temperature without being that body’s thermal state. A copied coordinate can match a distributed body’s centre of mass without becoming its collective configuration. A copied inscription can reproduce the word without repeating the original stroke-guided activity. The physical quantity cases have stronger constitutive specifications than the proposed placement of $Q$, so their rules cannot simply be transferred. They support a more general discipline: distinguish a tracked property-bearing performance from its indicator, description and product, while allowing justified macro attribution before an exhaustive microphysical reduction is available.

The principle’s extension to experience is accordingly a defended methodological and metaphysical step. An irreducible property may be a literal way the tracked performance occurs. Write $B$ for a genuinely exclusive qualitative bearer outside the nominated performance. This notation is distinct from the programme’s $U$ for the unsplit ground. To locate the qualitative mode in $B$ requires an instantiation account explaining why that qualitative reality belongs outside the performance, rather than another description or mode of it. The immanent account has its own debt: it must justify the nominated activity, phase and contributors, and must not assume a universal physical-to-quality map. Neither side escapes justification by redescribing the other as expensive.

### 4.4. Physicalism, intrinsic nature and the exclusive-bearer challenge {#section-4-4}

The proposed attribution belongs to a family of views on which one concrete event can be described physically and mentally without those descriptions being interchangeable. It is congenial to nonreductive physicalism: mental occurrences are physical occurrences, while their mental description need not reduce to a physical property type. Davidson’s anomalous monism provides a familiar example of the distinction between event identity and reduction (Davidson, 2001). The present argument does not adopt his full theory or infer its phenomenal claim from intentional rationality. It defends a local identity through the positively tracked performance and its independently supported way of occurring.

Compatible Russellian approaches instead emphasize an intrinsic nature not exhausted by the structural and dispositional descriptions used in physics (Alter and Nagasawa, 2012). If the qualitative nature is a mode of this very performance, that proposal can agree with the current bearer attribution without reducing quality to a structural description. Russellian views are diverse, however, and the proposal that qualitative grounds support physical powers does not by itself establish one joint presentation or identify its bearer. The relation between such grounds and mental causation also remains disputed (Baysan, 2025). No member of this family is a prerequisite for assessing the two warrants.

The decisive vulnerability is a genuinely exclusive bearer that grants the same current appearance, acquaintance, activity and complete physical history. That account can agree with every empirical likelihood while denying that the qualitative mode inheres in $P$. A mathematical description of the shared history cannot decide the disagreement. The positive case for $A=P$ rests on conservative performance reidentification; its rival must defend the contrary instantiation account. The principle therefore supplies a reasoned preference, not a result forced by the supporting theorems. Section 6 develops the exclusive account without treating it as an idle field or an extra complete comparator.

### 4.5. Counterexamples that correct the principle {#section-4-5}

Consider a controller receiving a multiplication result from a remote executor. The controller owns the task, receives the same source and emits the answer. It nevertheless does not perform the entire multiplication locally. Attribution must include the executing contributor or be narrowed to receipt and verification. A prosthesis or instrument may similarly execute part of a movement or assessment. Physical proximity to the person’s answer is not a boundary criterion.

Next consider a person simultaneously speaking and writing a word. Actor, purpose, content and time overlap, but two distinct executions are involved. A common owner does not identify their modes. Or consider an automated answer path running while the person looks at the same pair. A relational field can occur in the person without being the sensory way of the answer-producing operation. Widening $P$ to every process in the time window merely to capture that field converts performance attribution into containment.

Finally, copying a completed answer preserves a product, not its source-guided formation. A reader of the copy can perform a new act with a new relational presentation, but the old bearer’s identity does not migrate with the data. Adding inert matter or concatenating successive tasks likewise does not enlarge one literal mode simply by expanding a box. Phase individuation matters: a current qualitative way is not identified with the entire multi-phase response episode.

These examples support a corrected rule. Track actual performing roles and their current participation; include contributors shown to execute the relevant act; distinguish suppliers, verifiers, indicators and products; and specify the qualitative phase. The rule can be defeated by independently demonstrated omitted execution. It does not protect an internal-only boundary or forbid world-involving constitution.

### 4.6. Application to native comparison {#section-4-6}

In an ordinary appearance-directed comparison, current source use, task contingency, content-sensitive history, sensory inspection and operative uptake can support convergence between $A$ and $P$. The qualitative scene is treated as the way the comparison is undergone, not merely a true fact about the answer. The physical nomination follows the native acquisition/comparison engagement, not the output gate. The corrected rule thereby has a positive candidate and a literal mode to place.

The activity tether is indispensable. The documented looked-equal staircase and appearance-directed task history support the ordinary sensory-comparison interpretation; they are not dedicated basing measurements. Evidence of $Q$’s presence somewhere near an answer would not earn its projection as the sensory way of that comparison. The supported target must include actual source-directed use or ordinary basing. That conjunction can be projected to a new exercise without requiring current $Q$ as a class-membership gate. The evidence is not statistically independent: source history and task context contribute to several assessments. The logical roles of the premises nevertheless differ, and none is justified by its desired conclusion.

This basing judgment need not already identify a physical content vehicle as $Q$’s bearer. Genuine acquaintance with a current apparent relation can guide an agent’s act through a derivative physical reason vehicle. A genuinely exclusive qualitative bearer could permit that same source-guided activity. Identifying the experience-inclusive activity $A$ with physical $P$ remains the further constitutive step. [Supplement Module I](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-i) makes this separation explicit and distinguishes an operative current basis from faithful transmission of an earlier source.

A cached answer merely triggered by acquaintance can bypass the apparent relation’s content. A faithful message can instead lead to a fresh current field that alone supplies the comparison’s sensory basis. Both cases matter: semantic agreement or a measurable effect of a physical channel does not identify the current qualitative source. Conversely, if current appearance-directed comparison is independently warranted, gating its later expression tests that expression rather than discovering its earlier basing. These distinctions preserve the ordinary positive inference while preventing it from borrowing an unearned physical-constitution premise.

Together these grounds support an actual bearer attribution: continuity of the independently tracked performance is the preferred interpretation. That preference has no likelihood advantage over a hybrid that grants the same appearances and physical process. The inference would weaken if an unsuspected strategy change removed jointness, if the field merely accompanied an automated response, or if a general instantiation theory independently supported an exclusive bearer. These objections target different steps, developed in Section 6.

### Figure 1. How the two warrants connect {#figure-1}

1. **Nominate the physical activity.** A physically nominated native performance $P\in\mathcal{V}$ is supported by sensory task calibration and continuing material organisation.
2. **Project the sensory capacity.** Current relational $Q$ is a way of source-directed activity $A$, supported by ordinary jointness and activity-reference evidence.
3. **Apply the corrected concrete-performance principle.** The references are defeasibly reidentified, $A=P$, at the qualifying phase $\tau$.
4. **Substitute identicals.** The literal mode is attributed as $\operatorname{Mode}(Q,P,\tau)$.

Physical nomination does not include current $Q$. Appearance/activity projection and performance reidentification are defeasible; only the final mode substitution is deductive. No statistical independence of the supporting evidence is assumed.

The strongest appearance claim is not that equal physical diameters have become unequal. It concerns source-relevant apparent magnitudes and their presented spatial/comparative relation. Under that interpretation, a qualifying current presentation occurs by the response. The 2,645 ms value is a physical illumination-to-keypress interval, not a direct phenomenal timestamp. It does not transfer the mode to the key register, an upstream supplier, an anatomical union or the entire multi-second episode. This is the proposed combination of physically delimited occurrence projection and separately defended mode-bearing attribution. The mathematics does not establish the identity premise. The same physical/report process can support a competing exclusive-bearer account, considered next.

### 5.4. What could challenge the inference {#section-5-4}

The rule expects current relational appearance in further materially continuing exercises of $\mathcal{V}$, without using their present appearance verdict as admission. Source/context variation can change the contents while preserving the narrow activity kind. Motor-code remapping can preserve the sensory comparison while changing the reported bit; copying or replacing the bit cannot itself establish that comparison. A documented optical interruption, source substitution or changed task program can alter the physical regime, whereas a current wrong answer alone cannot retroactively do so.

The empirical claims can be challenged separately. Credible absence of the projected appearance in an independently maintained eligible exercise would count against occurrence projection; a missed response alone would not establish that absence. Evidence that the participant used only serial appearances and descriptors, with no current qualitative pair even at uptake, would undermine jointness. Serial eye movements alone would not suffice, since a new pair-scene could first appear at $J$. Evidence of a cached or automatic answer route, independently bypassing the relation’s current sensory use while the field merely accompanied the answer, would challenge basing. The present archive does not supply these stronger observations or a calibrated rate for any of them. An unsuccessful eligible case cannot be dismissed by defining a new subtype as the successful trials; its physical boundary would need independent support.

These possible findings concern the occurrence and role of appearance. They would change premises to which the constitutive principle is applied. An exclusive-bearer account that grants the same appearances, basing and physical records instead contests the placement principle itself. More accurate prediction of those shared records would not choose between the accounts. The illustration therefore contributes a concrete, criticisable application of the argument while leaving prospective validation and the constitutive disagreement distinct.

## 6. Three serious alternatives and their different force {#section-6}

### 6.1. Serial appearances without a joint scene {#section-6-1}

The serial-only account grants sensory appearances, physical source sensitivity and competent comparison. One magnitude appears, its descriptor is retained, another appears, and a relation proposition is inferred. The account can reproduce the entire behavioural record without ever presenting the two counterparts together. It challenges $Q$’s jointness rather than the physical nomination or source dependence.

The response is evidential, not a theorem that comparison entails a field. Ordinary current scene acquaintance and the appearance-directed protocol give reason to interpret the activity as a joint presentation. That interpretation is not defeated merely because the eyes inspect regions successively: successive acquisition can produce a currently joint scene. But evidence that a person used serial-only imagery or an inference-only strategy would directly weaken the target claim. A later genuinely joint $J$ presentation would satisfy the target; a word expressing the inferred relation would not by itself do so.

This is the most important limitation of the archive’s phenomenal evidence. There is no dedicated jointness or actual-basing probe. It remains legitimate to use ordinary appearance evidence at a modest level, while admitting that a stronger validation study would need an independent measure and eligible negative cases. The physical response does not repair a suspended jointness interpretation.

### 6.2. A field accompanying an automated answer {#section-6-2}

The passive-accompaniment account grants actual joint $Q$ in the participant. A parallel path, however, computes the answer from physical/context classes independently of the experienced relation. The participant can look at the pair while using a learned strategy. Thus $Q$, the source pair, owner, phase and matching answer can all coexist without $Q$ being the sensory mode of the chosen answer-producing operation.

This attacks activity tether and performance individuation. It is not a denial of appearance and not yet a different bearer ontology. Common actor or temporal overlap cannot settle it. The appropriate correction is to distinguish the sensory engagement from the automated response performance, or to narrow the nominated activity to the actual sensory/comparative execution. Enlarging a task-window box until it contains both would evade rather than solve the issue.

The positive case uses ordinary source-directed sensory basing. The appearance-directed instructions, initial looked-equal staircase, fresh contextual inputs and uptake support the interpretation that the current comparison is performed through the apparent relation. These grounds are fallible, and the data do not separately rule out unnoticed automatisation. The projected target is $Q$ as a sensory way of comparative activity; a matching correct answer alone does not supply that tether. A discovered bypass changes attribution even if appearance survives.

### 6.3. A genuinely exclusive experiential bearer {#section-6-3}

The strongest hybrid grants one agent, actual jointness, genuine qualitative grounding, the activity tether and the complete physical/report process. Physical member $R$ can be operative in a mixed act $K^*$ while qualitative $B$ is its literal experiential member. $B$ need not perform another entire comparison, add a physical force or require a trial-specific manifestation law. Describing this account as a second comparator or an idle epiphenomenon would miss its strongest form.

The disagreement concerns instantiation. If $B$ names a qualitative trope of $P$, the account is compatible with the proposed bearer claim. If it names a mode of the physical animal at a different grain, its grain requires justification. If it is genuinely exclusive of the nominated performance, it challenges conservative mode placement even when the other four claims survive.

The present argument prefers continuity of the positively tracked performance and its literal way of occurring. Grounding by $Q$ does not itself require distinct instantiation outside $P$; the same grounding role can be attributed to a mode of $P$. A different property type does not positively locate another bearer. However, an independently defended ontology of phenomenal instantiation could justify the exclusive account. So could evidence of an omitted current sensory executor or a distinct continuity pattern that the physical nomination overlooks.

Both positions must specify how their bearer is tracked, how it participates in the activity, and what replacements, divisions or extensions would defeat the attribution. The preference here rests on a general constitutive default and actual reference convergence. It claims neither an ontology-neutral theorem nor a statistical win. The hybrid’s coherence limits certainty without making every defended attribution arbitrary; mere failure to detect $B$ would not positively justify identity.

### 6.4. Other correction cases {#section-6-4}

Hidden state or strategy changes can undermine the native-capacity projection while leaving metadata and outputs similar. Eligible contrary appearances must be retained; an inferred “conscious-capacity” gate cannot remove them. A downstream physical constructor may generate the new $J$ third and belong to the inclusive current performance; this supports no claim that an earlier supplier alone is sufficient. Independently performing remote, prosthetic or world contributors can require enlarging or revising the nomination. Internal anatomical closure is not a premise.

Finally, an incorrect answer can coexist with a joint field. It may indicate wrong content classification, task misunderstanding, motor error or altered basing. It is not automatic absence of $Q$. Likewise, occurrence does not make every phenomenal judgment knowledge. These correction cases preserve the differences between target actuality, response accuracy, competent expression and constitution.

## 7. A positive connection with an inspectable warrant {#section-7}

The result is a positive argument for current relational appearance inclusively borne by a physically organised engagement in the stated native-human regime. The occurrence side uses a projectible sensory capacity whose physical admission conditions exclude present $Q$. The constitutive side uses an independently motivated and corrected concrete-performance/mode principle. A worked retrospective illustration exhibits how the two can be combined without treating an answer bit, a conditional bound or a causal score as the missing phenomenal premise.

Separating the warrants makes the connection assessable. The reader can accept the physical nomination while disputing jointness, accept joint $Q$ while disputing its activity tether, or accept the tether while disputing bearer reidentification. Each objection calls for different evidence or principles. The conclusion inherits the fallibility of those premises while continuing to concern actual appearance, not merely computational compatibility.

The existing monograph and development paper already propose whole-process constitution and native biological coupling, and develop source/readout distinctions and scaffold feedback (Rodgers, 2026e,c). ### 7.3. The current-act connection {#section-7-3}

Within the specified materially continuing native-human comparison regime, current qualitative counterparts and their presented relation have defensible empirical and phenomenological support. A corrected concrete-performance/mode principle supports attributing that qualitative way to the organised physical engagement at its qualifying phase. Delayed construction at current uptake is compatible with this connection; serial-only presentation, passive accompaniment and exclusive instantiation remain distinct possible defeats of its premises. These warrants advance the connecting obligation through an actual, scoped inference. Sensory projectibility and concrete-performance reasoning supply its force. They also preserve its limits: a timing bound does not identify onset, a physical supplier need not be the bearer, and the constitutive attribution remains open to independent criticism.

## Appendix A. Identity, mode and the scope of substitution {#appendix-a}

For the main argument, $P$ and $A$ denote concrete performances at a fixed activity grain; $\tau$ denotes the relevant qualitative phase. $\operatorname{Mode}(Q,x,\tau)$ is a literal predication about how $x$ occurs at that phase. It is not shorthand for “an output of $x$ describes $Q$” or “$Q$ occurs somewhere inside a region containing $x$”.

### Proposition A.1. Mode preservation under reidentification {#proposition-a-1}

In ordinary identity logic, $A=P$ and $\operatorname{Mode}(Q,A,\tau)$ entail $\operatorname{Mode}(Q,P,\tau)$. Neither $\operatorname{Causes}(P,A)$, $\operatorname{Contains}(P,A)$ nor $\operatorname{Constitutes}(P,A)$ alone supplies that entailment.

**Proof.** The positive conclusion is substitution of identicals in the predicate’s bearer position. For each claimed replacement relation, take two distinct objects $a,p$, interpret that relation as true of $(p,a)$, and make $\operatorname{Mode}(Q,a,\tau)$ true but $\operatorname{Mode}(Q,p,\tau)$ false. These are countermodels unless an additional mode-preservation axiom is imposed on the relation. An imposed axiom is a further constitutive commitment, not identity logic. $\square$

The proposition does not establish $A=P$. That premise is the defeasible conclusion of the corrected concrete-performance principle and actual participation/reference assessment in Section 4. It transfers neither $Q$ to every part nor $Q$ to every containing system. If different temporal grains are used, a warranted correspondence between the qualifying phases is also required; the whole response interval is not silently substituted for $\tau$.

## Appendix B. Common finalisation and passive fixed-apparatus marking {#appendix-b}

All sets in this appendix are finite. Let $\mathcal{B}$ be a nonempty set of future branches and $P_b$ probability laws on $H\times C$ with common $H$-marginal $\mu$. Let a common submeasure $r(h,c)$ satisfy $0\le r(h,c)\le P_b(h,c)$ for every $b$. Its mass is $\lVert r\rVert_1$.

### Theorem B.1. Abstract common-finalisation mass {#theorem-b-1}

The largest common-submeasure mass is [Equation (1)](#equation-1). It is attained by $r(h,c)=\min_b P_b(h,c)$. In a class freely designing branch-specific residual completions behind one common prefix, this mass is attainable as an all-branch early finalisation probability.

**Proof.** Every feasible $r$ is pointwise bounded by the minimum, proving the upper bound; the pointwise minimum is itself feasible. Write $a(h)=\sum_c r(h,c)\le\mu(h)$. At prefix $h$ of positive mass, use a common flag with probability $a(h)/\mu(h)$, and on flagged histories draw the common content with law $r(h,\cdot)/a(h)$. In branch $b$, draw residual content on unflagged histories with conditional mass proportional to $P_b(h,c)-r(h,c)$. Each branch has the required joint law. Zero-mass histories and cases $a(h)=0$ or $a(h)=\mu(h)$ are handled by omitting the absent component. The construction designs future completion kernels; it does not establish attainment in an independently fixed apparatus. $\square$

The common-mass identity is a finite specialisation of established maximal all-equal coupling (Makur and Singh, 2024). If $H$ is a singleton and the content laws are uniform on $\{0,1\}$, $\{1,2\}$ and $\{2,0\}$ respectively, every pair has overlap $1/2$ but no atom belongs to all three supports. Hence $\alpha=0$ despite pairwise compatibility.

Now hold the future mechanism fixed. Its branch kernels are $Q_b(c\mid h)$. An early marker chooses a flag $F$ and marked value $V$ using only $(H,R)$, where the private seed $R$ is conditionally independent of future content for every branch given $H$. The marker does not influence the future mechanism. Thus

<span id="equation-6"></span>

$$
C_b\perp(F,V)\mid H,\qquad \operatorname{Law}(C_b\mid H=h)=Q_b(\cdot\mid h). \tag{6}
$$

The requirement is $\mathbb{P}_b(F=1,V\ne C_b)=0$ for every $b$.

### Theorem B.2. Passive zero-error maximum {#theorem-b-2}

Under [Equation (6)](#equation-6), the maximal $\mathbb{P}(F=1)$ is [Equation (2)](#equation-2).

**Proof.** At any positive-mass history $h$, if the marker assigns positive conditional mass to $(F=1,V=c)$, conditional independence makes its branch-$b$ error mass proportional to $1-Q_b(c\mid h)$. Nonnegativity and zero total error therefore require $Q_b(c\mid h)=1$ for every $b$. No other history can be marked. At a history with such a common certain value, mark it with probability one and that value; leave other histories unmarked. This attains the stated maximum. $\square$

For a fixed future fair coin at the only history, all branch laws agree, so $\alpha=1$. No value is certain, so $\beta=0$. The unrestricted construction in [Theorem B.1](#theorem-b-1) can coordinate early marking with its designed future; the fixed passive marker cannot. Correlated latent seeds or marker interventions must be included in a different history/mechanism contract, not smuggled into [Equation (6)](#equation-6).

For a conditional error guarantee on each realised marked pair $(h,c)$, the criterion becomes $\min_b Q_b(c\mid h)\ge1-\varepsilon$. For aggregate branch budgets $\eta_b$, instead set $x_{hc}=\mathbb{P}(H=h,F=1,V=c)$ and maximise

<span id="equation-7"></span>

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\sum_{h,c}x_{hc},\\
&x_{hc}\ge0,\qquad\sum_c x_{hc}\le\mu(h),\\
&\sum_{h,c}x_{hc}\bigl[1-Q_b(c\mid h)\bigr]\le\eta_b\qquad(b\in\mathcal{B}).
\end{aligned}\tag{7}
$$

Randomisation can meet aggregate budgets without meeting a per-marker guarantee. [Supplement Module C](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-c) gives exact examples, robust certificates and sampling/resource contracts. These theorems concern irrevocable branch-consistent final content. They establish neither first appearance nor the constitutive bearer of a content token.

## Adequate sources, quotients and calibrated identification {#section-1}

A description earns its adequacy by preserving the distinctions the investigation actually needs. Returning the right answer on one task is not enough to identify the source of a presentation. This chapter develops the exact tests: continuation, common decoding, calibrated equality, native dependence and full-law comparison.

A physical description can preserve a response while omitting variables needed for another response or for its interpretation. Adequacy is therefore assessed relative to specified ports, preparations and readers. A quotient or statistic is not promoted to an experiential bearer merely because it is sufficient for one task. These distinctions develop the programme’s source and intrinsic-grain analysis (Rodgers, 2026h,i).

### 1.1 Controlled lumpability and continuation {#section-1-1}

Let $S$ be a finite physical state set, $a$ a native control, $y$ an observed output and $K_a(s,y,s')$ a joint transition instrument. Let $\phi:S\to B$ be a surjective proposed state quotient. It reproduces the complete nominated output/block process for arbitrary preparations exactly when

$$
\sum_{s':\phi(s')=b'}K_a(s,y,s')\quad\text{depends on }s\text{ only through }\phi(s). \tag{1}
$$

This holds for every $a,y,b'$. For necessity, compare point preparations in one fibre. For sufficiency, define the reduced kernel by this value and induct over trajectories. Adaptive controls are also preserved when their policy kernels depend on the corresponding prior outputs. A weaker port family gives a weaker adequacy condition; returning one display does not reset the whole state.

For deterministic controlled updates $F_a$ and output $g$, define $s\sim t$ when every finite control word gives the same future output word from $s$ and $t$. Then $g$ is constant on classes and $s\sim t\Rightarrow F_a(s)\sim F_a(t)$. Hence the classes form an autonomous output-sufficient quotient. Conversely, any autonomous quotient with $g=r\circ\phi$ has fibres contained in these classes, by induction on control words. Thus continuation equivalence is the coarsest such quotient. This is the familiar finite automata/sufficiency principle, now with an explicitly physical control alphabet. Refining the observation aperture may split these classes; it cannot justify deleting genuinely distinguishable native tests.

At zero error, distinguishable continuation classes need disjoint retained record supports. If a deterministic finite record has $K$ states, at most $K$ classes can be recovered. Counting distinct probability laws does not give that storage bound: many overlapping stochastic laws can use the same finite alphabet. Approximate or analogue records require a decision or information bound instead ([§3.5](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-c#section-3-5)).

For $F(a,b)=(b,a\oplus b)$ with output $g(a,b)=a$, $F^3=I$ and the first two outputs recover $(a,b)$. A report partition alone permits arbitrary refinements; requiring autonomous continuation and these separating outputs recovers the four-state quotient. The premise is physical observability, not a claim that four qualitative states exist.

### 1.2 Common decoder comparison {#section-1-2}

For finite source and target experiments $E_x,G_x$ on the same input alphabet, define one-decoder deficiency

$$
\delta(E,G)=\inf_D\max_x\operatorname{TV}(E_xD,G_x),\qquad
\operatorname{TV}(p,q)=\frac12\sum_z|p_z-q_z|.
$$

$D$ is one stochastic kernel, independent of the unknown input except through the accessible source. Total variation contracts under kernels, so any bounded target decision simulated through $D$ differs by at most $\delta$. If two preparations have target contrast $\gamma$ and source contrast $\zeta$, then $\gamma\le\zeta+2\delta$, by triangle inequality and contraction. Errors of two approximate source/target descriptions add. In particular, a common binary decision with errors $e_0,e_1$ requires source total variation at least $1-e_0-e_1$.

For binary symmetric channels parametrized by their contrast, write $E_x^\zeta(x)=(1+\zeta)/2$ and $G_x^\gamma(x)=(1+\gamma)/2$, with $0\le\zeta,\gamma\le1$. Then

$$
\delta(E^\zeta,G^\gamma)=\frac{(\gamma-\zeta)_+}{2}.
$$

Necessity is the contrast bound. If $\gamma\le\zeta$, a symmetric output flip reduces contrast to $\gamma$; otherwise the identity decoder attains the bound. For a constant source and a deterministic target naming one of $k$ inputs, the decoder is one common law $r$, so maximum error is $1-\min_xr_x\ge1-1/k$, attained by uniform $r$. Multiclass deficiency is a finite linear programme using absolute-value slacks. Coarsening target observations cannot increase it; common relabeling leaves it unchanged. Independent decoders selected separately for each input do not meet the premise.

An intermediate record may be better than the terminal output. If two inputs give intermediate binary contrast $.9$, a later fixed channel reduces it to $.18$, and target contrast is $.8$, intermediate deficiency is zero while terminal deficiency is $.31$. A terminally poor report is neither an absence of earlier information nor a timestamp for experience.

### 1.3 Exact qualitative equality certificates {#section-1-3}

Suppose independent calibration supplies exact same/different qualitative judgments on nominated tokens. Collapse the same-judgment components; different judgments give edges of a graph on the remaining $k$ components. Compatible qualitative partitions are precisely its proper colorings, with arbitrary unrestricted labels. Such data uniquely specify the partition if and only if this graph is complete: a missing edge permits those two components to merge; a complete graph forbids every merge. Autonomy can add equalities through continuation, but a report code alone cannot add calibration.

For $n$ original tokens and $k$ classes the smallest certificate has $n-k+\binom{k}{2}$ comparisons. Every class needs a connected equality graph, requiring its size minus one edges; each pair of classes needs an inequality. Spanning trees and one inequality per pair attain the lower bound. A fixed known number of qualitative labels imposes a different coloring problem and is not assumed here.

With known metric prototypes separated by $\Delta$, observation error at most $\epsilon$ uniquely identifies a prototype when $\Delta>2\epsilon$, by triangle inequality. If prototypes themselves are inferred from one noisy labelled anchor each, same-class points can be $2\epsilon$ apart and different-class points as close as $\Delta-2\epsilon$; separation $\Delta>4\epsilon$ suffices. The latter threshold is sharp without further information: at $\epsilon=.1$, anchors $.1,.5$ and new observation $.3$ fit centers $.2,.6$ with the middle point in the first class, or centers $0,.4$ with it in the second. Both prototype separations are $.4$. Boundary equality does not secure uniqueness.

Even a calibrated stochastic factorization can be nonunique. Let a report matrix be $R=ZD$, with positive row-stochastic full-rank factors. For an invertible $T$ near $I$ with $T\mathbf1=\mathbf1$, factors $ZT,T^{-1}D$ remain positive and row-stochastic for small perturbations, and have the same product. They need not be label permutations. If $D$ is independently known with full row rank, a right inverse instead recovers $Z$ uniquely. Factorization of public probabilities does not calibrate the latent labels by itself.

### 1.4 Native dependency and source fibres {#section-1-4}

Let $F:\prod_iS_i\to\prod_jS_j$ be a finite bijection with full Cartesian preparation support and nontrivial native ports. Join input $i$ to output $j$ when varying only $i$ can change $F_j$. The bipartite essential-dependency components give the unique finest routed factorization. Each output depends only on inputs in its component, so a product decomposition exists. Global injectivity and surjectivity force each component map to be bijective. Any other routed product cannot split an essential edge, proving fineness and uniqueness. For two bits, eight of the 24 bijections are products of local flips and a possible swap; sixteen are prime. Global mixed recoding is not native gauge: it can conjugate a swap to a controlled-XOR update while failing independent local clamp correspondence ([Module B](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-b#section-2-12)).

A downstream component without a return path into the executive component cannot alter that component’s kernels. The directed dependency graph’s return-strongly-connected component supplies a physical prefilter, not a phenomenal-inertness theorem for spectators.

For a graph on $n$ source bits, edge parities $x_i\oplus x_j$ have incidence rank $n-c$ over $\mathbb F_2$, where $c$ is the number of connected components. The kernel consists exactly of vectors constant on each component, hence has $2^c$ members. One source-bit anchor per component removes this ambiguity. Strong pair dependency does not entail a scalar triple term: the period-four map $(a,b,c)\mapsto(c,a\oplus c,b\oplus c)$ has a strongly connected dependency graph while every coordinate depends on at most two inputs. The graph is an organization descriptor, not an identified qualitative relation.

### 1.5 Restricted adaptation and calibrated contrast {#section-1-5}

Suppose two contexts read one baseline-corrected latent mean $z_h$ with fixed positive gains: $r_G=g_Gz_h$, $r_P=g_Pz_h$. Strict opposing means are impossible. If observed means are $(x,-y)$ with $x,y>0$ and total errors below $x,y$, this model is excluded. For fixed $k=g_P/g_G>0$,

$$
\inf_z\max\{|x-z|,|-y-kz|\}=\frac{kx+y}{1+k}. \tag{2}
$$

Indeed $kx+y=k(x-z)+(kz+y)\le(k+1)e$ for a fit of uniform error $e$; equality is attained at $z=(x-y)/(k+1)$. With unequal errors, the intervals $[x-e_G,x+e_G]$ and $[(-y-e_P)/k,(-y+e_P)/k]$ must intersect. Allowing arbitrary positive $k$ gives infimum $\min(x,y)$, sometimes only at an inadmissible zero/infinite gain limit.

A positive two-mode transfer matrix $B$ predicts opposing-dose responses $(b_{GG}-rb_{GP},b_{PG}-rb_{PP})$. Positive first and negative second occur exactly when $b_{PG}/b_{PP}<r<b_{GG}/b_{GP}$, a nonempty interval if and only if $\det B>0$. For

$$
B=\begin{pmatrix}4&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix},
$$

state $(1,-1)$ gives $(3,-1)$. This is a model-class consequence, not a general two-dimensional lower bound. A smooth scalar counterexample takes controls $z\mapsto z+1$, $z\mapsto z+2$ and readouts $f_G(z)=z$, $f_P(z)=z-z(z-1)(z-2)$: separate controls agree, their composition gives $(3,-3)$. Positive state-dependent gains on equally likely $z=\pm1$ can also give means $(1,-1)$, and increasing nonlinear functions $z$ and $e^z-1$ give opposite mean shifts for a half/half mixture of $-2,+1$. Test-dependent drift is a further escape. Fixed gains and a common latent mean are substantive.

For two histories, let source laws $P_w,P_v$, content laws $Q_w,Q_v$ and reader laws $R_w,R_v$ obey common kernels with approximation budgets $\operatorname{TV}(Q_i,P_iK)\le a_i$ and $\operatorname{TV}(R_i,Q_iJ)\le e_i$. Then

$$
\operatorname{TV}(P_w,P_v)\ge\operatorname{TV}(R_w,R_v)-a_w-a_v-e_w-e_v.
$$

This follows by contraction and triangle inequality. The budgets must include relevant calibration, drift and timing; a returning display alone supplies none.

### 1.6 What relational contrasts leave unidentified {#section-1-6}

For $\delta\in L^2(\mu\otimes\nu)$ define $a(x)=\mathbb E_y\delta$, $b(y)=\mathbb E_x\delta$, $m=\mathbb E\delta$, and

$$
D\delta=\delta(x,y)-\delta(x,y')-\delta(x',y)+\delta(x',y').
$$

Orthogonally decompose $\delta=m+(a-m)+(b-m)+r$ with both margins of $r$ zero. Independent product quadruples give $\|D\delta\|_2^2=4\|r\|_2^2$ because its four terms are orthogonal. Therefore

$$
\|\delta\|_2^2=\frac14\|D\delta\|_2^2+\|a-m\|_2^2+\|b-m\|_2^2+m^2. \tag{3}
$$

Contrasts alone miss additive rows/columns. For example, $\delta=\theta xy$ on independent uniform signs has contrast norm $2|\theta|$ and zero margins; adding $u(x)+v(y)$ leaves that contrast unchanged. Correlated or selected quadruples need a separate law; the identity cannot be assumed there.

For a finite difference space and accessible test vectors $v_j$, a full Gram operator $G=\sum_jv_jv_j^*$ with lower bound $G\succeq aI$, $a>0$, gives

$$
\|\delta\|^2\le a^{-1}\sum_j|\langle v_j,\delta\rangle|^2.
$$

Positive nonzero eigenvalues on a smaller span do not control its nullspace.

Finally, suppose finite atoms have diameter at most $D$, slacks vanish on an affinely independent face $F$ and sum to at least $\gamma$ outside. If a law $p$ has expected slack at most $\epsilon$, its escaped mass satisfies $\ell\le\epsilon/\gamma$. Let $q$ be on $F$ with mean within $\zeta$ of $p$, and let the inverse barycentric map have zero-sum norm bound $\|z\|_1\le M\|\sum_jz_ja_j\|$. For $\ell<1$, normalized $p_F$ has mean within $\ell D$ of $p$, hence

$$
\operatorname{TV}(p,q)\le\min\left\{1,\ell+\frac M2(\zeta+\ell D)\right\}.
$$

This supplies stable joint-law identification from support and means. Matching means is inadequate without the face/slack and inverse assumptions. Applying the bound requires physical tests that establish those data; the algebra alone does not determine what appeared.

Two further transfer facts make the full-law requirement explicit. If one common decoder satisfies $\sup_x\operatorname{TV}(R_x,D(P_x))\le\epsilon$, independent $m$-sample experiments differ after that decoder by at most $m\epsilon$. Couple the coordinates independently and bound the probability of any disagreement. A success probability $s$ on $R$ therefore transfers with lower bound $s-m\epsilon$; an input-dependent decoder would inject the answer.

For a full density comparison, let $f_i=e^{X_i}\xi_i>0$ and $Z_i=\int f_i$. If $\|X_2-X_1\|_\infty\le\epsilon$ and $Z_1^{-1}\int e^{X_1}|\xi_2-\xi_1|\le e^\epsilon-1$, expansion gives

$$
Z_1^{-1}\int|f_2-f_1|\le(e^\epsilon-1)(1+e^\epsilon)=e^{2\epsilon}-1.
$$

The normalizer difference costs at most the same $L^1$ discrepancy, so $\operatorname{TV}(f_1/Z_1,f_2/Z_2)\le\min\{1,e^{2\epsilon}-1\}$. Listed moments or separate margins do not supply these full positive-density and normalizer hypotheses.

## Occurrence, inclusion and ownership {#section-2}

Relational appearance requires an actual presentation of counterparts together with their relation. This chapter makes the distinctions exact: a shared description, a shared source and a shared occurrence are different achievements. Its mathematical results expose the extra commitments that turn evidence about descriptions into claims about jointness and ownership.

Compatible descriptions of two qualities do not establish relational appearance. The counterparts and their relation must be given in an actual presentation. This module distinguishes four questions: whether descriptions descend faithfully from occurrence tokens, whether several tokens have a common presentation, whether ownership is exclusive, and whether a physical relation supplies an occurrence law. These distinctions refine the source–aperture–readout framework of Rodgers (2026e) without assuming its constitutive assignment. A new presentation can meet the relational target; the original tokens need not fuse or continue.

Throughout, $P$ denotes a physical performance, $A$ an experience-inclusive act, and $Q$ its actual qualitative mode. A finite token set $E$, a partition $\Pi$, and a downward-closed family $K$ are mathematical objects. Their interpretation as experiential occurrences or domains requires independent grounds. In particular, a physical port, an equal content label, and a numerically identical qualitative occurrence are different things.

### 2.1 Faithful descent of occurrence identity {#section-2-1}

Let $E$ be a finite nonempty set, $\Pi$ a partition of $E$, and $q:E\to V$ a surjection. Write $\ker q$ for its fibre partition, and write $\Pi\preceq\Pi'$ when $\Pi$ refines $\Pi'$. A label partition is faithful if it preserves both sameness and difference of the nominated domains.

#### Proposition 2.1: Occurrence descent {#proposition-2-1}

There is a partition $\Lambda$ of $V$ satisfying

$$
e\sim_\Pi e'\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad q(e)\sim_\Lambda q(e')
$$

if and only if $\ker q\preceq\Pi$. When it exists, it is unique and its blocks are $q(B)$ for $B\in\Pi$.

**Proof.** If $q(e)=q(e')$, reflexivity in $\Lambda$ gives $e\sim_\Pi e'$, proving necessity. Conversely, if $q(B)$ and $q(B')$ intersect, some members of $B,B'$ lie in one fibre. Fibre purity then gives $B=B'$. Thus the nonempty block images partition $V$, and they satisfy the asserted equivalence. Surjectivity proves uniqueness. $\square$

The finest coarsening that admits descent is $\Pi\vee\ker q$, the equivalence relation generated by both partitions. Every descending coarsening must contain both relations; their join satisfies the proposition. This is a repair of a description, not evidence that actual domains merged.

An alternative is to preserve the missing identity. For each $v\in V$, let $m_v$ count the $\Pi$-blocks meeting $q^{-1}(v)$. A refining description $q'=(q,\mathrm{tag})$ needs at least $m_v$ labels over $v$, because distinct blocks in that fibre cannot share a refined label. The assignment $q'(e)=(q(e),[e]_\Pi)$ attains the bound. The minimum total alphabet is therefore $\sum_vm_v$; a uniform reusable tag alphabet needs $\max_vm_v$ values, or $\lceil\log_2\max_vm_v\rceil$ binary bits. These are coding bounds conditional on the independently nominated partition.

For example, take

$$
E=\{a,b_1,b_2,c\},\qquad \Pi=\{\{a,b_1\},\{b_2,c\}\},
$$

and give $b_1,b_2$ the same label $b$. The projected domains $\{a,b\}$ and $\{b,c\}$ cannot form a faithful label partition. Their transitive completion merges all three labels only because the description identified two tokens. Two physically separate recurrent cores can instantiate the same error: with independent native bits,

$$
T(a,b_1,b_2,c)=(b_1,a\oplus b_1,c,b_2\oplus c)
$$

is the product of two bijective two-bit maps. Equal current $b$ values or copied descriptions do not introduce a cross-core operation. Assigning qualitative occurrences to these cores would be an additional interpretation, not a consequence of $T$.

### 2.2 Pair witnesses, common witnesses and Fusion {#section-2-2}

Let $K\subseteq2^E$ contain every singleton and be closed under subsets. A face represents joint inclusion in at least one nominated domain; downward closure does not make every subset a separate subject. Define

$$
e\,R_K\,e'\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\{e,e'\}\in K.
$$

For $|E|\ge3$, the boundary family $2^E\setminus\{E\}$ and the full family $2^E$ agree on every proper-subset query. For three tokens, their pair relation is complete and transitive in both cases, but only one contains the common three-token face. The quantifiers differ:

$$
\forall e,e'\;\exists W\supseteq\{e,e'\}\qquad\text{versus}\qquad\exists W\supseteq E.
$$

Pairwise transitivity cannot exchange these quantifiers. For a fixed pair graph, every possible face must be a clique, but including every clique is an additional completion rule, and maximal cliques can overlap.

#### Proposition 2.2: Fusion and exclusive maximal domains {#proposition-2-2}

For finite $K$ as above, the condition

$$
F,G\in K,\quad F\cap G\ne\varnothing\quad\Longrightarrow\quad F\cup G\in K \tag{Fusion}
$$

holds if and only if the distinct maximal faces form a partition of $E$. Equivalently, $K$ consists of the subsets of partition blocks.

**Proof.** If two maximal faces intersect, Fusion places their union in the family, so maximality makes them equal. Finiteness and the singleton condition place each token in a maximal face. Downward closure supplies every subset of each block. Conversely, intersecting faces in a family of partition-block subsets lie in the same block, and their union is again a subset of it. $\square$

Fusion is a precise single-owner commitment about the same tokens. It does not follow from interaction or from a relation being computed. An overlapping-domain ontology can reject it without inconsistency. If a unique owner map is assumed at the outset, co-presence is equality of owners and a partition follows by definition; that assumption must not be mistaken for a physical discovery.

Uncertainty about token identity also yields a quantitative bound. On one common coupling, let $X$ indicate $a\sim b_1$, $Y$ indicate $b_2\sim c$, and $Z$ indicate $a\sim c$. Assume equivalence-domain ownership and $\Pr(b_1\ne b_2)\le\delta$. On the event that the middle token is identical, $XY\le Z$, hence $X+Y-Z\le1$; elsewhere its maximum is two. Therefore

$$
p_{ab}+p_{bc}-p_{ac}\le1+\delta. \tag{4}
$$

If each estimated marginal has absolute error at most $\epsilon_i$, add $\sum_i\epsilon_i$ to the right-hand side. Different trials do not automatically supply this coupling or stable middle-token identity. Passing the inequality also does not select a common higher face: the boundary-family example passes it.

### 2.3 Exact partial identification under exclusive ownership {#section-2-3}

For three nominated occurrences, permit absence but assume each present occurrence has at most one owner in the target occasion. The allowed pair-indicator patterns, ordered $(12,23,13)$, are

$$
000,\quad100,\quad010,\quad001,\quad111.
$$

Let their pair probabilities be $p_1,p_2,p_3$, and let $t$ be the mass of $111$.

#### Proposition 2.3: Three-pair identification {#proposition-2-3}

The exact identified interval is

$$
\max\left\{0,\frac{p_1+p_2+p_3-1}{2}\right\}\le t\le\min_i p_i.
$$

It is nonempty exactly when $0\le p_i\le1$ and $p_i+p_j-p_k\le1$ for all distinct $i,j,k$. If only jointly valid boxes $p_i\in[\ell_i,u_i]\subseteq[0,1]$ are available, the exact interval becomes $[L,H]$, where

$$
\begin{aligned}
L&=\max\left\{0,\max_{i<j}(\ell_i+\ell_j-1),\frac{\ell_1+\ell_2+\ell_3-1}{2}\right\},\\
H&=\min_i u_i.
\end{aligned} \tag{5}
$$

Compatibility is $L\le H$, equivalently $\ell_i+\ell_j-u_k\le1$ for all distinct $i,j,k$.

**Proof.** The atom masses must be $t$, $p_i-t$ for each single edge, and $1-\sum_ip_i+2t$ for $000$. Nonnegativity proves necessity and constructs every admissible exact solution. For boxes and fixed $t\le\min_i u_i$, choose $p_i=\max(\ell_i,t)$, which minimizes their sum. Feasibility is then $\sum_i\max(\ell_i,t)\le1+2t$. Expand the left side as the maximum, over $S\subseteq\{1,2,3\}$, of $\sum_{i\in S}\ell_i+(3-|S|)t$. Empty and singleton sets impose only $t\le1$ and $\ell_i\le1$; pairs and the full set give exactly the lower bounds in (5). The atom construction attains every resulting $t$. Taking an index minimizing $u_k$ proves the equivalent three box inequalities. $\square$

Triple tests do not characterize every global random partition. Split five tokens into $S$ of size two and $T$ of size three. Every partition satisfies

$$
\sum_{i\in S,j\in T}p_{ij}-\sum_{\{i,j\}\subset S}p_{ij}-\sum_{\{i,j\}\subset T}p_{ij}\le2. \tag{6}
$$

Indeed, a block containing $a$ members of $S$ and $b$ of $T$ contributes $ab-\binom a2-\binom b2\le\min(a,b)$. For $b\ge a$, subtracting $a$ gives $-(b-a)(b-a-1)/2\le0$, and the other case is symmetric. Summing blocks gives at most $\min(|S|,|T|)=2$; expectation proves the inequality. Set every cross marginal to $1/2$ and every within-side marginal to zero. All triple inequalities hold, but (6) has left side three. General finite compatibility is a linear programme over admissible partitions, or over a different domain family if exclusive ownership is rejected.

The probability polytope constrains one common target ensemble under the ownership premise. It neither infers that the nominated tokens exist nor imposes that premise on every possible presentation theory.

### 2.4 Reader validity, probe creation and context {#section-2-4}

Let $R_i$ be a positive assertion about a specified joint appearance, and $Z_i$ the occurrence at its readout time. Correct comparison of stimulus values does not by itself calibrate this assertion. Suppose, in the relevant assay context,

$$
\Pr(R_i=1\mid Z_i=0)\le\alpha_i<1.
$$

Writing $q_i=\Pr(R_i=1)$ and $z_i=\Pr(Z_i=1)$ gives $q_i\le\alpha_i+(1-\alpha_i)z_i$, hence

$$
z_i\ge\max\{0,(q_i-\alpha_i)/(1-\alpha_i)\}.
$$

No sensitivity premise is needed for this lower bound. Silence supplies no upper bound. A further guarantee $\Pr(R_i=0\mid Z_i=1)\le\beta_i<1$ gives $z_i\le\min\{1,q_i/(1-\beta_i)\}$.

Let $Y_i$ denote the same-episode earlier target, with independently defended creation/destruction allowances

$$
\Pr(Y_i=0,Z_i=1)\le d_i^+,\qquad \Pr(Y_i=1,Z_i=0)\le d_i^-.
$$

Then $\Pr(Y_i=1)\in[z_i-d_i^+,z_i+d_i^-]$, clipped to $[0,1]$. If the assay’s target distribution differs from a common reference ensemble by total variation at most $\rho_i$, subtract or add $\rho_i$ as well. Thus jointly valid response intervals $[q_i^-,q_i^+]$ give

$$
\ell_i=\max\left\{0,\frac{q_i^--\alpha_i}{1-\alpha_i}-d_i^+-\rho_i\right\}, \tag{7}
$$

$$
u_i=\min\left\{1,\frac{q_i^+}{1-\beta_i}+d_i^-+\rho_i\right\}. \tag{8}
$$

With specificity alone use $u_i=1$. These boxes can enter Proposition 2.3, but an accurate later report does not establish $d_i^+=0$: the probe may create the new third. Random selection after a completed episode is also insufficient unless its target and exogeneity contract are warranted.

For $m$ assays, with $n_i$ independent Bernoulli repetitions of common mean $q_i$ within assay $i$, the simultaneous Hoeffding radius

$$
s_i=\sqrt{\frac{\log(2m/\eta)}{2n_i}}
$$

gives $q_i^\pm$ by clipping $\widehat q_i\pm s_i$, with total failure probability at most $\eta$. Independence between assays is unnecessary for the union bound. Calibration uncertainty needs a separate joint budget. Repeated observations cannot calibrate the phenomenal specificity they presuppose. For illustration only, $q=.80$, $\alpha=.05$, $d^+=.02$, $\rho=0$ give a lower target probability $.7694736842$. These are stipulated assay parameters, not measurements of consciousness.

### 2.5 Fixed content maps and support intersection {#section-2-5}

Let a finite physical domain $X$ have independently warranted allowed-content sets $\Gamma(x)$, and let $\Delta:X\to Z$ claim sufficient target description. A function $h$ with $h(\Delta(x))\in\Gamma(x)$ exists exactly when every nonempty $\Delta$-fibre has nonempty intersection of its $\Gamma$ sets. Necessity follows from the common value $h(z)$; choosing one value per fibre proves sufficiency. Pairwise intersections are insufficient: $\{a,b\}$, $\{b,c\}$, $\{a,c\}$ have no common member.

If independent counterpart choices have $m,n$ values and the target preserves both projections, all $mn$ complete target values differ. A sufficient aperture needs at least $mn$ mathematical values. If each pair also has $r$ independently different presented relations, the count is $mnr$. This is not a Hilbert-dimension, orthogonality or accessible-bit theorem.

#### Proposition 2.4: Same-function support intersection {#proposition-2-4}

On a full Cartesian domain $X=\prod_{i=1}^nX_i$, suppose the same function $f$ factors through coordinate supports $S$ and $T$. Then it factors through $S\cap T$.

**Proof.** For $x,y$ agreeing on $S\cap T$, the Cartesian domain admits $w$ with $w_S=x_S$ and $w_T=y_T$. The prescriptions agree on the intersection. $S$-sufficiency gives $f(x)=f(w)$ and $T$-sufficiency gives $f(w)=f(y)$. Thus $f$ is constant on every intersection fibre. $\square$

Two separately feasible maps cannot be substituted for this premise. On binary $(x,y)$, take $\Gamma(0,0)=\{a\}$, $\Gamma(1,1)=\{b\}$ and both off-diagonal sets $\{a,b\}$. A map through $x$ and another through $y$ are feasible, while no constant map is. On a correlated diagonal $x=y$, even one nonconstant function has either coordinate as a sufficient description; the mixed point needed by the proof is absent. Nor is a quantum tensor factorization automatically a Cartesian domain of independently variable complete states.

### 2.6 Regularity and correlated indicators {#section-2-6}

On a fixed metric space with probability anchors $p(x_i)\in[\ell_i,u_i]$ and an independently warranted upper Lipschitz constant $L\ge0$, define

$$
\begin{aligned}
\underline p(x)&=\max\{0,\max_i[\ell_i-Ld(x,x_i)]\},\\
\overline p(x)&=\min\{1,\min_i[u_i+Ld(x,x_i)]\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

An admissible function exists exactly when $\ell_i\le u_j+Ld(x_i,x_j)$ for every pair, with valid anchor intervals. Necessity is the Lipschitz inequality. For sufficiency, maxima and minima of these cones are $L$-Lipschitz; the pair conditions place both envelopes within every anchor interval. The triangle inequality makes $\underline p\le\overline p$. Each envelope is itself admissible, and every admissible function lies between them, proving sharpness. The consistency lower bound

$$
L\ge\max_{i,j:d(x_i,x_j)>0}\frac{(\ell_i-u_j)_+}{d(x_i,x_j)}
$$

does not warrant the upper regularity used to transport an anchor.

For two indicators $Y_1,Y_2$ of one binary target $J$, independently justified error budgets imply

$$
\Pr(Y_1\ne Y_2)\le\Pr(Y_1\ne J)+\Pr(Y_2\ne J)\le\eta_1+\eta_2.
$$

A disagreement rate $.12$ contradicts two $.05$ budgets even when the indicators’ marginals agree. The exact unrestricted latent test uses nonnegative cells $\pi_{j,y_1,y_2}$, fixes their observed $(y_1,y_2)$ marginal, constrains $\sum\mathbf1_{y_i\ne j}\pi\le\eta_i$, and minimizes/maximizes $\sum\pi_{1,y_1,y_2}$. Every feasible cell table defines a joint law, so the linear-programme bounds are sharp for that model class. Without target-error restrictions, any target prevalence is compatible: draw $J$ independently of the complete observed pair. Hardware independence is not independence conditional on actual appearance, and an experienced word such as “different” need not present the original sensory counterparts.

### 2.7 Native articulation, manifestation and categorical grounding {#section-2-7}

The earlier proposals motivate distinct constitutive laws. The following arguments also identify the premises each law still requires.

#### Native Articulation Law {#native-articulation-law}

The Native Articulation Law (NAL) assigns an elementary presentation to each actual irreducible native transformation. Its motivation is immanence and structural fidelity: do not add a phenomenal fusion across independently operating transformations or a phenomenal division inside a native prime without further lawlike structure. It addresses organization rather than an ingredient list and needs no additional spectator. Its strongest objection is carrier-centered: an irreducible physical event can still have several carriers with separate qualitative states. Rejecting their experiential division already adopts the event-centered individuation criterion. Native elementary-event grain and qualitative duration also need independent specification. A content map could be constant, so one presentation per prime would not itself supply two qualitative counterparts and their relation.

#### Relational Manifestation {#relational-manifestation}

Relational Manifestation (RM) proposes that a concretely realized intrinsic relation has a manifest counterpart preserving its distinction, with some common presentation supported by its contributors. Ontological uniformity motivates the proposal: if intrinsic qualitative terms are real, wholly unmanifest connections between them should not simply be assumed. A serious rival nevertheless retains qualitative terms and real intrinsic relations without their being given together; it need not introduce another substance. Uniformity therefore supplies a reason to investigate the law, not a proof of its co-presentation clause.

This proposal also changes the object represented by the family. If $\mathcal R$ lists required realizing contributor supports, the least downward-closed family containing them is

$$
\mathcal F_{\min}=\{\varnothing\}\cup\{\{e\}:e\in E\}\cup\bigcup_{R\in\mathcal R}2^R.
$$

It records common realizing support. It is not the earlier family $K$ of qualitative occurrences jointly given. A subset of a realizing support need not be sufficient for the target. A compressed parity can preserve a distinction without presenting both full argument qualities. Identifying $\mathcal F$ with $K$ would need content fidelity and actual occurrence, neither supplied by set closure.

#### Intrinsic Qualitative Grounding {#intrinsic-qualitative-grounding}

Intrinsic Qualitative Grounding (IQG) adds: (i) actual concrete categorical character is qualitative; (ii) irreducible relational facts have categorical grounds; (iii) the nominated relation’s ground is a presentation in which that relation is manifest. The positive rationale integrates intrinsic actuality and accepted relational phenomenology without an added spectator. Clause (iii) is not a consequence of the first two: a relational ground can contain qualitative terms without one common presentation. The objection concerns assembly/ownership, not whether the relation exists. The broader categorical and whole-act alternatives are developed in [Module H](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-h). None of these principles is assumed to establish the main paper’s native-human induction.

### 2.8 Continuous interaction and primitive support {#section-2-8}

A finite tick does not independently individuate a physical elementary event. Let independently fixed parts have connected open coordinate domains $X_i$, and let $f(t,x)$ be a $C^1$ vector field on their full product, with local uniqueness. At $t_0$, join parts $i,j$ when a cross derivative $D_jf_i$ or $D_if_j$ is nonzero somewhere in the intervention domain at times arbitrarily near $t_0$.

#### Proposition 2.5: Finest interaction-germ decomposition {#proposition-2-5}

The connected components of this symmetric germ graph give the unique finest fixed-part product decomposition of the vector-field germ.

**Proof.** Every missing cross-block edge vanishes on some time neighbourhood. Finitely many pairs supply a common neighbourhood. Connected Cartesian coordinate domains and vanishing cross derivatives imply that each block’s vector field depends only on that block, giving local product flows. Conversely, a product germ has zero cross-block derivatives, so every germ edge must have its ends in one block. Each graph component must consequently lie in any admissible block. $\square$

Separate smooth invertible part-coordinate changes, consistent part permutations and orientation-preserving smooth time diffeomorphisms preserve the zero/nonzero cross-dependence. Arbitrary mixing of native parts does not. For $\dot x=\sin(2\pi t)y$, $\dot y=\sin(2\pi t)x$ on $[0,1]$, the flow is $\exp(M\alpha(t))$ with off-diagonal $M$ and $\alpha(t)=(1-\cos2\pi t)/(2\pi)$. It returns to identity while its intermediate flow couples the inputs.

A two-sided germ can depend on later coupling. A coupling zero for $t\le0$ and $e^{-1/t^2}$ afterward is included at zero despite an idle past and identical instantaneous derivatives. Coupling 1–2 only before zero and 2–3 only afterward connects all three at zero without simultaneous edges. Product indecomposability of a germ is thus different from instantaneous exchange or co-presentation.

For independently fixed finite tensor factors, let $E_i$ replace factor $i$ by its normalized partial trace times identity. The $E_i$ commute and are Hilbert–Schmidt orthogonal projections. Define

$$
P_S=\prod_{i\in S}(\operatorname{Id}-E_i)\prod_{j\notin S}E_j,\qquad H_S=P_SH.
$$

Expanding $\prod_i[E_i+(\operatorname{Id}-E_i)]$ proves $H=\sum_SH_S$; mutually orthogonal projector ranges prove uniqueness. Each included factor is traceless and each omitted factor is identity. Local unitary frames preserve support, while their derivative terms add only singleton generators; a scalar shift changes only the empty support. Pointwise support needs regularity: a change on one time point in an integrable generator does not alter the propagator. Pair supports need not exhaust state/history structure: $[Z_1X_2,Y_2Z_3]=2iZ_1Z_2Z_3$, and the commuting chain $Z_1Z_2+Z_2Z_3$ acting on $|+++\rangle$ at angle $\pi/4$ produces a pure state with every one-qubit reduction maximally mixed. It is entangled across each bipartition although the primitive triple support is zero.

### 2.9 Action, articulation rates and quantitative support {#section-2-9}

On fixed factors $\mathcal H_L\otimes\mathcal H_R$, with dimensions $d_L,d_R$, remove scalar and local Hamiltonian terms by

$$
H_\times=H-\frac{\operatorname{Tr}_R H}{d_R}\otimes I_R-I_L\otimes\frac{\operatorname{Tr}_L H}{d_L}+\frac{\operatorname{Tr}H}{d_Ld_R}I.
$$

For locally integrable Hermitian $H$, define

$$
\mu(I)=\hbar^{-1}\int_I\|H_\times(t)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\,dt.
$$

It is additive under faithful subdivision. An absolutely continuous local frame adds only local derivative terms and conjugates $H_\times$; an increasing absolutely continuous time change rescales the generator and preserves the integral.

#### Proposition 2.6: Zero action and product subintervals {#proposition-2-6}

The action vanishes exactly when every subinterval propagator is a product unitary up to scalar phase. With $H_0=H-H_\times$ and its propagator $V_0$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|V(t,s)-V_0(t,s)\|_{\mathrm{op}}&\le\mu([s,t]),\\
\frac12\|V\rho V^\dagger-V_0\rho V_0^\dagger\|_1&\le\min\{1,\mu([s,t])\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Proof.** Zero integral makes $H_\times=0$ almost everywhere, so the local generators yield product propagation. Conversely, the tangent of a product-unitary propagator at almost every identity-time lies in the local Lie algebra, excluding a cross term. Duhamel’s integral expression for $V-V_0$, unitary invariance of operator norm and integration give the first bound. Expanding the density difference into two terms gives trace norm at most $2\|V-V_0\|_{\mathrm{op}}$; trace distance is also at most one. $\square$

For $H=g(t)Z_LZ_R$, $\mu=\int|g|/\hbar$ while the accumulated angle is $\theta=\int g/\hbar$. A forward angle $\pi/4$ followed by its inverse has identity endpoint and action $\pi/2$. Exchange SWAP generated by $g(I+XX+YY+ZZ)/2$ has cross norm $3|g|/2$ and action $3\pi/4$ at its SWAP endpoint. Endpoint permutation does not certify an interaction-free physical history.

#### Finite-Rate Articulation {#finite-rate-articulation}

Finite-Rate Articulation (FRA) proposes joint-presentation occurrences with survival $S(\mu)$. If $S(0)=1$, continuity at zero and action-homogeneous memoryless survival imply $S(x+y)=S(x)S(y)$, then $S(\mu)=e^{-c\mu}$ for $c\ge0$. To prove this, continuity excludes a zero survival at finite action by repeatedly halving it; $-\log S$ is continuous and additive, hence linear. Probability of at least one occurrence is $1-e^{-c\mu}$. Finite response, composability and invariance under descriptive subdivision motivate the proposal, but no such property earns $c>0$, selects the norm, or makes survival memoryless.

For an external equal-probability lottery over actions $0,2x$, tokenwise conditioning gives $\tfrac12(1-e^{-2cx})$, not $1-e^{-cx}$; their gap is $\tfrac12(1-e^{-cx})^2$. A state-sensitive completion is also needed if the proposal is to distinguish $|00\rangle$ from $|++\rangle$ under $ZZ$: their action agrees despite different state evolution. Finally, alternating $g=\pm1$ on $2n$ equal bins gives fixed action one and maximum signed phase $1/(2n)$. Its propagator path tends uniformly to idle while the proposed occurrence probability stays $1-e^{-c}$. This is a dependence on actual control history, not a failure of invariance under redescribing one unchanged history.

#### Reciprocal Articulation Field {#reciprocal-articulation-field}

An alternative, the Reciprocal Articulation Field (RAF), introduces actual amplitudes $a_1,a_2$ with potential

$$
V=\frac r2(a_1^2+a_2^2)+\frac u4(a_1^4+a_2^4)-\kappa a_1a_2-h(a_1+a_2),\qquad r,u>0.
$$

At $h=0$, $\kappa\ge0$, the zero state’s Hessian eigenvalues are $r\mp\kappa$. For $\kappa>r$ the aligned minima have $a_1=a_2=\pm\sqrt{(\kappa-r)/u}$ and Hessian eigenvalues $2(\kappa-r),4\kappa-2r$. Small forcing satisfies $um^3+(r-\kappa)m=h$, giving susceptibility $[3um^2+r-\kappa]^{-1}$ along a stable aligned branch. Relaxation additionally requires a kinetic law: under $\dot a_i=-\gamma\partial_{a_i}V$, $\gamma>0$, the linear relaxation rates are $\gamma$ times the Hessian eigenvalues. If $\kappa$ depends on a physical coordinate, differentiating $V$ produces a corresponding backreaction force; the field is therefore a physical hypothesis, not a cost-free phenomenal label.

Those predictions do not validate the field or its qualitative interpretation. At every strictly positive finite temperature its Gibbs density is positive throughout the plane, giving positive $\mathbb E a_i^2$ even below threshold and zero $\mathbb E a_i$ in the symmetric equilibrium even above it. Quartic domination makes the finite partition function locally analytic in $\kappa$; the zero-temperature bifurcation is not a universal biological-temperature cutoff. Nonzero forcing already gives a nonzero response below threshold. If each uncoupled core is already ordered, replace $r$ by $-\alpha$, $\alpha>0$: aligned and antialigned energies are $-(\alpha+\kappa)^2/(2u)$ and $-(\alpha-\kappa)^2/(2u)$. Every positive $\kappa$ favours aligned global minima, while antialigned states remain locally stable only for $\kappa<\alpha/2$. Thus ordering does not settle a one-subject fusion boundary. FRA and RAF concern different physical/constitutive commitments and cannot be combined by sharing an articulation label.

### 2.10 Collective capacity and a conditional content-grounding bound {#section-2-10}

Pairwise generators can encode globally irreducible physical distinctions. For $n$ qubits with control $c$, take the bounded autonomous Hamiltonian

$$
H=\frac{\pi\hbar}{2T}\sum_{j=2}^n|1\rangle\langle1|_c\otimes(I-X_j).
$$

Its commuting pair terms implement controlled-$X$ at $T$, mapping $|\pm\rangle_c|0\rangle^{\otimes(n-1)}$ to $|\mathrm{GHZ}_\pm\rangle=(|0^n\rangle\pm|1^n\rangle)/\sqrt2$. The states are orthogonal and $X^{\otimes n}$ distinguishes them; tracing out any factor removes their differing coherence, so every proper reduction agrees. Primitive pair arity therefore supplies no upper bound on collective physical distinction order. This is not an observation of a GHZ presentation.

If, additionally, a reduced density operator is a sufficient target aperture and its finite content probabilities are affine on all density operators, they have POVM form $F(\rho)_k=\operatorname{Tr}(E_k\rho)$ with $E_k\ge0$, $\sum_kE_k=I$. An affine extension to Hermitian operators gives the trace representation; positivity on all pure states and normalization give the effects. Consequently $\operatorname{TV}(F(\rho_0),F(\rho_1))\le\tfrac12\|\rho_0-\rho_1\|_1$. A binary target distinguishing equiprobable preparations with average error at most $\epsilon$ thus requires

$$
\frac12\|\rho_0-\rho_1\|_1\ge1-2\epsilon. \tag{9}
$$

The lower bound follows because the two correct-decision errors sum to at most $2\epsilon$. Sufficient grounding and affinity are extra correspondence premises; a physical ensemble representation alone does not supply them.

For an explicit changing aperture, let $H=\hbar\omega|1\rangle\langle1|_L\otimes(I-X_R)$ act on $|\Phi_\pm\rangle=(|00\rangle\pm|11\rangle)/\sqrt2$. With $\theta=\omega t$,

$$
|\psi_\pm\rangle=\frac{|00\rangle\pm ie^{-i\theta}\sin\theta|10\rangle\pm e^{-i\theta}\cos\theta|11\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Direct partial traces give $D_{LR}=1$, $D_L=|\sin\theta|$, $D_R=0$. If the phase distinction were actually presented with error at most $.1$, then at $\theta=\pi/4$ neither local density aperture meets the required $.8$ contrast. At $\theta=\pi/2$ the states are $|\pm\rangle_L|0\rangle_R$, so the same target becomes locally realizable. No subject migration, preserved original token, or actual qubit appearance follows. Generator, realizing support, encoded distinction and qualitative presentation are separate objects.

### 2.11 Descriptive gluing versus actual common occurrence {#section-2-11}

The global-structure proposal of Lee-Youngzie et al. (2026) motivates testing whether independently measured local qualitative structure determines a broad structure. A presheaf $\mathcal F$ has identity/composition restrictions; a sheaf adds existence and uniqueness of a glue for every compatible local family. A category-valued dissimilarity object needs a specified section/underlying-set construction before these set-valued conditions apply.

Finiteness alone does not turn existence into uniqueness. On a discrete two-point space, let $\mathcal F(X)=\{0,1\}$ and let every proper-open set have a singleton section set, with both globals restricting to it. Every matching family glues: a cover containing $X$ fixes its global member, and a proper cover admits either global. The cover by the two singleton opens has two glues. Injective restrictions or a jointly separating restriction family can instead supply uniqueness, but their independent role, not finiteness, does the work.

Nor does an incompatible family disprove the sheaf condition. Binary functions on three positions form a sheaf; left/middle and middle/right assignments that disagree on the middle have no glue because they are not matching. An impossible three-dimensional interpretation likewise need not mean the actual two-dimensional scene lacks unity.

An expansive restriction postulated to obey

$$
d_X(x,x')\le d_V(r(x),r(x'))
$$

cannot forget a positively distinguishable feature: equal restricted values force zero broad dissimilarity. Restricting the left coordinate of binary pairs with normalized Hamming distance violates this for $(0,0),(0,1)$. An attentional manipulation may instead create a new context or token and sharpen discriminability. The arrow between attention contexts must then be distinguished from passive part restriction of one unchanged presentation; unchanged retinal input does not establish the latter.

Attach independently supported token sets $T_i(d_i)$ to local descriptions $d_i$: these are the nominated actual presentations of which the datum is warranted as an aspect. A common occurrence witness exists exactly when

$$
\bigcap_iT_i(d_i)\ne\varnothing.
$$

Compatible descriptions separately witnessed by $\tau_1,\tau_2$ need no common token. A new $\tau_3$ genuinely presenting both can supply the common witness without fusing $\tau_1,\tau_2$. Descriptive gluing does not establish that lifting. Even a common token needs independent evidence that the relation, rather than just separate counterparts, belongs to it. Equal binary local marginals conceal this: equal-pair and opposite-pair distributions both have uniform margins, while their joint-equality probabilities are one and zero. Independently measured broad relational data, with fixed readers and token/context indices, can discriminate them.

### 2.12 Native replacement and the corrected acquaintance condition {#section-2-12}

The fading/dancing-qualia argument supplies a cognitive-coherence motivation for organizational transfer (Chalmers, 1995). Its exact physical premise is stronger than boundary-output agreement. Fix components, local states, native independent preparations, controlled kernels, output ports, clock, initial law and environmental coupling. A product of local bijections and a component permutation, with corresponding interventions, is a native isomorphism only if every controlled kernel and those boundary/clock conditions commute. Induction proves equality of all finite trajectory laws, including adaptive policies that choose corresponding actions from matched prior outputs. The induction concerns physical trajectories; it does not identify a detector with direct acquaintance.

For example, $F(a,b)=(b,a)$ and $G(r,s)=(r\oplus s,s)$ obey $hF=Gh$ for $h(a,b)=(a,a\oplus b)$. Yet across the sixteen state/single-port/value preparations, eight corresponding same-port clamps fail: four first-port failures have clamped value different from $a$, and four second-port failures have $a=1$. No product bit-bijection with permutation conjugates them: under such maps both swap outputs remain cross-only dependencies, whereas $G$ has an isolated self-dependent second component. Global predictive recoding is not a physical-copy theorem.

Likewise, let a current/remembered comparator update $(x,m)'=(u,x)$, and suppose a material chart sends both presented values to $q_z(v)=v\oplus z$. Switching $z$ changes absolute labels in all four states while preserving their presented equality. A change between tokens does not itself produce a presentation comparing the old and new qualities. Occurrence transfer is weaker than exact qualitative-chart transfer, but still needs its own warrant.

The corrected no-silent-loss schema is especially instructive. Let $r_v$ mean occurrence, $k_v$ genuine current acquaintance, $B_v$ independently specified checking background, and $D_{uv}$ a possible physical discrepancy on a replacement edge. The proposed condition is

$$
B_u\wedge k_u\wedge r_u\wedge\neg r_v\quad\Longrightarrow\quad D_{uv}.
$$

Native matching supplies $\neg D_{uv}$. One anchored edge can yield $r_1$, but not $k_1$. On a three-vertex path, take $r=(1,1,0)$, $k=(1,0,0)$, all backgrounds true and no discrepancy. The schema holds in both directions on every edge, while endpoint occurrence fails. An occurrence-edge principle $r_u\wedge\neg r_v\Rightarrow D_{uv}$ repairs the first-loss proof; alternatively add $B_v\wedge r_v\Rightarrow k_v$ at every vertex. Each is a new premise, not an implication of transported occurrence. Interval occurrence also does not automatically mean an attended presentation at the checking instant. These are the exact obligations that a transfer account must earn, and they are distinct from the main paper’s narrow continuing-human capacity induction.

### 2.13 Awareness, access and the construction of a personal event {#section-2-13}

The awareness-first account distinguishes the proposed knowing aspect of reality, denoted $U$ in the monograph, from the differentiated contents available through a particular apparatus and history. This ground is not an additional localized subject. The monograph’s starting commitment and the development paper’s operational distinctions have different roles (Rodgers, 2026e,c). The former proposes an ontological basis; the latter distinguishes a source, the access available to an observer, and the representation constructed through that access. An operational model can characterize dependence on apparatus or acquired constraints without deriving the fundamental commitment. Conversely, granting that commitment does not determine the extension of a particular experiential predicate.

This distinction matters even if one accepts that all differentiated terms belong to one underlying reality. Two differentiated processes may have separate perspectives; they may contribute to a new shared presentation; or their interaction may introduce no additional joint perspective. A premise about the underlying basis alone does not decide among those local possibilities. The present paper’s current-act argument supplies a scoped positive attribution rather than treating fundamental awareness as a substitute for its activity and bearer premises.

Perceptual presentation is formed through bodily and sensory processing and acquired organisation, including classifications, memories, expectations, feelings and beliefs. These conditions shape the sensory presentation itself; they are not merely descriptions added after a consciously viewed raw image. Ongoing seeing is perceptual processing, which can continue when reflective naming, commentary or personal appropriation is suspended. Perceptual construction, recognition and the sense of an event happening to me are different functional roles; they can operate concurrently within one activity. A practical history can therefore alter the organisation of current access itself. In the development account, a relational product can be retained and become available to subsequent interactions; it need not be a third substance (Rodgers, 2026c, §3.1). Retained constraints then shape activity, and activity can revise them. The reflective-alignment paper develops that reciprocity (Rodgers, 2026b, §4.1); its operational description does not by itself establish one phenomenal subject.

The personal observer is also constructed within this process. A central self-image organizes ownership, relevance and narrative continuity. The proposed reflective recognition of its constructed character need not erase memory, practical identity or the usable self-model. It changes the model’s claim to be the exhaustive centre (Rodgers, 2026c, §5.4). Whether an artificial or biological implementation has such recognition as experience requires its own warrant; recursive updating or an articulate self-report does not settle it (Rodgers, 2026j,b).

### 2.14 A conditional connection to the full constitution {#section-2-14}

The current-act argument is developed independently of assuming the full A1–A3 constitution. A defensible application of SPC-2 requires a justified physical realisation and native operating doctrine (Rodgers, 2026e, ch. 17). **A1** assigns a perspective to a qualifying maximal internal strongly connected component. Qualification requires an executable covering return and at least two distinct native predictive classes in the actual operating/protocol type and resource context. Incompatible control modes cannot be combined into a fictitious return. These conditions are not strengthened here by a new requirement for language, autobiographical reflection or a rich human scene.

**A2** assigns phenomenal relational organisation as a structural copy of the core’s complete endogenous predictive object over all physically admitted finite continuations. The actual phenomenal point corresponds to the predictive class of the realised intrinsic state. Native operation and outcome labels, finite test laws, compatible horizon restrictions and well-defined class transition instruments are preserved. This is the full structural assignment, not a selected finite report profile or an investigator’s preferred time horizon. **A3** then determines episode continuation through nonbranching realised process provenance; matching memories or outputs alone do not supply that continuity.

To connect the present case to those laws, an appropriate phase of its performance would have to be shown to belong to a justified qualifying core, with the relational mode $Q$ identified as an aspect of the core’s current scene. A performance’s executing contributors and a subject’s boundary are different nominations requiring a bridge. An environmental source or instrument can contribute causally without thereby becoming part of that subject. With the realisation supplied and SPC-2 accepted, phenomenal states, their timing and continuation are assigned at its declared native temporal resolution. No such complete application is demonstrated here. The local current-act argument neither validates A2 nor independently selects the first onset of a scene, and it does not turn the subject boundary into the set of everything causally involved in the performance. First presentation at current uptake, $J$, remains an admitted option.

### 2.15 The October draft and the scope of the recollective thesis {#section-2-15}

The unpublished 12 October 2026 draft of *Experience as Recollective Interpretation: Awareness, Scaffolding and the Question of Machine Experience* (Rodgers, 2026d) defines experience as the interpretation of a retained occurrence as having occurred. Its Section 5 explicitly allows minimal retention without language, explicit temporal concepts or autobiographical memory. An ongoing act can interpret its recent history: the original process need not be complete before reflective uptake starts. The relevant temporal ordering concerns the particular registered material and its uptake, rather than a requirement that seeing stop before reflection can begin. It does not place a consciously viewed, unprocessed image before all interpretation: perceptual construction already shapes what is seen.

Table 1 makes the uses explicit. It records the roles of the terms rather than treating the companion draft’s vocabulary as a proof of its constitutive thesis.

#### Table 1: Terminology across the present argument and the unpublished October draft {#table-1}

| Term | Present paper | October recollective draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Occurrence | Actual taking place; a qualitative occurrence requires its own warrant. Retention is a separate question. | Something happening in a system and registered or retained by it; seeing can occur without being classified as experience. |
| Appearance | Actual qualitative character of a constructed presentation, including the joint relation targeted here. | No independent admission rule for this target is supplied; seeing can continue without reflective personal classification, while presence is identified with construction. |
| Interpretation | Perceptual construction and reflective uptake have distinguishable, possibly concurrent roles. | Uptake of retained material as prior to its interpretation; explicit concepts of time are unnecessary. |
| Experience | Used for a qualitative episode or mode unless a narrower use is specified. | Recollective interpretation of a retained occurrence as having occurred. |
| Personal experience | Appropriation and interpretation as happening to a person. | The further subjective or personalised layer organised through concepts, feeling and a constructed self. |
| Awareness | An ontological starting commitment, distinct from evidence for a specified local appearance. | The proposed undivided ground remaining when interpretative divisions are removed. |

The last column records that draft’s definitions and proposals, especially Sections 1, 5 and 7; it does not promote them to independently established results.

Part of the apparent tension is therefore terminological. The companion draft can withhold its defined word *experience* from seeing without reflective self-classification while leaving perceptual processing in place. This does not remove the acquired organisation shaping that processing. Part is a difference in scope. The present paper defends a local sensory-capacity projection and a contestable bearer attribution; the companion proposes an account of all experience and discusses artificial systems. Its Section 10 expressly acknowledges that the universal recollective thesis is not established by the relational-awareness results. In particular, allowing first appearance at current uptake, $J$, does not make every appearance a recollection or make every retained descriptor appear.

A substantive question remains after those distinctions. Section 7 of the companion draft argues that presence is no additional ingredient beyond the constructed process, and Section 8 declines a functional account that would leave presence as a further open fact. Those claims are not merely a preference for *personalised* over *subjective*. Their weakest reading denies a separate inner spectator; that is compatible with the present act/mode argument. A stronger reading identifies actual qualitative appearance with suitably organised retentive interpretation under the awareness premise. That identification needs substantive warrant. The present paper neither derives it for every interpreting system nor establishes an irreducible remainder that would refute it. The companion’s Section 7 itself records that the move from reflective classification to experiential constitution has not been proved, and that a defender of seeing without reflective classification as experience has not thereby been refuted. The precise unresolved choice is which constitutive identification is intended and what supports its extension beyond the locally warranted case.

The comparison also limits inferences about artificial systems. Section 8’s narrow verdict concerns interpretation and recollection without the human personal layer. It does not supply the present paper with an independent appearance anchor, jointness test or physical bearer identification for an artificial interpreter. Conversely, failure to replicate human autobiographical or emotional organisation alone would not exclude every possible appearance. Both claims concern the scope of an inference, not a verdict about a particular system’s consciousness.

### 2.16 Occurrence without recognition and the limiting memory question {#section-2-16}

Something occurring, its currently appearing, its recognition as a particular object or event, its appropriation as happening to a person, and its subsequent interpretation are distinct questions. They can overlap in ordinary life. The locally supported current presentation need not await a later autobiographical account, and no such account is required by the concrete-performance principle. This does not decide the companion draft’s universal identification of experience with recollective interpretation.

The limiting condition that removes all acquired scaffolding and the ability to form memory must be distinguished from suspending naming or personal recognition. Its proposed underlying basis does not itself specify an impression carrying a discriminating record. Something occurring and its recognition as an event are different claims; absence of recognition does not establish absence of every process or retained dependence. Neither the absence of a named object nor the inability to describe a condition entails that no change took place. Equally, the stronger limiting condition should not be replaced with an ordinary recognised scene and a failed report.

The positive recollective model in the October draft explicitly includes retention. Its Section 5 distinguishes such minimal retention from conceptual or autobiographical memory; its limiting seeing case withholds interpretative experience while preserving an occurrence. Those claims do not establish an unrecognised retained trace in the separate limiting condition that removes the ability to form memory. No retention mechanism for that stronger condition is supplied by this comparison.

The remaining explanatory question is specific. If later interpretations differ with a particular antecedent, what makes their dependence on that antecedent possible and their historical reference warranted? The claim that memory categories are supplied later does not by itself answer that question. The following elementary result identifies a stronger condition than absence of conscious, conceptual or autobiographical memory. It is a conditional diagnostic; it does not establish that the limiting condition actually satisfies that stronger condition.

#### Proposition 2.7: No antecedent discrimination without a dependence route {#proposition-2-7}

Let $A$ denote a nominated preceding condition. At the start of a later interpretation let $S$ include every variable available to that interpretation, including restored scaffolding, apparatus state and relevant continuing environment. Suppose $\operatorname{Law}(S\mid A=a)=\nu$ is the same for every admitted $a$, and a common stochastic kernel $T(\cdot\mid S)$ generates a later account $Y$ without a further $A$-dependent input. Then $\operatorname{Law}(Y\mid A=a)$ is the same for every admitted $a$.

**Proof.** For every account event $E$,

$$
\Pr(Y\in E\mid A=a)=\int T(E\mid s)\,\nu(ds),
$$

which is independent of $a$. An external input whose conditional law varies with $a$ would violate the stated completeness or common-kernel premise. $\square$

No conscious recollection, named object, faithful replay or material trace is assumed in this proof. The converse claim that every actual dependence must be a memory trace is not proved. A continuing external source, nonconceptual state or separately proposed nonphysical relation would constitute a different dependence route and would need to be specified and justified. Mere persistence of one undifferentiated basis does not yet explain discrimination among antecedents. Absence of an ability to form memory also does not by itself establish the independence premise for all these variables. The stronger no-dependence stipulation and the no-memory limiting condition must therefore not be silently identified.

If the apparent historical reference is instead entirely a present construction, the account must explain what warrants relating it to one particular antecedent. This is an open explanatory obligation, not an established retained-information mechanism. The current-act conclusion does not depend on choosing a resolution: ordinary current sensory evidence and the constitutive argument have their own stated domain.

The further proposal that fundamental awareness may be known as conceptual scaffolding falls away is philosophical and motivated by first-person experience. It does not follow from the impossibility of reproducing an occurrence in a description. The distinction preserves that proposal while keeping particular claims of occurrence, content, basing and bearer assessable.

## 3. Finalization, selection and physical access {#section-3}

The same retained record can support a provisional comparison, an eventual correct answer and a later current presentation. These are different targets. The source/readout contracts below distinguish these targets and establish accessibility constraints without dating first appearance. They show precisely what a physical record makes available, when an early value can be final, and how much information a later comparison actually requires.

### 3.1 Common prefixes and immutable finalization {#section-3-1}

Let $b\in\{1,\ldots,m\}$ index a later physical branch. Let $H$ denote an adequate observable history available before the branch, with common law $\mu$. A common nonanticipating kernel generates an early output from $H$ and independent private randomness. Then every branch has the same early output law: integrate that one kernel against $\mu$. This includes adaptive earlier operations if their full relevant controlled history is in $H$. Equality of displayed stimuli alone does not establish this contract.

Let $C$ be a nominated eventual content on a finite alphabet $\mathcal C$, $P_b(h,c)=\Pr_b(H=h,C=c)$ and $Q_b(c\mid h)=P_b(h,c)/\mu(h)$ when $\mu(h)>0$. An early flag $F$ with marker $V$ **finalizes** $C$ if, on $F=1$, $V=C$ in every branch under the stipulated contract. Its event must be determined early, not retrospectively defined as $\{V=C\}$. An immutable early value cannot have distinct certain eventual values across branches with a common adequate prefix. This does not prohibit provisional content or a correct early guess on a particular realized branch.

#### Theorem 3.1: Abstract common-mass maximum {#theorem-3-1}

If the joint early finalized submeasure is common to the branches, its mass is at most

$$
\alpha=\sum_{h,c}\min_b P_b(h,c).\tag{10}
$$

The bound is attained in the unrestricted finite class in which future completions may be jointly constructed to fit the prescribed $P_b$.

**Proof.** Write $f(h,c)=\Pr(H=h,F=1,V=c)$. Finality gives $0\le f(h,c)\le P_b(h,c)$ for every branch, proving the upper bound. For attainment take $f=\min_b P_b$. For each $h$, select early outcomes of masses $f(h,c)$ and a residual mass $\mu(h)-\sum_c f(h,c)$. On flagged outcomes force every eventual branch value to equal $c$. On unflagged outcomes use the normalized residual distributions $P_b(h,c)-f(h,c)$, using a joint conditional coupling if desired. Each branch has its required law. A null residual requires no further choice. $\square$

Equation (10) is established maximal all-equal coupling mathematics: the generalized Doeblin coefficient in Makur and Singh (2024, Theorem 2). The contribution here is its prefix/finality application and the distinction from fixed passive access, not a priority claim for the common-mass identity. Equivalently its elementary LP dual assigns nonnegative weights $\lambda_b(h,c)$ with $\sum_b\lambda_b(h,c)\ge1$ and minimizes $\sum_{b,h,c}\lambda_b(h,c)P_b(h,c)$; concentrating weight on a minimum branch at each atom certifies equality.

Pairwise overlap need not be global. On three content values, take

$$
P_1=(1/2,1/2,0),\qquad P_2=(0,1/2,1/2),\qquad P_3=(1/2,0,1/2).
$$

Every pair has common mass $1/2$, while $\alpha=0$. The history $H$ also matters: a fair history bit with $C=H$ in one branch and $C=1-H$ in another has identical marginal content distributions but zero joint history/content overlap. In contrast, dependence $H=b$ violates the common-prefix premise and can permit complete early finalization.

### 3.2 Fixed passive native apparatus {#section-3-2}

The attaining architecture above may engineer its future through $F,V$. Suppose instead a fixed apparatus has conditional future laws $Q_b$, and the early flag/marker is **passive**: conditional on the complete relevant pretime $H$, its private seed is independent of future innovations and $F,V$ do not influence future dynamics. $H$ excludes independent innovations that have not yet occurred; including their realized values would give premature access by definition.

#### Proposition 3.2: The passive zero-error optimum {#proposition-3-2}

In this class the optimal zero-error flagged mass is

$$
\beta=\sum_h\mu(h)\,\mathbf 1\{\exists c\in\mathcal C:Q_b(c\mid h)=1\text{ for all }b\}.\tag{11}
$$

**Proof.** If the early marker selects $c$ with positive mass at $h$, conditional independence makes its error probability in branch $b$ equal to $1-Q_b(c\mid h)$. Zero error requires this to vanish in every branch. Conversely flag every eligible $h$ and choose a common certain value there. No private randomization can turn an ineligible $h$ into an eligible one. $\square$

A single branch with a fair future coin and trivial history has $\alpha=1$ but $\beta=0$. Thus unrestricted coupling is not a capacity certificate for a passive physical controller. For a tolerance imposed conditional on each realized $(h,c)$ marker, eligibility is $\max_c\min_b Q_b(c\mid h)\ge1-\epsilon$. A tolerance averaged over flags or histories instead requires a different optimization.

For total branch error budgets $\varepsilon_b$, write $x(h,c)=\Pr(H=h,F=1,V=c)$. The exact passive optimum is the finite LP

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\max_x\sum_{h,c}x(h,c),\\
&x(h,c)\ge0,\qquad \sum_c x(h,c)\le\mu(h),\\
&\sum_{h,c}x(h,c)[1-Q_b(c\mid h)]\le\varepsilon_b
\qquad\text{for every }b.
\end{aligned}\tag{12}
$$

Necessity follows from conditional independence; sufficiency implements the selection probabilities $x(h,c)/\mu(h)$ with an independent seed. For two opposite deterministic branches, random guessing can share an allowed total error budget even though no individual marker is conditionally certain. An average max-min or sum-min expression cannot replace this LP.

### 3.3 Approximation and calibration {#section-3-3}

Given estimates $\widehat P_b$ and independently warranted bounds $\operatorname{TV}(P_b,\widehat P_b)\le\epsilon_b$, the unrestricted robust maximum is

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\max_{f,p_b}\sum_x f(x),\qquad x=(h,c),\\
&0\le f(x)\le p_b(x),\qquad p_b(x)\ge0,\\
&\sum_xp_b(x)=1,\qquad
\frac12\sum_x|p_b(x)-\widehat P_b(x)|\le\epsilon_b.
\end{aligned}\tag{13}
$$

If common history marginals are part of the contract, impose them as additional linear constraints. Absolute values can be represented by nonnegative slack variables, so this is a finite LP, not a floating-point heuristic.

For the cyclic three laws above and equal $\epsilon$, the exact optimum is $\min(1,3\epsilon)$. If a coordinate is zero in one estimated law, its common mass cannot exceed $\epsilon$, giving the upper bound $3\epsilon$. For $\epsilon\le1/3$, set $f=(\epsilon,\epsilon,\epsilon)$, and in each law put $\epsilon$ on its missing coordinate and $(1-\epsilon)/2$ on the others. Each change has TV $\epsilon$ and dominates $f$. At and above $1/3$ all three laws may be the uniform distribution. This is a synthetic sharp certificate, not an empirical error estimate.

More generally let early extended laws $(H,F,V)$ differ from a reference branch by at most $\delta_b$ in TV, and let branch finality error be at most $\varepsilon_b$. If $f_0$ is the reference finalized submeasure and $P_b$ the eventual joint law, then

$$
\sum_x(f_0(x)-P_b(x))_+\le\delta_b+\varepsilon_b.
$$

Indeed the early-law discrepancy bounds the positive difference from branch $b$'s finalized submeasure, and finality error bounds its deficit from $P_b$. Since $f_0-\min_b P_b\le\sum_b(f_0-P_b)_+$ atomwise,

$$
\Pr_0(F=1)\le\alpha(P_1,\ldots,P_m)+\sum_b(\delta_b+\varepsilon_b),\qquad \delta_0=0.\tag{14}
$$

Without warranted budgets the bound has no empirical force.

Report laws may replace content laws only through a defended common-alphabet coupling such as $\Pr(Y\ne C)\le\epsilon$, which implies TV error at most $\epsilon$. Correct task performance alone does not provide this coupling. The cyclic report laws are compatible with constant content and a branch-dependent physical report channel as well as with truthful varying content. These models require different finality inferences.

An imperfect occurrence witness need not be exhaustive. In this paragraph let $Z$ denote the target occurrence and $B$ its positive indication. If $\Pr(B=1\mid Z=0)\le a<1$, total probability gives the sharp bound

$$
\Pr(Z=1)\ge\max\left\{0,\frac{\Pr(B=1)-a}{1-a}\right\}.
$$

False-positive probability $a$ and sensitivity $1$ attain it. More general calibrated ranges $f\in[f_L,f_+]$, $s\in[s_L,s_+]$, $s_L>f_+$, give compatible prevalence bounds

$$
\max\left\{0,\frac{r-f_+}{s_+-f_+}\right\}
\le\Pr(Z=1)\le
\min\left\{1,\frac{r-f_L}{s_L-f_L}\right\},\qquad r=\Pr(B=1).
$$

Neither specificity nor sensitivity follows from ordinary task correctness; temporal and context transport require the separate bounds in [Module B, §2.4](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-b#section-2-4).

For a randomized physical intervention, let $(Z_0,Z_1,B_0,B_1)$ be potential outcomes on one justified coupling. If $\Pr(B_1\ne B_0,Z_1=Z_0)\le d$, then

$$
\Pr(Z_1\ne Z_0)\ge\max\{0,|\Pr(B_1=1)-\Pr(B_0=1)|-d\}.
$$

The report-change event splits into target-change and bypass cases; marginal contrast is at most its probability. Probabilities $.5$ for both changing, $.1$ for report-only change and $.4$ for neither attain the bound. Marginal response rates do not identify the bypass allowance or common coupling.

Calibration and sampling uncertainty impose separate obligations. For Bernoulli mean $r$, the centered log moment generating function has second derivative equal to a tilted Bernoulli variance, at most $1/4$, so it is bounded by $t^2/8$. Independence and exponential Markov inequality give tails $e^{-2n\epsilon^2}$. This proves the usual Hoeffding/union-bound intervals directly. Uncertain calibration needs its own simultaneous confidence budget.

### 3.4 Sharp sampling of the same completed episode {#section-3-4}

Sampling an episode differs from changing its mechanism. Let $p,q$ be two complete joint source laws and let one common conditional reader have event probabilities $k_x\in[0,1]$. If $p\cdot k\ge\ell$, the sharp transferred lower bound is

$$
\min\{q\cdot k:0\le k\le1,\ p\cdot k\ge\ell\}
=\max_{\lambda\ge0}\left[\lambda\ell-\sum_x(\lambda p_x-q_x)_+\right].\tag{15}
$$

The solution fills atoms in ascending $q_x/p_x$, possibly partially filling the last, and sets $k_x=0$ when $p_x=0$ unless irrelevant. To prove the dual, add multiplier $\lambda$ to the constraint and minimize each affine coordinate over $[0,1]$; sorting the ratios yields a feasible attaining point and multiplier. With multiple anchors $p_j\cdot k\ge\ell_j$, replace $\lambda\ell$ by $\sum_j\lambda_j\ell_j$ and $\lambda p_x$ by $\sum_j\lambda_jp_{jx}$. This is finite Neyman–Pearson/LP geometry, not a new measurement law.

For example $p=(2/5,7/20,1/4)$, $q=(17/20,1/10,1/20)$ and $\ell=2/5$ give $k=(0,3/7,1)$, hence minimum $13/140$. Here $\operatorname{TV}(p,q)=9/20$, so the coarse TV bound $\max(0,\ell-\operatorname{TV}(p,q))$ is zero. The improvement uses the full source distributions, not stronger phenomenal evidence. Independent input-only selection of the same completed physical episodes can preserve $k$; replacement of the substrate, later probe creation or different history need not. A selection that depends on unobserved response variables also need not preserve the stipulated conditional law.

For the displayed example, adding anchor $p_2=(1/10,1/10,4/5)$ with lower bound $9/10$ raises the optimum to $3/20$, attained by $(0,1,1)$. All anchors must concern the same conditional assignment. On a general source, write the absolutely continuous part of $q$ as $r\,dp$. Randomized selection gives the analogous infimum

$$
\int_0^\ell F_r^{-1}(u)\,du;
$$

put zero event probability on the $q$-singular part. This follows by the same exchange of equal $p$-mass from a higher to a lower likelihood ratio. The finite theorem already suffices for the examples.

### 3.5 Completing an inadequate source {#section-3-5}

Let a target variable $J$ take values in an alphabet of size $m\ge2$, and suppose the physical source chain is $J\to(R,M)\to\widehat J$, with the decoder also taking values in that alphabet. Write $\delta=\Pr(\widehat J\ne J)$ for its **actual error**. If only an upper budget $\delta\le e\le1$ is supplied, put $\bar e=\min\{e,1-1/m\}$. Fano's inequality and data processing give the correctly clipped budget bound

$$
I(J;M\mid R)\ge H(J)-h_2(\bar e)-\bar e\log_2(m-1)-I(J;R).\tag{16}
$$

Here $h_2(t)=-t\log_2t-(1-t)\log_2(1-t)$, with $0\log_20=0$. To prove the underlying actual-error bound, let $E=\mathbf1\{\widehat J\ne J\}$. Conditional on $\widehat J$, a correct answer fixes $J$, while an incorrect answer leaves at most $m-1$ possibilities. Thus

$$
H(J\mid R,M)\le H(J\mid\widehat J)\le h_2(\delta)+\delta\log_2(m-1).
$$

Subtract this from $H(J)$ and use $I(J;R,M)=I(J;R)+I(J;M\mid R)$. This actual-error form holds for every $\delta\in[0,1]$. Substitution of a larger budget needs care: the function $f_m(t)=h_2(t)+t\log_2(m-1)$ increases only up to $t=1-1/m$, where it equals $\log_2m$. Indeed its derivative on $(0,1)$ is $\log_2[(m-1)(1-t)/t]$. If $e\le1-1/m$, monotonicity gives the displayed budget bound. If $e>1-1/m$, the elementary bound $H(J\mid R,M)\le\log_2m=f_m(\bar e)$ gives it instead. At such loose budgets it may be uninformative. A fair binary target, constant source and constant decoder have zero information and actual error $1/2$: they satisfy the budget $e=1$, but one must use $\bar e=1/2$, not $h_2(1)=0$.

The minimum completion information for a general distortion measure is conditional rate distortion, provided encoding and decoding are unrestricted as specified. These are source-adequacy conditions; the variables' qualitative interpretation needs separate evidence.

For a fair bit independently erased in $R$ with probability $0<e_0\le1$, the optimal unrestricted reader need incur distortion only on erased cases. An allowed error budget $0\le e\le e_0/2$ gives the sharp minimum completion information $e_0[1-h_2(e/e_0)]$; it is zero for $e\ge e_0/2$, since a fair guess on erased cases meets the budget. Necessity applies binary Fano conditional on erasure in its increasing range; sufficiency transmits the erased bit through a binary symmetric channel of error $e/e_0$. At $e_0=0$ no completion is needed and no ratio $e/e_0$ is used. Exact binding of $n$ labelled positions has $n!$ alternatives and needs $\log_2(n!)$ retained information only under the stipulated zero-error complete-record convention, not $n!$ neurons.

For later random access, use the main article's notation: let $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$ be independent fair input bits, $W$ the complete retained state and $R$ an explicitly included auxiliary route or independent seed. The query index $J$ is uniform on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$, independent of $(X,W,R)$, and chosen after storage. A common reader answering bit queries with **actual average error** $e\in[0,1]$ must satisfy

$$
I(X;W,R)\ge n[1-h_2(e)].\tag{17}
$$

For a direct proof, let $\widehat X_i$ be the implemented answer to query $i$, with actual error $e_i$. At each fixed query the common reader is one stochastic kernel from $(W,R)$ to its answer, giving the Markov chain $X_i\to(W,R)\to\widehat X_i$. Any further decoder randomness must be independent of the whole $(X,W,R)$ conditional on that query. For binary variables the error indicator and $\widehat X_i$ together determine $X_i$, so

$$
H(X_i\mid W,R)\le H(X_i\mid\widehat X_i)\le h_2(e_i).
$$

Conditional entropy subadditivity and concavity of $h_2$ on its **whole domain** now give $H(X\mid W,R)\le\sum_i h_2(e_i)\le nh_2(e)$, since independent uniform query selection makes $e=n^{-1}\sum_i e_i$. Subtract from $H(X)=n$. No comparison with an optimal decoder, or monotonicity above error $1/2$, is used. A binary decoder that is always wrong has actual error one and can be inverted perfectly; the positive information requirement at $e=1$ is therefore consistent. If $e$ denotes only an upper budget instead, replace it in Equation (17) by $\min\{e,1/2\}$; a budget at least $1/2$ alone yields only the trivial bound zero.

An independent seed $R$ has $I(X;R)=0$, although its joint use with $W$ can improve decoding; it is not dropped from the joint information term. A further decoder seed independent only of $X$, rather than the whole $(X,W,R)$, can carry extra conditional information and is not free under this contract. If the query is known before storage, one stored bit suffices for that query. Source-correlated bypass information is counted in $R$ rather than treated as free randomness. Analogue noisy storage needs an operational information ceiling, not a count of mathematical real-valued states.

With exactly $K$ finite retained states and no bypass, uniform independent bits have optimal average distortion

$$
D_{n,K}=\frac1{n2^n}\min_{|A|\le K}\sum_{w\in\{0,1\}^n}\min_{a\in A}d_H(w,a).
$$

Deterministic encoders achieve the optimum, since mixing cannot improve a pointwise minimum. For $K=2$, let the codewords differ in $d$ coordinates. Expected nearest distance is $n/2-\mathbb E|B_d-d/2|$ with $B_d$ binomial. The absolute displacement of a symmetric random walk is a submartingale, so antipodal words attain the minimum. Thus $D_{3,2}=1/4$ and $D_{6,2}=11/32$. These exact finite examples do not establish a biological memory ceiling.

### 3.6 Persistent draws and adaptive threshold access {#section-3-6}

Let $X$ have one of two nonatomic laws $\rho_0,\rho_1$ on an interval and be retained across queries. Put $\nu=\rho_0-\rho_1$, and define

$$
D_L=\frac12\sup_{t_0<\cdots<t_L}\sum_{j=1}^L|\nu((t_{j-1},t_j])|,
$$

with the endpoints covering the full support. A $k$-step deterministic threshold decision tree has at most $2^k$ interval leaves; its transcript TV is exactly the half-sum over those leaves. Conversely balanced binary search realizes any ordered partition with at most $2^k$ leaves. Therefore adaptive and nonadaptive optimal TVs are respectively

$$
V_k=D_{2^k},\qquad W_k=D_{k+1}.\tag{18}
$$

Conditioning on an independent retained seed gives mixtures of trees and cannot exceed their maximum. Fresh independent draws would be a different experiment.

For $K=2^m$ equal bins with density $2$ on alternate bins under the two laws, each interval has signed mass magnitude at most $2/K$. Total positive and negative mass agree, so an $L$-interval partition has TV at most $2\lfloor L/2\rfloor/K$, capped at one. Alternating bin boundaries attain it: $D_L=\min\{1,2\lfloor L/2\rfloor/K\}$. Perfect distinction needs $m$ adaptive queries but $K-1$ nonadaptive queries.

The access norm matters. Define Volterra operators $Af(t)=\int_0^t f(x)\,dx$ and $A_Nf(t)=\int_0^{\lfloor Nt\rfloor/N}f(x)\,dx$ on $[0,1]$. The difference kernel has row and column integrals at most $1/N$, so the Schur bound gives $\|A-A_N\|_{2\to2}\le1/N$. Yet densities alternating on the $2N$ half-cells are completely sealed to grid-snapped thresholds and perfectly distinguishable by unrestricted threshold search of depth $\lceil\log_2(2N)\rceil$. Small operator error is not a sequential physical access bound.

With independent bit-flip noise $\eta<1/2$ on each threshold answer, repeat each of the $m$ adaptive comparisons $r$ odd times. If $e_r=\Pr\{\operatorname{Bin}(r,\eta)>r/2\}$, path success is at least $(1-e_r)^m$ and transcript TV at least $2(1-e_r)^m-1$. For $m=4$, $\eta=.1$, $r=5$ this exceeds $.932$. This is an achievable lower bound, not optimality of the noisy protocol. Bounds on unconditional one-query differences cannot be telescoped without controlling the conditional histories.

### 3.7 Reconstruction, current knowledge and timing {#section-3-7}

Fix $K$ distinct sites in a common geometry, with no three collinear, and use each once. The static contour joins consecutive sites. Two orders give the same undirected labelled path exactly when they reverse one another: choosing an endpoint fixes the whole traversal. Distinct edge sets give distinct embedded unions, since a segment cannot be covered by finitely many crossings with noncollinear other segments. There are $K!/2$ literal contours and $K!$ directed orders. Equality modulo alignment or rotation is a different quotient. Zero-error complete records need that many distinct states; four sites require $12$ contour or $24$ order states. Templates, coordinates and any external drawing archive are apparatus resources. A constant unordered inventory cannot track the independently selected contour beyond best-prior success.

A retained ordered word and a generic interpolation rule suffice to generate novel contours without learned names or a sensory movie. The same physical construction can assign a first presentation during integration or during later inspection, with a later current token in both accounts. Bounded displacement/uncertainty summaries likewise permit delayed reconstructions that preserve all nominated physical kernels. Equal likelihood leaves rational prior evaluation open. Spatial replay support requires actual content fidelity, not simply equality of a decoded average.

A genuinely current acquaintance with a relation does impose a current basis deadline. A report vehicle or stored answer alone does not establish such acquaintance. The main article defends a scoped current-human conclusion; these theorems delimit alternative mechanisms without dating first onset.

### 3.8 Common descendants and duplicate readers {#section-3-8}

For a deterministic finite source, exact output factorization $F=g\circ z$ implies $z(x)=z(y)\Rightarrow F(x)=F(y)$. A separator therefore needs at least the number of complete output classes. For $F(a,b)=(a,b,a\oplus b)$ there are four classes, so an XOR-only two-state record cannot recover the complete pair, whereas a two-bit record can. This sharp finite content constraint does not impose a human memory ceiling.

The scalar XOR coordinate has mixed difference $r_{00}-r_{01}-r_{10}+r_{11}=-2$. A fixed common-origin equality with no native comparison gives zero instead after independent preparations. In a finite causal graph unrolled over the fixed operation window, such mixed dependence requires an operation, possibly the output itself, to be a common descendant of both variations. Otherwise every output's ancestors omit one variation and mixed dependence vanishes. It does not require an earlier binding node or prove a passive presented relation must explicitly calculate XOR.

One retained pair can feed two separate readers performing the same local operation. A model assigning appearance both at integration and to each later current reader, and a model first assigning it to the later readers, have the same physical reports and genuine late acquaintance within their stipulated interpretations. Shared source does not make the reader tokens one scene. An outside-history freeze plus independently anchored current phenomenal variation under a local preparation establishes local involvement under supervenience on the nominated complete basis, not a sufficient or minimal bearer. A few fixed recorded channels are not the complete outside basis.

Likewise ordinary feedback $X'=Y$, $Y'=X$ gives causal sensitivity in both directions while a current mode determined by current $Y$ can remain unchanged under an $X$ preparation until the next update. Mutual sensitivity is not current constitution. For a report-rescue example, hold scene vehicles $X,Y$ fixed, let separate classifiers $C,T$ compute XOR at the next step, and let a later selector report one classifier. There is no classifier-to-scene feedback. Disabling $C$ removes its report; selecting $T$ restores the correct report for every pair. A declared joint-scene interpretation can leave its current relation unchanged. The physical counterexample separates classification from the stipulated scene; it does not observe preservation of actual appearance.

### 3.9 Repeated fields and historical dependence {#section-3-9}

Exact repetition supplies a further conditional connecting test. Take two registers $X,Y$, each with four active pair values and one inactive value. A load sets both to a supplied pair, reset clears them, native preparations set either register, and a probe copies the retained active pair. The update kernel is fixed. An early load and a later probe can leave the same $(v,v)$ state and hence all the same native future kernels. Their incoming operations and external phase/history differ.

If a deterministic occurrence law depends only on an independently adequate current field and is invariant under this native match, an accepted late current occurrence transfers to the earlier field. That is a one-step conditional result: applying the same function to matched arguments gives the same value. It does not promote an intermediate occurrence to a fresh knowledge anchor ([Module B, §2.12](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-b#section-2-12)), nor derive the needed psychophysical current-source law from future Markovity.

A fixed delayed rival uses the passive fact that the last nonidle operation was probe: it grants occurrence only for an active probed field. A controller bit storing that fact need not affect any module output. If included in the physical source, complete current packages differ; if erased, the law can depend on the causal-past fact instead. Arbitrarily long idle padding matches every fixed finite recent-history window without removing that one-bit historical distinction. Bounded record capacity is not a bound on influence duration. Omitted attention, boundary coupling or physical age can likewise invalidate the adequacy premise.

A different stronger match uses a bi-infinite periodic native process. For an eight-step closed cycle, positions separated by eight have identical relative material histories to every past depth. A deterministic history-covariant occurrence law gives the same occurrence there. An origin, age, external controller or environment tag is extra material data; if relevant it must be matched as well. A stochastic law gives equal probabilities rather than identity of actual occurrence tokens unless its relevant actual innovations are also matched. The recurrence tests therefore expose precise current/history invariance obligations; they do not earn those laws through repeated kernel equality or relocate a late anchor to an earlier time by themselves.

## 4. Retention, reset, recurrence and return {#section-4}

Persistence of accessible information, numerical continuity of an organism and occurrence of a current presentation are different claims. The models here isolate the first without assuming the others. Return arguments in the programme (Rodgers, 2026h,i) are used as source-adequacy tests, not as normalizations that determine otherwise unspecified phenomenal assignments. Records can survive, decay, return or be copied. Each mechanism gives a precise account of what remains accessible, and each leaves a distinct question about what currently appears.

### 4.1 Reversible records and finite recurrence {#section-4-1}

Classical XOR writing $(a,b,m)\mapsto(a,b,m\oplus a\oplus b)$ is a bijection. Applied twice it uncomputes the meter; endpoint identity does not mean absence of intervening dependence. A copy $(x,r)\mapsto(x,r\oplus x)$ with blank $r$ retains a basis value. A feedback latch and a copied archive may then have different later values; resetting scratch while keeping the archive has moved information, not destroyed it. A majority readout can be revised by a later input without revising the original archived token.

For any finite horizon $K$, an autonomous finite clock can perform integration and archival copy in its first three steps, apply identity padding through time $K$, and later uncompute on a finite cycle. Embed the program in the permutation $(c,x)\mapsto(c+1\bmod N,V_cx)$ with each $V_c$ bijective and a sufficiently long padded cycle. Its output can remain exact for $3\le t\le K$ without being absorbing. Program, blank preparation and clock are part of the apparatus, not free external resources.

#### Proposition 4.1: Finite recurrence rules out a strict permanent merger {#proposition-4-1}

Let $F$ be a permutation of a finite state space, and $\Pi(s)$ a state-local partition of a fixed labelled carrier set. If no class ever splits under one step, there is no strict permanent merger on an orbit.

**Proof.** The number of blocks cannot increase along the orbit. Finite recurrence returns to the original state and its original partition, so no strict decrease is possible. The orbit join $\bigvee_r\Pi(F^rs)$ is an invariant least common coarsening, but taking that join is an additional partition construction. $\square$

Open dynamics, changed carrier labels or time-dependent laws do not meet this premise. The proposition is not an objection to a new third relational presentation or to persistence of a person through changing presentations.

For a quantum clock $j=0,\ldots,n$, with controlled data steps $V_j$, take

$$
H=\hbar g\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\sqrt{(j+1)(n-j)}
\left(|j+1\rangle\langle j|\otimes V_j+|j\rangle\langle j+1|\otimes V_j^*\right).
$$

A clock-conditioned unitary gauge removes the $V_j$, leaving the spin-$n/2$ matrix $2\hbar gJ_x$. Transfer from $0$ to $n$ occurs at $T=\pi/(2g)$; at time $t$ the clock probabilities are binomial with parameter $\sin^2(gt)$. This follows by representing spin $n/2$ as the symmetric subspace of $n$ spin-$1/2$ factors. Padding yields a finite high-success interval. The norm is $n\hbar g$; bounded individual hopping scales require $g=O(1/n)$ and $T=\Omega(n)$. Adjacency of clock indices is not spatial locality of data couplings. This is a standard Hamiltonian-computation/perfect-transfer construction, not a permanent memory mechanism.

The limitation follows more generally from finite-dimensional unitarity. A finite subspace $\mathcal T$ invariant under a fixed unitary $V$ is reducing: finite dimension implies $V\mathcal T=\mathcal T$, hence $\mathcal T^\perp$ is invariant too. No state from its complement enters it. The same holds for self-adjoint continuous-time evolution. Approximate storage, open environments and timed decoupling require different contracts.

Parity premeasurement illustrates the same distinction. For $P_A=(I-Z_A)/2$, $P_B=(I-Z_B)/2$, the Hamiltonian $\hbar g(P_A+P_B)\otimes(I-X_M)$ at $\pi/(2g)$ writes classical parity into a blank meter. A source $|++\rangle$ becomes $(|E\rangle|0\rangle+|O\rangle|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$, where $|E\rangle,|O\rangle$ are even/odd Bell combinations. The local meter is maximally mixed; tracing it out removes inter-parity coherence, not selects an outcome. More generally conditional product environments leave overlap $\Gamma=\prod_i\langle e_1^{(i)}|e_0^{(i)}\rangle$. For branch amplitudes $\alpha_0,\alpha_1$, the trace distance from branch dephasing is $|\alpha_0\alpha_1\Gamma|$, by the eigenvalues of the off-diagonal $2\times2$ matrix. The product premise is essential; small global overlap does not make every environment fragment a readable record.

### 4.2 Exact integration-retention access {#section-4-2}

Consider a parity target written at exponential integration rate $\gamma$. Pre-write target contrast decays at rate $\lambda$ and post-write record contrast at rate $\mu$. Conditional on the first write time, the channels compose independently as specified. The retained contrast at read time $t$ is

$$
\begin{aligned}
r(t;\gamma,\lambda,\mu)
&=\int_0^t\gamma e^{-(\gamma+\lambda)s}e^{-\mu(t-s)}\,ds\\
&=\frac{\gamma(e^{-\mu t}-e^{-(\gamma+\lambda)t})}{\gamma+\lambda-\mu}.
\end{aligned}\tag{19}
$$

At equal denominator rates it is $\gamma t e^{-\mu t}$. Best binary accuracy is $(1+r)/2$. If two independent source bits are represented only by parity, best four-input identification success is $(1+r)/4$; one within-parity bit remains absent.

For $a=\gamma+\lambda$ and $\mu>0$, differentiation gives the unique peak

$$
t_* = \frac{\log(a/\mu)}{a-\mu},\qquad
r_* = \frac\gamma a e^{-\mu t_*},\tag{20}
$$

with $t_*=1/a$ when $a=\mu$. The acceptable set for threshold $\theta\in(0,1)$ is empty, one time, or a bounded interval according as $r_*<\theta$, $r_*=\theta$, or $r_*>\theta$. With $\mu=0$, $r(t)=\gamma(1-e^{-at})/a$: the limiting value is not attained at finite time. A deadline restricts the maximum to $\min(T,t_*)$ when $\mu>0$.

With $\mu>0$, optimized $r_*$ increases strictly with $\gamma$ when $\lambda,\mu$ are fixed. Set $v=\mu/a$; the factor $e^{-\mu t_*}=v^{v/(1-v)}$ decreases with $v$ because

$$
\partial_v\log[v^{v/(1-v)}]=\frac{\log v+1-v}{(1-v)^2}<0,
$$

with continuous limit $-1/2$ at $1$. Meanwhile $\gamma/a$ is nondecreasing. Limits as $\gamma\downarrow0$ and $\gamma\to\infty$ give $0$ and $1$, so every strictly intermediate optimized threshold has a unique critical $\gamma$. This optimized monotonicity need not hold at a chosen late time: faster writing can expose the record to decay longer.

#### Theorem 4.2: A robust saddle {#theorem-4-2}

Let rate uncertainty be a rectangular box with $\gamma_L>0$, nonnegative loss rates and $\mu_+>0$. Put $p_R=(\gamma_L,\lambda_+,\mu_+)$ and let $t_R$ be its peak. Then

$$
\max_{t\ge0}\min_{p\in\mathcal B}r(t;p)
=\min_{p\in\mathcal B}\sup_{t\ge0}r(t;p)
=r(t_R;p_R).
$$

**Proof.** The integral decreases with each loss rate. Fix their upper values and write $f(s)=e^{-\lambda s-\mu(t-s)}$. Integration by parts gives

$$
r=f(0)-e^{-\gamma t}f(t)+\int_0^te^{-\gamma s}f'(s)\,ds.
$$

If $\lambda\ge\mu$, then $f'\le0$ and differentiation shows $r$ increases with $\gamma$. If $\lambda<\mu$, then

$$
e^{\gamma t}\partial_\gamma r
=tf(t)-\int_0^t se^{\gamma(t-s)}f'(s)\,ds
$$

is strictly decreasing in $\gamma$, so $r$ has no interior minimum. Its limit as $\gamma\to\infty$ is $e^{-\mu t}$. Therefore for all $\gamma\ge\gamma_L$,

$$
r(t;\gamma,\lambda,\mu)\ge\min\{r(t;\gamma_L,\lambda,\mu),e^{-\mu t}\}.
$$

At $t_R$, formula (20) gives $r(t_R;p_R)=\gamma_L(\gamma_L+\lambda_+)^{-1}e^{-\mu_+t_R}$, no larger than that limiting value. Every model in the box thus gives at least the claimed score at $t_R$. Conversely $p_R$ belongs to the box, so no robust time can exceed its peak. The same inequalities give the minimax equality. $\square$

At any fixed time the exact box minimum is the lesser of its two integration endpoint values, at upper loss endpoints; acceptable windows intersect those two windows. With a deadline choose $\min(T,t_R)$: before $t_R$ the lower-rate score is still at most $e^{-\mu_+t}$, so the proof continues to apply. A correlated uncertainty set inside the box inherits its guarantee, not necessarily its exact optimum. For $\gamma\in[1.8,2.2]$, $\lambda\in[.35,.45]$, $\mu\in[.15,.25]$,

$$
t_R=\log3,\qquad r_R=\frac45\,3^{-1/4},\qquad
\frac{1+r_R}{2}\approx .80393427.
$$

The model units and threshold are stipulated, not human timescales.

An error certificate at arbitrary fixed $t$ is

$$
|r(t)-\widehat r(t)|\le t[\delta_\gamma+\max(\delta_\lambda,\delta_\mu)].
$$

The $\gamma$ derivative integrates $e^{-\gamma s}(1-\gamma s)e^{-\lambda s-\mu(t-s)}$, of absolute integral at most $t$. Changing loss rates along a line changes the exponent by at most $t\max(\delta_\lambda,\delta_\mu)$, while the remaining integral is at most $1$. Triangle inequality proves the result. It certifies a nominated task only if rate/error bounds and channel assumptions have independent warrant.

### 4.3 Marginals, fixed-time erasure and reset flags {#section-4-3}

Independent rate-$\nu$ flips on two bits and a common rate-$\nu$ flip have the same individual contrasts $e^{-2\nu t}$. It contains a triple, $a>b$, and each distinction's cause/effect union has cardinality four. Equation (28) gives $b$ for all 26 multi-distinction relations. The whole self-overlap gives $a$, and each triple self-overlap is its effect singleton, giving $b/4$. Relations sum to $a+27b$; distinctions sum to $a+4b$. Their total is $2a+31b=(6q^4+31q^3)L_q$.

For completeness, if a multi-distinction subset contains $m$ triples and includes $d_0$ with indicator $h$, its valid-face count is $(1+2m)3^h$: either no singleton effect is selected or exactly one is selected as effect or both; two different singleton effects cannot intersect. The whole distinction has three side choices. Each singleton distinction subset has one self-face. Counts by relation degree are as follows.

| Degree | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relations | 5 | 10 | 10 | 5 | 1 |
| Faces | 5 | 66 | 118 | 93 | 27 |

There are thus 31 relations and 309 faces. At $q=1$, the distinction sum is $7$, the relation sum $30$ and $\Phi=37$. $\square$

### 5.6 Symmetry does not select a spatial layout {#section-5-6}

All 24 permutations of native units commute with $T$ and preserve noise, state $0000$, selected distinctions and faces. Suppose a single four-point metric is determined solely by this native organization and respects these automorphisms. Pair transitivity forces every distinct pair to have the same distance $\lambda$. If $\lambda>0$, the three differences from one Euclidean point have Gram matrix $(\lambda^2/2)(I+J)$, whose determinant is $\lambda^6/2>0$. A faithful Euclidean realization needs dimension at least three, attained by a tetrahedron. A pseudometric may instead collapse all points. Likewise, an invariant nonempty strict line-betweenness relation is impossible because the group is transitive on ordered distinct triples.

This premise excludes independently specified geometry, asymmetry, state or history that breaks the automorphisms. It does not exclude a symmetric distribution over realized asymmetric layouts; such a law has an additional outcome/context input. The exact physical structure consequently establishes neither a line nor a general two-dimensional qualitative map.

## Spatial response, native equivalence and relational events {#section-6}

A spatial response can constrain a physical realization without settling whose experience it is. This module develops exact ways to tell those claims apart. Reciprocal conductance networks yield testable distance signatures. Complete relative stochastic processes can agree while responding differently to native interventions. Material clocks locate events without making coordinate labels into physical readings. Together these constructions sharpen the realization question that an account of appearance must answer.

### 6.1 One native edge and all-pair response {#section-6-1}

Let $L$ be the Laplacian of a connected undirected network of positive conductances on $n$ labelled native ports. Put $P=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^T/n$ and $G=L^+$, the inverse on the zero-sum subspace and zero on constants. For $b_{ij}=e_i-e_j$, resistance is $R_{ij}=b_{ij}^TG b_{ij}$. Adding conductance $\delta>0$ at native edge $(a,b)$ changes $L$ to $L'=L+\delta b_{ab}b_{ab}^T$.

#### Proposition 6.1: the complete resistance response {#proposition-6-1}

$$
R'_{ij}=R_{ij}-\frac{\delta(b_{ij}^TG b_{ab})^2}{1+\delta R_{ab}}.\tag{29}
$$

Every pair resistance weakly decreases, with equality exactly at zero transfer potential $b_{ij}^TG b_{ab}$.

**Proof.** Restrict to $\mathbf1^\perp$, where $L$ is positive definite. Direct multiplication verifies

$$
G'=G-\frac{\delta G b_{ab}b_{ab}^TG}{1+\delta b_{ab}^TG b_{ab}}
$$

there; both sides vanish on constants. Evaluate its quadratic form on $b_{ij}$. $\square$

This is the established rank-one resistance formula of Dörfler and Bullo (2013, Theorem III.14, Eq. 39), not a new network theorem. An embedded inverse with one grounded row/column set to zero is not itself $G$. Its zero-sum bilinear evaluations agree, and double projection gives $P G_{\mathrm{ground}}P=G$. Confusing these operators can corrupt an inverse or covariance calculation.

On a positive-resistance path, $R_{ij}$ is the sum of resistances along the unique interval. Decreasing one edge resistance by $\kappa$, less than its original value, decreases every crossing pair by $\kappa$ and leaves same-side pairs unchanged. For a unit path, a coordinate warp $f(i)=i-\kappa\mathbf1\{i>e\}$ preserves ordering when $0<\kappa<1$ and has exactly these distances. One downstream coordinate reader can therefore realize the same geometry without sharing the proposed network boundary. Represented coordinates are not occurrence times.

For four ordered sites $i<j\le a<b\le k<l$, a change in the middle edge contracts $R_{ik},R_{il},R_{jk},R_{jl}$ by the same absolute amount while $R_{ij},R_{kl}$ stay fixed. Transferring this signature to appearance requires fixed native counterparts and a calibrated linear distance map. A nonlinear reader, cyclic geometry or changed attention can alter it; four equal apparent contractions alone do not identify one neural edge.

For example, suppose each participant has one nonnegative absolute contraction $\kappa_i$ affecting training separation $S_i$ and outward separation $S_i+d$, with identical participant weights and a fixed linear reader. Their aggregate percentage ratio is a nonnegative weighted average of $S_i/(S_i+d)$, hence lies in

$$
\left[\frac{S_{\min}}{S_{\min}+d},\frac{S_{\max}}{S_{\max}+d}\right].
$$

Using $S_i\in[3.2,5.6]$ degrees and a $7.2\%$ training summary gives lower outward predictions $6.4\%$ at $d=.4$ and $5.76\%$ at $d=.8$. The $4.2\%$ and $3.6\%$ point summaries reported by Song et al. (2017) do not automatically corroborate that restricted equal-absolute-contraction model. This is not a statistical rejection: fitted optimum selection, uncertain group estimates, nonlinear calibration and graph geometry are outside those assumptions. No neural conductance measurement or raw-data fit is supplied by the comparison.

### 6.2 An exact inverse native-edge criterion {#section-6-2}

The full set of pair responses permits an inverse test that a few equal contractions cannot supply. For a symmetric zero-diagonal resistance matrix, $G=-PRP/2$. Indeed, $R_{ij}=G_{ii}+G_{jj}-2G_{ij}$ and $G\mathbf1=0$. Let $D=R-R'$, $B=-PDP/2=G-G'$, and suppose the baseline $L$ and native labels are independently known.

#### Theorem 6.2: when a response is exactly one native edge {#theorem-6-2}

A full exact response change is a single added edge $(a,b)$ with positive conductance if and only if

$$
LBL=c b_{ab}b_{ab}^T,\qquad c>0,\qquad cR_{ab}<1.\tag{30}
$$

Its conductance increment is $\delta=c/(1-cR_{ab})$.

**Proof.** Necessity follows from [Proposition 6.1](#proposition-6-1), with $c=\delta/(1+\delta R_{ab})$. Conversely, $B$ is centered and $GL=LG=P$, so (30) implies $B=cG b_{ab}b_{ab}^TG$. The strict bound makes $G-B$ positive definite on $\mathbf1^\perp$. Inverting that rank-one subtraction gives $L'=L+\delta b_{ab}b_{ab}^T$. A symmetric zero-diagonal distance matrix is uniquely determined by its centered Green operator: a centered-zero difference has form $\mathbf1u^T+u\mathbf1^T$, and its zero diagonal forces $u=0$. The complete response therefore agrees with the single-edge change. $\square$

Rank-one positive $B$ alone is insufficient. Take a four-port complete baseline with $L=wP$ and a small coefficient for source vector $v=(1,1,-1,-1)$. Set $B=cGvv^TG$. The inverse change is a multiple-source rank-one term proportional to $vv^T$; for sufficiently small coefficient, its off-diagonal entries still define a valid positive connected network. It contracts all pair resistances but $LBL=cvv^T$ has four-site support, not the two-site native-edge signature. Known baseline, full responses and native support are consequential hypotheses. Behavioral geometry fitted after the fact does not independently provide them.

### 6.3 Projected fluctuations and a retention tradeoff {#section-6-3}

Consider the reciprocal linear field on $\mathbf1^\perp$:

$$
dX=-LX\,dt+\sqrt{2D_0}\,P\,dW.
$$

Its stable covariance satisfies $LC+CL=2D_0P$, uniquely giving $C=D_0G$. Hence $\operatorname{Var}(X_i-X_j)=D_0R_{ij}$. Projection removes a freely diffusing common mode and supplies correlated noise; neither is an innocuous omission in another proposed dynamics. Directed drift or different noise needs its own Lyapunov equation.

For two units with conductance $w$, contrast $Z=X_1-X_2$ obeys

$$
dZ=-2wZ\,dt+\sqrt{4D_0}\,dB.
$$

Its mean decays as $e^{-2wt}$, stationary variance is $D_0/w$, and deterministic initial contrast gives variance $(D_0/w)(1-e^{-4wt})$. Initial variance contributes its own $e^{-4wt}$ term. Increasing $w$ both lowers stationary fluctuations and speeds free contrast decay at fixed $D_0$. A maintained input or another record need not obey that retention tradeoff; reduced variance does not make each noisy sample contract.

### 6.4 Equal relative laws, unequal native organizations {#section-6-4}

Compare three native systems with $n\ge2$ terminal units: a hidden-hub star, a complete reciprocal network and independent leaky units. Their free drift matrices are respectively

$$
L_s=\begin{pmatrix}wI_n&-w\mathbf1\\-w\mathbf1^T&nw\end{pmatrix},\qquad
L_c=wP_n,\qquad L_\ell=wI_n,\qquad
P_n=I_n-\mathbf1\mathbf1^T/n.
$$

Use projected white noise on connected graph modes and independent white noise for the leaky units, of scale $\sqrt{2D_0}$. Inputs are additive at terminals. Only $Y=P_nX_{\mathrm{term}}$ is observed.

#### Proposition 6.3: identical complete relative process laws {#proposition-6-3}

Matched initial joint laws of $Y$, independent future innovations and the same prescribed terminal inputs give identical complete joint relative process laws in these three systems. The conclusion also holds for one common well-posed nonanticipating output policy with independent private seed.

**Proof.** In the star, $P_nL_sX=wP_nX_{\mathrm{term}}$, since the hub term is common. The same identity holds for the other matrices. Projecting the star's connected-noise covariance onto terminal contrasts gives $P_n$, as do the other noise laws. All three thus have the same closed equation

$$
dY=-wY\,dt+P_nu(t)\,dt+\sqrt{2D_0}\,P_n\,d\widetilde W.
$$

Its explicit convolution solution proves equality for prescribed inputs. Under a common well-posed output policy, the same closed controlled equation and seed law give the same process law. Hidden/common variables never enter that equation. $\square$

Gaussian initial laws and deterministic inputs give Gaussian relative processes; arbitrary matched initial laws or nonlinear feedback still give equal laws but need not be Gaussian. In stationarity, pair variance is $2D_0/w$ and normalized time correlation $e^{-w|t-s|}$. Star and complete terminal graph resistances are $2/w$, but a leaky covariance does not thereby become ungrounded resistance of a disconnected terminal graph. Any physical ground must be specified in an expanded network. The hidden star mode $(1,\ldots,1,-n)$ has eigenvalue $(n+1)w$ and is orthogonal to all nominated contrasts. The systems have different native boundaries.

#### Proposition 6.4: a native hold distinguishes the organizations {#proposition-6-4}

A native hold of terminal $1$ at $1$, with all other units initially $0$, separates the three deterministic or conditional-mean responses. Unheld star terminals have first derivative $0$ and second derivative $w^2$ at zero; complete-network terminals have first derivative $w/n$; independent leaky terminals stay $0$. Held star and complete means converge to $1$, while unheld leaky means remain $0$.

**Proof.** Suppress the held terminal's update/noise and use the full physical drift, not a continuously imposed zero-mean constraint. Star symmetry gives an unheld value $y$ and hub $h$ with

$$
y'=w(h-y),\qquad h'=w[1+(n-1)y-nh].
$$

At zero, $h'=w$, $y'=0$, $y''=w^2$. Equilibrium is $y=h=1$, and the dimensionless characteristic polynomial $z^2+(n+1)z+1$ has two negative roots. The complete model gives $y'=(w/n)(1-y)$, hence $y=1-e^{-wt/n}$. Independent leaky means solve $y'=-wy$, $y(0)=0$. $\square$

Brownian sample paths have no such ordinary instantaneous derivatives; the statement is about means or noiseless paths. Positive-noise connected sample differences do not all converge to zero. Holding one unit changes the operator and common mode. It is not an additive input under the old free operator. The example therefore refutes inference of full native equivalence from complete nominated relative-output agreement, without assuming that no larger constructor presents a relational third.

### 6.5 Material event evaluation {#section-6-5}

Relational localization is useful independently of phenomenal attribution (Rovelli, 2002). Let $z:I\to M$ be a material worldline, $T$ a scalar clock and $R$ a scalar register. If $T(z(\lambda))=\tau$ has a unique root $\lambda_\tau$, define $O_R(\tau)=R(z(\lambda_\tau))$. Under a whole-package relabelling

$$
z'=\psi\circ z,\qquad T'=T\circ\psi^{-1},\qquad R'=R\circ\psi^{-1},
$$

this value is unchanged. A strictly monotone bijective reparameterization onto the same worldline image replaces the root by its image and also leaves the evaluation unchanged. Nonscalar registers require event-attached frames carried by the transformation.

A repeated or stalled clock may have several roots. An actual material episode counter/trigger can disambiguate them; a mathematical label is not a physical counter. Otherwise retain the full invariant set of matching events. No root makes the reading undefined, not evidence of absence of experience. For causal order, the metric and time orientation must transform as well:

$$
g'_{\psi p}(\psi_*v,\psi_*w)=g_p(v,w).
$$

Event localization alone proves neither a local-access law nor a canonical quantum Dirac observable.

Under an independently defended local-causal/nonanticipation contract, two adequate histories agreeing through the selected event have equal earlier register laws under one common kernel ([Module C, §3.1](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-c#section-3-1)). A complete invariant history is not a mechanism giving every history entry to a local observer. Gauge invariance neither establishes nor excludes a proposed psychophysical dependence on additional inaccessible history.

### 6.6 Prediction and feasible planning {#section-6-6}

A family admitting a publicly available exact binary prediction $p$ and a later complementary policy $A=1-p$ cannot have a universally correct predictor: $p=A$ would imply $p=1-p$. This diagonal argument is limited to the specified feedback policy family. It does not exclude hidden forecasts, probabilistic prediction or some knowable future actions.

Likewise, causal constraints do not forbid current multi-time planning. If eating $E$ requires prior baking $B$, feasible plans are $\{(0,0),(1,0),(1,1)\}$. One current deliberation may choose a feasible plan without independently recombining the impossible $(0,1)$. This limits the independent-recombination inference in agential discussion (Adlam, 2026); it does not explain an agent exercising agency at several events from a timeless epistemically symmetric standpoint. Current plans are not guaranteed knowledge that future actions will occur. The present construction is a classical event/access analysis, not a phenomenal bridge.

### 6.7 Optical geometry and worldly constituents {#section-6-7}

An object-involving account of perception must distinguish the identity of what is perceived from its qualitative determination. The following ideal geometry exposes that distinction without assuming an internal image is the bearer. Use a monocular pinhole at the origin and an image plane of focal length one. A frontoparallel planar disc with centre $(x,y,z)$, $z>0$, and radius $r>0$ projects to centre and radius

$$
\mathcal P(x,y,z,r)=(x/z,y/z,r/z).\tag{31}
$$

Indeed, a point $(x+a,y+b,z)$ with $a^2+b^2\le r^2$ lies on a ray whose image point is $((x+a)/z,(y+b)/z)$, giving exactly that circular mask. The concrete two-disc construction is

$$
\begin{aligned}
d_L&=(-3,0,3,3), &d_R&=(5,0,5,5),\\
\mathcal P(d_L)&=(-1,0,1), &\mathcal P(d_R)&=(1,0,1).
\end{aligned}
$$

Their material radii are three and five, but their projected radii are both one. For independently chosen positive scalars $\lambda,\gamma$,

$$
\mathcal P(\lambda d_L)=\mathcal P(d_L),\qquad
\mathcal P(\gamma d_R)=\mathcal P(d_R),\tag{32}
$$

since every numerator and depth has the same factor. The complete labelled two-disc geometric mask is thus unchanged, despite different material radii, depths and separation. The independent scaling is about the pinhole and includes each disc's radius, not just its centre.

The construction excludes binocular, motion, additional depth and occlusion cues and assumes frontoparallel planar surfaces. Illumination, luminance, reflectance and other photometry are not modelled. Equal untextured geometry does not by itself establish equal full retinal signals. This synthetic construction is not a fit or reanalysis of the contextual-disc experiment.

Replacing the two distal material tokens with different tokens in the same projection arrangement also preserves this geometry. An account under which those particular objects partly constitute the current perceptual engagement may therefore distinguish which things are perceived without yet determining how they appear. A *de re* constituent claim is not a law of qualitative co-presentation. Equal angular extent need not express equal intrinsic radius, and projection alone neither produces appearance nor establishes its jointness. Conversely, a retained bounded descriptor can generate a later relational presentation after the original objects are removed; that new third need not be current perception of the absent tokens. Object identity, optical supply, current engagement and qualitative appearance remain distinct targets. The main article's current-act argument supplies its separately defended occurrence and mode-placement warrant rather than deriving them from (31).

### 7.1 Identifying a measurement model without identifying appearance {#section-7-1}

Multiple indicators can identify a restricted latent measurement model even when their error rates are initially unknown. The binary case supplies an elementary instance of established latent-class identification (Allman et al., 2009); its phenomenal interpretation is a further question.

Let $Z\in\{0,1\}$ have prevalence $p\in(0,1)$ and let three binary indicators $W_1,W_2,W_3$ be mutually independent conditional on $Z$. Set

$$
f_j=\Pr(W_j=1\mid Z=0),\qquad s_j=\Pr(W_j=1\mid Z=1),\qquad d_j=s_j-f_j>0.
$$

The positive orientation removes latent-label permutation, but does not give $Z$ an independent phenomenal meaning. Write $m_j=\mathbb E W_j$, $C_{jk}=\operatorname{Cov}(W_j,W_k)$, and $T=\mathbb E\prod_{j=1}^{3}(W_j-m_j)$.

#### Proposition 7.1: Three-indicator inversion {#proposition-7-1}

Under these premises,

$$
m_j=f_j+d_jp,\qquad C_{jk}=d_jd_kp(1-p),\qquad T=d_1d_2d_3p(1-p)(1-2p).
$$

If all three pair covariances are positive, put

$$
r=\frac{T}{\sqrt{C_{12}C_{13}C_{23}}},\qquad p=\frac12\left(1-\frac{r}{\sqrt{r^2+4}}\right).
$$

Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
d_1&=\sqrt{\frac{C_{12}C_{13}}{C_{23}p(1-p)}},\\
d_2&=\sqrt{\frac{C_{12}C_{23}}{C_{13}p(1-p)}},\\
d_3&=\sqrt{\frac{C_{13}C_{23}}{C_{12}p(1-p)}},
\end{aligned}
$$

and $f_j=m_j-d_jp$, $s_j=f_j+d_j$ uniquely recover the oriented parameters. Recovered endpoints must lie in $[0,1]$ to be feasible probabilities.

**Proof.** Write $W_j-m_j=d_j(Z-p)+\epsilon_j$, where $\mathbb E(\epsilon_j\mid Z)=0$. Conditional independence removes mixed residual terms for distinct indices. The pair and triple centred moments therefore reduce to products of the $d_j$ times $\mathbb E(Z-p)^2=p(1-p)$ and $\mathbb E(Z-p)^3=p(1-p)(1-2p)$. Hence $r=(1-2p)/\sqrt{p(1-p)}$ and $r/\sqrt{r^2+4}=1-2p$, yielding the formulas. The seven nonconstant moments of a binary triple determine its eight-cell joint distribution. Feasible recovered parameters reproduce those moments and thus that distribution.

The inversion includes $p=1/2$, where $T=0$. It is singular at degenerate prevalence or uninformative channels and unstable near those cases. There are seven model parameters and seven observable degrees of freedom. A fit to one triple does not independently validate its conditional-independence or causal interpretation.

Transport across conditions provides additional restrictions only if the conditional channels remain fixed. Define product distributions

$$
A(w)=\prod_j f_j^{w_j}(1-f_j)^{1-w_j},\qquad B(w)=\prod_j s_j^{w_j}(1-s_j)^{1-w_j}.
$$

Then $Q_p=(1-p)A+pB$. Once a nondegenerate condition identifies $A,B$, new condition laws must lie on this one segment; a candidate prevalence predicts the whole joint law without new emission parameters. Direct subtraction gives

$$
\operatorname{TV}(Q_p,Q_q)=|p-q|\operatorname{TV}(A,B).\tag{33}
$$

If each actual conditional emission differs from its frozen version by at most $\epsilon$ in total variation, convexity bounds the actual mixture’s deviation by $\epsilon$. Two candidate predictions with respective full-channel uncertainty $\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2$ have disjoint permitted neighborhoods whenever

$$
|p-q|\operatorname{TV}(A,B)>\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2.
$$

Sampling uncertainty is additional. Dependence or omitted-source effects cannot be folded into a small drift bound without independent support. Failure of a transported segment restriction challenges the latent-and-measurement package, not just its proposed manifestation premise.

#### The common access gate {#common-access-gate}

Consider one fixed physical source with early relational representation $E$, access/retention gate $G$, and later available representation $C=EG$, all binary. Generate each $W_j$ independently conditional on $C$, with the same $f_j,s_j$. An early manifestation assignment associates appearance with $E=1$; a later one associates it with $C=1$. Neither assignment changes the source distribution, readout kernel or noise parameters. Their complete observable laws, and any equally specified physical intervention laws, are identical. Adding further indicators downstream of $C$ does not change that equivalence.

For independent $E,G$ of prevalences $e,g$, the moment method identifies $p_C=eg$, even under the early assignment whose appearance prevalence is $e$. Conditioning on early appearance leaves the shared gate:

$$
\Pr(W_j=1\mid E=1)=f_j+d_jg,\qquad\operatorname{Cov}(W_i,W_j\mid E=1)=d_id_jg(1-g).
$$

Conditioning on $C$ instead removes that gate and gives the declared conditional independence. Independent hardware noise therefore does not imply independence conditional on actual experience. Imposing the latter as an unearned appearance-facing premise can silently select the later assignment. Exact latent identification supplies a response/access class; independent target-sensitive warrant is still required to interpret it as occurrence. This construction is synthetic, not a fitted claim about any participant or a denial of early experience.

### 7.2 The archived Ebbinghaus experiment {#section-7-2}

Whitwell et al. (2023) compared size judgments, manual estimates and grasps using two centre discs surrounded by annuli of different sizes. The initial semi-formal staircase established each participant’s physical size difference $\delta$ at which the differently surrounded discs looked equal. In the public archived sample, $\delta$ ranges from 1 to 4 mm. Subsequent displays were of two kinds: physically unequal discs arranged to look equal (the experiment’s S configuration), and physically equal discs arranged to look different (D). These are experimental configuration labels, not independently measured appearance labels for each trial.

The response contingency assigned “same” to one side for each participant; “different” selected the other side. Board orientation varied. ### 7.4 From an answer to a current relational appearance {#section-7-4}

There are three distinct interpretive steps. First, the protocol asks how the discs look, and records an answer to that question while the sources remain available. The earlier contextual staircase and source-sensitive task history support the ordinary interpretation of a sensory comparison, rather than an arbitrary motor tag or a calculation of physical diameter.

Second, the minimum relational target is two qualitative apparent-size counterparts presented in a spatial or comparative arrangement. It does not require an additional irreducible “different” sensation, perceptual fusion of the discs, or veridicality. The ordinary current-scene interpretation of a simple paired visual encounter supports this togetherness defeasibly: the subject compares these apparent targets within their supplied spatial layout. The keypress code alone does not entail that interpretation. The experiment did not separately ask whether both counterparts were simultaneously presented.

A concrete serial-only alternative preserves the records: acquire one target’s appearance, retain a bounded magnitude/spatial descriptor, acquire the other, and compare the descriptors while never presenting a qualitative pair together. Ordinary comparisons of separately viewed objects can proceed this way. Its coherent possibility does not establish that it occurred in this simple current-pair setting. Documented serial-only strategy, source bypass with a credible denial of a current pair-scene, or other evidence of inference-only performance would weaken the current-scene interpretation. Serial eye sampling alone would not: later inspection may present a new pair. If it does, that new current field meets the target, even if earlier acquisition was wholly nonphenomenal.

Third, interpreting the comparison as using the presented pair is stronger than accepting occurrence. Source-directed instructions, calibration and ordinary task performance support the interpretation of an active visual comparison. They are not a separate intervention demonstrating that the qualitative relation caused the keypress. Passive appearance accompanying an automated answer remains a different challenge from serial-only appearance. The main argument defends the act interpretation and its mode placement separately; this archive provides no direct basing probe or anatomical bearer measurement.

### 7.7 A public feature-binding control and a restricted mixture test {#section-7-7}

Crayen et al. (2024) varied foveal and peripheral motion in a continuous-report paradigm. Participants reported the direction associated with a cued peripheral colour. Coherent central motion produces binding and misbinding conditions; ambiguous central motion provides another control. Connected, disconnected and yellow–blue layouts vary the configuration. Their geometrical comparison also changes eccentricity; it is not an isolated manipulation of continuity.

The public GRO.data release, DOI [10.25625/2RIA04](https://doi.org/10.25625/2RIA04), version 2.0, supplies 162 participant-condition summaries: 18 participants, three layouts and three motion conditions. The recovered `PERFORMANCE.txt` matches its repository checksum and is CC0. It contains mean performance, not trial direction marginals, trial counts, response latencies or histories. Consequently the following is a retrospective participant-level diagnostic, not a trial-level binomial fit or a measured phenomenal-error rate.

Consider a restricted selection model. With probability w, a coherent central direction governs the answer, giving accuracy 1 in binding and 0 in misbinding. Otherwise a common residual route succeeds with probability u. In the ambiguous condition the central route selects the correct direction with probability exactly 1/2, while w and u remain fixed across the three conditions. Its predictions are

$$
b=w+(1-w)u,\qquad m=(1-w)u,\qquad a=\tfrac12w+(1-w)u=\tfrac12(b+m).
$$

The midpoint restriction is therefore testable without treating w as a probability of consciousness. For participant $i$, set $e_i=a_i-(b_i+m_i)/2$. Paired t diagnostics use 17 degrees of freedom and Holm adjustment over the three midpoint tests. The estimates are:

### 7.8 Perturbation and sensory construction {#section-7-8}

#### Vernier integration and TMS {#vernier-integration}

Scharnowski et al. (2009) presented opposite-offset verniers for 30 ms each at one location. Participants judged the lower bar’s offset relative to the upper bar. Initial sign was randomized and pulse timing blocked and counterbalanced. Early occipital pulses favoured the second offset; later pulses favoured the first, through approximately 370 ms after onset. Single-vernier threshold controls, frontal stimulation and a focal-coil variant restricted particular artifacts and sites. The original experiment involved five participants; the frontal control involved four new participants. Small samples, common phosphenes, threshold sensitivity and connected-tissue effects limit anatomical interpretation. The full primary report and appendices were read; no trial-level TMS data were reanalysed.

The intervention establishes temporal susceptibility of retained relational processing. It does not measure first appearance at the pulse. A compact information restriction makes the distinction precise. Let balanced initial sign $S\in\{-1,+1\}$ influence retained physical state $Z$; let exogenous pulse $A$ act through a common downstream response kernel $K_A(Y\mid Z)$. If the two conditional $Z$ laws coincide, every pulse yields $\Pr(Y=S\mid\operatorname{do}(A))=1/2$. More generally data processing gives

$$
\operatorname{TV}\!\left(\operatorname{Law}(Z\mid S=+1),\operatorname{Law}(Z\mid S=-1)\right)\ge\left|2\Pr(Y=S\mid\operatorname{do}(A))-1\right|.
$$

This rules out complete sign erasure under the stated sufficiency and balance premises. It does not require two stored images: retain $Z=S$, one bit, and report $Y=S$ with pulse-dependent probability $p(A)$. Opposite second sign is then already determined. This fixed mechanism can reproduce any sign-dominance profile in this limited response family. No numerical human total-variation bound or fit is claimed.

#### Sequential neural decoding {#sequential-decoding}

Menétrey et al. (2026) released EEG and task material for sequential metacontrast. The repository inventory and selected source code were inspected; small behavioural MAT files were recovered and their table schemas examined, but opaque MATLAB table values were not decoded. Raw EEG, the full multi-gigabyte archive and participant decoding arrays were not downloaded or fitted. The inspected preprocessing includes condition, physical direction, response, correctness, time and reaction-time variables. Published late decoding uses correctness. For signed target $S$ and response $Y$, correctness $SY$ is not the direction content $S$ or $Y$; a classifier may carry agreement, certainty or decision quality. Fixed response mapping also aliases reported direction with motor choice. These are limits on interpretation, not a failed replication. A new direction/error-controlled analysis could test information mechanisms, but cannot distinguish appearance assignments sharing the entire physical law.

#### Cortical construction {#cortical-construction}

Beauchamp et al. (2020) used sequential electrical stimulation to produce reported forms in sighted and blind participants. Retinotopic receptive-field geometry in sighted participants provided one prediction route; measured phosphene locations provided another. These routes should not be conflated: phosphene calibration already uses experience reports. Spontaneous drawings before letter-label training support a sensory-form interpretation beyond mere learned naming. Dynamic/static comparisons concern the tested stimulation patterns and tasks, not every possible pair of currents or a universal capacity for static fusion.

Bosking et al. (2022) investigated multielectrode percepts and their spatial organization. Patterns containing separate reported phosphenes are relevant to minimum qualitative copresentation even without fusion into a recognizable shape. Their primary report and relevant pattern methods were inspected, together with the 2020 construction report. A stimulation schedule, retinotopic predictions and reported spatial patterns support causal content production by native visual organization. They do not identify the exact microphysical transition matrix, measure every instant of the reported field, or alone establish that the stimulated supplier is the whole experience-inclusive act. Complete microphysics is not a condition of the main macro-level inference; content supply is nonetheless distinct from constitution.

Parvizi et al. (2012) provides a further specific perturbation: electrical stimulation of human fusiform face-selective regions distorted face perception. Its value here is selective causal involvement in experienced content, not a general detector of relational appearance or proof that the stimulated population exclusively bears it.

### 7.9 Reports during REM sleep {#section-7-9}

Konkoly et al. (2021) demonstrated real-time communication with dreamers during physiologically staged REM sleep using prearranged signals and new questions. Arithmetic success establishes contingent communication; it is not by itself the specified sensory relational target. LaBerge et al. (2018) measured smooth tracking of dreamt visual targets, comparing lucid dreaming with waking perception and imagination. A moving single target and pursuit physiology do not by themselves establish two qualitative counterparts co-present in a field. The further inference from an actually presented, spatially resolved target is assessed in [Section 10.1](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-j#section-10-1); it uses additional appearance testimony rather than pursuit geometry.

Konkoly et al. (2026) used separate sniffing codes for dream visual presence/absence and dream-eye status. Eleven participants produced 150 REM-time signals; an additional signal rater was blind to initial ratings, dream reports and cues. The larger recruited sample was 13. Later narratives include relational scenes, but the online vocabulary encoded visual availability and eye status rather than the particular relations. Relevant methods, signal scoring and limitations were inspected from the author PDF; supplementary materials were not recovered and raw participant data were not analysed. Healthy-participant data and scripts are request-only; the narcolepsy data cannot be shared.

If a recorded signal completes at $b$ before awakening $w$, and its initiating judgment $j$ genuinely reports current vision, then some visual appearance occurs by $j\le b<w$. Presleep meaning, pre-awakening recording and blind signal scoring improve that current-report warrant. They do not separately place the narrative’s fingers/wall or person/cart relation at the binary signal. Nor do they exclude first presentation during dream-time inspection rather than earlier uninspected dream activity. This is a useful deadline for current visual appearance before awakening, not an estimate of first dream-scene onset or a calibrated bearer test.

### 7.10 Grouping, report-independent accumulation and motor correction {#section-7-10}

Vogelsang et al. (2024) investigated subjective grouping in sequential metacontrast alongside offset integration. Colour/grouping descriptions were collected around the experiment, with alternating-colour descriptions repeated at its beginning and end; they were not online onset stamps. Subjective segregation can coexist with measured feature integration. Numerical integration, phenomenal grouping and minimum joint presentation are therefore different targets. Two distinct objects may still appear together.

Stockart et al. (2025) compared immediate detection, delayed detection/confidence and passive viewing with intracranial high-gamma measures. Faces embedded for 600 ms produced accumulation-related activity during passive viewing too. Delayed reports occurred at least 1300 ms after face onset. The main methods and relevant results/discussion were inspected; the reported preregistration deviations and alternatives involving sensory encoding, covert decisions and task carryover remain relevant. Such activity cannot be identified exclusively with execution of an immediate keypress. Persistence without that keypress does not establish independence from every decision process or phenomenal onset at a decoder crossing.

The public OSF repository was inventoried before any proposed fitting. Seven small behavioural CSVs from one selected participant/session were recovered and matched repository hashes. Two passive blocks each contained 48 trials, a passive control 12, three delayed blocks 59 each, and an immediate/staircase block 40. Passive fields labelled physical high/low intensity; detection, response and response-time fields were missing and confidence was coded −1. Delayed blocks contained detection outcomes, confidence and response times. Thus passive “seen/unseen” or hit-like labels are not independent trial-level experience observations. The restored preregistration also records previously collected data and an accidentally deleted earlier file; its restored upload timestamp is not an independently verified original preregistration date. No raw neural arrays or full analysis code were replicated. These checked schemas restrict a proposed analysis, not the authors’ published neural results.

A shared physical accumulator can retain threshold status, a template index and confidence-relevant summary. A constructor can first present content at immediate or delayed inspection. The model needs neither a retained movie nor postponed early computation. If two interpretations share the full measured law, a better accumulator fit cannot choose their phenomenal assignment. Face detection also does not separately measure a specified joint relation between qualitative parts.

Merritt et al. (2017) compared online reach corrections in Parkinson’s disease and controls, with small and large target jumps and a separate displacement-judgment task. Corrections occurred under both access conditions. The judgment task was not a simultaneous trial-specific report of each reach’s scene. This result prevents generic successful online correction from identifying a current experience-inclusive act. An appearance-directed repair would require independent calibration of the operative comparative error and its role in correction. No such new intervention was performed here.

### 7.11 Affective counterparts and the limits of modality extension {#section-7-11}

Quevedo and Coghill (2009) studied synchronized five-second thermal stimulation in 13 participants, with hidden viewed sites, psychophysical calibration, intensity/unpleasantness scales and one/two-disconnected/two-connected pain classifications. The primary methods and relevant filling-in, radiation and connectivity results were inspected. Reported two-site or connected pain patterns support ordinary qualitative spatial testimony; they are not direct measurements of a nominated constitutive neural population. Classification followed overall rating, so it does not establish earliest presentation. Relative site-specific aversive pull, a relation’s intrinsic motive and actual relief priority were not measured. The philosophical distinction between unpleasantness, evaluative significance and actual desire matters here (Bain, 2019; $M$ørch, 2019). Neither a reported pain geometry nor an invented priority controller demonstrates that qualitative relations possess the controller’s causal powers.

These affective and dream cases broaden the evidence for native sensory capacities and sharpen temporal questions. They do not extend the physically delimited visual predicate $V$ to all modalities, sleeping states or artificial implementations. Each extension requires its own independently specified regime and appearance warrant.

### 7.12 What the combined evidence licenses {#section-7-12}

The archived case supports, at an ordinary defeasible phenomenological and testimonial level, a currently presented qualitative pair and relation by comparison uptake. Native-kind induction is physically specified and the current-scene interpretation is explicit. The transfer analysis restricts selected observable maps; the binding control rejects an exact restricted midpoint model; interventions and no-report results constrain particular physical processing accounts. None is a direct observation of literal mode placement. That further conclusion rests on the independently defended concrete-performance/mode principle and its actual application in the main paper. Earlier absence followed by a new current $J$ field is compatible with this scoped result. Serial-only presentation, passive accompaniment and exclusive bearer must be assessed at their distinct inferential steps rather than combined into one unspecified alternative.

## The physical act and its qualitative mode {#section-8}

The decisive question is what bears the appearance already warranted in a current act. This chapter develops the positive principle of concrete performance and mode placement, then makes its alternatives precise enough to test the inference.

Grounding, causation, realization and identity license different inferences. This module examines the alternatives that motivated the current-performance account and the positive principle that supports it. Its finite models isolate implications; their qualitative and epistemic interpretations are stated premises, not experiences generated by a calculation. The models concern the attribution of an actual act and the limits of extending that attribution. The finite checks described here are source-reported checks preserved from the publication.

Use $P$ for an independently nominated physical performance, A for an experience-inclusive act, and $Q$ for its actual qualitative mode. $R$ is a physically operative reason/control component, $V$ a bodily expression, $K$ a current acquaintance, and $K^*$ a composite knowledge performance. $B$ denotes a genuinely exclusive qualitative instantiator outside the proposed mode-bearing $P$. It is distinct from the programme’s unsplit-ground notation $U$. A numerically distinct property instance of $P$ is not such an exclusive bearer.

### 8.1 Current grounding and evidential parity {#section-8-1}

An actual relational presentation can be evidence even when its apparent objects are illusory. External inaccuracy is different from absence of appearance. Equally, a response about a relation need not itself contain the sensory counterparts. The appropriate parity principle is evidential: relational subject matter alone is no reason to disqualify otherwise credible current experiential evidence. It does not assign relational testimony the same error rate as unary-quality testimony. Retention, conceptual load and reconstruction can make relevant differences. Joint accessibility and phenomenal unity must also be distinguished (Bayne and Chalmers, 2003).

#### Nonfloating epistemic grounding {#nonfloating-grounding}

If $K$ is genuine direct current acquaintance with this $Q$, then $Q$ participates in its epistemic basis. A matching response generated independently of that basis is not thereby direct acquaintance with it. Under ordinary causal or synchronic constitutive ordering, an appearance wholly after $K$ cannot supply its present basis. The motivation is contact with this actual occurrence rather than an accidentally or inferentially correct proxy. It names no favoured neural stage and permits a newly constructed $Q$ during current comparison. Direct phenomenal concepts and their content-making basis are a substantive constitutional possibility (Chalmers, 2003; Balog, 2012); unchanged physical vehicles do not automatically preserve that basis.

This principle is compatible with noncausal justification. An abstract theorem can justify a belief without being a material part of it. Current qualitative acquaintance instead concerns a concrete instantiated basis, but concreteness does not by itself make that basis physical. A particular judgment also need not be knowledge merely because $Q$ occurs: its classification may be mistaken.

### 8.2 Anti-luck constraints and their physical antecedent {#section-8-2}

Safety is an independently motivated necessary condition on knowledge, relative to a belief’s basis and relevant alternatives; basis individuation is not settled by the principle (Pritchard, 2012). Let $x_0$ be an actual episode and $N(x_0)$ a defended local domain. For an assignment law $L$, let $M_L(x)$ indicate the nominated appearance, and let $B_L(x_0,x)$ mean that an intentional target belief is formed on the epistemically same basis. This relation need not be transitive. Let $Y(x)$ be a physical affirmative vehicle.

#### Proposition 8.1: Scoped safety constraint {#proposition-8-1}

Assume: (i) at $x_0$ the subject knows the current relational appearance obtains; (ii) within $N(x_0)$ every same-target, same-basis belief is true; and (iii) an independently justified physical relation $B^*$ satisfies $B^*\Rightarrow B_L$ for every retained assignment. Then

$$
\{x\in N(x_0):Y(x)=1,\ B^*(x_0,x)\}\subseteq\{x:M_L(x)=1\}.
$$

**Proof.** The lower relation supplies the relevant same-basis belief. If its target appearance were absent, that belief would be false within the safety domain, contradicting assumption (ii).

The proof is short because the substantive work lies in $B^*$. Preserving a circuit, checking disposition or physical string does not establish preservation of an experience-constituted basis. An ancillary variable with no physical effect on the reporting process could still enter a proposed appearance law; excluding its relevance needs a source/basis argument. A transported true occurrence also does not create fresh knowledge at the destination, so the proposition cannot be iterated as a truth-to-acquaintance induction. The explicit three-vertex failure and two repair schemas are in [Section 2.12](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-b#section-2-12).

#### Sound positive reports need not locate a bearer {#sound-positive-reports}

Let $C=\{(a,b,a\oplus b):a,b\in\{0,1\}\}$, and let distinguishable buffers $B_1,B_2$ each range over $C\cup\{\bot\}$. Define the common physical report

$$
Y(B_1,B_2)=\begin{cases}c,&B_1=B_2=c\in C,\\\bot,&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}
$$

One assignment associates a relational third with active $B_1$; another with active $B_2$. On every positive report both have the reported complete content. Disabling either buffer removes the report, so both physically matter. Yet at $(c,\bot)$ the occurrence assignments differ, and at unequal active values the content/bearer assignments differ. Both abstain publicly. Abstention is not an independently correct negative appearance judgment. This five-by-five model shows why positive soundness, diagonal anchors and difference-making do not provide occurrence completeness or unique carrier selection. Its actual-appearance labels are declarations used to test the inference.

### 8.3 A finite current scoped-carrier countermodel {#section-8-3}

Two candidate physical carriers can agree at every accepted current anchor without being physically interchangeable. Fix registers $u,v\in\mathcal P_2=\{0,1\}^2$, a bodily tag $b\in\{0,1\}$, and comparison mode $c\in\{0,1\}$. The registers are denoted $u,v$ here; the checker retains its historical register identifiers. The symbol $V$ denotes bodily expression and $B$ denotes the exclusive qualitative bearer. A state is $s=(u,v,b,c)$. There are thirteen native actions: idle $\iota$, two preparations each of $b$ and $c$, and four preparations each of $u$ and $v$. Their synchronous transition $T_d$ is

$$
u'=\begin{cases}p,&d=C^u_p,\\u,&\text{otherwise,}\end{cases}\qquad
v'=\begin{cases}p,&d=V_p,\\u,&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}
$$

The tag or mode is replaced only by its corresponding preparation. Crucially, ordinary copying into $v'$ uses the old $u$, including during a preparation of $u$. With $e(p)=\mathbf1_{\{p_1=p_2\}}$, the physical output is $y(s)=(v,e(v))$ when $c=1$ and $\bot$ otherwise. Both law extensions use this one transition/output process. Their hypothetical appearance assignments are

$$
\mathcal L_u(s)=\begin{cases}(u,e(u)),&c=1,\\\bot,&c=0,\end{cases}\qquad
\mathcal L_v(s)=\begin{cases}(v,e(v)),&c=1,\\\bot,&c=0.\end{cases}
$$

These stipulated charts test an inference; they are not measurements that the registers actually present qualities. Both allow first presentation only during the nominated active current comparison.

#### Proposition 8.2: Scoped agreement and off-anchor disagreement {#proposition-8-2}

The model has 64 states and 832 state/action entries. On the eight nominated anchors $u=v$, $c=1$, both appearance laws and the output agree. Across the remaining states, the laws disagree on counterparts in 24 states and on equality in 16 states.

**Proof.** There are $4\cdot4\cdot2\cdot2=64$ states and $64\cdot13=832$ entries. Four diagonal pairs and two tags give eight active anchors. Of the sixteen ordered $(u,v)$ combinations, twelve have $u\ne v$; with $c=1$ and two tags these give 24 counterpart disagreements. Each equality class has two pair values. Ordered cross-class choices number $2\cdot2\cdot2=8$, giving 16 equality disagreements after the tag factor. At $c=0$, both assignments are $\bot$.

Complementing both bits of both anchored registers, or changing the irrelevant tag, preserves agreement: the checker performs $8\cdot2\cdot2=32$ such checks, with repeated states rather than thirty-two new observations. Both registers have physical routes to reports. Preparing $v=p$ changes the immediate output to $(p,e(p))$; preparing $u=p$ and then idling changes the later output to that value. Each yields four distinct outputs. This establishes efficacy of physical values, not efficacy of phenomenal properties or genuine acquaintance under every action. At each fixed b, the anchors still contain four counterpart values, so b alone cannot determine their complete content under a fixed intrinsic map. This excludes an isolated tag, not a whole body containing the varying fields.

Let $h(u,v,b,c)=(v,u,b,c)$ and interchange the labels $C^u_p,V_p$ while leaving other actions fixed. For every action, $hT_d(s)=T_{h(d)}h(s)$ holds exactly when $u=v$: for idle/tag/mode actions the two sides copy different old sources off the diagonal; for a register clamp one retained coordinate still differs. Therefore

$$
\#\{(s,d):hT_d(s)\ne T_{h(d)}h(s)\}=13(64-16)=624.\tag{34}
$$

The proposed exchange is not a native interventional isomorphism. Carrier ambiguity here concerns two appearance extensions of common physics; it is not earned by physical symmetry. Off-anchor states have no independently accepted acquaintance metadata, so their differing labels do not establish differing experiences in validated interventions.

The full candidate packages are $\{u,c\}$ and $\{v,c\}$, because changing c changes either complete assignment even at fixed register content. Within an explicitly fixed active-c scope, saying “carrier u” or “carrier v” merely treats that mode as background.

#### Proposition 8.3: Robust container and shared parts {#proposition-8-3}

For an independently warranted nonempty family $\mathcal B$ of complete candidate bearer-part sets, a package $\Sigma$ contains a complete bearer under every candidate exactly when $\bigcup\mathcal B\subseteq\Sigma$. Its smallest robust container is the union. The intersection lists parts common to all candidates but need not be sufficient.

**Proof.** The containment claim is $B\subseteq\Sigma$ for every $B\in\mathcal B$, equivalent to inclusion of their union. Membership in the intersection says only that a part belongs to every candidate; it does not include each candidate’s remaining parts.

Here the robust union is $\{u,v,c\}$ and the intersection is $\{c\}$. Mode alone cannot determine the four anchored counterpart contents. The union result makes an inclusive current realization compatible with internal-carrier ambiguity, provided occurrence, candidate completeness and physical realization have independent warrant. It neither makes every union component constitutive nor identifies the container with the experience-inclusive act. An open-ended exclusive-bearer extension is outside this finite family. The whole-act/CMP argument below, rather than set containment or these label stipulations, supplies the paper’s separately defended constitutive attribution. The portable `check_scoped_carrier.py` enumerates the stated physical tables and candidate supports without treating them as consciousness data.

### 8.4 Causal exclusion does not fix the first stage {#section-8-4}

A causal argument has four separate commitments: actual qualitative efficacy toward a nominated physical effect; physical causal completeness for that effect; rejection of two ontologically independent sufficient causes systematically overdetermining it; and an interpretation of the remaining dependence as physical grounding, constitution or identity. The first three motivate pressure against an independently extra cause, but do not themselves prove the fourth or reduce qualitative property types. Causal completeness can support physical constitution while leaving reductive and nonreductive views open (Montero and Papineau, 2016).

Even strict event-token identity does not select an earlier stage. Independently fix input pair $(a,b)$, $c=(a,b,a\oplus b)$, and the physical chain

$$
I\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow J\longrightarrow R\longrightarrow V.
$$

At increasing clock times it computes, retains, retrieves and copies the tuple. Each of the first four stages has six controls: keep its incoming value, disable it, or prepare one of the four tuples. The memory therefore needs five values, not a sensory movie. In the early identity interpretation, active $I$ is an early relational presentation and active $J$ is a later presentation. In the delayed identity interpretation, $I$, $M$ are nonphenomenal for this target and active $J$ is its first presentation. Both identify their actual presentations with existing physically efficacious events, use the same transition functions and admit the same current acquaintance at $J$ when $R$ normally copies it. Serial antecedents are not independent overdetermining apparatuses.

There are $4\cdot6^4=5184$ controlled episodes, with no output disagreement. First-appearance assignments differ whenever $I$ is active, giving $4\cdot5\cdot6^3=4320$ cases. For each input, $J$ is active in $4\cdot36+29=173$ first-three-control combinations: four explicit active retrieval preparations, plus normal retrieval from an active memory; the latter has $4\cdot6+5=29$ cases. Normal copying by $R$ then gives $4\cdot173=692$ shared current-knowledge interpretations. These counts verify the stipulated finite rules; they neither certify knowledge nor show that the tuple has qualitative counterparts. The important result is that granting current acquaintance, closure, efficacy, no independent overdetermination and even token identity still leaves earlier occurrence open. A delayed third at $J$ meets the main target.

### 8.5 A reader model and a typed realization failure {#section-8-5}

A reported change need not be a quantified latent-appearance change without a reader restriction. For example, report occupancies .65 and .46 can coexist with latent occupancy .65 in both conditions: the first reader is accurate, while the second misclassifies a fraction 19/65 of positive cases and no negative cases. Its output is $.65(1-19/65)=.46$. Alternatively, accurate readers can track changed latent occupancies. This is an exact illustrative model, not a fit to participants. Adaptation and neural encoding may share the same physical relation variable under either assignment; their correlation does not supply an independently calibrated indicator error. Proposition 2.4 in [Section 2.5](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-b#section-2-5) cannot intersect different feasible assignment maps.

The analogous realization inference is invalid:

$$
\begin{gathered}
P\text{ realizes vehicle }V,\quad Q\text{ constitutes the content/basis of }K\\
\not\Rightarrow P\text{ realizes }Q.
\end{gathered}
$$

For a counterinterpretation, set $V=P$, let $\operatorname{content}(K)=f(V,Q)$, and let independently grounded $Q$ contribute the phenomenal dimension. Vehicle realization and content constitution both hold; a material bearer for $Q$ has not been supplied. A valid stronger premise would say that $P$ realizes the whole direct acquaintance token through a defended literal-constituent/mode-preserving relation. Giving its output vehicle the name “quotation” does not establish that premise.

Literal current embedding is nonetheless a substantive alternative. A complex can physically include current sensory submodules, a relation operation and a control frame rather than merely referring to missing targets. Live inclusion and a detached snapshot differ under post-loading native interventions. The proposal that such embedding constitutes conscious display is stronger than representational reference (Coleman, 2015). Its independently specified awareness role is still required; an unconscious controller can contain and use actual material counterparts. The constitution of phenomenal concepts can explain acquaintance while remaining neutral on physical versus nonphysical qualities (Balog, 2012). A new current counterpart token suffices: demanding the original source token would unnecessarily rule out imagery and reconstruction.

### 8.6 Possessed reasons, operative expression, and a mixed act {#section-8-6}

Possessing a reason while acting differs from acting because of it (Davidson, 1963). A person can currently apprehend a colour pair while an independent sensor makes their hand emit the same comparison. Truth and reliable agreement need not show which reason operated. The appropriate expression principle requires a suitable causal or constitutive participation relation when an utterance is explained as based on this particular current acquaintance. It does not require every external truth-maker or epistemic condition independently to move the body.

For an exact common-cause construction, fix physical input $x\in\{0,1\}$ and instructed motor code $c\in\{0,1\}$:

$$
R=x,\qquad V=R\oplus c,\qquad\operatorname{decode}(V,c)=x.
$$

Separately grant actual qualities $q_x$, genuine current acquaintance with proposition $\psi_x$, and a fixed law linking them to x. All four lawful rows have truthful decoded reports; physical input changes preserve safety and sensitivity, and code remapping preserves semantic content. If $P$ is their common source, these agreements do not establish a $Q/K\to V$ edge. A separate source test can exclude a stale record controller: replace $R=x$ with $R=b$, where b is independently variable, and vary b while independently preserving current $q_x$. The cached controller can be wrong while the current controller remains correct. The lawful common-cause escort passes the current-source test too.

The strongest rival improves on idle parallel knowledge. Grant a genuine contentful reason $R$, actual qualitative grounding and appropriate cognitive binding, and define

$$
K^*=(Q,R,\operatorname{Grounds}(Q,R)),\qquad V=f_c(R).\tag{35}
$$

$R$ is both a physical component of $P$ and an operative member of this one grounded act. $Q$ is a literal current qualitative constituent, but the exclusive-bearer interpretation assigns it to $B$ and denies that it is a mode of $P$. Whole-through-part attribution can make $K^*$ operative through $R$ without adding a second sufficient physical cause or making every constituent independently efficacious. This is not arbitrary grafting when the actual grounding and binding are granted; those are substantive premises shared with the competing interpretation. It contains one agent and one comparison.

An independent $Q$ override at fixed $R$ may remove factivity or make the report false, but it cuts the strict law linking them. It is not automatically an available native intervention or a nearby same-basis world. Removing $Q$ can leave a belief’s effect intact while changing knowledge to mere belief. Conversely, requiring $Q$ itself to form $R$ adds a causal-basing premise; even that relation can be mediated by a distinct bearer. Thus source-based expression improves act nomination but does not by itself identify the complete physical bearer.

#### Practical source use {#practical-source-use}

Deliberately selecting present apprehension rather than a remembered answer, knowing competent means and executing that undertaking normally can provide prima facie practical knowledge of the operative reason. This is stronger than merely feeling effective and is defeasible by bypass or confabulation. Ordinary task instructions support such an attribution less directly than an independently grounded practical claim. More importantly, the mixed act in (35) can genuinely use the selected reason through $R$. Practical source use identifies the actual activity; the claim that its whole qualitative basis is a mode of the physical execution still needs the constitutive rule developed below.

### 8.7 Categorical assembly and physical inherence {#section-8-7}

Russellian approaches motivate using qualitative or protophenomenal nature as the intrinsic ground of physical structure (Alter and Nagasawa, 2012). This promises explanatory integration beyond an arbitrary correlation, but requires separate steps. Local bases $b_i$, their natures and couplings $L$ may jointly ground component powers and, through composition, a whole native kernel. Grounding composition gives a plural ground; it does not give one presentation. The mental property whose causal role needs explanation must be distinguished from microphysical grounds (Baysan, 2025).

Fix four native units, all local grounds, couplings and intervention kernels. One extension admits a new relational field for each of the six pairs; another admits those fields plus a whole four-member field. Both can be permutation invariant and share one actual pair anchor. They differ in assembly while agreeing on physical power. If pair fields create fresh counterpart tokens, type symmetry cannot transport the same-token anchor. If they reuse the same tokens, every pair can share some field without one field containing all four, as [Section 2.2](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-b#section-2-2) showed.

There is a positive conditional repair. Let complete assembly-relevant data have automorphism group $G$, and let each fixed actual token $m_i$ have exactly one maximal owner $o(m_i)$. Assume pointwise covariance of same-owner relations and an anchored pair. Every pair in its G-orbit has equal owner values. If the resulting orbit graph connects all tokens, equality along paths gives one actual owner containing all of them. The proof uses the actual owner function, not existential pairwise transitivity. Complete categorical/material symmetry, fixed-token identification and unique ownership each need independent warrant; abstract native-kernel symmetry alone supplies none of them.

Even a directly proposed macro $Q$ grounding the whole power need not automatically inhere in $P$. The relations

$$
\operatorname{GroundFor}(Q,D(P))\qquad\text{and}\qquad\operatorname{Mode}(Q,P,\tau)
$$

are different. A distinct $B$ could primitively ground that power while denying qualitative inherence in $P$. A definition making categorical bases intrinsic properties of their power bearers would exclude this version, but the inherence premise would then be explicit. Grounding transitivity alone does not establish it.

### 8.8 Harmony and qualitative powers {#section-8-8}

Actual qualitative character fixes success conditions for comparison: judging which counterpart appears larger differs from producing whatever bit a controller emits. Unpleasantness supplies an intelligible reason for relief. This fit calls for an explanation. Its normative direction must still be distinguished from actual basing and from a physical causal power. Harmony arguments must also distinguish one global qualitative law from independently redrawn trial labels (Saad, 2022).

Let $G$ be a global appearance-law parameter and $Z$ a physical controller/genotype. Assume a product prior $\mu(G)\nu(Z)$ and physical-history likelihood $L(h\mid Z)$ independent of $G$. Then

$$
\Pr(G\mid h)=\mu(G)
$$

for positive-probability histories, because marginalizing $Z$ supplies the same factor for every $G$. This does not prevent physical selection increasing harmony within an already aligned world. For two genotypes with fitnesses four and one and initial shares 1/3, 2/3, the first share after t generations is $4^t/(4^t+2)$ under either law. Initial law/controller correlation or genuinely phenomenal evidence can change the law posterior; product independence is not a consequence of epiphenomenality alone.

For four independently ordered quality levels and one fixed permutation of four source levels, a single ordered-pair constraint leaves twelve of twenty-four bijections; repeating that noiseless pair leaves twelve, two disjoint ordered pairs leave six, and a spanning adjacent chain leaves one. Equality/difference preserves all twenty-four. The number 1/24 is a declared uniform-prior calculation, not an objective probability of fortuitous consciousness. A purpose-sensitive controller $a=b\operatorname{sign}(j-i)$, with instruction $b=\pm1$, can act appropriately under one fixed aligned quality law on every pair and both purposes while its physical arguments remain source and instruction. Flexible normative success alone does not identify $Q$ as its power.

#### A more direct powers proposal {#qualitative-powers}

Felt aversiveness makes relief-directed motivation intelligible from its character, rather than from a learned warning code alone. Experienced trying can nevertheless fail, so an intrinsic-power argument should distinguish a motive toward an attempt from successful movement ($M$ørch, 2019). Competing goals and independently specified enabling conditions matter. The pain-asymbolia objection must distinguish changed affective character from failed implementation; a shared pain label does not establish identical total phenomenology, and defining the relevant quality by withdrawal would beg the question. Seeking painful stimulation can express an opposing motive; unchanged aversiveness with intact enabling conditions, no opposing motive and no avoidance attempt would instead threaten strict necessitation. These are distinct philosophical tests, not clinical findings established here. A felt urge to settle a comparison can be an operative motivational facet without containing the complete sensory pair. Efficacy of that motive does not transfer to every qualitative constituent of the scene; a genuine power of $Q$ toward mental recognition also need not be a physical-report power. Fallible attribution of agency is a further reason to separate felt effort from its successful physical efficacy.

The stronger affective target makes the counterparts themselves aversive. Synchronized thermal-pair reports support jointly felt pain with connected/disconnected spatial content, including radiation at a neutral source location (Quevedo and Coghill, 2009). Those observations concern spatial appearance, not measured comparative aversive pull or relief priority. Felt badness, felt pressure, reason-giving force and implemented priority should not be defined by one another (Bain, 2019). For a proposed comparative-power test, relief alternatives require independently matched expected efficacy, delay, cost and side effects, understood targets and control capacity; the strongest felt pressure is not defined as whatever wins the motor competition.

An exact physical construction exposes the remaining inference. Set

$$
w=(a,dab,b),\qquad a,b\in\{0,1,2\},\quad d\in\{0,1\},
$$

and let a fixed selector choose the greatest active weight with a fixed tie rule. The central channel has no source there and has mixed difference one over $a,b\in\{1,2\}$ when $d=1$, so it is not a sum of separate source functions. At $a=b=2$, changing $d$ changes $(2,0,2)$ to $(2,4,2)$ and makes the central target dominant. Source-only fixed motives need current generation to account for that target. Yet even granting genuine physically efficacious qualitative modes to all active channels, an actual jointly presented field can be assigned either to the whole physical exercise or to exclusive $B$, with the same component powers and selector. A generated source-free motive does not prove whole-field inherence. No such gap-target priority was measured in the thermal study.

If the actual relational $Q$, in virtue of its qualitative nature, exercises the very causal power producing a nominated physical comparison effect, closure/exclusion provides additional placement pressure. Mental-only effects, grounds of another power and efficacy of a whole through one proper physical part do not establish that antecedent. The complete-act route avoids demanding a separate effect from every qualitative constituent.

### 8.9 Whole-act realization and literal mode preservation {#section-8-9}

Suppose $K^*$ genuinely guides $V$ through $R$, $Q$ is its literal current constituent or mode, and the whole $K^*$ has complete physical constitution. If the realization preserves those literal parts/modes rather than output-equivalent surrogates, a current physical act bears the actual qualitative basis. No separate $Q\to V$ edge is required. The difficult premise is complete whole-act constitution; physical sufficiency for $V$ realizes less.

The unrestricted rule “every whole with a physical effect is wholly physical” fails. For any effective physical $R$, a fusion $R$ + $B$ can inherit its effect while retaining a distinct constituent. The strongest mixed knowledge act makes $B$ genuinely epistemic, so dismissing it as arbitrary grafting begs the question. Restricting to minimal causal support would instead remove real constituents irrelevant to one tested output, losing the route’s purpose.

Immanent organization improves the nomination. Part roles belong to what the organized whole is doing rather than to an observer’s inventory: an input is this activity’s source, a state its reason, and a movement its uptake because of their participation in the maintained exercise. Hylomorphic accounts explicitly treat organization as immanent activity and perceptual engagement as coordinated exercise (Jaworski, 2020). The role description adds participation in the maintained activity to the specification of transitions. An immanently unified hybrid can also have genuine part roles without an external unifier, so a regress against separate unifiers does not establish exhaustive physical constitution.

Three relation types must remain fixed:

$$
\begin{aligned}
A=P &: \quad\text{token-performance identity},\\
\operatorname{Constitutes}(M,A) &: \quad\text{constitution, possibly }M\ne A,\\
\operatorname{Mode}(Q,A,\tau) &: \quad\text{literal qualitative mode in a phase.}
\end{aligned}
$$

From identity, $\operatorname{Mode}(Q,A,\tau)$ entails $\operatorname{Mode}(Q,P,\tau)$ by substitution. Constitution by numerically distinct $M$ does not transfer every predicate to $M$. Complete physical constitution may make the constituted A a physical bearer, but naming $M$ itself requires performance reidentification or an independently warranted mode-preserving realization relation. Neither identifies instantaneous $Q$ with an entire response window.

### 8.10 The corrected concrete-performance/mode principle {#section-8-10}

Process identity and multiple-description event individuation are established philosophical options (Place, 1956; Davidson, 1969). Mere systematic correlation is weaker than identity. Likewise, the stronger economy argument against irreducible psychophysical additions cannot simply be inherited when fundamental qualitative modes are permitted (Smart, 1959). The present principle therefore concerns actual instance placement, not a numerical event-saving proof.

#### Conservative mode placement {#conservative-mode-placement}

Given an independently established literal mode of a particular actual activity, presumptively assign it to that activity’s positively tracked concrete execution, at a fixed activity/phase grain and with its actual executing contributors included. Revise the attribution when independent evidence or a defended ontology identifies another instantiator, an omitted constitutive contributor, a merely supplied ground or a different activity. Descriptive vocabulary and irreducible property type alone do not establish a numerically different bearer.

The rule has three admission conditions. First, the mode is an actual way the activity is undergone or constituted, rather than only an external truth making a result correct. Second, the nominee is tracked through actor, source use, operations, uptake and participation history rather than output agreement. Third, the nomination respects known execution/phase counterexamples. These are grounds for a defeasible identity conclusion. “Complete experience-inclusive execution” must not be inserted into the physical premise.

Its positive warrant is continuity of a tracked instance across descriptions. A concrete property is a way an independently identified activity is; introducing a new property vocabulary need not introduce another performance. Positive execution paths distinguish this locus from its indicator, supplier or copy. At the same time, literal modes must not be transferred to every containing system. Consider the following corrections:

1. A client’s input/output history does not execute remote multiplication. If multiplication is delegated, the remote processor belongs to the calculation; if it merely supplies an operand, it need not be part of a subsequent local calculation.
2. A local signature can be the signing execution while protocol authorization additionally requires an actual remote verifier/ledger operation. Abstract validity rules are not automatically concrete parts; an actual completion operation is different.
3. Biological control does not close the boundary of a prosthetic grasp or instrumental performance. Include the actual jaws/contact or instrument when the selected act requires them.
4. Simultaneous saying and writing the same word can share actor, source, purpose and time while one vocal execution is only a member of the spoken-and-written whole. A continuously tracked single inscription instead supports bodily/intentional reidentification through actual stroke/mark participation.
5. A promise now is not its fulfillment tomorrow. Shared actor and intentional content do not erase the change of temporal grain.
6. A thermometer or copied inscription can track the same value/content without sharing the measured state’s instantiator or original execution. Common ownership and accuracy do not supply token identity.

These cases prevent selecting a desired bearer solely by actor, role or time. They supply constraints on the general rule rather than analogies proving qualitative reduction. In the main application, physically specified native sensory/comparison participation and independently ordinary joint-appearance/activity grounding converge on one current exercise. CMP supports reidentifying that experience-inclusive act with its organized physical engagement at the stated grain. That is the substantive, contestable extension; it is neither observed in a neural measurement nor a restatement that the desired stage appears.

#### A fair exclusive-bearer alternative {#exclusive-bearer}

The rival can retain one agent, one well-bound $K^*$, actual $Q$, physical $R$ and one simple generic content law. It denies that $Q$ is a mode of the nominated $P$. It need not add another complete comparator, physical force or separately fitted law for every trial. If the agreed observation laws coincide, their likelihood ratio is one; an independently defended instance principle can still supply a rational preference, but another fit of shared data cannot supply experimental ontology selection.

A copy or view paired bijectively with each performance token may merely redescribe that token. An ontically distinct qualitative trope u with $\operatorname{Instantiates}(P,u)$ likewise permits $P$ as bearer. The genuine rival adds $\operatorname{Bearer}(B,Q)$ and $\neg\operatorname{Mode}(Q,P,\tau)$, or an equivalent nonlifting constitution denial. That substantive placement needs its own individuation/grounding rationale. The immanent account also owes occurrence/content and extension laws; calling its primitive mode free would prejudice the comparison. Raw event or symbol counts depend on the ontology/coding language and do not settle these debts.

The rule can have modest actual warrant without eliminating every coherent rival. Its preference combines independent actual mode, positive role-sensitive execution nomination and a general placement discipline with correction cases. Absence of evidence for $B$ alone would be insufficient. An independently supported sensory executor outside the nomination, a bypassed reason, a distinct phase or a defended instantiator ontology can defeat or refine it.

### 8.11 Ownership, phase, and the broader programme {#section-8-11}

Identifying the ordinary experiencer with an animal identified through biological criteria can support literal ownership of current appearance (Olson, 2003). It does not establish which current organized event constitutes that appearance. A sole thinking-subsystem account can avoid duplicate subjects too. Moreover, strict current subject–animal identity has diachronic implications when the animal persists. The existing A3 perspective-continuation account treats a genuine qualification gap as ending a numerical experiential subject episode while biological/person continuity may remain (Rodgers, 2026e). These are different identity types. A subject-role or episodic-viewpoint claim is weaker than strict animal identity and must be named accordingly. Current CMP adopts neither a universal animalist persistence rule nor an equivalence with A3.

World involvement is also open at the correct grain. An external object need not itself perform the native comparison to be a constituent of the perceptual activity. Actual input, coupling and context should be explicit; the conclusion is not internal-only supervenience. Conversely, material source locations and represented locations are not the bearer. Direct cortical and neutral-site pain appearances constrain source-location shortcuts without fixing a unique current field.

The scaffold insight has a legitimate narrower role. Retained results can change future relevance, selection and interpretation; directed reconciliation of an actionable represented discrepancy differs from the mere existence of a physical difference. Thought produces a constrained representation rather than the source itself, and a successful local reconciliation can introduce new differences. This recursive development does not imply that every discrepancy controller has subjective will, that two communicating agents share one field, or that operational information is already qualitative (Rodgers, 2026c,a). It situates the present act within development while preserving the independently required occurrence/constitution distinction.

### 8.12 How the current connection differs from the conditional routes {#section-8-12}

The main inference separates occurrence projection from constitutive placement. Within the narrow physically nominated human regime, ordinary sensory experience, source-directed comparative practice and material continuity support projecting another current presentation. Prior task participation and sensory matching do not themselves provide dedicated measurements of jointness or basing. Present $Q$, seeing, current acquaintance and answer correctness are not eligibility predicates. The projected target is tethered to source-directed inspection/comparison, not just $Q$ somewhere in a task window. Biological/operational continuity and current participation are partly ordinary inferences, not complete neural measurements.

CMP then supports the actual activity’s reidentification and literal mode placement. It does not create the occurrence predicted by the first rule. A current-Q interpretation may subsequently support the prediction, but cannot retroactively select the eligible class. An eligible mistaken answer can coexist with joint appearance; a credible field-free eligible case counts against projection.

The three strongest objections therefore have different targets. A serial-only sequence with only a relation proposition challenges jointness. A passive current field accompanying an independently automated answer challenges the activity tether and nomination of the answer-producing operation; split or narrow the activities instead of widening a containing time-window. An exclusive-B hybrid can grant jointness and tether while challenging CMP. A delayed account that first presents both counterparts and their relation at current uptake $J$ satisfies the target and need not be defeated. Keeping these alternatives separate yields a positive current-act connection without asserting first sensory onset, universal machine admission, exact anatomy or reduction of qualitative types.

## Presented form, content signatures and current sensory basing {#section-9}

A single presented form can already give us a relation. A bent contour presents differently directed arms; an extended patch presents differently situated aspects. This chapter develops that minimum, calculates the exact distinctions a content law would need, and separates the current sensory basis of an act from the question of its physical bearer.

These contributions have distinct warrants: phenomenological and constitutive argument, a conditional mathematical theorem, and a defeasible epistemic principle. They sharpen what is presented, how its content is constrained, and what makes it the basis of a particular act. They do not replace the positive performance-identification argument in [Module H](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-h#section-8).

### 9.1 Relational aspects of one presented form {#section-9-1}

The minimum target requires qualitative counterparts and their relation together in an actual presentation. It does not require two independently existing objects, two original subjects, two input channels, or an overt comparison answer. One visibly extended patch can already have differently situated aspects; a visibly bent contour can have differently directed arms. Those aspects need not be numerically separate experiences or ontological parts of the bearer.

#### Principle 9.1: Finite spatial aspects {#principle-9-1}

If a current presentation genuinely has spatially extended or differentiated qualitative character, with sufficient presented resolution for at least two apparent localities or directions, then their spatial arrangement is an aspect of that presentation’s character.

The support for this principle is the distinction between presenting extent and merely signifying it. A nonspatial signal can mean “extended”, support a shape name, or control an accurate drawing. Those achievements do not make the signal itself visibly extended. What distinguishes the proposed antecedent is an apparent spatial organisation, rather than a label that refers to an organisation elsewhere. At the stated finite grain, differently located or directed features and their arrangement belong to how this one form appears. This is an account of presented character, not an inference from the geometry of a stimulus or an encoding.

The presented-resolution restriction does real work. An unresolved point, an undifferentiated shape-associated quality, or a sequence of separately experienced positions followed by an inferred shape need not satisfy it. Accurate recognition alone does not establish resolved aspects. A critic can also deny that apparent form has the proposed aspect structure; behavioral success does not decide that phenomenological disagreement. The principle does not infer a phenomenal token for every mathematical point, an infinite-resolution continuum, or the simultaneous appearance of every detail later accessible by shifting attention.

Once the principle is accepted for an independently warranted form token $a$, the target extraction is elementary. Let

$$
W=(a,F,i,j,q_i,q_j,r_{ij})
$$

record its actual form character $F$, two genuinely given aspects $q_i,q_j$ and their given arrangement $r_{ij}$. Then

$$
W\longmapsto(a;q_i,q_j,r_{ij})
$$

retains a witness to minimum relational appearance in the very same presentation. No inference combines separately anchored experiences into a new common field. This record already contains the relevant aspects; the projection is a certificate of their common witness, not a reconstruction algorithm that discovers jointness. The argumentative advance is the spatial-aspect justification for obtaining that witness from a form anchor.

This removes an evidential requirement for a two-object comparison answer. It does not establish that comparison, attention, drawing or covert inspection can be physically abolished while the same appearance survives. That causal preservation claim would require further evidence. A new form first constructed at current uptake $J$ can supply the witness: earlier sensory tokens need neither persist nor fuse. The spatial content also does not require a spatially congruent physical bearer. An exclusive qualitative bearer $\mathbb{B}$ could instantiate precisely this form. Aspect structure therefore clarifies jointness while leaving the distinct bearer argument intact.

### 9.2 The exact covariance contract for a content law {#section-9-2}

Let a finite group $G$ act on a set $X$ of physical descriptions and a set $C$ of candidate complete contents. Write $gx$ and $gc$ for the actions. An anchor supplies a particular $x\in X$ and $c\in C$. In a phenomenal application, the warrant that $c$ actually occurs, its subject, its phase and its relation to an activity must be supplied independently. Merely writing $c$ beside $x$ supplies no such warrant.

A deterministic law $F$ is equivariant on the physical orbit $Gx$ when

$$
F(gy)=gF(y)\qquad(g\in G,\ y\in Gx).
$$

The action has two importantly different interpretations. Under a **passive change of coordinates**, it redescribes one token; covariance is consistency between descriptions once token identity and the content chart are fixed. Under **active replacement, permutation of components, or transition to another token**, covariance is a substantive transport premise. An isomorphism of controlled physical kernels does not independently establish transport of actual experience. Only compositions of the specified $G$-actions are covered here. An additional intervention outside that family requires a new compatibility check.

For $x$ and $c$, define their stabilizers

$$
H=\operatorname{Stab}_G(x),\qquad G_c=\operatorname{Stab}_G(c).
$$

Thus $H$ consists of the transformations that leave the nominated physical description unchanged. The relevant obstruction is a member of $H$ that changes the particular anchored content; physical symmetry by itself is not an obstruction to every content law.

#### Theorem 9.2: Anchored orbit law {#theorem-9-2}

An equivariant map $F:Gx\to C$ with $F(x)=c$ exists if and only if $H\subseteq G_c$. When it exists, it is uniquely determined on $Gx$ by

$$
F(gx)=gc.
$$

**Proof.** If $h\in H$, equivariance gives $hc=F(hx)=F(x)=c$, proving necessity. Conversely, $gx=g'x$ implies $g'^{-1}g\in H\subseteq G_c$, so $gc=g'c$. The formula is well defined. Associativity proves equivariance, and the anchor and equivariance determine every orbit value. No assertion follows about a different physical orbit. $\square$

This elementary group-action result is useful because it makes a proposed content law answerable to a precise contract. It does not assume that equal observable likelihoods decide phenomenal identity, and it supplies neither the anchor nor the active transport premise.

### 9.3 An attained minimum content signature {#section-9-3}

Suppose the physical description omits distinctions needed for the anchored target. At the fixed base $x$, add a finite signature $z$ with an $H$-action and an $H$-equivariant recovery map taking $z$ to $c$. Put

$$
K=\operatorname{Stab}_H(c)=H\cap G_c.
$$

#### Theorem 9.3: Target-specific signature {#theorem-9-3}

Every such signature satisfies

$$
\operatorname{Stab}_H(z)\subseteq K,\qquad |Hz|\ge[H:K]=|Hc|.
$$

The bound is attained by $H/K$. A compatible augmentation over the entire physical orbit is

$$
G/K\longrightarrow G/H,\qquad gK\longmapsto gH,
$$

with recovery $gK\mapsto gc$. Each physical base has exactly $[H:K]$ signature values.

**Proof.** A transformation fixing $z$ must fix its recovered value $c$, giving the stabilizer inclusion. Orbit–stabilizer then gives the inequality. On $H/K$, the map $hK\mapsto hc$ is well defined and equivariant, and attains the bound. Since $K\subseteq H$, the displayed map from $G/K$ to $G/H$ is well defined; since $K\subseteq G_c$, so is recovery. Left multiplication commutes with both maps. The fibre above $gH$ is $\{ghK:h\in H\}$ and has $[H:K]$ members. Neither subgroup needs to be normal: these are coset sets with group actions, not quotient groups. $\square$

The count concerns distinguishable admissible signature states. It is not a count of neurons, material parts or measured memory bits. A fixed-width binary code would need at least $\lceil\log_2[H:K]\rceil$ bits, but actual geometry, history, boundary conditions or existing omitted variables might already supply the distinctions. The abstract construction does not show that a particular device can physically realize all its signature states. A proposed law that instead uses additional nonphysical inputs owes an account of those inputs; the theorem does not rule it out by definition.

If the target is coarsened by an equivariant map $\rho:C\to T$, use $t=\rho(c)$. Every transformation fixing $c$ fixes $t$, so $K\subseteq K_t=H\cap\operatorname{Stab}_G(t)$. Consequently

$$
[H:K_t]\le[H:K].
$$

Recovery of the coarser target can require fewer distinctions than recovery of complete content. It retains the group action, the anchor and the appropriate coset signature, rather than reconstructing a physical microstate or a finer phenomenal description.

#### Four interchangeable labels {#four-label-signatures}

Consider an abstract physical base fixed by all permutations of four labels, so $G=H=S_4$. Candidate contents are labeled relations on those four individuals. These are mathematical candidates, not claims that a four-port machine has experience.

| Target | $\lvert K\rvert$ | $[H:K]$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Strict line betweenness, unchanged by reversal | 2 | 12 |
| Unoriented four-cycle adjacency | 8 | 3 |
| Equal-distance complete four-point relation | 24 | 1 |
| Field occurrence alone, without geometry | 24 | 1 |

A strict order has 24 labelings, but reversing it preserves betweenness; there are $24/2=12$ distinct betweenness relations. A four-cycle has four rotations and four reflections, leaving $24/8=3$ adjacency relations. The complete equal-distance relation is unchanged by every permutation. Thus a line target needs twelve residual distinctions under this contract, whereas the square target needs three. Insisting on a complete oriented order would retain distinctions unnecessary for betweenness.

The invariant last row prevents a mistaken occurrence inference. Both the constant-present law and the constant-absent law are equivariant. A symmetry calculation therefore selects neither. An actual anchor together with independently warranted active transport would constrain occurrence on that transport orbit; the group calculation does not supply those premises. Nor does a signature’s physical carrier automatically become the bearer of the recovered appearance.

#### A nontrivial physical orbit {#nontrivial-physical-orbit}

For a separate check, let $S_3$ act on the unordered physical pair $x=\{0,1\}$, while the anchored target is the ordered pair $c=(0,1)$. Here $|H|=2$ and $K$ is trivial. There are three physical bases, six augmented ordered pairs, and two signature values above each base. Coarsening back to the unordered pair needs one. This example checks the construction when the physical base itself changes, rather than only at an $S_4$-fixed base.

### 9.4 Stochastic content and limited observations {#section-9-4}

A stochastic equivariant law at an $H$-fixed base has an $H$-invariant distribution. On a finite transitive content orbit $Hc$ of size $m$, every member has equal probability, conditional on that orbit. This follows because any two orbit members are related by an $H$-action, which preserves their probabilities. The conditional entropy is $\log_2m$. Different orbits may receive different total masses; uniformity across them does not follow. An intrinsically stochastic law can therefore have asymmetric realized outcomes without choosing a fixed preferred orientation. A deterministic description of the realized outcome would have to include its distinguishing draw, context or history.

For an unaugmented deterministic choice $c$ at an $H$-fixed base, covariance holds for exactly $|K|$ of the $|H|$ transformations. Its mismatch fraction under uniform enumeration is therefore $1-1/m$. For the line and square examples this is $11/12$ and $2/3$, respectively. These are symmetry-test fractions, not behavioral error rates.

#### Proposition 9.4: Limited observation of a uniform content orbit {#proposition-9-4}

Let a random content $Q$ be uniform on an $m$-member orbit, and let an observation $Z$ take at most $k$ values. For any observation channel and any decoder $d$,

$$
\Pr\{d(Z)=Q\}\le\min(1,k/m),\qquad H(Q\mid Z)\ge\max(0,\log_2m-\log_2k).
$$

The success bound is attainable.

**Proof.** The best deterministic decoder has success $\sum_z\max_q\Pr(Q=q,Z=z)$. Each summand is at most $1/m$, and there are at most $k$ summands. Randomized decoding cannot improve this linear objective. For $k\le m$, partition the contents into $k$ nonempty cells, reveal the cell, and choose one representative per cell: exactly $k$ contents are correctly decoded, giving $k/m$. For $k\ge m$, reveal the content itself. Finally, mutual information is at most $H(Z)\le\log_2k$, so $H(Q\mid Z)=\log_2m-I(Q;Z)$ gives the entropy bound, together with nonnegativity. Attainability of the success bound does not assert that the entropy bound is attained for every $m,k$. $\square$

Thus a two-valued observation cannot decode a uniform twelve-way line target with success above $1/6$, or a uniform three-way square target above $2/3$. These limits concern the stipulated information architecture. An uncounted analogue variable or history violates its observation-cardinality premise. The proposition does not identify experienced content with a decoded symbol, measure human memory, or select a phenomenal bearer.

### 9.5 Current basing without physical-port constitution {#section-9-5}

Current basing asks whether a particular actual relational presentation is the operative sensory basis of a nominated comparison, not merely an appearance accompanying a correct output. Let $B_{\mathrm{cur}}(Q,A)$ express that $Q$’s relational aspect is such a basis of the current activity $A$. This differs from identifying $A$ with an independently nominated physical performance $P$, or making a physical content vehicle a literal constituent of $Q$. Those latter questions concern constitution and bearer placement.

Four inputs keep the basing inference assessable:

1. An independently credible phenomenal anchor warrants a current $Q_J$ and its presented relational aspect $\rho$. Source geometry, a relation bit and answer correctness do not supply this alone.
2. Genuine current acquaintance $K_J$ addresses $\rho$ of this presentation. An equal-valued memory or another field is not the same source merely because its content agrees.
3. A nominated reason vehicle $R$ receives that comparative content nondeviantly, preserving its source and epistemic role. A start signal from $K_J$ followed by a cached answer is not such conveyance.
4. The nominated activity is the agent’s current exercise of that apprehension, with $R$ serving its authored comparative role. Ordinary source-specific practical knowledge, source selection, instruction-dependent retasking and checks for bypasses can support this application. Causal efficacy or acquaintance considered separately does not establish it.

These inputs separate phenomenal evidence, addressed epistemic contact, transmission history and practical use. Their conjunction is not established by defining the symbols. In particular the fourth input retains a substantive current-activity commitment; it cannot be inferred merely from a downstream controller’s success.

The proposed transmission warrant is ordinary and defeasible: when an agent genuinely apprehends a present relation, its content is conveyed without source substitution, and the agent knowingly exercises that apprehension in this current comparison, the relation remains an operative qualitative basis of the activity. The positive reason is source-guided execution, rather than accidental agreement between an output and a concurrent field. Failed source selection, misunderstood content, substitution or loss of the current exercise can defeat the inference. It is neither a theorem about physical bits nor a claim that every truth-maker must efficiently cause the action.

This warrant does not require $R$ to constitute $Q$. An explicit consistent countercase has one agent, an actual $Q_J$ borne exclusively by $\mathbb{B}$, genuine $K_J$, faithful delivery to physical $R$, and a current psychophysical comparison $A$ that uses this very apprehension. Then $B_{\mathrm{cur}}(Q_J,A)$ can hold even though $Q_J$ is not a mode of $R$ or of physical $P$. The rival expressly denies the transfer of the qualitative mode to $P$; merely assigning the name $\mathbb{B}$ to a property instance of $P$ would not make it exclusive. Identification of $A$ with $P$ remains the separate [CMP argument](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-h#section-8-10). This countercase establishes nonnecessity of port constitution for current basing. It does not show that every complete contract using port constitution is logically stronger than every contract using addressed transmission: their other requirements can differ.

#### Two substitutions that matter {#basing-substitutions}

A cached-answer route lets genuine $K_J$ trigger an action while a different record supplies its comparative value. This defeats faithful comparative conveyance. A different countermodel preserves genuine ancestry:

$$
Q_a/K_a\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow Q_b/K_b\longrightarrow R_b\longrightarrow\mathrm{action}.
$$

Here $M$ faithfully conveys the relation apprehended in $Q_a$, but a newly constructed $Q_b$ supplies the action’s current sensory basis. Both fields may genuinely occur and agree in content. Content fidelity and causal ancestry do not establish $B_{\mathrm{cur}}(Q_a,A)$. If $Q_b$ first appears at current uptake $J$, it may itself satisfy the target. The correct repair is to anchor and nominate that current field, not to demand persistence of an earlier one.

### 9.6 What a selective physical route contrast establishes {#section-9-6}

A physical contrast can strengthen content-provenance evidence while leaving the epistemic application explicit. Suppose a pre-gate code $r\in\{-1,+1\}$ has been nominated for a relational source. An outgoing gate either transmits $r$ or replaces it by a fair independent bit $k$. Let $\xi$ include all relevant pre-gate context and retained alternative routes. Conditional on $\xi$, the output has a stable mean $f_\xi(k)$. Gate assignment must average over the same conditional law of $\xi$ at fixed $r$; replacement must be independent of that context; and no gate flag, collateral timing change or feedback may reach the output except through $k$.

Under precisely this exclusion contract,

$$
\mu_{\mathrm{live}}-\mu_{\mathrm{repl}}=r\,\mathbb{E}\!\left[\left.\frac{f_\xi(+1)-f_\xi(-1)}{2}\right|r\right].\tag{36}
$$

For each $\xi$, replacement gives $[f_\xi(+1)+f_\xi(-1)]/2$; subtracting this from $f_\xi(r)$ proves the identity, after averaging over the common context law. Thus a direct-route controller with mean $\eta k$, $0<\eta<1$, has contrast $\eta r$. A controller that ignores $k$ and recomputes the same relation from a retained source has zero contrast, despite identical natural outputs. All output probabilities can remain positive.

The identity identifies a signed physical route contribution. It requires no reconstruction of the whole context. It does not identify a qualitative source, prove authorship, establish current basing, or label every individual episode in a heterogeneous ensemble. A gate inserted between $M$ and $Q_b$ in the preceding countermodel can affect the action even though $Q_a$ is not its current basis. Conversely, if $K_a$ is already independently warranted as the current comparison’s acquaintance, a gate after that uptake tests downstream expression; it does not discover the earlier basing. If the gate precedes field formation, separate anchors in the two arms can support separate ordinary applications, but not transport of one fixed pre-gate field.

The archival comparison in [Module G](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-g#section-7) contains no such selective outgoing gate. Its initial sensory calibration and appearance-directed instructions support the ordinary current-scene inference at their stated strength; jointness and basing were not measured by dedicated probes. Separating basing from port constitution therefore repairs an unnecessary premise without inventing additional validation. Epistemic ancestry, operative current basis and downstream expression remain distinct, as do ordinary warrant, physical intervention algebra and constitutive bearer attribution.

### Reproduction and verification limits {#reproducibility}

The publication identifies a separate archive, `Relational_Awareness_Reproducibility_v1_4.zip`, containing scripts, permitted supporting records, comparison outputs and instructions. Eleven checks can run directly: the eight inherited finite checks, the content-signature checker, the reduced neural-table refit/score check, and an added information/error checker for [Section 3.5](/consciousness/relational-awareness/module-c#section-3-5). Only that last, changed checker was newly executed for version 1.2, including from a fresh package extraction. It tests the large error-budget counterexample, clipped Fano range, actual-average-error readers and erasure endpoints. The other ten checks retain their successful version 1.1 execution records; they were not rerun in version 1.2. Versions 1.3 and 1.4 retain all eleven records without another scientific execution. Version 1.4 changes notation and three brief passages; its checks concern affected notation, references, rendered pages and compilation warnings.

Three inherited empirical analyses require separately obtained Crayen or Ebbinghaus data. Optional raw-prefix verification of the neural feature requires the separately acquired recording. Those analyses, the earlier contrast-only forecast and full neural extraction were not rerun in this revision. Their recorded outputs retain the earlier verification scope.

The archive README specifies commands, external acquisition routes, filenames, schemas, available hashes and licence conditions. Synthetic or derived comparison records are not additional independent data. The minimal neural table is derived from the CC0 source deposit and retains that attribution. Third-party raw datasets and papers are not included. The computational package as a whole is therefore not self-contained.
