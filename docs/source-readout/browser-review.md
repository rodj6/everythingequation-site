# Browser review

The built production HTML was served through the managed preview in a separate temporary mirror. The supplied website source and package scripts were not changed to accommodate preview infrastructure. This preview is local QA, not deployment.

Observed in the cloud desktop browser:

- The new programme entrance renders in the existing dark observatory style, with cyan accents, reading-path cards and the 7/5/3 publication structure.
- Full-text search for “minimal completion” returns actual article sections, and a result opens its complete foundational proof section.
- A foundational universal-completion section displays equations and proofs with the correct reader navigation and active contents item.
- The phase-conversion article exposes its complete contents and technical companion, and its bounded-conversion section displays source-vector formulas, matrices and full prose.
- Mathematical SVGs load successfully with nonzero intrinsic dimensions and accessible alternatives. Lazy assets may appear after the initial navigation observation.

This is representative desktop inspection, not an exhaustive browser visit to every chapter or a mobile-device run. The static production verification separately checks every new article and section route. Responsive layout and horizontal mathematical scrolling are implemented; device-specific testing remains advisable before publication.

The final styling aligns vector mathematics with the dark reader and uses scrolling mathematical panels to preserve readable scale rather than shrinking long expressions into small screens. Scientific source fidelity is recorded in the conversion audits.
