Purpose and conventions
This supplement preserves the mathematical, empirical and philosophical depth supporting
the main paper. It is organised by inference rather than research chronology. Module A devel-
ops adequate-source and calibration constraints; B treats occurrence, inclusion and ownership;
C distinguishes ﬁnalisation, access, sampling and resource contracts; D studies retention, reset
and return; E constructs and tests exact native causal ﬁelds; F develops metric and realisation-
identiﬁcation diagnostics; G records empirical methods and the retrospective application; H
preserves typed constitutive alternatives and countermodels. Each module states which con-
clusion its result can support and which connecting premise remains separate.

Dependency and reading guide. Readers assessing the main conclusion should begin
with the corrected concrete-performance argument in Sections 8.10–8.12, the actual archive
and its interpretive limits in Sections 7.2–7.5, and the presented-form and basing distinctions
in Sections 9.1 and 9.5. These develop the premises used by the main argument. The content-
signature theorem in Sections 9.2–9.4 is a separate conditional constraint on content laws, not
a proof of actual occurrence or bearer identity.
    For the main objections, Sections 8.3–8.5 separate causal, epistemic and bearer claims,
and Section 8.6 develops the mixed-act alternative. The pair/common-witness distinction
in Section 2.2 and the current-source counterexamples in Section 9.5 explain why separate
successful reports or faithful transmission need not establish one operative current ﬁeld. Em-
pirical provenance, exposure and supplementary analyses are in Module G; Module J provides
selected further applications and the ﬁxed predictive failure, with reproduction limits stated
at the end.
    Modules A and C–F provide optional technical investigations of adequate descriptions,
access, retention, native causal structures and metric recovery. Their full assumptions and
proofs remain available without being premises of every main-paper inference. In particular,
Sections 3.1–3.2 distinguish abstract common ﬁnalisation from a ﬁxed passive apparatus’s
early marking ability; their diﬀerence does not determine phenomenal onset. Readers pur-
suing those technical questions can follow their internal dependencies without treating the
supplement’s order as a cumulative proof of a universal manifestation law.
    The physical nomination P and experience-inclusive act A follow the main paper. Q
denotes a qualitative mode except in explicitly subscripted probability kernels Qb (· | h). H
is a causal-preﬁx history, C a nominated ﬁnal content, and µ a preﬁx law. Total variation is
TV(ν, ν 0 ) = supE |ν(E) − ν 0 (E)|. Finite models use sums; extensions to general spaces require

tution. Module J places selected empirical extensions and a failed ﬁxed predictive assay
beside the claims they constrain. Module B distinguishes occurrence, recognition, personal
appropriation and retrospective interpretation. These materials extend the argument without
changing the original archive into a fresh validation study or treating the open general
manifestation law as resolved.

1     Adequate sources, quotients and calibrated identiﬁcation
A physical description can preserve a response while omitting variables needed for another
response or for its interpretation. Adequacy is therefore assessed relative to speciﬁed ports,
preparations and readers. A quotient or statistic is not promoted to an experiential bearer
merely because it is suﬃcient for one task. These distinctions develop the programme’s source
and intrinsic-grain analysis (Rodgers, 2026h,i).

1.3   Exact qualitative equality certiﬁcates
Suppose independent calibration supplies exact same/diﬀerent qualitative judgments on nom-
inated tokens. Collapse the same-judgment components; diﬀerent judgments give edges of
a graph on the remaining k components. Compatible qualitative partitions are precisely its
proper colorings, with arbitrary unrestricted labels. Such data uniquely specify the partition
iﬀ this graph is complete: a missing edge permits those two components to merge; a complete
graph forbids every merge. Autonomy can add equalities through continuation, but a report
code alone cannot add calibration.
                                                                                
    For n original tokens and k classes the smallest certiﬁcate has n − k + k2 comparisons.
Every class needs a connected equality graph, requiring its size minus one edges; each pair
of classes needs an inequality. Spanning trees and one inequality per pair attain the lower
bound. A ﬁxed known number of qualitative labels imposes a diﬀerent coloring problem and
is not assumed here.
    With known metric prototypes separated by ∆, observation error at most ϵ uniquely
identiﬁes a prototype when ∆ > 2ϵ, by triangle inequality. If prototypes themselves are
inferred from one noisy labelled anchor each, same-class points can be 2ϵ apart and diﬀerent-
class points as close as ∆ − 2ϵ; separation ∆ > 4ϵ suﬃces. The latter threshold is sharp
without further information: at ϵ = .1, anchors .1, .5 and new observation .3 ﬁt centers .2, .6
with the middle point in the ﬁrst class, or centers 0, .4 with it in the second. Both prototype
separations are .4. Boundary equality does not secure uniqueness.
    Even a calibrated stochastic factorization can be nonunique. Let a report matrix be R =
ZD, with positive row-stochastic full-rank factors. For an invertible T near I with T 1 = 1,
factors ZT, T −1 D remain positive and row-stochastic for small perturbations, and have the
same product. They need not be label permutations. If D is independently known with full
row rank, a right inverse instead recovers Z uniquely. Factorization of public probabilities
does not calibrate the latent labels by itself.

Compatible descriptions of two qualities do not establish relational appearance. The counter-

2.7    Native articulation, manifestation, and categorical grounding
The earlier proposals motivate distinct constitutive laws. The following arguments also iden-
tify the premises each law still requires.

Native Articulation Law (NAL). The proposal assigns an elementary presentation to
each actual irreducible native transformation. Its motivation is immanence and structural
ﬁdelity: do not add a phenomenal fusion across independently operating transformations or
a phenomenal division inside a native prime without further lawlike structure. It addresses
organization rather than an ingredient list and needs no additional spectator. Its strongest
objection is carrier-centered: an irreducible physical event can still have several carriers with
separate qualitative states. Rejecting their experiential division already adopts the event-
centered individuation criterion. Native elementary-event grain and qualitative duration also
need independent speciﬁcation. A content map could be constant, so one presentation per
prime would not itself supply two qualitative counterparts and their relation.

Relational Manifestation (RM). RM proposes that a concretely realized intrinsic rela-
tion has a manifest counterpart preserving its distinction, with some common presentation
supported by its contributors. Ontological uniformity motivates the proposal: if intrinsic
qualitative terms are real, wholly unmanifest connections between them should not simply
be assumed. A serious rival nevertheless retains qualitative terms and real intrinsic relations

without their being given together; it need not introduce another substance. Uniformity
therefore supplies a reason to investigate the law, not a proof of its co-presentation clause.
   This proposal also changes the object represented by the family. If R lists required realizing

Intrinsic Qualitative Grounding (IQG). IQG adds: (i) actual concrete categorical char-

2.12    Native replacement and the corrected acquaintance condition
The fading/dancing-qualia argument supplies a cognitive-coherence motivation for organiza-
tional transfer (Chalmers, 1995). Its exact physical premise is stronger than boundary-output
agreement. Fix components, local states, native independent preparations, controlled kernels,
output ports, clock, initial law and environmental coupling. A product of local bijections and
a component permutation, with corresponding interventions, is a native isomorphism only
if every controlled kernel and those boundary/clock conditions commute. Induction proves
equality of all ﬁnite trajectory laws, including adaptive policies that choose corresponding
actions from matched prior outputs. The induction concerns physical trajectories; it does not
identify a detector with direct acquaintance.
    For example, F (a, b) = (b, a) and G(r, s) = (r ⊕ s, s) obey hF = Gh for h(a, b) = (a, a ⊕ b).

clamps fail: four ﬁrst-port failures have clamped value diﬀerent from a, and four second-port
failures have a = 1. No product bit-bijection with permutation conjugates them: under
such maps both swap outputs remain cross-only dependencies, whereas G has an isolated
self-dependent second component. Global predictive recoding is not a physical-copy theorem.
   Likewise, let a current/remembered comparator update (x, m)0 = (u, x), and suppose a
material chart sends both presented values to qz (v) = v ⊕ z. Switching z changes absolute
labels in all four states while preserving their presented equality. A change between tokens
does not itself produce a presentation comparing the old and new qualities. Occurrence
transfer is weaker than exact qualitative-chart transfer, but still needs its own warrant.
   The corrected no-silent-loss schema is especially instructive. Let rv mean occurrence, kv
genuine current acquaintance, Bv independently speciﬁed checking background, and Duv a

2.13    Awareness, access and the construction of a personal event
The awareness-ﬁrst account distinguishes the proposed knowing aspect of reality, denoted U in
the monograph, from the diﬀerentiated contents available through a particular apparatus and
history. This ground is not an additional localized subject. The monograph’s starting com-
mitment and the development paper’s operational distinctions have diﬀerent roles (Rodgers,

                                               18
2026e,c). The former proposes an ontological basis; the latter distinguishes a source, the
access available to an observer, and the representation constructed through that access. An
operational model can characterize dependence on apparatus or acquired constraints with-
out deriving the fundamental commitment. Conversely, granting that commitment does not
determine the extension of a particular experiential predicate.
   This distinction matters even if one accepts that all diﬀerentiated terms belong to one
underlying reality. Two diﬀerentiated processes may have separate perspectives; they may
contribute to a new shared presentation; or their interaction may introduce no additional joint
perspective. A premise about the underlying basis alone does not decide among those local
possibilities. The present paper’s current-act argument supplies a scoped positive attribution
rather than treating fundamental awareness as a substitute for its activity and bearer premises.
    Perceptual presentation is formed through bodily and sensory processing and acquired
organisation, including classiﬁcations, memories, expectations, feelings and beliefs. These
conditions shape the sensory presentation itself; they are not merely descriptions added after
a consciously viewed raw image. Ongoing seeing is perceptual processing, which can continue
when reﬂective naming, commentary or personal appropriation is suspended. Perceptual
construction, recognition and the sense of an event happening to me are diﬀerent functional
roles; they can operate concurrently within one activity. A practical history can therefore alter
the organisation of current access itself. In the development account, a relational product can
be retained and become available to subsequent interactions; it need not be a third substance
(Rodgers, 2026c, §3.1). Retained constraints then shape activity, and activity can revise
them. The reﬂective-alignment paper develops that reciprocity (Rodgers, 2026b, §4.1); its
operational description does not by itself establish one phenomenal subject.
    The personal observer is also constructed within this process. A central self-image orga-
nizes ownership, relevance and narrative continuity. The proposed reﬂective recognition of
its constructed character need not erase memory, practical identity or the usable self-model.
It changes the model’s claim to be the exhaustive centre (Rodgers, 2026c, §5.4). Whether
an artiﬁcial or biological implementation has such recognition as experience requires its own
warrant; recursive updating or an articulate self-report does not settle it (Rodgers, 2026j,b).

2.14    A conditional connection to the full constitution
The current-act argument is developed independently of assuming the full A1–A3 constitution.
A defensible application of SPC-2 requires a justiﬁed physical realisation and native operating
doctrine (Rodgers, 2026e, ch. 17). A1 assigns a perspective to a qualifying maximal internal
strongly connected component. Qualiﬁcation requires an executable covering return and at
least two distinct native predictive classes in the actual operating/protocol type and resource
context. Incompatible control modes cannot be combined into a ﬁctitious return. These
conditions are not strengthened here by a new requirement for language, autobiographical
reﬂection or a rich human scene.
    A2 assigns phenomenal relational organisation as a structural copy of the core’s complete
endogenous predictive object over all physically admitted ﬁnite continuations. The actual
phenomenal point corresponds to the predictive class of the realised intrinsic state. Native op-
eration and outcome labels, ﬁnite test laws, compatible horizon restrictions and well-deﬁned
class transition instruments are preserved. This is the full structural assignment, not a se-
lected ﬁnite report proﬁle or an investigator’s preferred time horizon. A3 then determines
episode continuation through nonbranching realised process provenance; matching memories
or outputs alone do not supply that continuity.
   To connect the present case to those laws, an appropriate phase of its performance would


                                               19
have to be shown to belong to a justiﬁed qualifying core, with the relational mode Q identiﬁed
as an aspect of the core’s current scene. A performance’s executing contributors and a subject’s
boundary are diﬀerent nominations requiring a bridge. An environmental source or instrument
can contribute causally without thereby becoming part of that subject. With the realisation
supplied and SPC-2 accepted, phenomenal states, their timing and continuation are assigned
at its declared native temporal resolution. No such complete application is demonstrated
here. The local current-act argument neither validates A2 nor independently selects the ﬁrst
onset of a scene, and it does not turn the subject boundary into the set of everything causally
involved in the performance. First presentation at current uptake, J, remains an admitted

2.16    Occurrence without recognition and the limiting memory question
Something occurring, its currently appearing, its recognition as a particular object or event,
its appropriation as happening to a person, and its subsequent interpretation are distinct
questions. They can overlap in ordinary life. The locally supported current presentation need
not await a later autobiographical account, and no such account is required by the concrete-
performance principle. This does not decide the companion draft’s universal identiﬁcation of
experience with recollective interpretation.
    The limiting condition that removes all acquired scaﬀolding and the ability to form mem-
ory must be distinguished from suspending naming or personal recognition. Its proposed
underlying basis does not itself specify an impression carrying a discriminating record. Some-
thing occurring and its recognition as an event are diﬀerent claims; absence of recognition
does not establish absence of every process or retained dependence. Neither the absence of
a named object nor the inability to describe a condition entails that no change took place.
Equally, the stronger limiting condition should not be replaced with an ordinary recognised
scene and a failed report.
    The positive recollective model in the October draft explicitly includes retention. Its
Section 5 distinguishes such minimal retention from conceptual or autobiographical memory;
its limiting seeing case withholds interpretative experience while preserving an occurrence.
Those claims do not establish an unrecognised retained trace in the separate limiting condition
that removes the ability to form memory. No retention mechanism for that stronger condition
is supplied by this comparison.

3.7   Reconstruction, current knowledge and timing
Fix K distinct sites in a common geometry, with no three collinear, and use each once. The
static contour joins consecutive sites. Two orders give the same undirected labelled path ex-
actly when they reverse one another: choosing an endpoint ﬁxes the whole traversal. Distinct
edge sets give distinct embedded unions, since a segment cannot be covered by ﬁnitely many
crossings with noncollinear other segments. There are K!/2 literal contours and K! directed
orders. Equality modulo alignment or rotation is a diﬀerent quotient. Zero-error complete
records need that many distinct states; four sites require 12 contour or 24 order states. Tem-
plates, coordinates and any external drawing archive are apparatus resources. A constant
unordered inventory cannot track the independently selected contour beyond best-prior suc-
cess.
    A retained ordered word and a generic interpolation rule suﬃce to generate novel con-
tours without learned names or a sensory movie. The same physical construction can assign
a ﬁrst presentation during integration or during later inspection, with a later current token in
both accounts. Bounded displacement/uncertainty summaries likewise permit delayed recon-
structions that preserve all nominated physical kernels. Equal likelihood leaves rational prior
evaluation open. Spatial replay support requires actual content ﬁdelity, not simply equality
of a decoded average.
    A genuinely current acquaintance with a relation does impose a current basis deadline. A
report vehicle or stored answer alone does not establish such acquaintance. The main article
defends a scoped current-human conclusion; these theorems delimit alternative mechanisms
without dating ﬁrst onset.

3.9   Repeated ﬁelds and historical dependence

5.6   Symmetry does not select a spatial layout
All 24 permutations of native units commute with T and preserve noise, state 0000, selected
distinctions and faces. Suppose a single four-point metric is determined solely by this native
organization and respects these automorphisms. Pair transitivity forces every distinct pair to
have the same distance λ. If λ > 0, the three diﬀerences from one Euclidean point have Gram
matrix (λ2 /2)(I + J), whose determinant is λ6 /2 > 0. A faithful Euclidean realization needs
dimension at least three, attained by a tetrahedron. A pseudometric may instead collapse all
points. Likewise an invariant nonempty strict line-betweenness relation is impossible because
the group is transitive on ordered distinct triples.
    This premise excludes independently speciﬁed geometry, asymmetry, state or history that
breaks the automorphisms. It does not exclude a symmetric distribution over realized asym-
metric layouts; such a law has an additional outcome/context input. The exact physical
structure consequently establishes neither a line nor a general two-dimensional qualitative
map.

6.6   Prediction and feasible planning
A family admitting a publicly available exact binary prediction p and a later complementary
policy A = 1 − p cannot have a universally correct predictor: p = A would imply p = 1 − p.
This diagonal argument is limited to the speciﬁed feedback policy family. It does not exclude
hidden forecasts, probabilistic prediction or some knowable future actions.
   Likewise causal constraints do not forbid current multi-time planning. If eating E re-
quires prior baking B, feasible plans are {(0, 0), (1, 0), (1, 1)}. One current deliberation may
choose a feasible plan without independently recombining the impossible (0, 1). This limits
the independent-recombination inference in agential discussion (Adlam, 2026); it does not
explain an agent exercising agency at several events from a timeless epistemically symmetric
standpoint. Current plans are not guaranteed knowledge that future actions will occur. The
present construction is a classical event/access analysis, not a phenomenal bridge.

7.4    From an answer to a current relational appearance
There are three distinct interpretive steps. First, the protocol asks how the discs look, and
records an answer to that question while the sources remain available. The earlier contextual
staircase and source-sensitive task history support the ordinary interpretation of a sensory
comparison, rather than an arbitrary motor tag or a calculation of physical diameter.
    Second, the minimum relational target is two qualitative apparent-size counterparts pre-
sented in a spatial or comparative arrangement. It does not require an additional irreducible
“diﬀerent” sensation, perceptual fusion of the discs, or veridicality. The ordinary current-scene
interpretation of a simple paired visual encounter supports this togetherness defeasibly: the
subject compares these apparent targets within their supplied spatial layout. The keypress
code alone does not entail that interpretation. The experiment did not separately ask whether
both counterparts were simultaneously presented.
    A concrete serial-only alternative preserves the records: acquire one target’s appearance,
retain a bounded magnitude/spatial descriptor, acquire the other, and compare the descriptors
while never presenting a qualitative pair together. Ordinary comparisons of separately viewed
objects can proceed this way. Its coherent possibility does not establish that it occurred
in this simple current-pair setting. Documented serial-only strategy, source bypass with a
credible denial of a current pair-scene, or other evidence of inference-only performance would
weaken the current-scene interpretation. Serial eye sampling alone would not: later inspection
may present a new pair. If it does, that new current ﬁeld meets the target, even if earlier
acquisition was wholly nonphenomenal.
    Third, interpreting the comparison as using the presented pair is stronger than accepting
occurrence. Source-directed instructions, calibration and ordinary task performance support
the interpretation of an active visual comparison. They are not a separate intervention demon-
strating that the qualitative relation caused the keypress. Passive appearance accompanying
an automated answer remains a diﬀerent challenge from serial-only appearance. The main
argument defends the act interpretation and its mode placement separately; this archive pro-
vides no direct basing probe or anatomical bearer measurement.

7.11    Aﬀective counterparts and the limits of modality extension

7.12    What the combined evidence licenses

8      Typed epistemic and constitutive arguments
Grounding, causation, realization and identity license diﬀerent inferences. This module ex-
amines the alternatives that motivated the current-performance account and the positive
principle that supports it. Its ﬁnite models isolate implications; their qualitative and epis-
temic interpretations are stated premises, not experiences generated by a calculation. The
models concern the attribution of an actual act and the limits of extending that attribution.
    Use P for an independently nominated physical performance, A for an experience-inclusive
act, and Q for its actual qualitative mode. R is a physically operative reason/control com-
ponent, V a bodily expression, K a current acquaintance, and K ∗ a composite knowledge
performance. B denotes a genuinely exclusive qualitative instantiator outside the proposed
mode-bearing P . It is distinct from the programme’s unsplit-ground notation U. A numeri-
cally distinct property instance of P is not such an exclusive bearer.

8.1   Current grounding and evidential parity
An actual relational presentation can be evidence even when its apparent objects are illusory.
External inaccuracy is diﬀerent from absence of appearance. Equally, a response about a
relation need not itself contain the sensory counterparts. The appropriate parity principle
is evidential: relational subject matter alone is no reason to disqualify otherwise credible
current experiential evidence. It does not assign relational testimony the same error rate as
unary-quality testimony. Retention, conceptual load and reconstruction can make relevant
diﬀerences. Joint accessibility and phenomenal unity must also be distinguished (Bayne and
Chalmers, 2003).

Nonﬂoating epistemic grounding. If K is genuine direct current acquaintance with this
Q, then Q participates in its epistemic basis. A matching response generated independently
of that basis is not thereby direct acquaintance with it. Under ordinary causal or synchronic
constitutive ordering, an appearance wholly after K cannot supply its present basis. The
motivation is contact with this actual occurrence rather than an accidentally or inferentially
correct proxy. It names no favoured neural stage and permits a newly constructed Q during
current comparison. Direct phenomenal concepts and their content-making basis are a sub-
stantive constitutional possibility (Chalmers, 2003; Balog, 2012); unchanged physical vehicles
do not automatically preserve that basis.
    This principle is compatible with noncausal justiﬁcation. An abstract theorem can justify
a belief without being a material part of it. Current qualitative acquaintance instead concerns
a concrete instantiated basis, but concreteness does not by itself make that basis physical. A
particular judgment also need not be knowledge merely because Q occurs: its classiﬁcation
may be mistaken.

8.4   Causal exclusion does not ﬁx the ﬁrst stage
A causal argument has four separate commitments: actual qualitative eﬃcacy toward a nomi-
nated physical eﬀect; physical causal completeness for that eﬀect; rejection of two ontologically
independent suﬃcient causes systematically overdetermining it; and an interpretation of the
remaining dependence as physical grounding, constitution or identity. The ﬁrst three moti-
vate pressure against an independently extra cause, but do not themselves prove the fourth
or reduce qualitative property types. Causal completeness can support physical constitution
while leaving reductive and nonreductive views open (Montero and Papineau, 2016).



                                                59
    Even strict event-token identity does not select an earlier stage. Independently ﬁx input
pair (a, b), c = (a, b, a ⊕ b), and the physical chain

                                 I −→ M −→ J −→ R −→ V.

At increasing clock times it computes, retains, retrieves and copies the tuple. Each of the ﬁrst
four stages has six controls: keep its incoming value, disable it, or prepare one of the four
tuples. The memory therefore needs ﬁve values, not a sensory movie. In the early identity
interpretation, active I is an early relational presentation and active J is a later presentation.
In the delayed identity interpretation, I, M are nonphenomenal for this target and active
J is its ﬁrst presentation. Both identify their actual presentations with existing physically
eﬃcacious events, use the same transition functions and admit the same current acquaintance
at J when R normally copies it. Serial antecedents are not independent overdetermining
apparatuses.
    There are 4·64 = 5184 controlled episodes, with no output disagreement. First-appearance
assignments diﬀer whenever I is active, giving 4·5·63 = 4320 cases. For each input, J is active
in 4 · 36 + 29 = 173 ﬁrst-three-control combinations: four explicit active retrieval preparations,
plus normal retrieval from an active memory; the latter has 4·6+5 = 29 cases. Normal copying
by R then gives 4·173 = 692 shared current-knowledge interpretations. These counts verify the
stipulated ﬁnite rules; they neither certify knowledge nor show that the tuple has qualitative
counterparts. The important result is that granting current acquaintance, closure, eﬃcacy,
no independent overdetermination and even token identity still leaves earlier occurrence open.
A delayed third at J meets the main target.

8.5    A reader model and a typed realization failure
A reported change need not be a quantiﬁed latent-appearance change without a reader re-
striction. For example, report occupancies .65 and .46 can coexist with latent occupancy .65
in both conditions: the ﬁrst reader is accurate, while the second misclassiﬁes a fraction 19/65

8.7   Categorical assembly and physical inherence
Russellian approaches motivate using qualitative or protophenomenal nature as the intrinsic
ground of physical structure (Alter and Nagasawa, 2012). This promises explanatory integra-
tion beyond an arbitrary correlation, but requires separate steps. Local bases bi , their natures
and couplings L may jointly ground component powers and, through composition, a whole
native kernel. Grounding composition gives a plural ground; it does not give one presenta-
tion. The mental property whose causal role needs explanation must be distinguished from
microphysical grounds (Baysan, 2025).
   Fix four native units, all local grounds, couplings and intervention kernels. One extension
admits a new relational ﬁeld for each of the six pairs; another admits those ﬁelds plus a
whole four-member ﬁeld. Both can be permutation invariant and share one actual pair anchor.
They diﬀer in assembly while agreeing on physical power. If pair ﬁelds create fresh counterpart

every pair can share some ﬁeld without one ﬁeld containing all four, as Section 2.2 showed.
    There is a positive conditional repair. Let complete assembly-relevant data have automor-

Assume pointwise covariance of same-owner relations and an anchored pair. Every pair in
its G-orbit has equal owner values. If the resulting orbit graph connects all tokens, equal-

owner function, not existential pairwise transitivity. Complete categorical/material symme-
try, ﬁxed-token identiﬁcation and unique ownership each need independent warrant; abstract

8.9    Whole-act realization and literal mode preservation
Suppose K ∗ genuinely guides V through R, Q is its literal current constituent or mode, and
the whole K ∗ has complete physical constitution. If the realization preserves those literal
parts/modes rather than output-equivalent surrogates, a current physical act bears the actual
qualitative basis. No separate Q → V edge is required. The diﬃcult premise is complete
whole-act constitution; physical suﬃciency for V realizes less.
    The unrestricted rule “every whole with a physical eﬀect is wholly physical” fails. For any
eﬀective physical R, a fusion R + B can inherit its eﬀect while retaining a distinct constituent.
The strongest mixed knowledge act makes B genuinely epistemic, so dismissing it as arbitrary
grafting begs the question. Restricting to minimal causal support would instead remove real
constituents irrelevant to one tested output, losing the route’s purpose.
    Immanent organization improves the nomination. Part roles belong to what the organized
whole is doing rather than to an observer’s inventory: an input is this activity’s source, a state
its reason, and a movement its uptake because of their participation in the maintained exer-
cise. Hylomorphic accounts explicitly treat organization as immanent activity and perceptual
engagement as coordinated exercise (Jaworski, 2020). The role description adds participation
in the maintained activity to the speciﬁcation of transitions. An immanently uniﬁed hybrid
can also have genuine part roles without an external uniﬁer, so a regress against separate
uniﬁers does not establish exhaustive physical constitution.
   Three relation types must remain ﬁxed:

                              A=P :       token-performance identity,
                  Constitutes(M, A) :     constitution, possibly M 6= A,
                      Mode(Q, A, τ ) :    literal qualitative mode in a phase.

From identity, Mode(Q, A, τ ) entails Mode(Q, P, τ ) by substitution. Constitution by numer-

may make the constituted A a physical bearer, but naming M itself requires performance
reidentiﬁcation or an independently warranted mode-preserving realization relation. Neither
identiﬁes instantaneous Q with an entire response window.

8.10    The corrected concrete-performance/mode principle
Process identity and multiple-description event individuation are established philosophical
options (Place, 1956; Davidson, 1969). Mere systematic correlation is weaker than identity.
Likewise, the stronger economy argument against irreducible psychophysical additions cannot
simply be inherited when fundamental qualitative modes are permitted (Smart, 1959). The
present principle therefore concerns actual instance placement, not a numerical event-saving
proof.


                                               64
Conservative mode placement. Given an independently established literal mode of a
particular actual activity, presumptively assign it to that activity’s positively tracked con-
crete execution, at a ﬁxed activity/phase grain and with its actual executing contributors
included. Revise the attribution when independent evidence or a defended ontology identiﬁes
another instantiator, an omitted constitutive contributor, a merely supplied ground or a dif-
ferent activity. Descriptive vocabulary and irreducible property type alone do not establish a
numerically diﬀerent bearer.
    The rule has three admission conditions. First, the mode is an actual way the activity is
undergone or constituted, rather than only an external truth making a result correct. Second,
the nominee is tracked through actor, source use, operations, uptake and participation history
rather than output agreement. Third, the nomination respects known execution/phase coun-
terexamples. These are grounds for a defeasible identity conclusion. “Complete experience-
inclusive execution” must not be inserted into the physical premise.
    Its positive warrant is continuity of a tracked instance across descriptions. A concrete
property is a way an independently identiﬁed activity is; introducing a new property vocabu-
lary need not introduce another performance. Positive execution paths distinguish this locus
from its indicator, supplier or copy. At the same time, literal modes must not be transferred
to every containing system. Consider the following corrections:
1. A client’s input/output history does not execute remote multiplication. If multiplication is
   delegated, the remote processor belongs to the calculation; if it merely supplies an operand,
   it need not be part of a subsequent local calculation.
2. A local signature can be the signing execution while protocol authorization additionally
   requires an actual remote veriﬁer/ledger operation. Abstract validity rules are not auto-
   matically concrete parts; an actual completion operation is diﬀerent.
3. Biological control does not close the boundary of a prosthetic grasp or instrumental per-
   formance. Include the actual jaws/contact or instrument when the selected act requires
   them.
4. Simultaneous saying and writing the same word can share actor, source, purpose and
   time while one vocal execution is only a member of the spoken-and-written whole. A
   continuously tracked single inscription instead supports bodily/intentional reidentiﬁcation
   through actual stroke/mark participation.
5. A promise now is not its fulﬁllment tomorrow. Shared actor and intentional content do
   not erase the change of temporal grain.
6. A thermometer or copied inscription can track the same value/content without sharing the
   measured state’s instantiator or original execution. Common ownership and accuracy do
   not supply token identity.
    These cases prevent selecting a desired bearer solely by actor, role or time. They supply
constraints on the general rule rather than analogies proving qualitative reduction. In the
main application, physically speciﬁed native sensory/comparison participation and indepen-
dently ordinary joint-appearance/activity grounding converge on one current exercise. CMP
supports reidentifying that experience-inclusive act with its organized physical engagement
at the stated grain. That is the substantive, contestable extension; it is neither observed in a
neural measurement nor a restatement that the desired stage appears.

A fair exclusive-bearer alternative. The rival can retain one agent, one well-bound K ∗ ,
actual Q, physical R and one simple generic content law. It denies that Q is a mode of the
nominated P . It need not add another complete comparator, physical force or separately
ﬁtted law for every trial. If the agreed observation laws coincide, their likelihood ratio is
one; an independently defended instance principle can still supply a rational preference, but

another ﬁt of shared data cannot supply experimental ontology selection.
    A copy or view paired bijectively with each performance token may merely redescribe that
token. An ontically distinct qualitative trope u with Instantiates(P, u) likewise permits P as
bearer. The genuine rival adds Bearer(B, Q) and ¬ Mode(Q, P, τ ), or an equivalent nonlift-
ing constitution denial. That substantive placement needs its own individuation/grounding

    The rule can have modest actual warrant without eliminating every coherent rival. Its

and a general placement discipline with correction cases. Absence of evidence for B alone
would be insuﬃcient. An independently supported sensory executor outside the nomination,

8.11    Ownership, phase, and the broader programme
Identifying the ordinary experiencer with an animal identiﬁed through biological criteria can
support literal ownership of current appearance (Olson, 2003). It does not establish which
current organized event constitutes that appearance. A sole thinking-subsystem account can
avoid duplicate subjects too. Moreover, strict current subject–animal identity has diachronic
implications when the animal persists. The existing A3 perspective-continuation account
treats a genuine qualiﬁcation gap as ending a numerical experiential subject episode while
biological/person continuity may remain (Rodgers, 2026e). These are diﬀerent identity types.
A subject-role or episodic-viewpoint claim is weaker than strict animal identity and must be
named accordingly. Current CMP adopts neither a universal animalist persistence rule nor
an equivalence with A3.
    World involvement is also open at the correct grain. An external object need not itself
perform the native comparison to be a constituent of the perceptual activity. Actual input,
coupling and context should be explicit; the conclusion is not internal-only supervenience.
Conversely, material source locations and represented locations are not the bearer. Direct
cortical and neutral-site pain appearances constrain source-location shortcuts without ﬁxing
a unique current ﬁeld.
    The scaﬀold insight has a legitimate narrower role. Retained results can change future
relevance, selection and interpretation; directed reconciliation of an actionable represented
discrepancy diﬀers from the mere existence of a physical diﬀerence. Thought produces a con-
strained representation rather than the source itself, and a successful local reconciliation can
introduce new diﬀerences. This recursive development does not imply that every discrepancy
controller has subjective will, that two communicating agents share one ﬁeld, or that opera-
tional information is already qualitative (Rodgers, 2026c,a). It situates the present act within
development while preserving the independently required occurrence/constitution distinction.

8.12    How the current connection diﬀers from the conditional routes
The main inference separates occurrence projection from constitutive placement. Within
the narrow physically nominated human regime, ordinary sensory experience, source-directed
comparative practice and material continuity support projecting another current presenta-
tion. Prior task participation and sensory matching do not themselves provide dedicated
measurements of jointness or basing. Present Q, seeing, current acquaintance and answer
correctness are not eligibility predicates. The projected target is tethered to source-directed
inspection/comparison, not just Q somewhere in a task window. Biological/operational con-

                                              66
tinuity and current participation are partly ordinary inferences, not complete neural measure-
ments.
    CMP then supports the actual activity’s reidentiﬁcation and literal mode placement. It
does not create the occurrence predicted by the ﬁrst rule. A current-Q interpretation may
subsequently support the prediction, but cannot retroactively select the eligible class. An
eligible mistaken answer can coexist with joint appearance; a credible ﬁeld-free eligible case
counts against projection.
   The three strongest objections therefore have diﬀerent targets. A serial-only sequence
with only a relation proposition challenges jointness. A passive current ﬁeld accompanying
an independently automated answer challenges the activity tether and nomination of the
answer-producing operation; split or narrow the activities instead of widening a containing
time-window. An exclusive-B hybrid can grant jointness and tether while challenging CMP.
A delayed account that ﬁrst presents both counterparts and their relation at current uptake

admission, exact anatomy or reduction of qualitative types.

The current-act argument distinguishes what is presented, how its content is constrained, and
what makes that presentation the basis of a particular act. Three developments sharpen those
distinctions. A presented spatial form can supply the minimum relational target without two
external objects. A ﬁnite symmetry calculation identiﬁes exactly which additional distinctions

ﬁrst identifying a physical content vehicle as the appearance’s bearer. These contributions

mathematical theorem, and a defeasible epistemic principle, respectively. None replaces the
positive performance-identiﬁcation argument in Module 8.

9.1   Relational aspects of one presented form
The minimum target requires qualitative counterparts and their relation together in an actual
presentation. It does not require two independently existing objects, two original subjects,
two input channels, or an overt comparison answer. One visibly extended patch can already
have diﬀerently situated aspects; a visibly bent contour can have diﬀerently directed arms.
Those aspects need not be numerically separate experiences or ontological parts of the bearer.

Principle 9.1 (Finite spatial aspects). If a current presentation genuinely has spatially ex-
tended or diﬀerentiated qualitative character, with suﬃcient presented resolution for at least
two apparent localities or directions, then their spatial arrangement is an aspect of that pre-
sentation’s character.

    The support for this principle is the distinction between presenting extent and merely
signifying it. A nonspatial signal can mean “extended”, support a shape name, or control
an accurate drawing. Those achievements do not make the signal itself visibly extended.
What distinguishes the proposed antecedent is an apparent spatial organisation, rather than
a label that refers to an organisation elsewhere. At the stated ﬁnite grain, diﬀerently located
or directed features and their arrangement belong to how this one form appears. This is


                                              67
an account of presented character, not an inference from the geometry of a stimulus or an
encoding.
    The presented-resolution restriction does real work. An unresolved point, an undiﬀerenti-
ated shape-associated quality, or a sequence of separately experienced positions followed by
an inferred shape need not satisfy it. Accurate recognition alone does not establish resolved
aspects. A critic can also deny that apparent form has the proposed aspect structure; be-
havioral success does not decide that phenomenological disagreement. The principle does not
infer a phenomenal token for every mathematical point, an inﬁnite-resolution continuum, or
the simultaneous appearance of every detail later accessible by shifting attention.

9.4   Stochastic content and limited observations
A stochastic equivariant law at an H-ﬁxed base has an H-invariant distribution. On a ﬁnite
transitive content orbit Hc of size m, every member has equal probability, conditional on that
orbit. This follows because any two orbit members are related by an H-action, which preserves
their probabilities. The conditional entropy is log2 m. Diﬀerent orbits may receive diﬀerent
total masses; uniformity across them does not follow. An intrinsically stochastic law can
therefore have asymmetric realized outcomes without choosing a ﬁxed preferred orientation.
A deterministic description of the realized outcome would have to include its distinguishing
draw, context or history.
   For an unaugmented deterministic choice c at an H-ﬁxed base, covariance holds for exactly
|K| of the |H| transformations. Its mismatch fraction under uniform enumeration is therefore
1 − 1/m. For the line and square examples this is 11/12 and 2/3, respectively. These are
symmetry-test fractions, not behavioral error rates.
Proposition 9.4 (Limited observation of a uniform content orbit). Let a random content Q
be uniform on an m-member orbit, and let an observation Z take at most k values. For any
observation channel and any decoder d,

           Pr{d(Z) = Q} ≤ min(1, k/m),         H(Q | Z) ≥ max(0, log2 m − log2 k).

                                              70
The success bound is attainable.
                                                   P
Proof. The best deterministic decoder has success z maxq Pr(Q = q, Z = z). Each summand
is at most 1/m, and there are at most k summands. Randomized decoding cannot improve
this linear objective. For k ≤ m, partition the contents into k nonempty cells, reveal the
cell, and choose one representative per cell: exactly k contents are correctly decoded, giving
k/m. For k ≥ m, reveal the content itself. Finally, mutual information is at most H(Z) ≤
log2 k, so H(Q | Z) = log2 m − I(Q; Z) gives the entropy bound, together with nonnegativity.
Attainability of the success bound does not assert that the entropy bound is attained for every
m, k.

above 1/6, or a uniform three-way square target above 2/3. These limits concern the stipulated
information architecture. An uncounted analogue variable or history violates its observation-
cardinality premise. The proposition does not identify experienced content with a decoded
symbol, measure human memory, or select a phenomenal bearer.

9.5   Current basing without physical-port constitution
Current basing asks whether a particular actual relational presentation is the operative sensory
basis of a nominated comparison, not merely an appearance accompanying a correct output.
Let Bcur (Q, A) express that Q’s relational aspect is such a basis of the current activity A.
This diﬀers from identifying A with an independently nominated physical performance P , or
making a physical content vehicle a literal constituent of Q. Those latter questions concern
constitution and bearer placement.
   Four inputs keep the basing inference assessable:
1. An independently credible phenomenal anchor warrants a current QJ and its presented
   relational aspect ρ. Source geometry, a relation bit and answer correctness do not supply
   this alone.
2. Genuine current acquaintance KJ addresses ρ of this presentation. An equal-valued mem-
   ory or another ﬁeld is not the same source merely because its content agrees.
3. A nominated reason vehicle R receives that comparative content nondeviantly, preserving
   its source and epistemic role. A start signal from KJ followed by a cached answer is not
   such conveyance.
4. The nominated activity is the agent’s current exercise of that apprehension, with R serving
   its authored comparative role. Ordinary source-speciﬁc practical knowledge, source selec-
   tion, instruction-dependent retasking and checks for bypasses can support this application.
   Causal eﬃcacy or acquaintance considered separately does not establish it.
    These inputs separate phenomenal evidence, addressed epistemic contact, transmission
history and practical use. Their conjunction is not established by deﬁning the symbols. In
particular the fourth input retains a substantive current-activity commitment; it cannot be
inferred merely from a downstream controller’s success.
    The proposed transmission warrant is ordinary and defeasible: when an agent genuinely
apprehends a present relation, its content is conveyed without source substitution, and the
agent knowingly exercises that apprehension in this current comparison, the relation remains
an operative qualitative basis of the activity. The positive reason is source-guided execution,
rather than accidental agreement between an output and a concurrent ﬁeld. Failed source
selection, misunderstood content, substitution or loss of the current exercise can defeat the



                                              71
inference. It is neither a theorem about physical bits nor a claim that every truth-maker must
eﬃciently cause the action.
    This warrant does not require R to constitute Q. An explicit consistent countercase has one
agent, an actual QJ borne exclusively by B, genuine KJ , faithful delivery to physical R, and
a current psychophysical comparison A that uses this very apprehension. Then Bcur (QJ , A)
can hold even though QJ is not a mode of R or of physical P . The rival expressly denies the
transfer of the qualitative mode to P ; merely assigning the name B to a property instance
of P would not make it exclusive. Identiﬁcation of A with P remains the separate CMP
argument (Section 8.10). This countercase establishes nonnecessity of port constitution for
current basing. It does not show that every complete contract using port constitution is
logically stronger than every contract using addressed transmission: their other requirements
can diﬀer.

Two substitutions that matter. A cached-answer route lets genuine KJ trigger an action
while a diﬀerent record supplies its comparative value. This defeats faithful comparative
conveyance. A diﬀerent countermodel preserves genuine ancestry:
                     Qa /Ka −→ M −→ Qb /Kb −→ Rb −→ action.
Here M faithfully conveys the relation apprehended in Qa , but a newly constructed Qb supplies
the action’s current sensory basis. Both ﬁelds may genuinely occur and agree in content.
Content ﬁdelity and causal ancestry do not establish Bcur (Qa , A). If Qb ﬁrst appears at current
uptake J, it may itself satisfy the target. The correct repair is to anchor and nominate that
current ﬁeld, not to demand persistence of an earlier one.

10     Presented form, empirical tests and the limits of transfer
The distinction between a mode’s occurrence and its placement in a physical performance
has empirical consequences. Evidence can support an actual visual form without identifying
its smallest bearer; a physically well-speciﬁed predictor can fail without undermining the
testimony on which the form was identiﬁed. This section brings those cases together. Its
purpose is to establish what the additional evidence contributes to the argument, rather than
to count positive studies as independent conﬁrmations of one metaphysical conclusion.

10.1    From an extensive form to a modest native application
The presented-form argument concerns the qualitative organisation of an actual presentation.
A suﬃciently resolved patch can have diﬀerently situated aspects within one apparent extent.
A location code, an unresolved point, a remembered shape label and an accurate drawing need
not have that organisation. The empirical question is therefore whether the record gives reason
to accept the extensive presentation, rather than merely a spatially competent response. The
subsequent aspect inference and the concrete-performance/mode principle remain separate
steps.

Cortically induced extent. In Beauchamp et al. (2020), phosphene mapping included
appearance-directed outline, size and location matching. For the acquired-blind participant
03–281, the single-electrode calibration reports apparent enlargement after brightness satura-
tion. This is positive ordinary evidence for some extensive visual instances: it concerns how
the patch appeared, beyond which location or letter was selected. The 120 Hz calibration
and the separate 60 Hz current-steering experiment must remain distinct. The latter’s ellipses
summarise variability in reported location; they do not measure individual phosphene extent
or jointness. Neither protocol supplies a dedicated ﬁnite-aspect probe or a trialwise jointness
rate. The supported inference concerns the calibrated native sensory regime and its extensive
instances, rather than every commanded pulse.
    This application removes an intact retinal input and an overt two-object comparison as
necessary conditions of the minimum target. It does not remove native sensory processing
or identify the stimulating electrode with the whole activity. A physically nominated sensory
exercise can include a downstream current constructor. A newly formed extensive presentation
at uptake J meets the minimum without requiring an earlier image to survive unchanged.

A surface with historical and current suppliers. van Lier et al. (2009) report that,
following adaptation to coloured regions around a grey centre, subsequent achromatic out-
lines determine diﬀerently coloured afterimages extending through the outlined area. Their
appearance-directed account and centre-colour matching give modest ordinary warrant for an
actual ﬁlled surface. The illustration of appearance is explanatory, rather than a photograph
of a ﬁeld. Matching alone would not establish spread. The inspected main report leaves the


                                              73
detailed adjustment-display chronology, gaze history and dedicated extent or basing probes
unavailable.
    Here a later boundary is a possible supplier of the current form. Its presence defeats an
inference that the earlier retained state was already a ﬁlled ﬁeld, but need not defeat the
positive interpretation of the later surface. The activity to nominate is the history-sensitive
current sensory engagement, including its relevant suppliers and inspection, rather than the
old adaptor alone. Acceptance of that sensory interpretation permits the spatial-aspect argu-
ment; it does not locate ﬁrst appearance within either 500 ms outline phase, identify a unique
circuit, or supply an independent proof of physical inherence.

An endogenous form during REM sleep. LaBerge et al. (2018) combine physiologically
staged REM, prearranged eye-movement signals bracketing a tracking task, and subsequent
descriptions of following a perceived dream thumb or nail. The sensory testimony gives modest
retrospective warrant for some extensive dream-target instances, although less directly than
an explicit enlargement or contour probe. Pursuit geometry alone would not do so. The
published sensory descriptions and duration/success criteria select the analysed records; their
reported success fraction is not an all-attempt relational-occurrence rate. The supplementary
narratives were not recovered for this assessment.
    A single actually extensive target can meet the minimum without a second dream object
or a simultaneously visible motion path. This revises what the evidence must establish,
rather than adding an observation to the study. Its time reference remains retrospective:
the physiology nominates a sleep engagement, while testimony attributes the visual form to
that engagement. First formation at a current dream-time J is compatible with the inference.
Assigning all form only to the waking interview instead challenges the accepted retrospective
reference; it does not preserve that reference while merely choosing a later phase. Neither
interpretation is settled by eye movements alone.
    These examples support separately delimited native applications at ordinary phenomeno-
logical strength. They do not silently enlarge the original visual predicate V to every sleeper,
implanted system or artiﬁcial implementation. For each, the appearance evidence, sensory-
activity tether, physical nomination, time reference and constitutive placement must be as-
sessed in their own right. The strongest exclusive-bearer alternative can grant all three actual
forms and their genuine acquaintance while reserving qualitative inherence to B. The addi-
tional applications therefore extend the reach of the defended current-act reasoning without
becoming independent experimental proofs of its bearer principle.

10.4    A felt act and its bodily execution
Desmurget et al. (2009) supply a pertinent boundary on inferring execution from experience
of acting. In seven awake neurosurgical patients, posterior stimulation could elicit reported
urge without observed movement or detected activity in the monitored muscles; stronger stim-
ulation could elicit illusory movement reports. Premotor stimulation could elicit movement
without reported awareness of it. Responsive-site selection, diﬀerent clinical cohorts, limited
muscle monitoring and the absence here of the detailed supporting protocol restrict the com-
parison. It is not an all-trigger randomized double dissociation or a measurement of global
phenomenal absence.
    A genuine feeling of execution can thus misrepresent whether the nominated bodily move-
ment occurred. This does not make the feeling unreal, remove its physical induction, or show
that felt motives never contribute to action. Likewise, movement without reported awareness
of that movement does not imply an entirely experience-free subject. What the current-act
argument must track is the actual sensory or comparative activity, with its operative con-
nection to any further execution independently warranted. The represented act, its present
qualitative character and the peripheral bodily result cannot simply be identiﬁed. The study
supplies a concrete limit on that transfer, rather than a new relational-minimum instance,
mode-preserving intervention or bearer test.

10.5    What the additional evidence warrants
The new empirical integration is selective. Extensive-form testimony supports bounded actual
native applications of the aspect argument. A failed ﬁxed predictor and a causal execution dis-
sociation discipline the proposed connections between those appearances and physical records.

                                              76
Neither positive testimony nor negative prediction is assigned authority over every other in-
ferential step. The resulting position remains positive at the defended grain: actual native
sensory performances can be warranted as having current relational modes, and the concrete-
performance/mode argument supports their physical attribution. Broader occurrence condi-
tions, ﬁne phase identiﬁcation, dedicated same-ﬁeld basing and stronger material constitution
retain their separate outstanding obligations.

directly: the eight inherited ﬁnite checks, the content-signature checker, the reduced neural-

execution records; they were not rerun in version 1.2. Versions 1.3 and 1.4 retain all eleven
records without another scientiﬁc execution. Version 1.4 changes notation and three brief
passages; its checks concern aﬀected notation, references, rendered pages and compilation
warnings.
    Three inherited empirical analyses require separately obtained Crayen or Ebbinghaus data.
Optional raw-preﬁx veriﬁcation of the neural feature requires the separately acquired record-
ing. Those analyses, the earlier contrast-only forecast and full neural extraction were not
rerun in this revision. Their recorded outputs retain the earlier veriﬁcation scope.
   The archive README speciﬁes commands, external acquisition routes, ﬁlenames, schemas,
available hashes and licence conditions. Synthetic or derived comparison records are not
additional independent data. The minimal neural table is derived from the CC0 source deposit

    The editable sources for both documents are provided in Relational_Awareness_LaTeX

placement and native occurrence projection retain the philosophical and empirical warrant
