                                            Abstract
         When a person compares apparent sizes, what warrants saying that the relation actu-
     ally appears, and that the organised physical activity bears that appearance? This paper
     defends an answer through two distinct warrants. Within a speciﬁed, continuing human
     visual-comparison setting, sensory calibration and ordinary appearance-directed evidence
     support a revisable expectation of current relational presentation. A separately defended
     concrete-performance principle supports treating that qualitative way as a mode of the
     materially tracked comparison itself. A worked retrospective illustration from an archived
     contextual-size task shows how these warrants separate and combine. Prior archive expo-
     sure and the absence of dedicated jointness and sensory-basing probes limit its evidential
     force; it is not prospective validation. First construction at current uptake is compati-
     ble with the claim. The decisive remaining constitutive limitation is that an empirically
     equivalent account can grant the same appearance and activity while assigning qualitative
     instantiation to an exclusive bearer. The physical attribution therefore depends on the
     contestable performance principle, rather than on the supporting mathematics. The result
     is a scoped positive argument about actual current appearance, not a general law deriving
     experience from physical organisation.

Keywords: relational appearance; phenomenal unity; physical constitution; event identity;
sensory comparison; causal realisation.


1    Relational appearance and the connecting problem
A person looks at two objects and compares their apparent sizes. A physical system acquires
signals, combines information, retains a descriptor and produces an answer. The relation
between these descriptions is the problem of this paper. The physical sequence can be present
without yet establishing that two qualitative counterparts and their relation are presented
together. Conversely, accepting a current appearance does not identify which physical event
bears it. The central question is how an independently speciﬁed organisation can support an
actual relational presentation and a warranted attribution of its bearer.
    The claim is that, in a continuing adult human visual comparison, there is reason to regard
the presented relation as a way that the organised physical comparison itself occurs. This
matters because producing the right answer, undergoing a sensory presentation and bearing
that presentation are diﬀerent achievements. The argument uses two warrants. Sensory
calibration and ordinary evidence about what the person is doing support the occurrence of
a current relational presentation. A general principle for tracking the concrete activity then
supports attributing its qualitative way to the materially tracked performance. Both warrants

are defeasible: relevant contrary evidence or a better constitutive account can overturn them.
Their combination is a positive argument, with a defended philosophical premise at its centre.
    The relevant native-human setting uses the participant’s own biological sensory and con-
trol organisation, in a materially continuing preparation. A qualitative mode means how an
activity is actually undergone, rather than a message or product it emits. Physical eligibil-
ity is speciﬁed before interpreting the present appearance evidence. These restrictions keep
the inference inspectable without claiming that a physical transition table alone establishes
appearance or that every system producing the same answer qualiﬁes.
    This addresses an outstanding obligation in the relational-awareness programme. Ear-
lier work distinguishes source organisation, accessible readout and perspective, and proposes
conditional relations between native structure and experience (Rodgers, 2026e,f,h,c). Even a
valid conditional assignment leaves a further obligation: its appearance-facing premise needs
support beyond the assignment it licenses. The present contribution pursues that obligation
through current human activity. The background concepts are introduced here; acceptance
of the programme’s wider awareness-ﬁrst ontology or its complete structural admission rules
is unnecessary.

1.1   What must actually appear
Deﬁnition 1.1 (Relational appearance). Relational appearance is an occurrence in which
qualitative counterparts are presented together with a relation or arrangement among them.
A counterpart is something as it appears within that presentation; it need not be an enduring
external object or a preserved earlier phenomenal token.

   For example, two diﬀerently situated apparent magnitudes may be jointly presented in a
spatial scene. A subsequent proposition, “they are diﬀerent”, can describe that presentation
but is not identical with it. A person may instead experience only one remembered size,
then another, and infer the proposition. Relationally meaningful language can itself appear
without displaying the original poles: understanding that a mountain is taller than a hill need
not present two visual heights together. The target therefore exceeds a relation bit, verbal
competence or informational dependence.
    It is also weaker than a universal theory of phenomenal fusion. An appearance generated
at current comparison can contain new counterparts related within a new presentation. The
old sensory tokens, their subjects or their physical carriers need not merge. Nor must the
counterparts be experienced as one object. Two segregated objects can appear together; inte-
gration into a single attributed object and joint presence within a ﬁeld are diﬀerent questions.
Experimental grouping and feature-integration measures accordingly bear on particular forms
of organisation rather than deﬁning minimal relational appearance (Vogelsang et al., 2024).
    An externally described correlation is diﬀerent again. Two registers can jointly encode a
parity without any accepted phenomenal target. The investigator’s ability to describe their
relation does not install that relation as an appearance in either register or in their union.
Such a physical model is valuable when it speciﬁes a mechanism or tests a proposed law; its
informational relations are not observations of consciousness.

1.2   Presented form already has relational aspects
The minimal target does not require two separately recognised objects. A genuinely pre-
sented spatial form, with suﬃcient presented resolution for at least two apparent localities
or directions, has those aspects in an arrangement. Taking two such aspects from that same
presentation does not require combining two earlier experiences or ﬁrst identifying them by

                                               2
name. A resolved visible outline can therefore support the thin relational minimum even when
it is experienced as one object.
   The argument begins from credible evidence of presented extent. Physical size, a successful
shape label or an investigator’s partition of an image is insuﬃcient. It also establishes no
unity among otherwise separate ﬁelds. Its value is to remove an unnecessary multi-object
requirement, rather than to infer consciousness from geometry. Supplement Module I develops
the argument and its limits. This is consistent with the distinction between pre-reﬂective
appearance and recognised personal experience: the relevant arrangement can be present
without already being described as a red square or as an event in one’s history.

1.3   Five questions that must not be collapsed
Occurrence asks whether anything of the target kind actually appears. Jointness asks whether
the qualitative counterparts and their relation are together in a presentation, rather than sep-
arately available for inference. Activity or basing asks whether this appearance is the sensory
way of the particular comparative activity under discussion, rather than a concurrent ﬁeld ac-
companying an automated answer. Timing asks when the appearance occurs, distinguishing
physical event time from represented temporal content. Bearer asks which concrete event or
organisation instantiates that qualitative way.
    These questions interact without becoming interchangeable. A recorded response can
support a current deadline on the ordinary interpretation of the task, but not the ﬁrst onset.
Selective stimulation can show a causal role in producing content without establishing that
the stimulated site is the whole bearer. An animal can own an experience without the animal’s
entire lifespan being identical with its current sensory performance. A retained description
may suﬃce to generate a later presentation without having borne an earlier one.
    The term physical engagement refers here to an organised activity at an explicit grain,
including relevant acquisition, transformation and uptake. Correlation strength alone does not
ﬁx its boundary. Instruments that execute part of the act, and relevant world relations, may
be constitutive of a spatially distributed performance. A container that includes a possible
bearer is not thereby the bearer of the activity.

1.4   A serious delayed rival
Consider two accounts with the same acquisition, computation, bounded memory and report-
ing process. On an early account, a relational scene occurs during integration I. On a delayed
account, the system stores an adequate relational summary and ﬁrst constructs a joint scene
during current inspection or judgment J. Both can subsequently produce the same testimony.
The delayed account does not need an unlimited movie archive, a special law for every trial, or
an absence of genuine current acquaintance. A generic parser acting on a bounded descriptor
can construct new counterparts at J.
    If the accounts stipulate the same observable likelihoods, reﬁtting those observations can-
not select their phenomenal stage assignment. This limitation calls for a diﬀerent warrant; it
is not a universal impossibility theorem about consciousness. The present argument targets
current relational appearance, which both accounts can accept, and separates the positive
occurrence inference from the constitutive attribution. An early onset is not a condition of
success for this target.
    The paper develops that route in seven sections. Section 2 recovers the motivating philo-
sophical distinctions and earlier connecting proposals. Section 3 explains what formal con-
straints and sensory evidence contribute. Section 4 defends concrete-performance mode place-

2     Awareness, scaﬀolding and earlier connecting principles
2.1   Fundamental awareness and diﬀerentiated presentation
The motivating account takes awareness as fundamental: the knowing aspect of reality is not
ﬁrst produced by a reﬂective self-description. The monograph denotes reality prior to the
operational source/readout distinction by U; this is not a separately acting observer. Dif-
ferentiated presentation, on this proposal, arises within that basis through contrast, relation
and the organisation of access. Awareness, sensory appearance, reﬂective recognition, appro-
priation as my experience, and a later account of what happened have diﬀerent explanatory
roles. Those roles can occur together within one ongoing activity; distinguishing them does
not require a succession of separate experiential stages.
    This is an explicit philosophical starting point, not the conclusion of the physical models
below. Granting fundamental awareness does not yet specify which diﬀerentiated process
presents a relation, whether several contributors share one presentation, or which concrete
activity bears it. The current-act argument addresses a local part of that further obligation.
Its sensory and constitutive warrants can be assessed without accepting the entire awareness-
ﬁrst ontology (Rodgers, 2026e,c).

2.2   Organisation, apparatus and acquired scaﬀolding
A list of ingredients is often inadequate to describe an occurrence. Hands, motion and air are
components of a clap, but their inventory does not describe the organised act or its sound.
The useful lesson is that organised occurrences can have properties whose attribution requires
attention to their arrangement and activity. The analogy does not prove that any interaction
is conscious or that an additional substance appears whenever a relation is realised.
    The relational-awareness investigation begins with the same distinction. A third presen-
tation can be a new organisation of counterparts rather than a third substance or a fusion
of previously separate subjects. Once retained, that organisation can become an input to
later reﬂection and action. Recursion combined with retention permits a scaﬀold to develop;
recursion without retained consequences can merely repeat. Greater complexity alone does
not identify one perspective: a coordinated crowd may have a complicated common activity
without thereby constituting one experiential subject.
    Apparatus and acquired scaﬀolding make distinct contributions to perceptual construction.
Sensory and bodily organisation, acquired classiﬁcations, memories, expectations, feelings and
beliefs shape the presentation through which the surroundings are encountered. They do not
merely add descriptions after a consciously viewed raw image has already appeared. In this
sense ongoing seeing is already interpretative perceptual processing. Reﬂectively naming
what is seen, considering its signiﬁcance and relating it to a personal history are further roles
that can occur within the same activity. Suspending naming or inner commentary does not
establish that perceptual processing or its acquired organisation has stopped. A presentation
can be source-constrained without being source-complete, and the dependence of knowledge
on access does not by itself establish the proposed numerical unity of its underlying basis
(Rodgers, 2026e,c).

2.3   The constructed observer and personal experience
The observer in this account is itself a constructed central image: the model through which
events acquire a personal location, history and signiﬁcance. It need not be an additional
spectator inside the person. Recognising that construction need not abolish useful memory
or practical self-reference. The proposed change is a loss of the personal model’s privileged
status as an exhaustive centre, while the model remains available for ordinary life (Rodgers,
2026c, §5.4). This is the motivation for the related recursively closed observer proposal, rather
than a result established by successful self-description alone (Rodgers, 2026j,b).
    Scaﬀolding provides a way to discuss this development without collapsing its levels. Ac-
quired diﬀerentiations support memory, reﬂection and control. They shape ongoing activity,
which can in turn revise the retained constraints and alter accessible source conditions. This
reciprocal dependence is developed in the reﬂective-alignment paper (Rodgers, 2026b, §4.1).
The operational feedback can be modelled without claiming that a functioning loop already
establishes awareness or one phenomenal subject.
   The related distinction between functional and subjective will is instructive. Mere diﬀer-
ence does not entail will. Directed reconciliation of an actionable represented discrepancy
can characterise a control function, while felt eﬀort or intention raises a further phenomenal
question. Such reconciliation can revise a model to ﬁt the world rather than force the world
to ﬁt a defended self-image. This distinction locates control operations and their histories
without deriving subjective will from a thermostat or treating every revised representation as
experienced.

2.4   Occurrence, appearance and recollective interpretation
The companion draft Experience as Recollective Interpretation, dated 12 October 2026, gives
these distinctions a particular use (Rodgers, 2026d). It calls an occurrence something that
happens and is registered or retained, experience the interpretation of a retained occurrence
as having occurred, and subjective or personalised experience its organisation through feel-
ing, identity and personal signiﬁcance. Here occurrence means actual taking place, without
deﬁning it by later retention; appearance names actual qualitative presentation, and personal
experience names its appropriation as happening to someone. Appearance is the qualitative
character of the constructed presentation, not a raw image awaiting a second internal viewer.
Perceptual construction, reﬂective recognition and personal appropriation are distinguishable
functions that can operate concurrently. The word experience, when used for a current quali-
tative mode in this paper, consequently has a broader use than the companion draft’s deﬁned
recollective use.
    This diﬀerence in terminology does not settle the philosophical question. The companion
draft preserves seeing without reﬂective classiﬁcation while withholding its stipulated term
experience; this does not require seeing to be unprocessed sensory material. Its Section 7 also
argues that presence is no further ingredient beyond construction, and Section 8 explicitly
declines an account that deﬁnes experience functionally while leaving presence as an additional
open fact. That is a substantive proposal about constitution. It need not conﬂict with
attributing a qualitative mode to an organised activity: denying a separate spectator or added
substance does not deny the activity’s character. But an identiﬁcation of actual appearance
with retentive interpretation needs more than a decision about words. The present warrants do
not establish that identiﬁcation for every interpreting process, and they do not independently
refute the companion proposal. The unresolved issue is the reach of that identiﬁcation, rather
than whether personal narrative is required for every appearance.
   There is nevertheless a precise agreement about timing. Recollective uptake can occur


                                               5
within a continuing process over a short interval; the antecedent need not have ﬁnished before
its recent history is interpreted (Rodgers, 2026d, §5). A descriptor retained earlier may
ﬁrst contribute to a relational presentation at current uptake, J. Earlier storage does not
establish earlier appearance, and a present scene can concern an earlier occurrence. Neither
observation proves that all appearance is recollective. The companion draft’s Section 10
expressly recognises that its universal recollective thesis goes beyond the relational-awareness
result. The local current-act argument accordingly remains distinct from that wider thesis.
    The limiting case that removes all acquired scaﬀolding and the ability to form memory
is a stronger stipulation than the absence of naming, commentary or personal recognition.
It should not be recast as an ordinary recognised scene with an omitted report. The Octo-
ber draft’s positive recollective model instead requires minimal retention, without requiring
language or autobiographical memory. These are diﬀerent conditions. If later interpretation
varies with a particular antecedent, the dependence and its historical reference still need an
account; the comparison supplies no retained-trace mechanism for the stronger limiting case.
Supplement Module B develops this distinction and the conditional limits of inference without
retention.
    Descriptions can point to an occurrence without reproducing it. That limitation motivates
restraint about an exhaustive description of fundamental awareness; it does not establish
a mathematical incompleteness theorem or make every local claim inaccessible to evidence.
Whether a relation appears, when it appears, how it informs an activity and where its mode
inheres remain particular questions with particular warrants.

2.5   Why earlier articulation proposals were reasonable and insuﬃcient
The ﬁrst constitutive proposal considered here is a native articulation principle: elementary
native physical performances are candidates for elementary presentations. Its motivation is
immanence and structural ﬁdelity. A phenomenal fusion across independently transforming
systems, or a phenomenal division within one native performance, appears to require addi-
tional lawlike structure. This is a reasoned proposal rather than an arbitrary label.
    Its vulnerability is that physical irreducibility does not alone individuate the experiential
event. One interaction can contain several carriers or roles; a carrier-centred account need not
treat the interaction’s native prime as one ﬁeld. The argument therefore needs an indepen-
dently defended event-centred criterion and an appropriate duration. Endpoint factorisation
does not supply these: a swap can be implemented by an interacting sequence even if its
endpoint admits a simple factorisation. Native physical organisation constrains a proposed
realisation law without automatically ﬁxing its phenomenal extension.
    A second proposal, relational manifestation, reasons that intrinsic relations among qual-
itative terms should have a manifest aspect rather than remain wholly unmanifest glue. It
has an intelligible ontological motivation. A rival can nevertheless admit qualitative intrinsic
terms and real relations among them without one common presentation. Realising contribu-
tor supports also need not be phenomenal argument tokens: a compressed parity value can
have a presented character without two identiﬁable original counterparts. The mathemati-
cal family of common contributors must therefore be distinguished from the family of actual
co-presented occurrences.
   A third proposal appeals to intrinsic categorical grounds of physical powers. Qualitative
actuality may be an attractive candidate for such grounds, and plural grounds may jointly
support a whole power. Neither point by itself establishes that those grounds appear in
one ﬁeld or inhere in the nominated physical performance. Pairwise grounded relations need
not have a single common occurrence witness. A reciprocal additional ﬁeld would make


                                               6
diﬀerent physical predictions, but the programme contains no conﬁrmation of that ﬁeld or its
coeﬃcients.
    The present argument retains these motivations while pursuing a narrower route. Cred-
ible appearance/activity evidence supports a projection of a recurring sensory capacity; a
corrected concrete-performance principle then addresses the bearer of the resulting literal
mode. No universal prime-to-presentation law, relational manifestation law or additional ﬁeld
is assumed. Supplement Modules B and H preserve the earlier arguments, formal distinctions
and counterexamples.

2.6   Continuity has more than one object
The current appearance token, the organised performance, the living animal, the person and
an experiential subject episode have diﬀerent continuity conditions. In the monograph’s A3
proposal, a genuine interruption of qualiﬁcation can end the numerical experiential subject
episode while biological and personal continuity remain (Rodgers, 2026e). A strict identiﬁ-
cation of the experiencer with the continuing animal would carry diﬀerent diachronic impli-
cations (Olson, 2003). Calling an episodic subject a role of the animal is weaker than strict
identity and must be stated accordingly.
   The current-act thesis does not silently settle this disagreement. Its bearer claim concerns
the present organised performance and its qualitative phase. It neither infers a migrating
subject from transfer of a relational distinction nor requires the original subjects of separate
inputs to fuse. This allows a new current presentation to be constitutionally signiﬁcant without
becoming a universal theory of persistence.
    The current-act argument does not assume the full A1–A3 constitution. A connection to
the Shadow Psychophysical Constitution, SPC-2, would require a further application of its
existing laws (Rodgers, 2026e, ch. 17). A1 admits a perspective through a physically justi-
ﬁed qualifying core; A2 assigns its complete endogenous predictive object over all physically
admitted ﬁnite continuations as phenomenal organisation; A3 speciﬁes nonbranching process
continuation. An appropriate phase of the present performance would have to be located
within such a core, with Q identiﬁed as an aspect of its current scene. Contributors to a
performance do not thereby deﬁne its subject boundary, and causal involvement does not
automatically make a worldly source or instrument part of the subject. Given a justiﬁed
realisation and the accepted laws, SPC-2 assigns phenomenal states and their timing at the
declared native resolution. This paper does not complete that application, validate A2 or
select ﬁrst phenomenal onset. Its modest current-appearance conclusion remains compatible
with ﬁrst presentation at uptake, J.

2.7   The existing scholarly baseline
Whole-process constitution and biological coupling are not new general ideas. The monograph
already treats thought and noticing as aspects of a speciﬁed whole process and proposes a
biologically realised coupling to a current scene (Rodgers, 2026e, ch. 2). Paper 2 distinguishes
silent identity declarations from empirically constrained identiﬁcation (Rodgers, 2026f). The
development paper already articulates third structure, scaﬀold feedback and conditional con-
scious constitution, while requiring independent appearance-facing support (Rodgers, 2026c).
Outside this programme, process identity and event individuation have long-standing philo-
sophical defences (Place, 1956; Davidson, 1969); Adlam proposes that conscious agency may
inhere in holistic input-to-decision relations while leaving the qualifying relations open (Adlam,
2026).

    The contribution claimed here is more speciﬁc: the conjunction of a physically delim-

current-mode placement and an actual later-token application, with independently articu-

It does not assert priority for holistic constitution, event identity or relational scaﬀolding.

reﬁnements.

3.2   Appearance-directed evidence and its interpretation
Ordinary sensory testimony is evidence that something appears, although it can be mistaken
about the external stimulus, the experienced relation, or how an answer was reached. Refus-
ing all such evidence until a neural detector is independently established would exclude the
appearance-facing anchors needed to assess a detector. Accepting every response as a direct
jointness label would make the opposite mistake. The relevant assessment asks what a person

                                               9
was instructed to compare, what sources and actions were involved, and whether the response
is credibly an expression of current sensory activity.
    Direct cortical stimulation contributes useful causal evidence. Local perturbation can al-
ter facial appearance, and spatial stimulation patterns can occasion drawn forms or multiple
phosphenes (Parvizi et al., 2012; Beauchamp et al., 2020; Bosking et al., 2022). These eﬀects
are harder to understand as arbitrary names assigned to unchanged sensory content, espe-
cially when initial drawings precede learned naming. They nevertheless leave open whether
stimulated activity supplies a downstream current constructor. Reported multiplicity also
varies: two stimulation sites do not uniformly yield two apparent spots.
    Where credible testimony concerns a resolved extended form, its presented aspects can
already support the minimal relational target. This is a positive phenomenal interpretation,
not an inference from electrode separation or a drawing’s geometry alone. A newly formed
current ﬁeld can supply it. Supplement Module J applies this distinction to selected visual
and dream ﬁndings, retaining each source’s timing, reporting and basing limits rather than
treating every induced or tracked response as a direct jointness measure.
    Other sources sharpen the same distinction. Report-independent accumulation establishes
processing under diﬀering report demands, but passive stimulus-intensity labels do not them-
selves label current seeing or jointness (Stockart et al., 2025). Grouping and feature integration
can dissociate without deciding whether distinct objects share a scene (Vogelsang et al., 2024).
A global measurement diagram requires compatible token and context indices before its local
descriptions can be interpreted as one actual presentation (Lee-Youngzie et al., 2026). None
is merely negative evidence. Each identiﬁes which independent phenomenon or mechanism
was established and which inference still requires a premise.

3.3   A physically delimited recurring exercise
Let V denote the narrow native-human visual regime used in the application below. Its
components are speciﬁed without asking whether the present target appears:
   1. A materially continuing adult participant with actual biological optical acquisition and
      native visual/control organisation, in the same experimental preparation.
   2. The independently speciﬁed physical source pair, annulus contexts, optical geometry,
      illumination and acquisition arrangement.
   3. A previously installed comparison instruction and response contingency, with current
      source-sensitive transformations entering the acquisition/control process.
   4. The material and procedural session history, current acquisition episode and uptake
      interval, with relevant background alterations accounted for.
This is a macro physical nomination, not a measured complete neural state. Actual physiology
and sampling participation are partly ordinary task/biological inferences. The record does not
include EEG staging, so “conﬁrmed awake consciousness” is not a measured admission ﬁeld.
Current Q, successful acquaintance and answer correctness are excluded from admission.
    The initial verbal looked-equal staircase varied the real disc-size diﬀerence under con-
trasting annulus contexts to estimate each participant’s point of subjective equality. Later
variation in real disc diameters and annulus arrangements, together with instructions to judge
how the discs look, supports a recurring sensory comparison sensitive to context, rather than
a context-free classiﬁcation of physical diameter. Interpreting the apparent counterparts as

    This appearance-directed evidence supports a recurring capacity whose projected target
is current relational Q as the sensory way of source-directed comparative activity. Material
continuity and relevant source/context variation make continued exercise of that capacity
a reasonable causal expectation. The projected target is what the independently speciﬁed
physical class is expected to occasion in a new exercise; it does not deﬁne class membership.
The strongest projection is within the same participant and preparation. Transferring to an
altered supplier or an unanchored machine incurs additional obligations.
    This induction uses no numerical reliability estimate. Its content is a defeasible default: an
episode in V is expected to occasion source-dependent apparent counterparts and a presented
relation during some current inspection/comparison phase, by qualifying uptake J. It neither
predicts the exact same/diﬀerent content nor assumes appearance from stimulus onset. A
credible eligible ﬁeld-free case must count against the projection. One cannot redeﬁne the
class as “trials with conscious capacity” merely because a prediction failed.
   The projection is therefore directional without being prospective validation. It permits
physical nomination before present appearance evidence is interpreted. Its actual application
in Section 5 shows that this separation can be maintained. The bearer question remains
separate: a capacity for Q does not by itself identify its instantiator.


4     Concrete performance and literal mode
4.1   The two references and the substantive inference
Let P name the independently nominated physical engagement at the relevant activity/phase
grain. Let A name the actual source-directed comparative act as independently supported
by appearance-directed evidence, and let Q name its qualitative way. These references are
not deﬁned as equal. P can be nominated by material acquisition, transformations, control
history and uptake even if the phenomenal claim is disputed. A is identiﬁed through the
person’s current comparative activity, whose sensory way includes the relational presentation.
The conclusion to defend is that these references converge on one concrete performance.
   Three distinct relationships must be kept apart. A cause can occasion a performance
without being that performance. A containing system can include a performer without itself
performing the attributed act at the relevant grain. A constituent can help constitute an act
while remaining numerically distinct from it. Only justiﬁed reidentiﬁcation, A = P , licenses
substitution of the literal mode:

   Neither physical closure nor mental eﬃcacy supplies that conclusion alone. A physical
member R may produce an answer while a qualitative constituent Q genuinely grounds a
composite reason K ∗ . Eﬃcacy inherited through R does not make every member of K ∗

Conversely, irreducible qualitative properties do not force a new concrete bearer whenever the

                                               11
4.2   A concrete-performance/mode principle
Principle 4.1 (Conservative mode placement). When a literal mode of an actual activity is
independently grounded, presumptively attribute that mode to the activity’s independently
and positively tracked concrete performance, at a ﬁxed activity/phase grain and with its
actual executing contributors included. Revise the attribution when evidence or a separately
defended instantiation account supports a diﬀerent bearer, an omitted constituent, a merely
supplied ground or a diﬀerent activity. A new property type or descriptive vocabulary alone
is not evidence of a numerically diﬀerent instantiator.

    This is an openly contestable constitutive principle. It does not say that every mode is
physical or that every activity has a phenomenal mode. The concrete performance nominated
here is physical because its acquisition/control execution is independently tracked materially.
The principle supplies a defeasible preference for treating its independently supported qualita-
tive way as a mode of that execution. It permits fundamental qualitative properties without
deriving them from the current physical description.
    Its admission conditions are substantive. There must be credible evidence of a literal
mode of this activity; the nominee must be tracked through actual source use and partici-
pation, rather than selected by matching output; and known executor, phase and indicator
counterexamples must be respected. The absence of an identiﬁed extra executor checks for a
defeater; it is not the positive ground for attribution. The conclusion remains weaker where
activity reference is supported only by one actor, a common time or an answer code.
    The principle is not “if Q occurs, assign Q to whichever stage is preferred”. It constrains
which nomination is eligible for attribution. It can favour current judgment rather than early
integration, demand inclusion of a remote physical executor, or require separating sensory
participation from an automated answer. Those consequences occur independently of the
motivating phenomenal assignment.

4.3   Independent motivation: continuity of the tracked instance
The general rationale is explanatory continuity across ways of describing a concrete occurrence.
A particular act can be tracked materially, intentionally, skilfully and semantically. The fact
that those descriptions have diﬀerent explanatory roles does not itself multiply the acts. A
handwritten inscription may be described through contact and strokes, through a skill being
exercised, and through its legibility or meaning. The realisation of a new property can require
a richer state description or a new constitutive rule without requiring another performance
elsewhere.
    This rationale belongs to a tradition distinguishing token identity from simple correlation
(Place, 1956; Davidson, 1969). It does not commit the present argument to reduction of quali-
tative property types or to a complete causal-proﬁle criterion for events. Such a proﬁle has not
been measured for A and P . There is a positively tracked candidate for the particular execu-
tion and independently anchored evidence of the qualitative way of the comparative activity.
Convergence on the nominated physical execution is the defended inference. Maintaining one
instantiator preserves the supported reference and participation facts unless there is reason
to divide them.
    Three cases show why correlation is insuﬃcient. A thermometer reading can track a
body’s temperature without being that body’s thermal state. A copied coordinate can match
a distributed body’s centre of mass without becoming its collective conﬁguration. A copied
inscription can reproduce the word without repeating the original stroke-guided activity. The
physical quantity cases have stronger constitutive speciﬁcations than the proposed placement


                                              12
of Q, so their rules cannot simply be transferred. They support a more general discipline:
distinguish a tracked property-bearing performance from its indicator, description and prod-
uct, while allowing justiﬁed macro attribution before an exhaustive microphysical reduction
is available.
   The principle’s extension to experience is accordingly a defended methodological and meta-
physical step. An irreducible property may be a literal way the tracked performance occurs.
Write B for a genuinely exclusive qualitative bearer outside the nominated performance. This
notation is distinct from the programme’s U for the unsplit ground. To locate the qualitative
mode in B requires an instantiation account explaining why that qualitative reality belongs
outside the performance, rather than another description or mode of it. The immanent ac-
count has its own debt: it must justify the nominated activity, phase and contributors, and
must not assume a universal physical-to-quality map. Neither side escapes justiﬁcation by
redescribing the other as expensive.

4.4   Physicalism, intrinsic nature and the exclusive-bearer challenge
The proposed attribution belongs to a family of views on which one concrete event can be
described physically and mentally without those descriptions being interchangeable. It is
congenial to nonreductive physicalism: mental occurrences are physical occurrences, while
their mental description need not reduce to a physical property type. Davidson’s anomalous
monism provides a familiar example of the distinction between event identity and reduction
(Davidson, 2001). The present argument does not adopt his full theory or infer its phenomenal
claim from intentional rationality. It defends a local identity through the positively tracked
performance and its independently supported way of occurring.
    Compatible Russellian approaches instead emphasize an intrinsic nature not exhausted
by the structural and dispositional descriptions used in physics (Alter and Nagasawa, 2012).
If the qualitative nature is a mode of this very performance, that proposal can agree with
the current bearer attribution without reducing quality to a structural description. Russellian
views are diverse, however, and the proposal that qualitative grounds support physical powers
does not by itself establish one joint presentation or identify its bearer. The relation between
such grounds and mental causation also remains disputed (Baysan, 2025). No member of this
family is a prerequisite for assessing the two warrants.
    The decisive vulnerability is a genuinely exclusive bearer that grants the same current
appearance, acquaintance, activity and complete physical history. That account can agree
with every empirical likelihood while denying that the qualitative mode inheres in P . A
mathematical description of the shared history cannot decide the disagreement. The positive
case for A = P rests on conservative performance reidentiﬁcation; its rival must defend the
contrary instantiation account. The principle therefore supplies a reasoned preference, not a
result forced by the supporting theorems. Section 6 develops the exclusive account without
treating it as an idle ﬁeld or an extra complete comparator.

4.5   Counterexamples that correct the principle
Consider a controller receiving a multiplication result from a remote executor. The controller
owns the task, receives the same source and emits the answer. It nevertheless does not perform
the entire multiplication locally. Attribution must include the executing contributor or be
narrowed to receipt and veriﬁcation. A prosthesis or instrument may similarly execute part
of a movement or assessment. Physical proximity to the person’s answer is not a boundary
criterion.
   Next consider a person simultaneously speaking and writing a word. Actor, purpose,

                                              13
content and time overlap, but two distinct executions are involved. A common owner does
not identify their modes. Or consider an automated answer path running while the person
looks at the same pair. A relational ﬁeld can occur in the person without being the sensory
way of the answer-producing operation. Widening P to every process in the time window
merely to capture that ﬁeld converts performance attribution into containment.
    Finally, copying a completed answer preserves a product, not its source-guided formation.
A reader of the copy can perform a new act with a new relational presentation, but the
old bearer’s identity does not migrate with the data. Adding inert matter or concatenating
successive tasks likewise does not enlarge one literal mode simply by expanding a box. Phase
individuation matters: a current qualitative way is not identiﬁed with the entire multi-phase
response episode.
    These examples support a corrected rule. Track actual performing roles and their current
participation; include contributors shown to execute the relevant act; distinguish suppliers,
veriﬁers, indicators and products; and specify the qualitative phase. The rule can be defeated
by independently demonstrated omitted execution. It does not protect an internal-only bound-
ary or forbid world-involving constitution.

4.6   Application to native comparison
In an ordinary appearance-directed comparison, current source use, task contingency, content-
sensitive history, sensory inspection and operative uptake can support convergence between
A and P . The qualitative scene is treated as the way the comparison is undergone, not
merely a true fact about the answer. The physical nomination follows the native acquisi-
tion/comparison engagement, not the output gate. The corrected rule thereby has a positive
candidate and a literal mode to place.
    The activity tether is indispensable. The documented looked-equal staircase and
appearance-directed task history support the ordinary sensory-comparison interpretation;
they are not dedicated basing measurements. Evidence of Q’s presence somewhere near an
answer would not earn its projection as the sensory way of that comparison. The supported
target must include actual source-directed use or ordinary basing. That conjunction can be
projected to a new exercise without requiring current Q as a class-membership gate. The
evidence is not statistically independent: source history and task context contribute to several
assessments. The logical roles of the premises nevertheless diﬀer, and none is justiﬁed by its
desired conclusion.
    This basing judgment need not already identify a physical content vehicle as Q’s bearer.
Genuine acquaintance with a current apparent relation can guide an agent’s act through a
derivative physical reason vehicle. A genuinely exclusive qualitative bearer could permit that
same source-guided activity. Identifying the experience-inclusive activity A with physical P
remains the further constitutive step. Supplement Module I makes this separation explicit
and distinguishes an operative current basis from faithful transmission of an earlier source.
    A cached answer merely triggered by acquaintance can bypass the apparent relation’s
content. A faithful message can instead lead to a fresh current ﬁeld that alone supplies the
comparison’s sensory basis. Both cases matter: semantic agreement or a measurable eﬀect
of a physical channel does not identify the current qualitative source. Conversely, if current
appearance-directed comparison is independently warranted, gating its later expression tests
that expression rather than discovering its earlier basing. These distinctions preserve the ordi-
nary positive inference while preventing it from borrowing an unearned physical-constitution
premise.

5.3      Exactly what the application supports
The strongest appearance claim is not that equal physical diameters have become unequal.
It concerns source-relevant apparent magnitudes and their presented spatial/comparative re-

inferred from serial presentations, so the code alone does not entail the target.

    The activity claim likewise goes beyond common owner, time and content. On the ordinary
protocol interpretation, this presentation is a sensory way of the participant’s source-directed
comparison. If a separate automatic route instead produced the key while a ﬁeld merely
accompanied it, the answer-producing and sensory performances would need to be distin-

same physical/report process can support a competing exclusive-bearer account, considered

5.4   What could challenge the inference
The rule expects current relational appearance in further materially continuing exercises of
V, without using their present appearance verdict as admission. Source/context variation
can change the contents while preserving the narrow activity kind. Motor-code remapping
can preserve the sensory comparison while changing the reported bit; copying or replacing
the bit cannot itself establish that comparison. A documented optical interruption, source
substitution or changed task program can alter the physical regime, whereas a current wrong
answer alone cannot retroactively do so.
   The empirical claims can be challenged separately. Credible absence of the projected
appearance in an independently maintained eligible exercise would count against occurrence
projection; a missed response alone would not establish that absence. Evidence that the
participant used only serial appearances and descriptors, with no current qualitative pair even
at uptake, would undermine jointness. Serial eye movements alone would not suﬃce, since
a new pair-scene could ﬁrst appear at J. Evidence of a cached or automatic answer route,
independently bypassing the relation’s current sensory use while the ﬁeld merely accompanied
the answer, would challenge basing. The present archive does not supply these stronger
observations or a calibrated rate for any of them. An unsuccessful eligible case cannot be
dismissed by deﬁning a new subtype as the successful trials; its physical boundary would need
independent support.
    These possible ﬁndings concern the occurrence and role of appearance. They would change
premises to which the constitutive principle is applied. An exclusive-bearer account that
grants the same appearances, basing and physical records instead contests the placement
principle itself. More accurate prediction of those shared records would not choose between
the accounts. The illustration therefore contributes a concrete, criticisable application of the
argument while leaving prospective validation and the constitutive disagreement distinct.

6     Three serious alternatives and their diﬀerent force
6.1   Serial appearances without a joint scene
The serial-only account grants sensory appearances, physical source sensitivity and competent
comparison. One magnitude appears, its descriptor is retained, another appears, and a relation
proposition is inferred. The account can reproduce the entire behavioural record without ever
presenting the two counterparts together. It challenges Q’s jointness rather than the physical
nomination or source dependence.
    The response is evidential, not a theorem that comparison entails a ﬁeld. Ordinary current
scene acquaintance and the appearance-directed protocol give reason to interpret the activity
as a joint presentation. That interpretation is not defeated merely because the eyes inspect
regions successively: successive acquisition can produce a currently joint scene. But evidence
that a person used serial-only imagery or an inference-only strategy would directly weaken
the target claim. A later genuinely joint J presentation would satisfy the target; a word
expressing the inferred relation would not by itself do so.
    This is the most important limitation of the archive’s phenomenal evidence. There is no
dedicated jointness or actual-basing probe. It remains legitimate to use ordinary appearance
evidence at a modest level, while admitting that a stronger validation study would need an
independent measure and eligible negative cases. The physical response does not repair a
suspended jointness interpretation.

6.2   A ﬁeld accompanying an automated answer
The passive-accompaniment account grants actual joint Q in the participant. A parallel path,
however, computes the answer from physical/context classes independently of the experienced
relation. The participant can look at the pair while using a learned strategy. Thus Q, the
source pair, owner, phase and matching answer can all coexist without Q being the sensory
mode of the chosen answer-producing operation.
    This attacks activity tether and performance individuation. It is not a denial of appearance
and not yet a diﬀerent bearer ontology. Common actor or temporal overlap cannot settle
it. The appropriate correction is to distinguish the sensory engagement from the automated
response performance, or to narrow the nominated activity to the actual sensory/comparative
execution. Enlarging a task-window box until it contains both would evade rather than solve
the issue.
    The positive case uses ordinary source-directed sensory basing. The appearance-directed
instructions, initial looked-equal staircase, fresh contextual inputs and uptake support the
interpretation that the current comparison is performed through the apparent relation. These
grounds are fallible, and the data do not separately rule out unnoticed automatisation. The
projected target is Q as a sensory way of comparative activity; a matching correct answer
alone does not supply that tether. A discovered bypass changes attribution even if appearance
survives.

6.3   A genuinely exclusive experiential bearer
The strongest hybrid grants one agent, actual jointness, genuine qualitative grounding, the
activity tether and the complete physical/report process. Physical member R can be operative
in a mixed act K ∗ while qualitative B is its literal experiential member. B need not perform
another entire comparison, add a physical force or require a trial-speciﬁc manifestation law.
Describing this account as a second comparator or an idle epiphenomenon would miss its

                                              18
strongest form.
    The disagreement concerns instantiation. If B names a qualitative trope of P , the account
is compatible with the proposed bearer claim. If it names a mode of the physical animal at
a diﬀerent grain, its grain requires justiﬁcation. If it is genuinely exclusive of the nominated
performance, it challenges conservative mode placement even when the other four claims
survive.
    The present argument prefers continuity of the positively tracked performance and its
literal way of occurring. Grounding by Q does not itself require distinct instantiation outside
P ; the same grounding role can be attributed to a mode of P . A diﬀerent property type
does not positively locate another bearer. However, an independently defended ontology
of phenomenal instantiation could justify the exclusive account. So could evidence of an
omitted current sensory executor or a distinct continuity pattern that the physical nomination
overlooks.
   Both positions must specify how their bearer is tracked, how it participates in the activity,
and what replacements, divisions or extensions would defeat the attribution. The preference
here rests on a general constitutive default and actual reference convergence. It claims neither
an ontology-neutral theorem nor a statistical win. The hybrid’s coherence limits certainty
without making every defended attribution arbitrary; mere failure to detect B would not
positively justify identity.

6.4   Other correction cases
Hidden state or strategy changes can undermine the native-capacity projection while leaving
metadata and outputs similar. Eligible contrary appearances must be retained; an inferred
“conscious-capacity” gate cannot remove them. A downstream physical constructor may gen-
erate the new J third and belong to the inclusive current performance; this supports no
claim that an earlier supplier alone is suﬃcient. Independently performing remote, prosthetic
or world contributors can require enlarging or revising the nomination. Internal anatomical
closure is not a premise.
    Finally, an incorrect answer can coexist with a joint ﬁeld. It may indicate wrong content
classiﬁcation, task misunderstanding, motor error or altered basing. It is not automatic
absence of Q. Likewise, occurrence does not make every phenomenal judgment knowledge.
These correction cases preserve the diﬀerences between target actuality, response accuracy,
competent expression and constitution.


7     The achieved connection and its scope
The result is a positive argument for current relational appearance inclusively borne by a
physically organised engagement in the stated native-human regime. The occurrence side uses
a projectible sensory capacity whose physical admission conditions exclude present Q. The
constitutive side uses an independently motivated and corrected concrete-performance/mode
principle. A worked retrospective illustration exhibits how the two can be combined without
treating an answer bit, a conditional bound or a causal score as the missing phenomenal
premise.
    Separating the warrants makes the connection assessable. The reader can accept the
physical nomination while disputing jointness, accept joint Q while disputing its activity
tether, or accept the tether while disputing bearer reidentiﬁcation. Each objection calls for
diﬀerent evidence or principles. The conclusion inherits the fallibility of those premises while
continuing to concern actual appearance, not merely computational compatibility.

The existing monograph and development paper already propose whole-process constitution
and native biological coupling, and develop source/readout distinctions and scaﬀold feedback

7.2    Boundaries and future tests
A ﬁxed retrospective neural prediction assay supplies a useful negative check on the tempta-
tion to identify a physical score with the needed bridge. Adding the speciﬁed neural feature
worsened the primary held-out Brier score from 0.2854131 to 0.2983571; improvement in a sec-
ondary ranking measure did not satisfy that criterion. The comparison concerns the archived
prediction target, not an independently calibrated label of actual relational appearance. Its
failure neither refutes the current-act argument nor warrants a replacement consciousness de-
tector. Supplement Module J gives the ﬁxed split, models, exact scores, source dependencies
and veriﬁcation limits.
    No universal necessary-and-suﬃcient occurrence law, reductive quality map, smallest tis-
sue bearer, all-machine detector or ﬁrst-onset estimate is established. The result leaves
awareness-ﬁrst origins, subject partition, alternative physical grains, and the A3/animal conti-
nuity disagreement open. Bounded agency, recursively closed observer proposals and machine

alignment remain distinct projects requiring their own realization and appearance-facing war-
rant (Rodgers, 2026a,j,b). Operational revision, self-reference or a generated artifact cannot
inherit this human-native conclusion by matching outputs.
    A stronger empirical assessment would freeze physical eligibility, preserve contrary cases,
and obtain independent jointness and basing evidence. Manipulations separating motor
remapping from sensory-source replacement would test whether the forecast follows the nom-

time-sensitive appearance anchor. These are discriminating extensions with identiﬁable obli-
gations, rather than demands that every question be settled before this argument can stand.

7.3   Conclusion
Within the speciﬁed materially continuing native-human comparison regime, current qualita-
tive counterparts and their presented relation have defensible empirical and phenomenological
support. A corrected concrete-performance/mode principle supports attributing that qualita-
tive way to the organised physical engagement at its qualifying phase. Delayed construction
at current uptake is compatible with this connection; serial-only presentation, passive accom-
paniment and exclusive instantiation remain distinct possible defeats of its premises.
     These warrants advance the connecting obligation through an actual, scoped inference.
Sensory projectibility and concrete-performance reasoning supply its force. They also preserve
its limits: a timing bound does not identify onset, a physical supplier need not be the bearer,
and the constitutive attribution remains open to independent criticism.


A     Identity, mode and the scope of substitution
For the main argument, P and A denote concrete performances at a ﬁxed activity grain; τ
denotes the relevant qualitative phase. Mode(Q, x, τ ) is a literal predication about how x
occurs at that phase. It is not shorthand for “an output of x describes Q” or “Q occurs
somewhere inside a region containing x”.

Proposition A.1 (Mode preservation under reidentiﬁcation). In ordinary identity logic,
A = P and Mode(Q, A, τ ) entail Mode(Q, P, τ ). Neither Causes(P, A), Contains(P, A) nor
Constitutes(P, A) alone supplies that entailment.

Proof. The positive conclusion is substitution of identicals in the predicate’s bearer position.
For each claimed replacement relation, take two distinct objects a, p, interpret that relation as
true of (p, a), and make Mode(Q, a, τ ) true but Mode(Q, p, τ ) false. These are countermodels
unless an additional mode-preservation axiom is imposed on the relation. An imposed axiom
is a further constitutive commitment, not identity logic.

    The proposition does not establish A = P . That premise is the defeasible conclusion of
the corrected concrete-performance principle and actual participation/reference assessment in
Section 4. It transfers neither Q to every part nor Q to every containing system. If diﬀerent
temporal grains are used, a warranted correspondence between the qualifying phases is also
required; the whole response interval is not silently substituted for τ .
